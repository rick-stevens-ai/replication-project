\section*{Open Questions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Open Questions}
Five open research questions arising directly from this replication (full machine-readable
form in \texttt{report/open\_questions.json}):

\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textbf{Q1}] \textbf{Loop-map-driven clustering.} Does the GLOBLE giant-loop
clustering assumption hold when DSB spatial distributions are taken from measured
Hi-C / chromatin-loop maps rather than idealized uniform $\sim$2\,Mbp domains?
\emph{Next:} drive the clustering kernel with cell-line-specific loop-size
distributions from Hi-C data and test whether fitted $\varepsilon_i/\varepsilon_c$
ratios track measured loop statistics.

\item[\textbf{Q2}] \textbf{Recovering the blocked empirical overlay.} Is the exact
pointwise overlay against measured survival curves (blocked Claim 7) recoverable by
re-digitizing the paper's figure PDFs, and does GLOBLE still fit within experimental
error across all 17 lines? \emph{Next:} controlled WebPlotDigitizer pass with
uncertainty propagation and no-refit $\chi^2$ goodness-of-fit.

\item[\textbf{Q3}] \textbf{Column-dependent median HLT$_i$.} Why does the paper's
median HLT$_i$ (0.458\,h) match only the split-dose column and not the dose-rate
column (0.487\,h)? \emph{Next:} test whether a single HLT$_i$ can jointly fit both
assay types, or whether the $\sim$6\% difference reflects a real dual-timescale
repair process.

\item[\textbf{Q4}] \textbf{Dose-rate limit and FLASH.} At what dose rate does the
high-dose-rate ``instantaneous'' limit break down for clinical fractionation, and
does GLOBLE remain accurate in the FLASH regime? \emph{Next:} map the convergence
residual vs dose rate per HLT$_i$ and confront emerging FLASH survival datasets.

\item[\textbf{Q5}] \textbf{Extension to mixed-LET / ions.} Can the five-level
DSB-clustering framework extend to ion beams without abandoning the giant-loop
assumption, and does $\varepsilon_i \ll \varepsilon_c$ survive at high LET?
\emph{Next:} couple GLOBLE to a track-structure DSB generator, recompute
$\varepsilon_i/\varepsilon_c$ vs LET, and test whether an intra-track clustering
term is required.
\end{enumerate}
