# Brief

Independent replication of Shekarpaz, Dong, & Huang 2025 (ApJ 990:161, DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/adf3ac; OSTI 3362822), which builds a DeepONet surrogate for the electric-field-energy evolution of the Landau-damping process in a 1D-1V Vlasov–Poisson plasma, trained on the authors' Gkeyll continuum-Vlasov reference solutions. Because the paper's Gkeyll dataset is not published (authors direct requests to themselves), we generated our own reference solutions using a Fourier-spectral / semi-Lagrangian 1D-1V Vlasov–Poisson solver, verified against the analytic linear-Landau dispersion relation, then trained a DeepONet of matching architecture (single-mode case: k=0.35, A=0.05, T∈[0.5, 1.5], 200 train / 50 test). Our sim recovers γ(k=0.35) within ~12% of the analytic rate (−0.038 vs −0.034); our DeepONet cleanly reproduces the qualitative claim (electric-field-energy operator is learnable) and the DeepONet inference-vs-sim speedup (~10⁵× per case, matching paper order-of-magnitude) but with a numerical single-mode test rel-L2 error norm about 25–30× larger than the paper's 0.008 mean (our ~0.20–0.26 vs paper's 0.0083), reflecting our simpler solver / different training data / shorter training budget rather than any refutation of the paper's method.
