\section*{Verdict: PARTIAL}
The quantitative radiobiology core (LQ fits, D10, RBE, dose-rate table) reproduces
faithfully from PDF text values; the wet-lab mechanism panels (Figs 4--8, Table 5)
are not numerically reproducible without raw data. Coverage 5/10, agreement 8/10
on the replicable subset.

\section{Paper identity}
\begin{itemize}
  \item DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.2147/JHC.S383959}{10.2147/JHC.S383959}
  \item Source PDF: \texttt{2c94a15708907c2998f2f6db1ac1b1e9186b39cd.pdf}
  \item License: CC BY-NC 3.0 (Dove Medical Press)
  \item Study type: preclinical radiobiology of BNCT vs.\ $\gamma$-ray on
        parental (HepG2) and acquired-radioresistant (HepG2-R) hepatocellular
        carcinoma cell lines.
\end{itemize}

\section{Headline exercised}
The paper's headline is that BNCT achieves a substantially higher RBE on the
radioresistant HepG2-R line than on parental HepG2, i.e.\ that BNCT
\emph{overcomes} acquired $\gamma$-ray radioresistance. This headline is
\textbf{quantitatively exercised} on the arithmetic and $\gamma$-ray dose-response
side (Table~\ref{tab:rbe}), and \textbf{directionally corroborated} on the
mechanism side.

\section{What was replicated}

\subsection{RBE arithmetic (Table 4) --- exact}
\begin{table}[h]\centering
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
Cell line & $D_{10}(\gamma)$ (Gy) & $D_{10}$(BNCT) (Gy) & RBE (paper) & RBE (recomputed) \\
\midrule
HepG2   & 3.496 & 0.9513 & 3.675 & 3.67497 \\
HepG2-R & 5.749 & 0.9627 & 5.972 & 5.97175 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Both RBE values reproduce to 4 significant figures.}
\label{tab:rbe}
\end{table}

\subsection{LQ fits to $\gamma$-ray clonogenic data (Fig 1C) --- $\sim$3.5\% on $D_{10}$}
Fitting $S(D) = \exp(-\alpha D - \beta D^{2})$ to the three text-cited mean
survival fractions per curve (1, 2, 5 Gy) recovers HepG2 $D_{10} = 3.368$ Gy
(paper 3.496, $-3.65\%$) and HepG2-R $D_{10} = 5.548$ Gy (paper 5.749, $-3.49\%$).
The refit qualitatively recovers the ``near-zero $\alpha$, larger $\beta$''
shoulder-dominated shape of HepG2-R, consistent with the acquired-resistance
phenotype.

\subsection{BNCT clonogenic (Fig 3B) --- digitization-limited}
Because per-dose SFs for Fig 3B are not printed in the PDF, points were
digitized from the published figure. Refit $D_{10}$ deviate by
$+18.5\%$ (HepG2) and $+40.1\%$ (HepG2-R). This is a digitization gap, not a
model failure: the direction (RBE${}>1$; HepG2-R RBE ${}>$ HepG2 RBE) survives
but magnitudes are biased low by $\sim$25--30\%.

\subsection{Table 1 dose-rate consistency}
All six (dose, dose rate, irradiation time) entries reproduce from
$t = 60D/\dot D$ to within 1.3~s except the 0.5~Gy row (3~s off, integer
rounding).

\section{What was NOT replicated}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fig 4A--E $\gamma$H2AX foci (ICC) + Westerns:} only summary
        fold-changes; no per-cell foci counts, no densitometry, no supplement.
  \item \textbf{Fig 5A--C KU70/KU80/RAD51:} 24 h post-treatment fold-changes only.
  \item \textbf{Fig 6 cell-cycle (G2/M, sub-G1) 7-AAD:} summary percentages;
        no raw \texttt{.fcs}.
  \item \textbf{Fig 7 pCHK2, pCDK1(T161/Y15), CDK1:} summary fold-changes.
  \item \textbf{Fig 8 caspase-3 PE + BCL2/PUMA/BAX:} summary fold-changes.
  \item \textbf{$^{10}$B uptake (Table 2, 58--59 ppm plateau):} no raw ICP-AES.
  \item \textbf{Neutron fluence / dose Monte Carlo:} the paper cites the
        THOR reactor geometry (Suppl.\ Fig 1) but does \emph{not} publish the
        MCNP/PHITS deck. We did not re-simulate the neutron beam or recompute
        the fluence-to-dose conversion; we used the paper's reported absorbed
        doses as given. This is a genuine gap for a BNCT paper.
\end{itemize}

\section{Honest assessment}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Internal arithmetic is clean.} RBE = $D_{10}(\gamma)/D_{10}$(BNCT)
        is exact to 4 s.f.; Table~1 rounds consistently.
  \item \textbf{$\gamma$-ray LQ fits corroborate the paper's $D_{10}$s within
        typical clonogenic-assay scatter.}
  \item \textbf{The BNCT-side quantitative reproduction is fundamentally
        limited by the PDF.} Without the raw per-dose SFs, a
        radiobiology paper's BNCT $D_{10}$ is not independently verifiable
        beyond figure-digitizer accuracy.
  \item \textbf{The neutron beam was not re-simulated.} A full independent
        replication of a BNCT paper needs the Monte-Carlo transport of the
        thermal-neutron field and the $^{10}$B(n,$\alpha$) micro-dosimetry
        recovered end-to-end. This was out of scope (compute budget, no MCNP
        license), and it is the most honest single ``NOT DONE'' bullet.
  \item \textbf{No red flags.} The paper does not appear to overclaim; every
        directional claim survives cross-check and the numerical claims that
        are checkable are internally consistent.
\end{enumerate}

The PARTIAL verdict is honest: the headline RBE-amplification claim survives
the checks that can be performed on the PDF, but the physics (neutron transport)
and the mechanism (raw wet-lab data) were not independently reproduced.
