# BVBRC-72 brief

Independent replication of Liu et al. 2020 (J Microbiol Biotechnol, doi:10.4014/jmb.1906.06055, PMID 31601062, PMC9728402) — "Genomics and LC-MS Reveal Diverse Active Secondary Metabolites in Bacillus amyloliquefaciens WS-8". The paper reports a complete PacBio SMRT-sequenced 3,929,787 bp WS-8 chromosome (no plasmid), NCBI-annotated at 3,895 predicted genes / 3,777 CDS / 86 tRNA / 27 rRNA / 5 ncRNA, with 19 antiSMASH-3 predicted secondary-metabolite BGCs — 7 with >70% similarity to known BGCs (fengycin, iturin, surfactin, bacillibactin, bacillaene, macrolactin, difficidin, bacilysin) plus one class-II lanthipeptide cluster — and LC-MS confirmation that iturins and fengycins are the dominant antifungal lipopeptides. We pulled the deposited genome (GenBank CP018200.1 / assembly GCF_001922005.1, BioProject PRJNA354791) from NCBI, recomputed genome-level statistics with Biopython, and ran a fresh antiSMASH web + local (v7 relaxed / v8 with KnownClusterBlast) BGC prediction.
