## 1. Verdict (one paragraph)

This is a **wet-lab in vivo mouse paper**, mis-tagged as "simulation/model replication" in the LUCID100 master TSV. There is no model, no simulation, no equation, no code. The single high-throughput dataset (bulk RNA-seq of FACS-sorted LSK cells, n=3/group, 1 h post 4 Gy, commissioned to BGI) **was not deposited** (NCBI GEO returns 0 hits; data-availability is "available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request"). All quantitative figures rest on confocal imaging or flow cytometry analyzed in GraphPad Prism 6.01 with Student's t-test on n=3–6 mice/group, with no underlying numerical tables published. **Independent quantitative replication from public artifacts is not feasible.** First-pass artifact harvest is **partial-but-clean**: main PDF (4.34 MB), JATS XML (130 KB), full text (679 lines), 6 figure JPEGs, and a full qPCR primer table were captured. All six supplementary files (S1–S6) are blocked behind a Wiley anti-bot wall and a broken PMC re-render pipeline; their *contents* are inferable from in-text references (sex-matched control panels, female-mouse parallel figures, additional replicate plots) but their numerical contents are unreadable.

## 2. Paper at a glance

- **Question:** Does RPRM knockout protect mouse hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) from ionizing radiation, and through what mechanism?
- **Model system:** RPRM⁻/⁻ ("KO") vs RPRM⁺/⁺ ("WT") C57BL/6 mice, 6–8 weeks old, both sexes, SPF facility at Soochow University. RPRM-KO line was generated by the same lab in Zhang et al. 2021.
- **Insult:** Whole-body X-irradiation on an X-RAD320ix (320 kVp, PXi) at 2.0 Gy/min; doses 4 Gy (main) or 6 Gy (Fig 3n–o).
- **Pharmacology:** ± Erlotinib (selective EGFR inhibitor, Selleck); ± NU7441 (selective DNA-PKcs inhibitor, Selleck, 10 mg/kg); ± G-CSF (2 µg/20 g, Novoprotein) as positive radioprotectant control.
- **Readouts:** (i) BM/peripheral CBC; (ii) flow phenotyping of LSK, LT-HSC, ST-HSC, MPP, CMP, GMP, MEP, CLP; (iii) γ-H2AX (flow + IF); (iv) alkaline comet assay tail moment; (v) imaging-flow p-DNA-PKcs(T2609), p-EGFR(Y1068), Lin28a; (vi) BrdU + DAPI cell-cycle; (vii) Ki67 + 7-AAD cell-cycle; (viii) MethoCult colony-forming assay; (ix) RT-qPCR of 10 genes (Ccl11, IL-13, TNF-α, RPRM, IL-1α, IL-1β, MCP-1, Lin28a, EGFR, Xrcc6) normalized to GAPDH; (x) Western blot for Lin28a, p-STAT3(Y705); (xi) one bulk RNA-seq experiment (Fig 4a KEGG bubble chart).
- **Statistics:** Student's t-test, GraphPad Prism 6.01. p<.05 significance. Effect sizes/CIs not reported as numerical tables.

## 6. What a true replication would require

### 6a. Direct replication (out-of-scope per task; documented for completeness)
- The Soochow RPRM⁻/⁻ C57BL/6 line under MTA, *or* an independently generated RPRM-KO line (CRISPR knockout in a standard C57BL/6J background).
- IACUC-approved BSL-1 mouse facility + X-ray cabinet (X-RAD320ix or equivalent at 320 kVp, 2.0 Gy/min).
- Standard hematology + multi-color flow panel for mouse HSCs (Lin/c-Kit/Sca-1/CD48/CD150/CD16-32/CD34/CD127 stack); erlotinib + NU7441 pharmacology; comet assay kit; BrdU.
- Bulk RNA-seq of sorted LSK cells, n≥3/group/sex.
- **Time/cost estimate:** 6–9 person-months, ~$80–150 k including animal costs; needs a hematology immunologist + a radiation source. **Not feasible in the LUCID100 in-silico framework.**

### 6b. Computational-only proxy (potential future work — flag for separate slot)
1. Re-mine a public sorted-LSK or BM HSC RNA-seq series in GEO that includes a 1–24 h post-IR timepoint (e.g., GSE145976 LSK irradiation, GSE172252 HSC stress series — these need verification, not done in this slot). Check whether EGFR / Lin28a / Xrcc6 / Egfr-target genes follow the direction the paper claims in a *different* genetic background (no RPRM KO available, so this would test only the "EGFR-axis activates DNA repair" half).
2. Use a published p53/RPRM regulatory network (e.g., from STRING + the cited Ohki 2000, Saavedra 2015 work) to **scope** whether RPRM-loss → EGFR-upregulation is a topologically plausible direct or one-hop relationship. This would be a *hypothesis-pretest*, not a replication.
3. **If the Fang 2020 Theranostics paper** (10.7150/thno.60143, cited as ref Fang 2020) **deposited HSC RNA-seq**, that is the more replicable anchor for the EGFR→DNA-PKcs→HSC-radioprotection axis. Recommend a *separate* LUCID100 slot for Fang 2020 before attempting any computational anchor for the EGFR claim in this paper.
