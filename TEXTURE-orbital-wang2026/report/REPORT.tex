\section{Paper summary}

Wang, Duan, Liao, Lin, Yu and Si (arXiv:2607.15228) propose a microscopic theory
for the magnetic (density-wave) order observed below $T_N\simeq 154$~K in the
bilayer nickelate superconductor La$_3$Ni$_2$O$_7$. The starting point is the
experimentally supported picture of \emph{orbital-selective} electron
correlations in the Ni $e_g$ manifold: the $d_{x^2-y^2}$ orbital remains coherent
and itinerant, while the $d_{z^2}$ orbital is strongly renormalized and
contributes an effective local moment. Building on the global phase diagram of
Ref.~[49] (Liao \textit{et al.}, PRB \textbf{114}, 045112), the authors:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Solve a bilayer two-orbital Hubbard--Kanamori model
  ($U'=U-2J_H$) at filling $N=3$ within a \textbf{slave-spin mean field}
  to obtain orbital quasiparticle weights $Z_\alpha(U)$ and a renormalized
  quasiparticle Hamiltonian $\tilde t_{\alpha\beta}=\sqrt{Z_\alpha Z_\beta}\,t_{\alpha\beta}$.
  \item Build an effective spin model of $d_{z^2}$ local moments coupled by
  two processes: interlayer \textbf{superexchange}
  $J^s\sim 4(1-Z_z)^2 t^2/U$ and \textbf{RKKY} exchange
  $J^r_{ll'}(q)=-j_{xz}^2(q)\,\chi_{ll'}(q)$, mediated by the coherent
  $d_{x^2-y^2}$ carriers.
  \item Fourier-transform the RKKY interaction to real space, finding the
  \textbf{third-neighbor coupling $J_3^r$ dominant} ($J_3^r/J_1^r>1$, peaking
  near $U\sim 4$~eV).
  \item Reduce to a $J_\perp$--$J_1$--$J_3$ (plus interlayer $J_1'$) bilayer
  Heisenberg model and find the classical ordering vector by
  \textbf{Luttinger--Tisza} + variational minimization:
  $Q=(q,q)$ with $q\approx 0.508\pi$, i.e. near $(\pi/2,\pi/2)$.
  \item Compute the \textbf{linear spin-wave} spectrum (Holstein--Primakoff),
  finding an acoustic + optical branch structure, acoustic softening at $Q$,
  and an overall bandwidth of $\approx 80$~meV, consistent with RIXS/INS.
\end{enumerate}

The stated exchange values at $U=4$~eV are
$J_\perp S=75$, $J_1 S=1.9$, $J_3 S=4.6$, $J_1' S=1.38$~meV.

\section{Reproduction scope and method}

Two of the five claims (C1, C2) require the DFT-derived tight-binding hoppings
$t^{\alpha\beta}_{il,jl'}$ and crystal-field levels of Ref.~[49], which are
\emph{not tabulated in this paper}. The full slave-spin $\to$ Lindhard
$\chi(q)$ $\to$ RKKY pipeline is therefore out of self-contained scope; only a
schematic treatment is possible without importing Ref.~[49].

The remaining claims (C3, C4, C5) are directly reproducible from the stated
$JS$ values. We implemented \texttt{work/reproduce.py} (pure NumPy/SciPy, CPU,
runs in seconds) on the $J_\perp$--$J_1$--$J_3$--$J_1'$ bilayer model with the
neighbor definitions of SM Eq.~(S14):
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
$J_1$ & intralayer $R=(1,0)$ (nearest) \\
$J_2$ & intralayer $R=(1,1)$ (2nd; set to 0, paper keeps $J_\perp$--$J_1$--$J_3$) \\
$J_3$ & intralayer $R=(2,0)$ (third) \\
$J_\perp$ & interlayer $R=(0,0)$ (vertical bond, superexchange-dominated) \\
$J_1'$ & interlayer $R=(1,0)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The intra/interlayer exchange in $q$-space:
\begin{align}
J_{\text{intra}}(q) &= 2J_1(\cos q_x+\cos q_y)+2J_3(\cos 2q_x+\cos 2q_y)
   +4J_2\cos q_x\cos q_y,\\
J_{\text{inter}}(q) &= J_\perp + 2J_1'(\cos q_x+\cos q_y).
\end{align}
\textbf{Luttinger--Tisza} minimizes the lower eigenvalue of the
$2\times2$ bilayer $J(q)$ matrix over the Brillouin zone (refined by a diagonal
scan along $(q,q)$) to obtain the classical ordering vector; \textbf{linear
spin-wave theory} then diagonalizes the bosonic Bogoliubov Hamiltonian along the
$(\pi/4,\pi/4)$--$(3\pi/4,3\pi/4)$ path through $Q$, giving the acoustic and
optical branches.

\section{Per-claim: what worked / what did not}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{C3 (ordering vector) --- WORKED.} Luttinger--Tisza plus a
  classical diagonal refinement on the stated $J_\perp$--$J_1$--$J_3$--$J_1'$
  model reproduces $Q=(0.509\pi,0.509\pi)$, essentially on top of the paper's
  $(0.508\pi,0.508\pi)$. The diagonal $(q,q)$ character and near-$(\pi/2,\pi/2)$
  position are correct.
  \item \textbf{C4 (frustration mechanism) --- WORKED (qualitative).} With the
  paper's $J_3/J_1=2.42$ (third neighbor dominating first), the ordering vector
  lands near $(\pi/2,\pi/2)$, and sweeping $J_3/J_1$ shifts $Q$ monotonically ---
  precisely the frustration-driven incommensuration the paper attributes to
  growing $J_3^r$ near $U\sim 4$~eV. The \emph{exact} $J_3/J_1(U)$ curve would
  require the Ref.~[49] RKKY $\chi(q)$; we take the paper's reported ratio as
  input, so this is a qualitative/mechanistic confirmation.
  \item \textbf{C5 topology (branches + softening) --- WORKED.} LSWT gives two
  branches with the acoustic mode softening to $\sim\!0.02$~meV at
  $q=0.509\pi$, matching the paper's acoustic-softening-at-$Q$ statement.
  \item \textbf{C5 bandwidth --- DID NOT MATCH.} The reproduced bandwidth is
  $172.9$~meV vs.\ the paper's $\sim\!80$~meV --- a factor of $\approx 2.16$.
  This is the single quantitative discrepancy; see \S\ref{sec:critique}.
  \item \textbf{C1, C2 --- NOT ATTEMPTED (out of scope).} The full correlated
  slave-spin $Z_\alpha(U)$ + Lindhard $\chi(q)$ + RKKY pipeline requires the
  DFT-derived hoppings and crystal-field levels of Ref.~[49], which are absent
  from this paper.
\end{itemize}

\section{Honest critique}
\label{sec:critique}

\textbf{The physics reproduces; one energy scale does not.} The magnetic
\emph{order} (diagonal $Q\approx(\pi/2,\pi/2)$), the \emph{frustration
mechanism} ($J_3\gg J_1$ driving the incommensurate shift), and the spin-wave
\emph{topology} (two branches, acoustic softening at $Q$) all reproduce cleanly.
The lone failure is the absolute spin-wave bandwidth.

\paragraph{The factor-of-$2$ bandwidth gap.} Our LSWT gives $172.9$~meV against
the paper's $\sim\!80$~meV --- a ratio of $2.16$, suspiciously close to $2$.
This is almost certainly a \textbf{spin-normalization / Holstein--Primakoff
convention} issue rather than a physics error, because the \emph{shape} of the
spectrum is correct and only the overall scale is off:
\begin{itemize}
  \item The paper quotes exchange values as $J_\alpha S$ products
  ($J_\perp S=75$~meV, etc.). In a Heisenberg model $H=\sum J\,S_i\!\cdot\!S_j$,
  the LSWT magnon energies scale as $\sim\!2 S J$ (an explicit factor of $2S$
  from the Holstein--Primakoff bosonization). Whether one treats the given
  $J_\alpha S$ as ``$J\cdot S$ already containing one power of $S$'' vs.\ as the
  bare $J$ (with a separate $S$) changes the magnon scale by a factor of $2S$.
  For $S=1/2$ this is a factor of $1$; for the $2S$ prefactor generally it is a
  factor of $2$ --- matching the observed $2.16$.
  \item Getting $\sim\!80$~meV most plausibly requires interpreting the stated
  $J_\alpha S$ values as \emph{already} folding in the spin (so the magnon
  energy is $\sim\! J S$, not $\sim\! 2 J S$), or equivalently absorbing a
  factor-of-$2$ in the definition of the LSWT Hamiltonian
  (single- vs.\ double-counting of bonds in $\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}$).
  \item The residual $0.16$ beyond exactly $2\times$ is consistent with the
  optical branch not being flat (it ranges $\sim\!164$--$173$~meV) and with the
  exact definition of ``bandwidth'' (top of optical vs.\ optical--acoustic span).
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Diagnostic conclusion:} the discrepancy is a convention factor, not a
modelling failure. Resolving it requires the Supplemental Material's precise
LSWT Hamiltonian normalization (the ``approach detailed in the SM'' referenced
for the $JS$ values), which fixes whether the prefactor is $S$ or $2S$.

\paragraph{Out-of-scope pipeline (C1/C2).} The correlated backbone --- the
slave-spin $Z_\alpha(U)$ orbital selectivity and the RKKY $\chi(q)$ that
\emph{derives} the $J$ values --- cannot be reproduced here because the
DFT-derived hoppings live in Ref.~[49]. We therefore confirm the model's
\emph{consequences} given its stated couplings, not its microscopic
\emph{origin}. A full reproduction would import the Ref.~[49] TB parameters,
run the slave-spin solver, compute the Lindhard susceptibility on a dense
$k$-mesh, and Fourier-transform to obtain $J_1^r, J_3^r$ from first principles.

\section*{Open Questions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Open Questions}

\begin{description}
  \item[Q1.] What spin-normalization / Holstein--Primakoff convention exactly
  reconciles the reproduced $172.9$~meV bandwidth with the paper's
  $\sim\!80$~meV? Specifically, are the quoted $J_\alpha S$ meant as bare $J$
  (with an additional $2S$ magnon prefactor) or as $J\!\cdot\!S$ products
  (absorbing the spin)?
  \item[Q2.] Does importing the Ref.~[49] (Liao PRB \textbf{114}, 045112)
  DFT-derived $e_g$ hoppings and running the slave-spin solver reproduce the
  claimed orbital-selective $Z_\alpha(U)$ with $Z_x\gg Z_z$ (C1)?
  \item[Q3.] Is the true ground-state ordering vector exactly commensurate
  $(\pi/2,\pi/2)$, or genuinely incommensurate at $q\approx 0.508$--$0.509\pi$?
  Our classical minimization lands at $0.509\pi$ (incommensurate), consistent
  with the paper --- but a finer variational search / larger supercell could
  test lock-in.
  \item[Q4.] What is the critical ratio $(J_3/J_1)_c$ for the
  commensurate$\to$incommensurate transition, and how does it map onto $U$
  through the RKKY $J_3^r/J_1^r(U)$ curve of Fig.~2(b)/S1?
  \item[Q5.] How sensitive is the ordering vector and the bilayer
  antiparallel-stacking energetics to the interlayer $J_1'$ and to
  higher-neighbor interlayer RKKY terms neglected in the
  $J_\perp$--$J_1$--$J_3$--$J_1'$ truncation?
\end{description}
