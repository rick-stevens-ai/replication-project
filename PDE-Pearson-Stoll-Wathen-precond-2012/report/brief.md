**What / why:** Independent replication of Pearson, Stoll & Wathen, "Regularization-Robust Preconditioners for Time-Dependent PDE-Constrained Optimization Problems" (SIAM J. Matrix Anal. Appl. 33(4):1126–1152, 2012; DOI 10.1137/110847949). We implement the paper's block preconditioner for the all-at-once KKT system arising from a backward-Euler discretization of distributed heat-equation control on the unit square, and independently confirm the paper's headline claim: MINRES iteration counts are **bounded (essentially constant) as the mesh is refined and as the Tikhonov regularization parameter β varies over six orders of magnitude**, while unpreconditioned MINRES and a naive mass-matrix preconditioner both blow up.
