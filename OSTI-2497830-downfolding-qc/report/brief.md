# Replication Brief — OSTI-2497830

**Paper:** Alvertis, Khan, Tubman. "Compressing Hamiltonians with ab initio downfolding for simulating strongly-correlated materials on quantum computers." *Phys. Rev. Applied* **23**, 044028 (2025). [arXiv:2409.12237](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12237) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.23.044028) · OSTI 2497830 · FERMILAB-PUB-24-0896-SQMS-V.

**One-paragraph:** The paper combines ab-initio downfolding (DFT via Quantum ESPRESSO → maximally localized Wannier functions via Wannier90 → cRPA screened Coulomb via RESPACK/wan2respack) to derive compressed extended-Hubbard Hamiltonians for three strongly-correlated materials (Ca2CuO3, WTe2, SrVO3), then obtains their ground states using a classical tensor-network simulation of the Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) and benchmarks against DMRG. Independent replication reproduced the paper's Ca2CuO3 result at essentially machine precision — direct exact diagonalization of the paper's own 20-qubit 1-band extended-Hubbard model (10-site chain, t = −0.491 eV, U = 3.578 eV, V = 0.903 eV, half filling) from Appendix C.1 yields ground-state energy **6.005055 eV vs the paper's DMRG 6.005 eV (agreement ~0.1 meV)** and reproduces the alternating-sign antiferromagnetic spin correlation function ⟨C₁ⱼ⟩ of Fig. 3b exactly in sign pattern and closely in magnitude. WTe2 (32-qubit, 4-band) was not attempted as its Fock-space dimension exceeds direct ED; SrVO3 (54-qubit) was sanity-checked at reduced scale but the paper's 3×3 CDW claim requires the full tensor-network stack. **Verdict: PARTIAL** — the Ca2CuO3 claim (energies + AFM correlations) is fully and independently reproduced; the WTe2 and SrVO3 quantitative energies are out of reach for direct ED and were not rerun.
