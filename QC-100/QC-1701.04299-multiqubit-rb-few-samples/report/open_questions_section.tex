\section{Open questions and next steps}

Five concrete follow-up experiments raised (but not answered) by this
replication. Each is testable on free endpoints.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Does measured $\sigma_r(N)$ match the paper's predicted
  variance form, not just qualitatively?}\\
  \emph{Basis.} This replication verified the qualitative ``few samples
  suffice'' claim but did not overlay measured $\sigma_r(N)$ against the
  eq.\,(10) prediction $\sigma_r(N,m,r,d,u)$.\\
  \emph{Next.} Wider $N$ sweep with $N_\mathrm{boot}\geq 1000$; overlay
  measured vs predicted $\sigma_r(N)$ on log--log axes and quote
  $\chi^2$.

\item \textbf{Does ``few samples'' survive non-Markovian / coherent /
  crosstalk noise?}\\
  \emph{Basis.} Both the paper and this reproduction assume Markovian
  depolarizing noise; the variance bound is derived under that
  assumption.\\
  \emph{Next.} Swap the noise model to (a) coherent over-rotation,
  (b) $1/f$ dephasing, (c) crosstalk; re-run bootstrap; compare against
  the (formally inapplicable) Markovian bound.

\item \textbf{Does the claim carry over to character RB / cycle
  benchmarking (post-2017 RB variants)?}\\
  \emph{Basis.} 1701.04299's variance analysis is written for standard
  Clifford RB. Modern variants use different estimators whose
  sample-complexity may or may not inherit qubit-count independence.\\
  \emph{Next.} Re-derive an eq.-(10)-analogue for character RB; simulate
  on 2q Aer; check whether ``$N\!\sim\!20$'' suffices.

\item \textbf{Does the qubit-count independence (C5) hold in simulation
  at experimentally realistic $q=5,6$?}\\
  \emph{Basis.} C5 was spot-checked from Fig.\,2 only. At small $q$
  prefactors may still dominate.\\
  \emph{Next.} Parameterize over $q\in\{2,\dots,5\}$; plot $N$ needed
  for 2\,\% rel-std vs $q$; compare against eq.\,(11).

\item \textbf{Can ML-boosted estimators beat curve\_fit at $N\leq 5$?}\\
  \emph{Basis.} At very low $N$, \texttt{curve\_fit} on
  $A f^m + B$ becomes unstable; learned estimators trained on synthetic
  RB traces might extract $r$ more robustly.\\
  \emph{Next.} Generate 100k synthetic RB experiments; train a small
  MLP/1D-CNN mapping (survival vector, $m$-grid, $N$) $\rightarrow$
  $\hat r$; benchmark against curve\_fit+bootstrap at $N\in\{3,5,7,10\}$;
  test OOD robustness.
\end{enumerate}
