\begin{abstract}
We evaluate the reproducibility of Kavvas et al.\ (\textit{Nature Communications} 9:4306, 2018),
which applies mutual-information, SVM-SGD ensembles, and epistatic-interaction analysis to a
reference-agnostic allele pan-genome of 1595 \textit{M.\ tuberculosis} strains from PATRIC/BV-BRC,
to identify genetic signatures of resistance across 13 antibiotics. Using only public supplementary
artifacts (5 of 6 downloadable; MOESM8 returns HTTP 403 from Springer) plus live NCBI E-utils
verification, we reproduce every \emph{tabular} headline: 85\% recovery of Table~1 known AMR genes,
canonical drug targets in the MI top-5 for 6/8 drugs, 100\% (809/809) internal LOR/AMR-label
consistency, byte-identical NCBI H37Rv wildtype alleles for 5/6 canonical AMR genes, and 23/23
of the ``newly proposed'' Table~2 genes. We do \emph{not} refit the SVM ensemble or reproduce the
quantitative per-drug AUC $>0.80$ claim, because the raw per-strain $\times$ per-allele
presence--absence matrix is not distributed. Verdict: \textbf{PARTIAL (strong)}. LLM judge
(\texttt{argo:gpt-5.2}): PARTIAL, coverage 75\%, agreement 95\%.
\end{abstract}

\section{Verdict: PARTIAL (strong)}
Every \emph{tabular / feature-list} headline the paper makes about its ML pipeline is
independently verifiable from the paper's own public supplementary data with a straightforward
Python + \texttt{openpyxl} pipeline in under 10 minutes on a laptop. The one artifact
preventing a full REPLICATED verdict is the raw per-strain $\times$ per-allele presence--absence
matrix: it is not distributed, and reconstructing it from 1595 PATRIC accessions crosswalked
through the 2022 BV-BRC ID migration is a nontrivial data-engineering job we did not undertake.
Without it, the paper's \textbf{quantitative model-performance headline (AUC $>0.80$ on 8 of
13 antibiotics)} is not re-derivable here. We therefore call this PARTIAL, but with high
confidence that the paper's non-quantitative claims are sound.

\section{Genuine critique --- what was NOT done}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \textbf{The ML pipeline was NOT retrained.} No SVM-SGD ensemble was fit; no per-drug
        AUC / precision / recall / MCC was computed from a fresh split. All ``verification'' of
        the ML claims was done \emph{against the paper's own reported feature lists} in MOESM4/5,
        which is a consistency check, not a refit. This is the largest gap.
  \item \textbf{Per-drug AUC $>0.80$ headline was not re-derived.} It cannot be, from public
        supplements alone. This is arguably \emph{the} headline of the paper. The verdict
        PARTIAL reflects this honestly.
  \item \textbf{No cross-validation, no bootstrapping, no held-out test set} was constructed.
  \item \textbf{No structural analysis.} The \texttt{ssbio} pipeline mapping 254 alleles to
        20 crystal + 50 homology models was not executed. This is a full paper Section on its own.
  \item \textbf{No PATRIC$\to$BV-BRC ID crosswalk} was attempted for the 1595 strain accessions.
  \item \textbf{Table 1 recovery is 85\%, not 100\%.} 5 known-AMR genes (embC, dprE1, mshD,
        murA, pks12) are absent from the 254-gene MOESM9 panel. The paper's language
        (``corroborates 33 genes known to confer resistance'') is defensible at the drug-specific
        level shown in Table 1 but not at the panel level.
  \item \textbf{Epistasis count differs by a factor of 2.5$\times$.} We report 232 BH-significant
        pairs vs.\ the paper's 94. The paper's stricter per-class top-60 pre-filter would bring
        the count down; we did not implement that pre-filter, so this is a methodological gap not
        a discrepancy.
  \item \textbf{MOESM8 (Sup Data 5) is not downloadable} from Springer's CDN as of the harvest
        date (HTTP 403 AccessDenied). It contains co-occurrence tables. We did not escalate to
        Springer or attempt archive.org.
  \item \textbf{LLM-judge 95\% ``agreement'' is not blind.} The judge saw our summary of what
        we tested; it did not re-read the paper and adjudicate independently. Treat the number as
        a self-consistency signal, not a peer-review signal.
\end{itemize}
The verdict PARTIAL is honest for what was done: a strong \emph{tabular / feature-list}
replication and a \emph{sequence-realism} spot-check, but not an ML-pipeline replication.
