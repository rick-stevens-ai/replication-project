# Brief

**Paper:** Grimm & Eaves (2025), *Accurate Numerical Simulations of Open Quantum Systems Using Spectral Tensor Trains* (J. Chem. Phys.; OSTI 2564727, DOI 10.1063/5.0228873).

**What/why:** The paper introduces **Q-ASPEN**, a method that computes the noise-averaged reduced density matrix of the Stochastic Liouville Equation (SLE) by representing the influence kernel as a *spectral tensor train* and optimizing it with ML-style training. Its entire numerical construction rests on one **exact analytic statement** (their Eqs. 16–17): in the extrinsic **Markov** limit, averaging the SLE trajectories with white noise `<ξ(t)ξ(s)>=γδ(t−s)`, `H=H₀+ξ(t)V`, reduces *exactly* to the Lindblad master equation `d⟨ρ⟩/dt = −i[H₀,⟨ρ⟩] + γ(V⟨ρ⟩V − ½{V²,⟨ρ⟩})`. We independently reproduce this foundational claim from the equations alone (no paper code): we integrate the exact Lindblad ME as a reference and, separately, Monte-Carlo average the underlying SLE stochastic-Schrödinger trajectories for three distinct qubit systems (including the paper's Fig. 3 geometry). The two agree to sampling+Trotter precision, and an analytic dephasing case matches `0.5 e^{−2γt}` to machine precision. The full STT/tensor-train machinery and non-Markovian regime were not reimplemented (out of scope for a ~25 min replication).
