# Brief — BVBRC-99

Yamaguchi et al. (2018, *J Genomics* 6:30–33, PMID 29576807) is a complete-genome
announcement for the freshwater bloom-forming cyanobacterium
*Microcystis aeruginosa* NIES-2481 (group G, Lake Kasumigaura, Japan). They
report a 4.29-Mbp circular chromosome + 147,539-bp plasmid, 4,332 + 167
protein-coding genes, 42.91%/41.66% GC, 2 rRNA operons, 41 tRNA genes, 28
antiSMASH secondary-metabolite clusters (aeruginosin, micropeptin, microviridin
present, **microcystin absent**), 5 CRISPR loci, and a comparative analysis
against sister group-G strain NIES-2549 (identical 16S rRNA, tiny ~1.2-kbp
chromosome-size difference, small-scale synteny rearrangement). Because the raw
public artifacts (both NCBI GenBank chromosome and plasmid records) are
deposited under CP012375/CP025929 and CP011304/CP026286, this is an **ideal
"pull-and-verify" replication**: every genome-level quantitative claim can be
re-derived from the deposited sequences with Biopython + local BLAST.
