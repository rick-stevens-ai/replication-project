# Brief — OSTI 3367074

Huang, Fujii & Liang (Nucl. Sci. Tech. 37:150, 2026), "Taming nuclear mass models with Gaussian processes", claim that Gaussian-Process (Matérn-3/2 kernel) regression on the residuals of 8 nuclear mass models — using an 8-D physics-informed input space — pushes training-set RMSE below 100 keV on 2272 AME2016 nuclei and gives ≤150 keV RMSE on 74 new AME2020 nuclei for the best-performing models (DZ28, WS3), plus better empirical coverage than an RBF kernel. We independently pulled the raw AME2016 (2271 experimental nuclei with σ<100 keV, Z,N≥8 — 1 shy of the paper's 2272) and AME2020 (74 new nuclei — exact match) tables from the IAEA AMDC, fit a 5-parameter Bethe–Weizsäcker liquid-drop baseline as a stand-in bare mass model, and ran GP-2D + GP-8D with Matérn-3/2 and RBF kernels (scikit-learn) on uicgpu. The pipeline and reported reductions in training RMSE (bare 2900 keV → GP-2D-Matérn 179 keV; RBF 249 keV) plus the qualitative "Matérn beats RBF on coverage" claim are confirmed at the methodology level. Absolute test-set RMSEs on AME2020 exceed the paper's numbers because our bare LDM is much weaker than DZ28/WS3 and residual extrapolation inherits that.
