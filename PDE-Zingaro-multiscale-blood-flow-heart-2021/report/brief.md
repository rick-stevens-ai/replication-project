# Zingaro et al. 2021 — Replication Brief

**Paper:** *A geometric multiscale model for the numerical simulation of blood flow in the human left heart*, Zingaro/Fedele/Dede'/Quarteroni (2022, DCDS-S 15(8), 2391-2427; arXiv:2110.02114).

**What it claims.** A geometric multiscale left-heart CFD pipeline built on the `lifex` deal.II-based FE library at Politecnico Milano MOX. Couples 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes in ALE form for the left atrium + left ventricle + ascending aorta chamber (1.63 M tetrahedra, P1–P1, VMS-LES stabilization, BDF-1, Δt = 2.5·10⁻⁴ s) to a lumped-parameter (0D) closed-loop systemic/pulmonary circulation model, with four cardiac valves treated by the RIIS (Resistive Immersed Implicit Surface) method. Domain motion is one-way–driven by a 3D electromechanics simulation of the LV only; motion is extended to LA & AA endocardia by a harmonic-lift preprocessor plus a 0D-driven LA displacement. Six hemodynamic biomarkers (Table 3) are reported and validated against in-vivo literature: SV = 82.6 ml, EF = 55.8 %, peak AV flow 493.3 ml/s, peak LV pressure 121.2 mmHg, peak E = 0.96 m/s, peak A = 0.71 m/s (E/A = 1.35).

**Why it matters.** It's the reference multiphysics left-heart CFD pipeline in the lifex ecosystem: the same authors released `lifex-cfd` (Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.13941312, LGPLv3) with a runnable Linux AppImage plus 4 example configurations, one of which (`atrium`, 390K-cell Zygote LA mesh + 3-heartbeat prescribed displacement + RIIS mitral valve + CSV boundary pressures) directly instantiates the paper's methodology on the LA subsystem.

**What this replication does.** (1) Independent 0D/1D surrogate on CherryRd for the whole-cycle biomarker table (prescribed-volume LV ejection + 3-element systemic Windkessel + Gaussian E/A model). (2) Real Zingaro-family lifex-cfd run on uicgpu (255 cores, 2 TiB RAM): cylinder ALE+RIIS benchmark (32-core, ρ=1.06·10³ kg/m³, μ=3.5·10⁻³ Pa·s, dt=2.5·10⁻⁴ s — **identical numerics to paper Table 2**) to verify solver executes the ALE+RIIS+SUPG-PSPG pipeline on real hardware, then (if time) a scaled left-atrium run. (3) LLM-judge scoring of surrogate vs paper Table 3.
