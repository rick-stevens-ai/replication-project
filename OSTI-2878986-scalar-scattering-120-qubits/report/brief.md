# Brief — OSTI 2878986 / arXiv 2411.02486

**Paper.** Nikita A. Zemlevskiy (IQuS, Univ. Washington), *"Scalable Quantum Simulations of Scattering in Scalar Field Theory on 120 Qubits"*, DOI 10.1103/qr72-51v1, published 2025-08-12 (arXiv posted 2024-11-04). Single-author, IBM Heron `ibm_fez` hardware, 120 physical qubits, digitized (nq=2 qubits/site) lattice ϕ⁴ scalar field theory in 1+1D. The paper introduces scalable variational circuits (SVCs) with an SC-ADAPT-VQE vacuum, brickwall wavepacket preparation, brickwall Trotter time evolution, and a new vacuum-evolution-based error-mitigation strategy (ODR on top of DD + Pauli twirling + TREX). The reported hardware executes circuits with up to 4924 two-qubit gates and 2-qubit gate depth 103.

**Why replicate.** All quantum-hardware runs and MPS 120-qubit simulations are far beyond free-endpoint compute. What *is* independently reproducible: the paper's classical benchmark itself — the digitized lattice ϕ⁴ Hamiltonian, its vacuum, the free-theory dispersion, and the qualitative signatures (translation-invariant vacuum, smaller ⟨ϕ²⟩_vac in the interacting theory, and attenuation of the collision peak by ~15–35% when λ turns on). Reproducing these on a smaller lattice with exact statevector diagonalization directly checks whether the paper's small-scale MPS/qiskit exact reference (which the 120-qubit device is judged against) is itself internally consistent with the theory as stated.

**What we did.** Built the paper's digitized JLP-basis Hamiltonian (nq=2, ϕ_max=1.5, m=1/2) exactly for L=6 and L=8 (Hilbert dims 4096 and 65 536) via sparse numpy/scipy; Lanczos ground state; expm-based real-time evolution of a smeared two-wavepacket state; measured ⟨ϕ²_j⟩(t)−⟨ϕ²_j⟩_vac and the analytic dispersion E_k=√(m²+4sin²(k/2)); scored 5 testable claims (C1–C5). No LLM inference was used inside the physics calculation; Argo (free) was used only for the final LLM-judge verdict.
