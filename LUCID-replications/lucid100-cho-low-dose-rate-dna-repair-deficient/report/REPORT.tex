\section{Paper summary}

Buglewicz DJ, Haskins JS, Haskins A, Su C, Gius D, Kato TA. \emph{Exploring DNA repair
deficient CHO cell response to low dose rate radiation.} \emph{Biochem Biophys Res Commun}
(BBRC) \textbf{698}: 149539 (2024). DOI: \texttt{10.1016/j.bbrc.2024.149539}. PMID 38271835.

The paper (from the abstract + MeSH headings + companion-paper Methods --- the full text is
paywalled and no OA copy exists) examines a panel of Chinese hamster ovary (CHO) mutant cell
lines defective in specific DSB-repair pathways (canonical NHEJ, homologous recombination) under
low-dose-rate (LDR) $\gamma$-irradiation. It contrasts acute vs LDR clonogenic survival, $\gamma$-H2AX
foci kinetics, cell-cycle redistribution, and growth inhibition. The central novel claim is the
observation of an \emph{inverse dose-rate effect} (IDRE) in NHEJ-deficient lines: LDR
irradiation is \emph{more} lethal per Gy than acute in that background, contrary to the
classical dose-rate sparing seen in WT and HR-deficient lines. This is mechanistically
interpreted as accumulation of unrepaired DSBs and mitotic catastrophe when NHEJ (the pathway
that closes the vast majority of IR-induced DSBs in G1) is compromised.

\textbf{Cell panel (inferred from Kato-lab companions PMC10727999, PMC6467899).} Likely core
set: 10B2 or AA8 (WT), 51D1 (HR$^-$, $Rad51D$), V3 (DNA-PKcs$^-$, NHEJ$^-$), and possibly
xrs-5/xrs-6 (Ku80$^-$, NHEJ$^-$), irs1SF (XRCC3$^-$, HR$^-$), and other Kato-lab CHO panel
members. \emph{Exact panel composition and dose-rate values are paywalled.}

\section{Method}

Because the primary paper is closed-access and no source data have been deposited, a wet-lab
reproduction is impossible and a direct numerical reproduction of the paper's figures is
impossible. Two honest options existed: (a) refuse all replication and stop, or (b) build an
explicitly labelled qualitative smoke model that exercises the named claims using
\emph{substitute} anchors from the same lab's OA companion papers. We chose (b).

The smoke model (\texttt{scripts/replicate\_smoke.py}) implements:
\begin{itemize}
\item Linear-Quadratic (LQ) survival: $\mathrm{SF}(D) = \exp\!\bigl(-\alpha D - \beta G(\lambda) D^2\bigr)$.
\item Lea--Catcheside dose-protraction factor
  $G(\lambda) = \frac{2}{(\lambda T)^2}\bigl(\lambda T - 1 + e^{-\lambda T}\bigr)$
  with per-line sublethal-repair rate $\lambda = \ln 2 / \tau$.
\item A \emph{phenomenological} NHEJ-IDRE term: $\alpha_{\rm eff}(\dot D) = \alpha \cdot (1 + \phi\,e^{-\dot D / \dot D_0})$
  that boosts effective $\alpha$ at very low $\dot D$ to represent accumulation of
  unrepaired/misligated DSBs in NHEJ-deficient backgrounds.
\end{itemize}
Per-line $(\alpha, \beta, \tau, \phi, \dot D_0)$ are illustrative --- chosen so that the acute
SER ordering matches the Kato-lab companion (PMC10727999, Table~1: WT 1.00 / 51D1 1.63 / V3
1.72) --- and are \emph{not} lifted from the target BBRC paper (which is unreadable).

\section{Critique (honest)}

This backfill treats the earlier ``SMOKE PASS'' checks as \emph{evidence of mechanism
plausibility, not evidence of paper reproduction}. The following limitations are intentional
and material.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Only 3 of a likely 8--12 CHO mutant lines were modeled.} The paper likely covers
  the full Kato-lab panel spanning NHEJ (V3, xrs-5, xrs-6, XR-1), HR (51D1, irs1SF, irs-2,
  irs-3), NER (UV5, UV41, UV61), and XRCC1/PARP-axis (EM9, EM-C11) mutants. The smoke
  addresses one representative from three of those classes (WT + one HR$^-$ + one NHEJ$^-$).
  \emph{All mutant lines were NOT tested.}
\item \textbf{Dose-rate range is not anchored to physical $\dot D$ values from the paper.} The
  smoke sweeps $\dot D$ continuously in Gy/h, but the paper's actual $\dot D$ grid (which
  likely spans a modest range, not from the true instantaneous limit down to genuinely
  protracted $>$24\,h exposures) is paywalled. In particular we cannot verify whether the
  paper covers the truly protracted regime (say $<$0.1 Gy/h with $>$24\,h exposure) where
  cell-cycle progression during exposure dominates the biology; the LQ + Lea--Catcheside
  formalism is known to under-represent that regime.
\item \textbf{The reduced dose-rate effect in NHEJ-deficient cells was not quantitatively fit;
  the smoke merely reproduces the sign.} The IDRE term $\alpha_{\rm eff}(\dot D) = \alpha(1 +
  \phi e^{-\dot D / \dot D_0})$ has two free parameters that were hand-set to make the ratio
  drop below unity in the middle of the swept window. No fit residual, no confidence interval,
  no comparison to a specific reported ratio. This is a \emph{mechanistic
  plausibility demonstration}, not a reproduction of the paper's quantitative IDRE curve.
\item \textbf{5 of 8 claims are not tested at all.} $\gamma$-H2AX foci, cell-cycle
  redistribution, giant-cell formation, growth-inhibition curves, and the ``cross-sensitivity
  to major DNA damaging agents'' claim all require the paper's Methods/Results text and
  ideally the source data. None of that is available.
\item \textbf{Queue mismatch flagged.} The LUCID-100 queue lists this slot as
  ``\textsc{REPLICATED}''. The on-disk REPORT.md verdict is ``\textsc{NO-GO}'' (re-tiered
  2026-06-25 from an initial ``SPOT-CHECK''). \textbf{The on-disk NO-GO is the correct
  verdict.} Queue label is stale and should be corrected upstream.
\item \textbf{No claim of ``replicated''.} Under the 80\%/80\% (coverage/agreement) bar for
  REPLICATED and the corresponding thresholds for PARTIAL, this slot cannot rise above
  SPOT-CHECK from public artifacts; under Rick's hard-ceiling rule it lands at NO-GO.
\end{itemize}
