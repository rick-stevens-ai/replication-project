# Brief

Independent replication of **"A Performant Energy-conserving Particle Reweighting Method for Particle-in-Cell Simulations"** (Boerner, Hall, et al., Sandia SAND2024-16128J, *J. Comput. Phys.* 2024; OSTI 2480245). The paper introduces new **particle-based split and merge operators** for PIC/DSMC that dynamically adjust macroparticle weights while conserving mass, momentum and energy per-particle and preserving velocity/energy distribution functions, implemented in Sandia's proprietary Aleph code. Because Aleph is not public, we reimplemented the split (Eqs 4–5) and merge (Eqs 6–12) operators from the paper's equations in Python/numpy and independently verified their stated conservation properties using the paper's own Test-4.1 exponential sheath field, plus reproduced the Test-4.2 structural claim that the real-electron exponential growth rate is independent of the target computational particle count. All operator-level conservation claims reproduce to machine precision; the growth-rate-independence structure reproduces (β=3.05 s⁻¹ vs paper ~3.06 s⁻¹, bounded computational count). The full coupled PIC sheath transient and DSMC/cross-section physics of Tests 4.1/4.2 were out of scope (require Aleph).
