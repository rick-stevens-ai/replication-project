\begin{abstract}
This report backfills the 8-artifact standard for a LUCID-100 slot whose
target is a single-author \emph{review / synthesis} paper by Anders
Brahme in \emph{Annals of Case Reports} 9:1625 (Gavin Publishers), 2024,
DOI \texttt{10.29011/2574-7754.101625}. The paper contains no primary
data, no released code, no supplementary material, no parameter tables,
and no original quantitative figures. All 38 figures are conceptual
diagrams or re-plots of the author's own prior publications. The only
analytically-complete quantitative object in the paper is Eq.~(1),
the \emph{complication-free-cure} objective
$P^{+} = P_B - P_I + \delta(1-P_B)P_I$ with $\delta \approx 0.2$.
We re-implemented Eq.~(1) on canonical Brahme/K\"allman Poisson
sigmoids, swept $\delta$ and $\gamma_C$, and proved six algebraic
limits (19/19 PASS). The audit confirms the qualitative directions the
paper states for $\delta$ and $\gamma_C$, but $\ge 9$ of the paper's
13 testable claims cannot be reached from this paper alone because the
parameters and data live in the author's cited prior work.
\textbf{Verdict: REPLICATED (formalism only) --- retiered on 2026-06-25
to NO-GO for LUCID-100 promotion because the paper is a review with no
reproducible primary artifact.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper summary}
Brahme reviews his own multi-decade programme on radiobiological
optimization of external-beam radiotherapy. Themes: (i) the K\"allman /
Brahme Poisson TCP/NTCP formalism, (ii) the ``complication-free-cure''
objective $P^{+}$, (iii) the RHR (Repairable-Homologous-Recombination)
survival model as an extension of LQ / RCR, (iv) the LET dependence of
the tumor-control slope $\gamma_C$ via microdosimetric heterogeneity,
(v) a proposed clinical workflow called BIOART / IVPA (In-Vivo
Predictive Assay) that uses FDG-PET-CT before and after
$\approx 18$\,Gy to build per-voxel effective-dose maps $D_{0,\text{eff}}$,
and (vi) qualitative reviews of neutron-, light-ion-, and heavy-ion
therapy series. The article is styled as a review/opinion; there is no
Methods section, no reported new experiment, and no data availability
statement.

\section{Method}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Fetched the OA PDF from Gavin Publishers
      (\url{gavinpublishers.com/assets/articles_pdf/New-Radiation-Oncology-...pdf}).
      SHA-256 \texttt{b768585e326b83b9e51165978c9fe3a1a9711d369762ea65b442e9077fcd00b9}.
\item Text extraction with \texttt{pdftotext -layout} (Poppler); 2\,159 lines
      into \texttt{paper.txt}. Internal LLM \texttt{pdf} tool was
      unavailable on the run date (see failure\_analysis.md).
\item Manual re-reading located Eq.~(1), the definitions of $P_B$, $P_I$,
      $\gamma_C$, $D_{50}$, and the qualitative claims C3--C7 that
      depend on $\delta$ and $\gamma_C$.
\item Implemented Eq.~(1) on canonical K\"allman/Brahme Poisson sigmoid
      $P(D) = 2^{-\exp(e\,\gamma_{50}\,(1-D/D_{50}))}$
      with $D_{50,T}=60$\,Gy, $\gamma_C\in\{3.0,1.8\}$, $D_{50,N}=70$\,Gy,
      $\gamma_N=4.0$. Swept $\delta\in\{0,0.2,1\}$ and continuous
      $\delta\in[0,1]$ (41 steps). Dose grid 0--100\,Gy at 0.1\,Gy.
      Script: \texttt{smoke/p\_plus\_smoke.py}, numpy + matplotlib.
\item Wrote an independent algebraic-limits harness
      \texttt{smoke/eq1\_internal\_consistency.py}: 6 limits (L1--L6)
      on $20{,}000$ random $(P_B, P_I)$ draws, plus re-verification of
      the headline peak numbers from scratch.
\item All runs on CherryRd (Darwin 25.3.0, CPU, Python 3, numpy
      2.x, matplotlib 3.x). Total wall time $<$ 2\,s per full run.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Algebraic-limit checks (\texttt{eq1\_internal\_consistency.py})}
\begin{itemize}
  \item L1: $P^{+}(P_B=0,P_I) = 0$. \textbf{PASS.}
  \item L2: $P^{+}(P_B,P_I=0) = P_B$. \textbf{PASS.}
  \item L3: $P^{+}(P_B=1,P_I) = 1 - P_I$. \textbf{PASS.}
  \item L4: $\partial P^{+}/\partial P_B > 0$ for $\delta<1, P_I<1$. \textbf{PASS.}
  \item L5: $\partial P^{+}/\partial P_I < 0$ for $\delta<1, P_B<1$. \textbf{PASS.}
  \item L6: $\partial P^{+}/\partial \delta = (1-P_B)P_I \ge 0$. \textbf{PASS.}
\end{itemize}
19/19 checks PASS with $\max|\text{err}|\!=\!1.1\!\times\!10^{-16}$.

\subsection{Direction agreement on qualitative claims}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{C3 (role of $\delta$):} $P^{+}_{\max}$ is monotone non-decreasing
      in $\delta$ (L6), matching the paper's assertion that larger $\delta$
      (\emph{less} volume-effect penalty) inflates achievable cure.
\item \emph{C4 (role of $\gamma_C$):} Halving $\gamma_C$ from 3.0 to 1.8
      drops $P^{+}_{\max}$ from 0.512 to 0.474 ($-3.8$~pp) and pulls
      $D^{*}$ from 63.1\,Gy to 61.4\,Gy. Direction matches paper.
\end{itemize}

\section{Per-claim what worked / what didn't}
\textbf{Worked.} C1--C4 and C10 are the analytically complete parts of
the paper; the re-implementation is trivial and every limit and direction
predicted by the text is reproduced.
\textbf{Did not work.} C5--C9 and C11--C13 are unreachable from this
paper alone. C5 and C6 in principle admit quantitative tests but the
underlying TCP fits and repair-kinetics parameters are not deposited
here. C11 (the numerical value $\delta\approx 0.2$) is asserted, not
derived; we can confirm $\delta$'s qualitative role but not the specific
value. C12--C13 need clinical data that the author does not release.

\section{Critique of the replication}
This is the honest section that the previous REPORT.md hedged on.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Choice-of-target risk.} Selecting this paper into LUCID-100
      was itself a low-value slot: a review in a
      predatory-adjacent venue, with no released code or data, cannot
      generate a genuine reproduction verdict. The 2026-06-25 retiering
      to NO-GO was correct. The ``REPLICATED'' tag on the priority queue
      reflects only that the one available equation was audited; it does
      \emph{not} mean the paper's scientific content was reproduced.
\item \textbf{The formalism smoke is a very narrow test.} Eq.~(1) is a
      one-line convex-combination-style objective that reduces to
      identities under trivial limits. That 19/19 identities pass is
      unsurprising and does not vindicate the paper's clinical claims.
\item \textbf{The direction-only tests are weakly informative.}
      Confirming that $P^{+}$ rises with $\delta$ and falls with lower
      $\gamma_C$ is a monotonicity check that the equation is built to
      satisfy by construction. Any well-formed sigmoid $\times$ affine
      combination reproduces these directions.
\item \textbf{Choice of $\gamma$/$D_{50}$ is not audited.} We used
      Brahme/K\"allman canonical values from the wider literature
      ($D_{50,T}=60$\,Gy, $\gamma_C=3.0$, etc.). The paper never
      publishes its own values; a different reasonable choice would
      shift $P^{+}_{\max}$ by several percentage points without changing
      the qualitative story. Coverage of parameter uncertainty is zero.
\item \textbf{No cross-model check.} We did not implement RHR or RCR to
      compare against LQ, because the paper does not publish parameters.
      The paper's core mechanistic claim (RHR is preferable to LQ for
      LDHS) is left completely untested by our audit.
\item \textbf{Figure-embedded numerics missed.} The $\gamma_C$/$\sigma_D/\bar{D}$/RBE
      tabular insert lives inside Figure~15 as rendered text.
      \texttt{pdftotext} does not recover it. A GPU-parsed
      Marker/Nougat pass could plausibly rescue it, but nothing in the
      pipeline attempted this.
\item \textbf{Provenance / venue.} \emph{Annals of Case Reports}
      (Gavin Publishers) is widely flagged as predatory; no peer-review
      artifact is discoverable. The audit did not attempt to verify the
      cited Brahme refs [1--3, 23, 34, 45] whose parameters are the
      substrate for most of the paper's claims.
\item \textbf{Toolchain fragility.} On the run date the internal LLM
      \texttt{pdf} extraction plugin was blocked (Anthropic credits,
      Gemini model-name mismatch, OpenAI extract plugin failure).
      Falling back to \texttt{pdftotext} is fine for prose but not for
      figure-embedded tables (see item 6). This friction is a
      generalizable weakness of the LUCID pipeline.
\end{enumerate}

Net critique: what got done is honest and small; the ``coverage 4/10,
agreement 7/10'' score genuinely reflects that only 1 of the paper's
scientific artifacts is analytically complete inside the paper. The
retiering to NO-GO is the correct signal for a corpus-level curator.

\section{Verdict + justification}
\textbf{REPLICATED (formalism only); retiered NO-GO for LUCID-100.}
Justification: the single equation the paper writes down explicitly is
algebraically consistent and its qualitative predictions reproduce, but
the paper contains no primary artifact whose replication would carry
scientific weight. For downstream use, the LUCID slot should point to
the primary Brahme refs [1--3, 23, 34, 45], not this synthesis.
