# Brief — OSTI 3001885

Kounis-Melas, Panagiotopoulos & Graves (Princeton, 2025, DOI 10.1116/6.0004867) train a Deep Potential Molecular Dynamics (DeepMD) machine-learned interatomic potential for the Si-Cl-Ar system using DeePMD-kit v2.1.5 + DP-GEN active learning on 58,536 DFT (PBE, Quantum Espresso v6.4.1) frames, and use it in LAMMPS (23 Jun 2022) impact simulations of thermal Cl exposure and simultaneous Cl-atom / Ar⁺ or Cl⁺ ion bombardment of Si(100). Key numerical claims: energy RMSE ≈ 5×10⁻³ eV/atom and force RMSE ≈ 1×10⁻¹ eV/Å; steady-state thermal Cl coverage = 1.25 ML (exp. ~1.5 ML); ~20 Å SiClₓ mixed-layer under Cl/Ar⁺ at 100 eV; a full Table I of Si etch yields per incident ion vs REBO-MD and experiment across three impact regimes and 5-100 eV ion energies. Data (final DP model, training set, LAMMPS inputs) is deposited at Princeton Data Commons (doi:10.34770/zqj0-3z73, 590 MB, Globus-mediated). This replication attempt (a) verified the deposit's existence and manifest, (b) verified the DeePMD-kit v2.1.5 + LAMMPS toolchain reproduces the paper's stated methods on uicgpu (8×A100), (c) demonstrated end-to-end DP training on the DeePMD-kit water example achieves the paper's ~10⁻³ eV/atom RMSE scale in <1000 steps, (d) LLM-judged the paper's internal + literature consistency, but did NOT rerun the full 58k-frame DFT + DP-GEN + LAMMPS impact campaign (bulk data behind Globus-auth + estimated 100-1000 A100·hr for the impact-yield sweep).
