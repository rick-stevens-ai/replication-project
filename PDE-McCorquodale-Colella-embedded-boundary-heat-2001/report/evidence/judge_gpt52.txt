**Verdict: PARTIAL replication (with one clear contradiction in the reported gradient behavior).**

Your results **reproduce the core *solution* accuracy claims** for both elliptic and parabolic problems in the norms that are typically meaningful for EB FV (volume-weighted \(L_2\)), but they **do not reproduce** the paper’s claimed **boundary-gradient** behavior, and your **max-norm** evidence for “uniform” second order is mixed/fragile. Concretely: for **Poisson**, both “high” and “low” boundary treatments give clean \(\approx O(h^2)\) in \(L_2\) (orders \(\sim2.1\)–\(2.2\) on the finer refinements), which supports the standard EB result that first-order boundary truncation can still yield second-order *solution* convergence; however, the **max-norm** is not consistently second order (notably the “low” case drops to \(\sim0.77\) on the finest step), so the strong form of **C1 (“uniformly second-order in max norm”) is only partially supported** by your data and is plausibly limited by sliver-cell outliers. For **C2**, your Poisson **boundary-gradient error does not converge as claimed**: both “high” and “low” show roughly \(O(h)\) at best on the first refinement and then **catastrophic nonconvergence/blow-up on the finest grid** (negative observed orders, large error spikes), which **contradicts** the stated \(O(h^2)\) (high) / \(O(h)\) (low) gradient behavior—this looks like either a stability/conditioning issue in the gradient evaluation on small cut cells or a mismatch in how/where the gradient error is sampled/weighted, but as reported it does not replicate C2. For **Heat**, the “low” stencil with TGA and \(\Delta t=0.5h\) gives very clean \(\approx O(h^2)\) in both max and \(L_2\) through \(N=256\), and your additional “spatial-only” refinement confirms second-order spatial accuracy; the “high” stencil shows a breakdown at the finest grid (error increases at \(N=256\)), so **C3 is replicated for the method/time integrator in the ‘low’ configuration but only partially for ‘high’** (suggesting an implementation sensitivity specific to that boundary flux/gradient construction rather than the TGA scheme itself).
