# BVBRC-15 — Brief

**Paper:** Bu QT, Yu P, Wang J, Li ZY, Chen XA, Mao XM, Li YQ.
*Rational construction of genome-reduced and high-efficient industrial Streptomyces chassis based on multiple comparative genomic approaches.*
Microbial Cell Factories (2019). **DOI:** [10.1186/s12934-019-1055-7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12934-019-1055-7) — PMID 30691531 — PMCID PMC6348691.

## Core claims
1. Multiple comparative-genomics methods can identify large non-essential genomic regions in *Streptomyces chattanoogensis* L10.
2. An optimized Cre/loxP system + universal suicide plasmids enables high-efficiency large deletions in *Streptomyces*.
3. Two genome-streamlined mutants were built — **L320** (1.3 Mb deletion) and **L321** (0.7 Mb deletion) — from the parental industrial natamycin producer L10.
4. L321 outperforms L10 in growth, transformation efficiency, intracellular ATP / NADPH/NADP⁺, and heterologous polyketide / protein expression.

## Replication scope (short pass)
- Confirm species/strain availability in BV-BRC for L10/L320/L321.
- Cross-check S. chattanoogensis genome size to sanity-check the 1.3 Mb deletion magnitude (~13–15% of an ~9 Mb genome).
- Note that the *wet-lab* claims (engineered chassis growth, polyketide titer) cannot be reproduced without strains + media + Cre/loxP plasmids.
