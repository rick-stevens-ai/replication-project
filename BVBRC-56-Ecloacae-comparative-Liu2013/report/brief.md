# Brief — BVBRC-56

**What:** Independent replication of Liu et al. (2013) *"Comparative genome analysis of Enterobacter cloacae"* (PLoS One 8(9):e74487), the first comparative-genomics study of the *E. cloacae* species, centered on the plant growth-promoting endophyte *E. cloacae* subsp. *cloacae* ENHKU01 (GenBank CP003737.1) compared against 3 other complete *E. cloacae* genomes and 4 other *Enterobacter* species (+ 3 *Pantoea* outgroups).

**Why:** Rank-42 top-up candidate for the BVBRC-100 set; genuinely new organism (no prior *Enterobacter* replication in the set), fully open-access paper with all genome accessions public in NCBI, and a workflow (genome stats + pan-genome/core + comparative feature analysis + phylogenomics) that maps directly onto BV-BRC Comparative Systems / Proteome Comparison + Codon Tree tools. All 11 genomes were re-downloaded from NCBI by accession and every analysis re-run from scratch on real data (DIAMOND/BLAST/Biopython/NJ) on uicgpu. Verdict: **PARTIAL** — descriptive genome statistics and comparative pan-genome/phylogenomic structure reproduce cleanly; annotation-dependent functional counts do not fully match the paper's RAST/IMG-curated numbers.
