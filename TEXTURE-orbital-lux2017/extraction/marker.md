                                                                      Using a semiclassical Green’s function formalism, we discover the emergence of chiral and topological orbital
                                                                   magnetism in two-dimensional chiral spin textures by explicitly finding the corrections to the orbital magneti-
                                                                   zation, proportional to the powers of the gradients of the texture. We show that in the absence of spin-orbit
                                                                   coupling, the resulting orbital moment can be understood as the electronic response to the emergent magnetic
                                                                   field associated with the real-space Berry curvature. By referring to the Rashba model, we demonstrate that by
