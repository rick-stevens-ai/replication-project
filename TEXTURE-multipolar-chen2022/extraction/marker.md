## Headline claim (verbatim, abstract)
"We explore the Coulombic antiferromagnet in the spin-1/2 pyrochlores with
dipole-octupole doublets. This fractionalized antiferromagnetic state carries both
antiferromagnetic order and emergent quantum electrodynamics with the gapless gauge
photon and fractionalized quasiparticles ... anomalously large T^3 specific heat and
the broad spinon continuum in the inelastic neutron scattering."
