                                                                   Motivated by the recent development in strong spin-orbit-coupled materials, we consider the
                                                                dipole-octupole doublets on the triangular lattice. We propose the most general interaction be-
                                                                tween these unusual local moments. Due to the spin-orbit entanglement and the special form of its
arXiv:1608.07008v1 [cond-mat.str-el] 25 Aug 2016




                                                                wavefunction, the dipole-octupole doublet has a rather peculiar property under the lattice symmetry
                                                                operation. As a result, the interaction is highly anisotropic in the pseudospin space, but remarkably,
                                                                is uniform spatially. We analyze the ground state properties of this generic model and emphasize
                                                                the hidden multipolar orders that emerge from the dipolar and octupolar interactions. We clarify
                                                                the quantum mutual modulations between the dipolar and octupolar orders. We predict the exper-
                                                                imental consequences of the multipolar orders and propose the rare-earth triangular materials as
                                                                candidate systems for these unusual properties.
