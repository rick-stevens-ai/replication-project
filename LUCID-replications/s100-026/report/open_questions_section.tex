\section{Open Questions}

The following five questions remain unresolved after this replication and would
require either author response or additional compute to close.

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{What volume convention yields the paper's 0.225~wt\% AuFeNP loading?}
Our audit reconstructs 0.014\% (whole 20-$\mu$m cell), 0.047\% (13.8-$\mu$m
nucleus), and 0.96\% (100-nm shell around nucleus). None match 0.225\% within
a factor of 2.
\emph{Next steps:} (a) open a GitHub issue on
\verb|AKlapproth/MultiScale_AuNP_TOPAS| asking for the denominator; (b) try a
factor-4-reduced NP count ($\sim 2.5\times 10^5$) hypothesis; (c) try Au-only
(no Fe$_2$O$_3$) mass in the numerator with the 100-nm-shell denominator;
(d) request the AuFeNP coordinate dump and compute wt\% directly.

\item \textbf{Is OH enhancement $2.05$--$2.23$ a single-seed artifact?}
The paper documents no RNG seeds for any of the 12 scenario runs; the reported
per-species ratio ranges are tight enough that seed sensitivity could plausibly
dominate.
\emph{Next steps:} run Stage 3 alone (skipping mouse + tumor) across
$\sim 10$ seeds at (200~kVp, Center) using a proxy monoenergetic spectrum
around 110~keV effective; report empirical seed spread and compare to the
paper's cross-scenario spread. If seed spread $\gg$ scenario spread, the
depth/energy trends are within noise.

\item \textbf{Does the specific AuFeNP spatial realization matter, or is it
self-averaging at $10^6$ NPs?}
Chemical species killed at NP surfaces depend on which molecules encounter
which NP, which depends on the exact cloud.
\emph{Next steps:} generate two independent $10^6$-NP clouds under the same
``$\leq 100$~nm, uniform random'' rule; rerun Stage 3 at one condition;
quantify per-species enhancement-ratio spread across realizations. If $<5$\%,
the coordinate gap is not a blocker.

\item \textbf{Are the Stage-1/Stage-2 phase-space files essential for the Table 3
numbers, or can Stage 3 stand alone with a synthetic kV spectrum?}
The intermediate \verb|.phsp| files are not released; this forces any replicator
into the full multi-kJob upstream campaign.
\emph{Next steps:} take a published SARRP-effective SpekCalc spectrum and drive
Stage 3 directly. If OH enhancement matches Table 3 within $\sim 10$\%, the
phase-space files are ``nice to have'' rather than blocking; if not, they are
a genuine reproducibility blocker and the ``code on GitHub'' claim is weaker
than it looks.

\item \textbf{Is the claim ``indirect SBs $\approx 2\times$ direct SBs'' driven
by the choice of 40\% OH$\to$SB conversion probability?}
Halving this probability would roughly halve indirect SBs and collapse the
2:1 ratio to 1:1, making the 2:1 claim a tautology of the parameter choice.
\emph{Next steps:} sweep the OH-to-SB probability over $\{20, 30, 40, 50,
60\}$\% at one condition; record indirect:direct ratio. Verify whether the
2:1 finding is robust to $\pm 50$\% variation in this bridge parameter or is
localized to the chosen 40\%.

\end{enumerate}
