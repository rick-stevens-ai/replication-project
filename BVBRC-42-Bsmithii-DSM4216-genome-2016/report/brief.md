# Brief — BVBRC-42

**Paper:** Bosma EF, Koehorst JJ, van Hijum SAFT, Renckens B, Vriesendorp B, van de Weijer AHP, Schaap PJ, de Vos WM, van der Oost J, van Kranenburg R. "Complete genome sequence of thermophilic *Bacillus smithii* type strain DSM 4216ᵀ." *Standards in Genomic Sciences* 11:52 (2016). DOI 10.1186/s40793-016-0172-8. PMC4995803.

**What/why:** The paper reports the finished genome of the facultatively thermophilic, lactate-producing biotech-relevant bacterium *B. smithii* DSM 4216ᵀ (chromosome CP012024.1 + plasmid CP012025.1) with a RAST-based annotation, genome-statistics tables, a COG functional-category breakdown, and a reconstruction of central carbon metabolism that highlights a "striking" absence of the standard acetate-production pathway (phosphotransacetylase *pta* + acetate kinase *ackA*) and of several pyruvate-dissimilation enzymes. We independently re-pull the actual public genome from NCBI (GCF/GCA_001050115.1, BioProject PRJNA258357), recompute genome statistics, feature counts, and COG functional categories from the real sequence, and directly test the paper's headline present/absent metabolic-gene claims by tblastn of curated reference enzymes against the assembly.
