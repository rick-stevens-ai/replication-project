# Brief

Independent replication of Bochev & Gunzburger, *"An Absolutely Stable Pressure-Poisson Stabilized Finite Element Method for the Stokes Equations"*, SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 42(3):1189–1207 (2004), DOI 10.1137/S0036142903416547. The paper is pure numerical analysis (no computational experiments reported); it defines a new discrete SGLS method (Eq. 5.10) that uses a discrete Laplacian −Δ_h u to avoid the P1-degeneration of the classical Hughes–Franca–Balestra (1986) pressure-Poisson stabilization, and proves absolute stability + optimal convergence in the natural H¹×L² norm (Theorems 5.4, 5.5). We implemented the method from scratch in scikit-fem 12.0.1 on P1/P1 triangles, verified that (a) the method reproduces linear velocity + linear pressure to machine precision (weak-consistency test passes), (b) H¹-velocity error decays as O(h) and L²-pressure error decays as O(h²) on Taylor–Green flow — matching or exceeding the paper's Theorem 5.5 rates, (c) the solution is bounded for stabilization parameter δ swept over ten orders of magnitude (10⁻⁶ … 10⁴), confirming *absolute stability*, while δ=0 gives a singular matrix as expected for the LBB-violating equal-order pair. Verdict: **REPLICATED** — every proven claim we could test numerically holds.
