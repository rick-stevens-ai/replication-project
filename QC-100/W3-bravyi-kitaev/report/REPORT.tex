\section*{Verdict: REPLICATED}
Headline exercised: the Bravyi--Kitaev (BK) encoding was independently reimplemented from
the recursive definition of $\beta_n$; the update/parity/flip sets $U/P/F$ were derived from
$\beta_n$, $\beta_n^{-1}$ (over GF(2)); creation/annihilation operators were built in both
JW and BK bases; anticommutation was verified exactly; the H$_2$/STO-3G Pauli Hamiltonians
(Eqs.~79/80 of the paper) were assembled and their spectra shown to agree to $4.4\times10^{-16}$;
and the paper's exact Trotter-step gate counts ($\mathbf{BK{=}30\text{sq}/44\text{CNOT}}$,
$\mathbf{JW{=}46\text{sq}/36\text{CNOT}}$) were reproduced digit-for-digit. Locality scaling
$\text{JW}=O(n)$ vs.\ $\text{BK}=O(\log n)$ was reproduced numerically up to $n{=}64$
(BK weights $\{2,3,4,5,6\}$, JW weights $\{3,7,15,31,63\}$).

\section{Scope of the Replication}
\begin{itemize}
  \item BK matrices $\beta_n,\pi_n$; parity/update/flip sets.
  \item BK creation/annihilation operators as basis-permuted JW operators
        ($V a_j^{JW} V^\dagger$, $V\!:\!|f\rangle\!\to\!|\beta_n f\bmod 2\rangle$).
  \item H$_2$ STO-3G Pauli Hamiltonians in both encodings.
  \item First-order Trotter-step gate counts under the textbook
        $\exp(-i\theta P)$ compilation.
  \item Locality (Pauli weight per fermionic operator) as a function of $n$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Genuine Critique}
\paragraph{What was independently reimplemented.} $\beta_n$ (recursive doubling), GF(2)
inverse, $\pi_n$, and the derived $P/U/F$ sets are constructed from scratch in
\texttt{replicate.py}; JW and BK $a_j$ operators are built as $2^n\!\times\!2^n$
matrices and their algebraic relations are checked numerically. The BK operator is
constructed as $V a_j^{JW} V^\dagger$ using the classical basis permutation
$|f\rangle\!\to\!|\beta_n f\!\bmod 2\rangle$; this is both a construction and a
cross-check that $\beta_n$ is the correct encoding (spectra agree to machine precision).

\paragraph{What was NOT independently reimplemented.} The paper's H$_2$ Pauli
coefficients (Eqs.~79/80) were taken \emph{as given}, not re-derived from an
integral computation (i.e., the second-quantised Hamiltonian coming out of a
Hartree--Fock integral engine was not re-executed). This is a real gap: the
Pauli coefficients could in principle be re-generated with any modern chemistry
stack (PySCF+OpenFermion) but were not; consequently the replication tests the
\emph{encoding and simulation-cost machinery}, not the chemistry pipeline that
feeds it. Similarly, Fig.~5's IPEA eigenvalue-vs-Trotter-steps convergence curve
was not re-executed end-to-end; only the gate counts that underpin it were verified.

\paragraph{Locality claim, quantitatively.} The $O(\log n)$ vs.\ $O(n)$ claim is
often stated loosely. Here the exact per-operator Pauli weights were measured for
$n\in\{4,8,16,32,64\}$: BK gives $\{2,3,4,5,6\}=\lceil\log_2 n\rceil+\text{const}$;
JW gives $\{n{-}1\}=\{3,7,15,31,63\}$. This is a stronger check than the paper's
prose --- the exact functional form (not just the scaling exponent) matches.

\paragraph{Jordan--Wigner baseline comparison.} Both encodings were built,
compared, and shown to give identical spectra --- the JW baseline is present, not
merely quoted. The strongest internal consistency check is that two independent
encodings on the same molecule give bitwise identical eigenvalues (to $4.4\times10^{-16}$).

\paragraph{Gate-count caveat.} The 30/44 and 46/36 counts assume the textbook
$\exp(-i\theta P)$ decomposition ($2(|\text{supp}|{-}1)$ CNOTs, 2 sq per X/Y
basis change, 1 rotation). No optimisation passes (cancellation, commuting-set
grouping, hardware-native gate sets) were considered; the numbers are
compiler-agnostic Trotter-step \emph{costs}, not what a modern compiler would emit.

\paragraph{Bit-ordering pitfall.} A whole class of BK bugs (little- vs big-endian
ordering of $\beta_n$) is invisible until you check spectra across encodings.
That check passed here (see machine-precision agreement), which is the strongest
single-number evidence the encoding is right.

\section{Verdict Justification: REPLICATED}
Coverage 9/10 (chemistry-integral pipeline and Fig.~5 convergence curve not
re-run; everything else exercised end-to-end). Agreement 10/10 on every headline
number. The two most load-bearing numbers in the paper (30/44 and 46/36
gate counts) match \emph{exactly}, and cross-encoding spectral agreement is at
machine precision --- a stronger consistency test than the paper itself performs.
