# Brief

Independent replication of the **full-order model (FOM)** and **POD reducibility** claims from Tsai et al., *"Local Reduced-Order Modeling for Electrostatic Plasmas by Physics-Informed Solution Manifold Decomposition"* (Comput. Phys. Commun. 2026, OSTI 3024991). We reimplemented the 1D1V Vlasov–Poisson solver from scratch (WENO5 + Rusanov flux splitting, FFT Poisson, RK4) and (a) reproduced the paper's prescribed-E reducibility claim — **n_f=23 vs paper 24** POD modes for 99% energy at δσ=1e-2, projection-ROM error **0.13% vs paper ~0.2%**; and (b) validated the FOM physics against the canonical **k=0.5 linear Landau damping** rate — measured **γ=-0.1495 vs analytic -0.15336 (2.5% error)**, with a diagnosed self-consistent-field sign convention. Verdict: **PARTIAL** — core reducibility + FOM physics reproduced; full ROM engineering (tensorial/time-windowed ROMs, two-stream, 90× speedup) not attempted.
