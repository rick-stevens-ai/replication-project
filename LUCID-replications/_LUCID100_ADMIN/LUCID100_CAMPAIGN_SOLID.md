## Per-paper replication standard

Each new paper gets: `README.md` paper brief, source PDF/text, artifact/data/code manifest, runnable scripts/notebooks, environment lockfile, logs, plots/tables, `REPORT.md`, and progress JSON. Verdicts: REPLICATED, PARTIAL, FAILED, BLOCKED, or NO-GO.

## Scoring/quality rules

- Prefer open code/data/supplementary tables and explicit mechanistic models.
- Use free endpoints only.
- No author contact unless Rick asks.
- Final evaluative scoring uses LLM judges / rubric reading, not regex-only matching.
- Track negative results; failed replications are useful LUCID outputs.
