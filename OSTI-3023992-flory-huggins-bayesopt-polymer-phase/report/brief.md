# Brief — OSTI 3023992

Independent numerical replication of Hughes, York, Yager, Osuji & Composto (2026, Digital Discovery, DOI 10.1039/d5dd00556f), which introduced a Flory–Huggins-informed, human-in-the-loop Bayesian optimization workflow (Matérn-3/2 GP with sigmoid-scaled FH spinodal prior mean, boundary-seeking acquisition `fa = σ + 0.25·tanh(0.1/|μ−0.5|)`) to map the LCST phase diagram of PMMA/SAN in 4 initial + 20×5 samples with gpCAM. We could not repeat the wet-lab cloud-point campaign, but we (a) implemented the full FH thermodynamics (χ=A+B/T, ∂²ΔF/∂φ²) analytically for the paper's blend parameters (Mw+Ð → N_PMMA≈804, N_SAN≈567), (b) built the paper's BO stack (Matérn-3/2 GP + FH-prior mean + custom acquisition + Δφ=0.025 / ΔT=1 °C exclusion mask + differential-evolution-style multistart hyperparameter refit) in Python, and (c) drove it on ground-truth cloudiness synthesized from the same FH+sigmoid model at the paper's fitted (A=0.022, B=−8.22 K). Our reproduction recovers the FH spinodal minimum at **T=159.16 °C at φ=0.46** (paper LCST ≈160 °C), fits back **A=+0.022, B=−8.37 K** from BO data (paper: A=0.022, B=−8.22 K), extracts an LCST of **159.6 °C** from the posterior (paper's computed 158.8 °C, extracted 154.8 °C), and reaches boundary RMSE ≈0.9 °C vs the true spinodal after the same 104-sample budget — a ~4× improvement over the Newby-style 7×N grid baseline (3.7 °C).
