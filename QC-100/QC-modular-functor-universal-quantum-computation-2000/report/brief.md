# Brief

Freedman, Kitaev, Larsen & Wang (2000, arXiv:quant-ph/0001108) prove that
the SU(2) Chern-Simons modular functor at the fifth root of unity ("CS5",
the Fibonacci/level-3 theory) is universal for quantum computation:
braiding the Jones representation on 3k-strand braids gives a poly-time
approximation of any quantum-circuit gate. This is a foundational
theory paper for topological quantum computation. We independently
reproduce the paper's concrete testable content: the CS5 space
dimensions (V_3^1=2, V_3^3=1, V_6^0=5, V_6^2=8), the Jones-representation
braid generators for the (2,5)-Young diagrams λ=[2,1] (dim 2), [3,3]
(dim 5), [4,2] (dim 8), verify their unitarity + braid + TL relations,
verify the eigenvalue spectrum {-1, q} with the paper's predicted
multiplicities 3,5 for λ=[4,2], and empirically demonstrate density of
the braid image via 50k random-braid samples and a length-11 explicit
approximation of the Hadamard gate. Every concrete claim we could test
independently is reproduced.
