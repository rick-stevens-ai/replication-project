PAPER
-----

Perez, Gangnet, Blake (2003) "Poisson Image Editing" SIGGRAPH.
Core method: solve discrete Poisson equation Δf = div v on region Ω with Dirichlet BC
f|∂Ω = f*|∂Ω, where f* is destination image, g is source image, and guidance field v is:
  - seamless cloning: v = ∇g  (discrete: v_pq = g_p - g_q)
  - mixed gradients: v_pq = argmax over {f*_p - f*_q, g_p - g_q} in absolute value
Discretization (eq. 7):
  |N_p| f_p - sum_{q in N_p ∩ Ω} f_q  =  sum_{q in N_p ∩ ∂Ω} f*_q  +  sum_{q in N_p} v_pq
Solved as sparse SPD linear system, per RGB channel independently.
Paper reports 0.4 s per solve on Pentium 4 for ~60k-pixel disk-shaped region
using Gauss-Seidel/SOR or V-cycle multigrid.


CLAIMS TO VERDICT (one per row)
- C1: seamless cloning eliminates visible seams: at ∂Ω, the interior gradient reproduces the source's local gradient (not the naive src-vs-dest step)
- C2: the discrete Poisson solve gives the correct membrane interpolant (Laplace equation, v=0 case, has Δf ≈ 0 in the interior of Ω up to numerical precision)
- C3: mixed-gradient guidance preserves salient source AND destination structure (higher total absolute gradient than either seamless or naive)
