                                                             Abstract –Topological insulator is a new state of matter which exhibits exotic surface electronic
                                                             properties. Determining the spin texture of this class of material is of paramount importance for
                                                             understanding its topological order and can lead to potential applications in spintronics. Here,
                                                             we have investigated the nature of the surface state of the topological insulator with hexagonal
                                                             warping subjected to an off-resonant circularly polarized light. The resulting electronic ground
                                                             state exhibits a novel feature of spin texture breaking the conventional spin-momentum locking
                                                             present on a topological insulator surface. The observed spin texture is shown to be a consequence
                                                             of the symmetry group of the underlying crystal. The generalisation of our method to the other
                                                             2D graphene-like systems are straightforward. Our calculation charts a simple experimental route
                                                             for a realisation of the non-trivial spin-textures.
