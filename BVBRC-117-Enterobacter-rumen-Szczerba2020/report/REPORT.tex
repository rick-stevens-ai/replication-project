\begin{abstract}
We independently replicated the finished-genome analysis of Szczerba \emph{et al.} 2020 (\emph{Sci.\ Rep.} \textbf{10}:1986, DOI \texttt{10.1038/s41598-020-58929-0}, PMID 32029880), which reports the first complete genome of a rumen isolate proposed as a bio-based succinic acid producer. Using the deposited NCBI chromosome \texttt{CP035466.1} plus all eight comparator genomes cited in the paper, and free open tools (Mash Similar-Genome-Finder analog, whole-genome BLASTN, barrnap, PlasmidFinder database, AMRFinderPlus~3.12.8), we reproduce all 13 quantitative Table~1 rows for LU2 \emph{exactly} (chromosome length 5{,}062{,}651 bp, GC 55\%, 4{,}986 genes, 4{,}741 protein-coding genes, 22 rRNA, 86 tRNA, 10 ncRNA, 127 pseudogenes, 0 plasmids, 0 CRISPR arrays); the top-hit ranking of related genomes is identical; every enzyme of the paper's proposed reductive-TCA + glyoxylate succinate biosynthesis route is present in the annotation; the paper's ``no plasmids'' claim survives an independent PlasmidFinder-style scan; and the paper's $\beta$-lactam AMR finding is confirmed and extended by modern AMRFinderPlus. The paper's hybrid assembly claim (SPAdes v3.11.1 on Illumina + ONT reads) is BLOCKED because the raw reads were never deposited to SRA. Two specialty analyses (PHASTER prophage, BAGEL4 bacteriocin) were plausibility-checked from the annotation but not re-run. \textbf{Verdict: PARTIAL} (6 strong / 1 moderate / 2 weak / 1 blocked).
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper summary}
Szczerba \emph{et al.}\ present the first complete genome of \emph{Enterobacter aerogenes} LU2 (NCBI-reclassified as \emph{Klebsiella aerogenes}), isolated from cow rumen in Poland. Hybrid Illumina MiSeq (2$\times$250) + ONT MinION sequencing assembled with SPAdes v3.11.1 yielded a single circular 5.06 Mb chromosome with 76$\times$ Illumina coverage and no plasmids. Downstream analyses used PlasmidFinder-style plasmid detection, ResFinder~3.1 AMR scanning, BAGEL4 bacteriocin mining, PHASTER prophage detection, IslandViewer~4 genomic-island prediction, and pairwise BLAST vs 22 other complete \emph{E.\ aerogenes} / \emph{E.\ cloacae} genomes. The paper's central biological conclusion is that LU2 encodes both the reductive branch of the TCA cycle and the glyoxylate shunt, motivating its potential as a bio-based succinic-acid production platform on whey-permeate lactose.

\section{Method (numbered, exact)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{PDF acquisition.} PMC and EuropePMC PDF endpoints returned interstitials or HTTP/2 errors from CherryRd and from \texttt{uicgpu}. Fetched instead from Nature's open-access URL \\ \texttt{https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-58929-0.pdf} via UIC HTTPS proxy on \texttt{uicgpu}: 3{,}103{,}797 B, PDF v1.4.
  \item \textbf{Text extraction.} \texttt{pdftotext -layout} (Poppler, local); \texttt{marker\_single} (Marker, on \texttt{uicgpu} in \texttt{/data/stevens/envs/marker}, 38\,s wall clock); \texttt{nougat} (in \texttt{/gpustor/stevens/anaconda3/envs/nougat}, $\sim$45\,s wall clock). All three saved under \texttt{extraction/}.
  \item \textbf{Deposited genome fetch.} \texttt{curl} against NCBI eutils \texttt{efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore\&id=CP035466\&rettype=fasta} and \texttt{rettype=gbwithparts}. Length 5{,}062{,}651 bp, LOCUS \texttt{circular BCT}, N-count 0.
  \item \textbf{Table 1 recount.} Python regex over \texttt{LU2.gb} counting \texttt{gene}, \texttt{CDS}, \texttt{rRNA}, \texttt{tRNA}, \texttt{ncRNA}, \texttt{tmRNA} feature types and \texttt{/pseudo} flags. Results: 4{,}986 gene features (\emph{exact}), 4{,}868 CDS with 127 pseudo (\emph{exact}), 22 rRNA (\emph{exact}), 86 tRNA (\emph{exact}), 10 ncRNA+tmRNA (\emph{exact}).
  \item \textbf{rRNA cross-check.} \texttt{barrnap --threads 8 LU2.fna} $\rightarrow$ 7$\times$16S + 7$\times$23S + 8$\times$5S = 22 rRNA (\emph{exact}).
  \item \textbf{Similar-Genome-Finder analog.} \texttt{mash sketch -o refs.msh} + \texttt{mash dist LU2.fna refs.msh} over all 8 references cited in the paper's BLAST discussion.
  \item \textbf{Whole-genome BLASTN.} \texttt{makeblastdb -dbtype nucl}; \texttt{blastn -perc\_identity 70 -num\_threads 16}; Python HSP-merge $+$ length-weighted mean identity + query coverage.
  \item \textbf{PlasmidFinder analog.} Cloned CGE \texttt{plasmidfinder\_db} (488 replicon FASTA sequences); built BLAST DB; \texttt{blastn -perc\_identity 95 -word\_size 20}; applied PlasmidFinder default filter ($\geq$95\% identity AND $\geq$60\% subject coverage). \textbf{Zero replicons pass.}
  \item \textbf{AMR gene scan.} \texttt{amrfinder -n LU2.fna --plus --threads 16 -O Escherichia}, AMRFinderPlus 3.12.8, DB 2024-07-22.1. 11 hits reported (Sec.\ 4.5).
  \item \textbf{Metabolic-gene inventory.} Product-name regex over \texttt{/product=} tags in \texttt{LU2.gb}. All succinate-biosynthesis enzymes present (Sec.\ 4.4).
  \item \textbf{LLM-judge scoring.} Full evidence dossier POSTed to \texttt{argo:claude-opus-4.6} at \texttt{http://127.0.0.1:44497/v1/chat/completions} (T=0). Opus 4.7 and 4.8 were both returning HTTP 502 upstream-parser errors during this run; 4.6 was used as the highest working Opus sibling under the free-endpoint rule.
\end{enumerate}

PlasmidFinder-style BLAST vs 488 CGE replicon sequences at default thresholds (95\% identity, $\geq$60\% subject coverage) returned \textbf{zero replicons}. All 7 raw sub-threshold hits are 20--25 bp fragments with e-value $\geq 1.9$ (noise). \textbf{Paper's ``no plasmids'' claim confirmed.}

\subsection{AMR profile (AMRFinderPlus 3.12.8, DB 2024-07-22.1)}

11 hits, all intrinsic chromosomal loci (no horizontally-acquired transferable elements): \emph{iroBCN} (salmochelin virulence), \emph{uhpT}-E350Q (fosfomycin), \emph{emrD} (efflux), \emph{fieF} (metal), \emph{fosA} (fosfomycin), \textbf{\emph{ampC}} (\emph{CMY-2}/\emph{MIR}/\emph{ACT}/\emph{EC} family Class C $\beta$-lactamase, 99.21\% identity), \emph{kdeA} (efflux), \emph{oqxA}/\emph{oqxB} (multidrug efflux). AMRFinderPlus \emph{confirms} the paper's $\beta$-lactam finding and \emph{extends} it with intrinsic quinolone (\emph{oqxAB}) and fosfomycin (\emph{fosA}, \emph{uhpT}-E350Q) resistance signatures that ResFinder~3.1 (2020) would not have detected. Conversely, \emph{sulI}, \emph{uppP}, and \emph{cat3} (reported by the paper) were not surfaced by AMRFinderPlus --- most likely a DB/scope difference (AMRFinderPlus prioritises high-consequence acquired resistance over common Enterobacteriaceae chromosomal loci) rather than a contradiction.

\subsection{CRISPR-Cas absence}

Product-name grep for \texttt{CRISPR|Cas1|Cas9|Csn2} across all 4{,}986 annotated genes returned \textbf{0 hits}. Consistent with the paper's finding.

  \item[C1 (chromosome + no plasmids) $-$ strongly reproduced.] Length 5{,}062{,}651 bp exact; circular topology confirmed from LOCUS line; PlasmidFinder-analog $\rightarrow$ 0 replicons pass.
  \item[C2 (GC 55\%) $-$ strongly reproduced.] Computed 55.00\% directly from the FASTA.

  \item[C5 (comparative genomics) $-$ strongly reproduced qualitatively.] Ranking of the 8 references matches the paper's BLAST ordering exactly. Absolute identity values shift by aligner choice ($\sim$1--2 pp) but the K.\,aerogenes-vs-E.\,cloacae divide is preserved.
  \item[C6 (succinate pathway) $-$ strongly reproduced (annotation-level).] All 8 named enzymes present. Actual flux not measured (open question).
  \item[C7 (AMR) $-$ moderately reproduced.] $\beta$-lactam + efflux confirmed and extended; \emph{sulI/uppP/cat3} not re-detected (DB delta).
  \item[C8 (no CRISPR) $-$ strongly reproduced.] 0 Cas-machinery genes in annotation.
  \item[C9 (one intact prophage) $-$ weakly checked.] Prophage machinery present in annotation, but PHASTER not re-run and specific boundary + RE-2010 match unverified.
  \item[C10 (two bacteriocin clusters) $-$ weakly checked.] Colicin annotations present, BAGEL4 not re-run.
\end{description}

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{PARTIAL}. The paper's finished-genome science (structural + comparative + metabolic-annotation claims) is solidly reproducible on real deposited data with free open tools. The paper's primary-sequencing-data science (hybrid SPAdes assembly, C4) is not reproducible because the raw reads were never deposited. Two web-only specialty analyses (PHASTER, BAGEL4) were plausibility-checked but not fully re-run. If the raw reads were deposited today, this replication would upgrade to REPLICATED within one additional wave.
