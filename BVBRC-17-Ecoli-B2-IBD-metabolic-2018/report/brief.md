# BVBRC-17 — Brief

**Paper:** Fang X, Monk JM, Mih N, Du B, Sastry AV, Kavvas E, Seif Y, Smarr L, Palsson BO.
*Escherichia coli B2 strains prevalent in IBD patients have distinct metabolic capabilities that enable colonization of intestinal mucosa.*
BMC Systems Biology (2018). **DOI:** [10.1186/s12918-018-0587-5](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12918-018-0587-5) — PMID 29890970 — PMCID PMC5996543.

## Core claims
1. A pan-genome of **110 E. coli strains** (including 53 IBD isolates) reveals metabolic genes differentially distributed between phylogroup B2 and other strains.
2. Both comparative genomics and genome-scale metabolic models (GEMs) indicate B2 strains have an advantage in degrading and using **mucus-glycan-derived sugars**.
3. B2 GEMs reveal energy-utilization differences (e.g., absence of Amadori product degradation enzymes, leveraging neighbor bacteria for cross-feeding).

## Replication scope (short pass)
- Confirm the canonical B2/AIEC reference strains used in the field (LF82, UTI89, NRG857c) are available in BV-BRC.
- Confirm that BV-BRC has *more than enough* E. coli complete genomes to reconstruct the 110-strain pan-genome today.
- Note that the *GEM-based* claims require COBRApy + iJO1366-derived models, which is out of scope for a metadata pass.
