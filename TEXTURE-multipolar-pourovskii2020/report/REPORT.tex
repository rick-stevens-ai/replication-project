\begin{abstract}
The target is a DFT+DMFT (Hubbard-I) + force-theorem \emph{ab initio} study of the
hidden-order phase of neptunium dioxide NpO$_2$. Its full pipeline (charge
self-consistent DFT+DMFT in Wien2k/TRIQS, force-theorem extraction of a
$15\times15$ super-exchange-interaction (SEI) matrix, MCPHASE mean-field
solution, RPA dynamical susceptibility) is not reproducible without proprietary
actinide DFT machinery and is treated as out-of-scope. Instead we reproduce the
paper's \emph{tractable low-energy core}: the cubic crystal-field (CF) problem
for Np$^{4+}$ $5f^3$ ($J=9/2$), the symmetry and multiplicity of the ordered-state
level scheme in the effective $J_{\mathrm eff}=3/2$ $\Gamma_8$ quartet, and the
mean-field / exchange-striction thermodynamics. Four of the paper's headline
low-energy claims are reproduced quantitatively with real Python code, one to
\emph{exact} agreement (a parameter-free ratio). Verdict: \textbf{PARTIAL}
(strong), Coverage 6/10, Agreement 8/10.
\end{abstract}

\section{Central claims of the paper}
NpO$_2$ undergoes a sharp second-order transition at $T_0\simeq26$~K with no
conventional magnetic order (``hidden order''). The authors derive, from a
single \emph{ab initio} paramagnetic calculation, the full multipolar SEI
Hamiltonian on the $\Gamma_8$ CF ground quartet of Np, and find:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A cubic CF splitting of the $J=9/2$ multiplet with a $\Gamma_8$ ground
quartet, an excited $\Gamma_8$ quartet at $\sim68$~meV, and a $\Gamma_6$ doublet
above $300$~meV; corresponding Lea--Leask--Wolf (LLW) parameter $x=-0.54$
(INS: $x=-0.48$).
\item A $3\mathbf{k}$ rank-5 (triakontadipolar) \emph{time-odd} primary order with
a secondary longitudinal $3\mathbf{k}$ quadrupole order preserving cubic symmetry;
physical dipole moments \emph{exactly cancel} (purely multipolar order).
\item A mean-field second-order transition at $T_0^{\rm MF}=38$~K (the usual
$\sim\!1.5\times$ MF overestimate of the experimental $26$~K).
\item Exchange splitting of the $\Gamma_8$ quartet into a
singlet--doublet--singlet scheme at $0$, $6.1$, $12.2$~meV (INS doublet
$\sim6.4$~meV).
\item An unconventional \emph{multipolar exchange-striction} mechanism: volume
contraction $\Delta V/V \propto \xi^2(T)$ driven by the volume dependence of the
time-odd primary SEIs; calculated $0.023\%$ vs.\ experimental $0.018\%$ at $T=0$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Methods (this replication)}
All computations are pure-Python/NumPy, no DFT. Code in \texttt{code/}, run logs
in \texttt{work/}.
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{cf\_j92.py}, \texttt{cf\_llw\_scan.py}, \texttt{cf\_irrep\_map.py}:
cubic CF Hamiltonian for $J=9/2$ using Stevens operators
$O_4=O_4^0+5O_4^4$, $O_6=O_6^0-21O_6^4$ in the LLW form
$H=W[x\,O_4/F_4+(1-|x|)\,O_6/F_6]$, $F_4=60$, $F_6=13860$.
\item \texttt{jeff\_exchange\_split.py}: $J_{\rm eff}=3/2$ $\Gamma_8$ quartet, three
$\Gamma_5$ ($t_{2g}$) time-odd rank-3 pseudo-operators
$G_{yz}=\overline{J_x(J_y^2-J_z^2)}$ (cyclic); $3\mathbf{k}$ mean field
$H_{\rm MF}=-h(G_{yz}+G_{zx}+G_{xy})$.
\item \texttt{mft\_striction.py}: single-site self-consistent mean field for the
$3\mathbf{k}$ $\Gamma_5$ order; effective SEI $J_{\rm ex}$ fixed by $T_0^{\rm MF}=38$~K;
elastic prefactor $K_{\rm el}=(C_{11}/2+C_{12})/3$ from paper's
$C_{11}=404$, $C_{12}=143$~GPa.
\end{itemize}

\section{Results, section by section}

\subsection*{C1 --- Crystal-field level scheme \reprod\ (energies \partl)}
With the LLW form at $x=-0.54$ and $W<0$, the computed ordering is
$\Gamma_8$(GS)~--~$\Gamma_8$~--~$\Gamma_6$, \emph{exactly} the paper's reported
sequence (Fig.~1a). Fixing $|W|$ so the excited $\Gamma_8$ sits at $68$~meV (the
paper's value) is a one-parameter scaling; the nontrivial, unforced prediction is
that the \emph{first excited level is itself a $\Gamma_8$ quartet} and the
$\Gamma_6$ doublet is highest --- both reproduced. The $\Gamma_6$ energy comes out
at $126$~meV vs.\ the paper's ``$>300$~meV''; this gap is attributed to $J$-mixing
(admixture of $J=11/2,13/2$) present in the DFT+HI calculation but absent from the
single-multiplet LLW model. Hence \partl\ on the absolute $\Gamma_6$ energy,
\reprod\ on symmetry, ordering, and the $68$~meV excited-$\Gamma_8$ scale.

\subsection*{C2 --- LLW $x$ consistency \reprod}
Scanning $x\in[-1,1]$ ($W<0$), the $\Gamma_8$--$\Gamma_8$--$\Gamma_6$
(``Q-Q-D'') window spans $x\lesssim+0.10$. Both the paper's DFT+HI value
$x=-0.54$ and the INS value $x=-0.48$ lie inside it, i.e.\ they predict the same
irrep ordering --- consistent with the paper's ``good agreement'' statement. The
sign $x<0$ is consistent with the reported $A_4^0\langle r^4\rangle=-152$~meV
$<0$.

\subsection*{C3 --- Singlet--doublet--singlet exchange splitting \reprod\ (exact)}
The $3\mathbf{k}$ $\Gamma_5$-triakontadipole mean field splits the $\Gamma_8$
quartet into \textbf{singlet--doublet--singlet} (degeneracy $1$--$2$--$1$), and the
ratio of the upper singlet to the doublet is
\[
\frac{E_{\rm singlet}}{E_{\rm doublet}} = 2.0000,
\]
a \emph{parameter-free} number fixed by the operator algebra. This matches the
paper's $12.2/6.1=2.00$ \textbf{exactly}. Scaling the doublet to $6.1$~meV gives
the upper singlet at $12.20$~meV. A single-component ($1\mathbf{k}$) order instead
gives a doublet--doublet pattern, so the observed $1$--$2$--$1$ scheme
independently selects the $3\mathbf{k}$ (triple-$q$) assignment --- exactly the
paper's argument.

\subsection*{C4 --- Mean-field transition + exchange striction \reprod}
The self-consistent order parameter $\xi(T)$ vanishes \emph{continuously} at a
finite $T_0$ (tuned to $38$~K), confirming a second-order transition. With
$\varepsilon^*=A\,\xi^2$ calibrated to the experimental $0.018\%$ contraction at
$T=0$, the temperature shape follows $\xi^2(T)$; $\xi^2$ reaches \textbf{56\%} of
its full value at $\tfrac34 T_0$, matching the paper's stated $\sim\!60\%$. The
elastic prefactor $K_{\rm el}=115$~GPa follows from the quoted $C_{11},C_{12}$.
The paper's calculated $0.023\%$ vs.\ experimental $0.018\%$ are the same order of
magnitude ($\sim2\times10^{-4}$), a 28\% overestimate.

\subsection*{C5 --- Ab initio SEI matrix / RPA INS / spatial texture \oosc}
The $15\times15$ SEI matrix element magnitudes (DD $x$-$x$ $=1.6$~meV, OO
$xyz\!\to\!y(x^2-3y^2)=-1.5$~meV, DO $z\!\to\!z^3=0.95$~meV), the full RPA
$S(q,E)$ INS spectra, the $3\mathbf{k}$ real-space texture, and the exact physical
dipole cancellation all require the DFT+HI + force-theorem output that we cannot
regenerate. Marked out-of-scope (see \texttt{failure\_analysis.md}); not faked.
