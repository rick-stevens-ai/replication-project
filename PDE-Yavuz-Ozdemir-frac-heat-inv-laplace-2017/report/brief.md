# Brief

Yavuz & Özdemir (Thermal Science, 2018, vol. 22 Suppl. 1, pp. S185-S194; DOI 10.2298/TSCI170804285Y) propose the "Laplace Homotopy Perturbation Method" (LHPM) — He's homotopy perturbation of the Laplace-transformed fractional PDE, with Stehfest's numerical inverse Laplace to recover u(x,t) — for 1-D time-fractional heat / Burgers equations with Caputo derivative. They demonstrate the method on three examples: (E1) fractional inhomogeneous Burgers where LHPM converges to the closed-form u=x²+t², (E2) fractional heat with source where the α=2 limit gives u=x³t³ after noise-term cancellation, and (E3) a fractional heat equation on [0,2] with zero IC/BC whose exact solution is a Mittag-Leffler-like series and where they tabulate absolute errors (Table 1) of order 1e-10 to 1e-7 across x∈{0.1,0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9}, t∈{0.1,0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9}, α∈{0.25,0.40,0.75}. This replication reimplements LHPM + Stehfest inversion in Python and re-derives (a) the closed-form for E1, (b) the series solution for E3, and (c) the E3 absolute error table.
