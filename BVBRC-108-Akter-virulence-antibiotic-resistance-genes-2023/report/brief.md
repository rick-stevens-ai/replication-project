# Brief

Independent replication of Akter et al. 2023 (*Sci Rep*, PMID 36707682): whole-genome sequencing of three fish-pathogenic *E. faecalis* strains (BFFF11, BFF1B1, BFPS6) reporting 69 virulence factors and 39 antibiotic-resistance genes. We fetched all three assemblies from NCBI (CP045918, CP046022, GCF_021375735.1), ran AMRFinderPlus 3.12.8 (DB 2024-07-22.1) and tblastn against VFDB set-A, and cross-tabulated hits against Table 1 (genome features), Table 2 (AMR genes), and the paper's per-strain virulence-gene claims. Genome-size/GC/N50/L50 match to the bp; all "present in every strain" virulence genes recovered; capsule-cluster BFFF11-uniqueness reproduced (9 of 11 cps genes); prgB/asa1 unique-to-BFFF11 reproduced; ctrA absent-in-BFPS6 reproduced; cylI-in-BFPS6 and cylR2-in-BFF1B1 both reproduced; tet(L)/tet(M) BFPS6-only reproduced; tet(S)/tet(45) not called by AMRFinderPlus (paper called via ARG-ANNOT/ResFinder at different thresholds). Also uncovered a paper Table-1 column-label swap between BFFF11 and BFF1B1 (a paper-side clerical error). LLM-judge (Argo `claude-sonnet-4.6`) verdict: **PARTIAL REPLICATION** with 72% coverage / 85% agreement. See REPORT.md for the full claims table + methods.
