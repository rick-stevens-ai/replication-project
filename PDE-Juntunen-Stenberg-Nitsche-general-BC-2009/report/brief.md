# Brief

We independently replicate Juntunen & Stenberg (2009), *"Nitsche's method for general boundary conditions"* (Math. Comp. 78:1353–1374, DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-08-02183-2), a foundational finite-element paper extending Nitsche's Dirichlet-BC formulation to a one-parameter family of boundary conditions ε ≥ 0 that unifies Dirichlet (ε=0), Robin (0<ε<∞), and Neumann (ε=∞). Using **scikit-fem** (Python OSS finite-element library) we implement the bilinear form Bₕ (Eq. 2.5) and load Fₕ (Eq. 2.6) with the paper's γ=0.1 stability parameter, solve the paper's manufactured Poisson problem (Sec. 6, model 6.1) on the rectangle Ω=(0,1)×(0,0.3), and quantitatively verify the paper's four main theoretical claims: (T1) consistency (Lemma 2.1), (T2) coercivity independent of h and ε (Theorem 3.2), (T3) optimal a priori convergence rates (Theorem 3.5), and (T4/T5) the paper's central applied claim — Nitsche's condition number is ε-independent whereas the traditional method's condition number grows as 1/ε (Fig. 5). All four principal claims reproduce cleanly, including a sharpness verification of the coercivity bound at γ ≈ 2. A fifth claim (Theorem 4.2 a posteriori estimator two-sided bound) is only partially checked via a simplified surrogate estimator that omits the full boundary-jump form of Eq. (4.1); qualitative effectivity is confirmed. We also caught a typographical error in the paper's manufactured-source formula for g (paper writes sinh/cosh but coth is required for the ε-independence to hold).
