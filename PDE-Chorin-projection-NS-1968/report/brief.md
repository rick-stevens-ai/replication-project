# Brief — Chorin (1968) projection method

Independent replication of Alexandre Chorin's foundational 1968 paper introducing the projection / fractional-step method for the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations (Math. Comp. 22, 745–762). We implemented the scheme from scratch in Python/NumPy on a 2D MAC staggered grid — explicit-Euler advection+diffusion for the provisional velocity, direct sparse Poisson solve for the pressure with Neumann BCs, then velocity correction — and validated it on three independent tests: (a) the divergence-free property of the projection (Chorin's central algorithmic claim); (b) Chorin's own Pearson exact-solution test problem from Section 5 of the paper; (c) the community-standard lid-driven cavity benchmark at Re=100 and Re=400, comparing centerline velocity profiles to the canonical Ghia, Ghia & Shin (1982) reference table; and (d) spatial + temporal convergence rates on a manufactured solution. All computations local CPU (CherryRd), no external solver library, no LLM-in-the-loop for the numerics. LLM (Argo `claude-sonnet-4.6`) was used only as an unbiased judge over the numerical evidence.
