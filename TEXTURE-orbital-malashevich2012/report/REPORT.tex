\section{Headline claim}
The paper computes, from first principles, the linear transverse magnetoelectric (ME)
response of antiferromagnetic Cr$_2$O$_3$ at $T=0$:
\[
  |\alpha_\perp| = 1.04~\text{ps/m},
\]
in good agreement with experiment ($0.7$--$1.6$ ps/m). Table~II decomposes this into
spin/orbital $\times$ electronic/lattice channels. Crucially, $\alpha_\perp$ is
\emph{$\sim$98\% spin}: spin-lattice $0.77$ + spin-electronic $0.26$ = $1.03$ ps/m,
while the two orbital contributions sum to only $0.011$ ps/m ($<2\%$).

\section{Scope and strategy}
Reproducing the absolute $1.04$ ps/m requires a spin-orbit-coupled DFT+U campaign
(Quantum ESPRESSO): finite-electric-field magnetization tracking, phonons and Born
effective charges (spin-lattice term), and the spin susceptibility (spin-electronic
term). This is far outside a $<6$~min from-scratch tight-binding run and we scope it
honestly. What \emph{is} tractable, and is the physically distinctive part of this
paper, is the \textbf{orbital / Berry-phase machinery}, which we rebuild from scratch:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The \textbf{Chern--Simons (axion) ME coupling}
        $\alpha_{CS} = (\theta/\pi)\,(e^2/2h)\,\mu_0$, whose quantum the paper quotes
        as $24.3$ ps/m. We compute the axion angle $\theta$ of a 3D Wilson--Dirac
        insulator from inversion parities at the 8 TRIM (Fu--Kane / Essin--Moore--Vanderbilt).
  \item The \textbf{itinerant-circulation} orbital operator $L_z=\tfrac12(\mathbf r\times\mathbf v)$,
        reusing the g\"obel2024 Berry kernel, to demonstrate the IC branch of $\alpha^{orb}_\perp$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Method: orbital ME quantities}
\subsection{Chern--Simons quantum}
With $e$, $h$, $\mu_0$ in SI, $(e^2/2h)\,\mu_0 = 24.34$ ps/m, matching the paper's
$24.3$ ps/m to $<0.2\%$. For a 3D Wilson--Dirac Hamiltonian
$H(\mathbf k)=A\sum_i\sin k_i\,\alpha_i + M(\mathbf k)\beta$, $M(\mathbf k)=M_0-2B\sum_i(1-\cos k_i)$,
the occupied-band parity at a TRIM is $\mathrm{sgn}\,M(\mathbf k_{TRIM})$. An odd number
of band inversions gives strong index $\nu_0=1\Rightarrow\theta=\pi\Rightarrow
\alpha_{CS}=24.3$ ps/m (topological); an even number gives $\theta=0$ (trivial).

\subsection{Itinerant circulation}
Using the g\"obel2024 itinerant $L_z=\tfrac12(X v_y - Y v_x)$ operator on a chiral
texture yields a finite accumulated occupied orbital moment, confirming the operator
that generates the IC branch (paper: $\alpha^{orb}_\perp$ IC electronic $=-0.0084$ ps/m).
