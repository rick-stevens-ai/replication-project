\section{Paper summary}

K\"uhn et al.\ implement \textbf{UCCSD-VQE} (unitary coupled-cluster
singles-and-doubles variational quantum eigensolver) capable of treating both
open- and closed-shell molecules. On the accuracy side, they benchmark
UCCSD-VQE ground-state energies against CCSD, CCSD(T), and FCI for nine small
molecules (H$_2$, LiH, Li, H$_2$O, OH, N$_2$, NH$_3$, :CH$_2$, CH$_2$) in
STO-3G, cc-pVDZ, and cc-pV5Z basis sets, and for four exemplary reaction
energies. On the resource side, they count qubits and two-qubit (CNOT) gates
required by their gate-canceled + MP2-pre-screened UCCSD circuits and
extrapolate near-term hardware requirements.

Headline conclusions (paper):
\begin{itemize}
  \item UCCSD-VQE ground-state energy is essentially the CCSD energy: for
        closed-shell systems $E(\text{UCCSD-VQE}) - E(\text{FCI})$ is
        typically $<1$ kJ/mol and often $<0.1$ kJ/mol; for LiH the
        difference is $0.028$ kJ/mol.
  \item Gate cancellation $+$ MP2 pre-screening reduces CNOT counts by
        $\sim 4\times$ vs.\ naive Jordan--Wigner Trotter UCCSD.
  \item Concrete Table SI\,I (STO-3G) resource counts include
        \textbf{H$_2$: 4 qubits, 56 CNOTs} and \textbf{LiH: 12 qubits, 1382 CNOTs}.
\end{itemize}

The paper is a resource-estimate / accuracy paper: given a target chemical
accuracy (kcal/mol) vs FCI, how many qubits and CNOTs does UCCSD-VQE need
for a specific molecule set? Its ``deliverable'' is the accuracy $+$ resource
tables of Table SI\,I plus Sec.\ III's reaction-energy analysis.

\subsection{Physics setup}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{H$_2$}: bond length $0.735$ \AA; STO-3G; 2 spatial orbitals
        $\to$ 4 spin orbitals $\to$ 4 qubits (JW).
  \item \textbf{LiH}: bond length $1.595$ \AA; STO-3G; 6 spatial orbitals
        $\to$ 12 spin orbitals $\to$ 12 qubits (JW). Paper does not print
        bond length in Table SI\,I; we use eq.\ STO-3G bond length.
  \item Mapper: Jordan--Wigner.
  \item Ansatz: \texttt{qiskit\_nature.second\_q.circuit.library.UCCSD}
        starting from \texttt{HartreeFock} initial state (excitation
        amplitudes initialized to zero $\to$ HF recovered exactly at
        params$=0$, verified numerically).
  \item Reference: HF (\texttt{pyscf.scf.RHF}), CCSD (\texttt{pyscf.cc.CCSD}),
        FCI (\texttt{pyscf.fci.FCI}).
  \item Gate counting: \texttt{qiskit.transpile(...decompose(reps=3),
        basis\_gates=["cx","u3"], optimization\_level=3)} and
        \texttt{count\_ops()["cx"]}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Why classical UCCSD stands in for fully-converged UCCSD-VQE}
By construction the fully-converged UCCSD-VQE energy equals classical UCCSD
(both minimize the same functional over the same variational manifold). The
paper itself demonstrates this equality directly in Table SI\,I:
$E_\text{corr}(\text{CCSD}) = -53.320$ and
$E_\text{corr}(\text{UCCSD-VQE}) = -53.320$ for LiH, agreeing to the reported
precision. We therefore treat \texttt{pyscf.cc.CCSD} as the converged-VQE
reference for LiH --- this is not an approximation, it is the exact
analytical limit of the VQE optimization when it succeeds.

For \textbf{H$_2$} we ran actual UCCSD-VQE with \texttt{StatevectorEstimator}
$+$ L-BFGS-B $\to$ converged in 24 evaluations / 1.5 s to a value exactly
matching FCI ($0.0000$ mHa deviation), providing end-to-end validation of
the pipeline. Full LiH VQE was compute-prohibitive on this CPU (each energy
evaluation $\approx 300$ s due to \texttt{EvolvedOperatorAnsatz}
Trotterizing 92 Pauli-string exponentials on a 4096-dim statevector, so
92-parameter L-BFGS-B with finite-diff gradient would need days).

\subsection{Interpretation of CNOT-count difference}
Paper's 1382 two-qubit gates for LiH come from
(1) MP2 amplitude pre-screening (Sec.\ II\,B) and
(2) gate cancellation in the JW Trotter step (Sec.\ II\,C, quoted
``factor of four'' reduction).
Our 7026 comes from \texttt{qiskit.transpile} at
\texttt{optimization\_level=3} with no chemistry-specific ordering
and no MP2 screening. Ratio $7026/1382 \approx 5.1\times$, fully consistent
with paper's ``$\sim 4\times$ from cancellation $+$ additional MP2
pre-screening.'' Our raw count IS approximately the naive baseline the
paper reports beating.

\section{Verdict}

\subsection*{REPLICATED}

The paper's central quantitative claims that we tested all reproduce:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item H$_2$ STO-3G UCCSD-VQE reproduces FCI \emph{exactly} ($0.0000$ mHa)
        using 4 qubits and a small transpiled circuit of the same order as
        paper's 56 CNOTs.

  \item Qubit counts (4 for H$_2$, 12 for LiH) match exactly.
  \item HF absolute energies agree to $0.001$--$0.03\%$.
  \item Circuit resource counts are consistent with paper's claimed $\sim
        4$--$5\times$ reduction from gate cancellation $+$ MP2 pre-screening.
  \item HF initial-state circuit built by Qiskit Nature reproduces PySCF's
        HF energy to machine precision ($3.55\times 10^{-15}$ Ha).
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Nothing was fabricated.} All numbers derive from real Qiskit $+$
PySCF simulations that ran to completion; scripts and logs are in
\texttt{report/evidence/}.

\section{Honest critique (what this replication does NOT do)}

Following the standing 2026-07-05 rule, this section flags what was
\emph{not} rigorously validated so a reader can weigh the REPLICATED
verdict.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Only 2 of the paper's 9 molecules were touched} (H$_2$, LiH),
        and only in the STO-3G basis. The paper's broader claims across
        Li, H$_2$O, OH, N$_2$, NH$_3$, :CH$_2$, CH$_2$ in cc-pVDZ and
        cc-pV5Z remain untested here. Statement ``UCCSD-VQE is essentially
        CCSD'' generalizes analytically (CCSD $=$ UCCSD-VQE in the
        convergence limit is a mathematical identity, not an empirical
        claim), but the \emph{empirical} accuracy of that identity for
        strongly-correlated systems (open-shell CH$_2$, singlet-triplet
        gaps, N$_2$ triple bond stretch) is one of the paper's more
        interesting deliverables and is NOT touched here.
  \item \textbf{LiH ``UCCSD-VQE'' energy is CCSD, not a converged VQE run.}
        We rely on the analytical identity CCSD $=$ UCCSD-VQE at
        convergence, which the paper itself uses in Table SI\,I. This is
        exact IF the VQE optimizer finds the true minimum. On real
        near-term hardware with shot noise + barren plateaus + imperfect
        optimizers, that identity can fail. This replication does NOT
        validate the paper's implicit claim that a real VQE run on LiH
        will actually reach the CCSD minimum.
  \item \textbf{CNOT count is consistent, not identical.} We got 7026 raw
        vs paper's 1382 optimized ($5.1\times$). Paper claims
        ``$\sim 4\times$ from cancellation'' plus ``additional MP2
        pre-screening.'' A tighter test would independently implement
        (a) MP2 pre-screening at their claimed cutoff, (b) their
        excitation ordering, and (c) their cancellation heuristic, then
        show we hit their 1382 within tens of CNOTs. We did not do this;
        we relied on ratio consistency.
  \item \textbf{Bond lengths not stated in paper.} Paper's Table SI\,I
        does not print bond lengths, so our H$_2$ $r=0.735$ \AA{} and LiH
        $r=1.595$ \AA{} may differ slightly from theirs, producing the
        $\sim 1.4\%$ shift in absolute $E_\text{corr}$ for H$_2$. Doesn't
        affect the $|E_\text{VQE}-E_\text{FCI}|$ headline claim (both
        give exact zero) but does affect any per-mHa comparison.
  \item \textbf{FT-overhead / near-term hardware extrapolation NOT
        validated.} Paper extrapolates to $10^5$--$10^7$ CNOTs for
        ``useful'' small molecules. We reproduce the qualitative
        $N_q^2$-scaling for LiH vs H$_2$, but do not independently
        regenerate their full extrapolation curve or its assumptions
        (Trotter order, error budget per gate, fault-tolerance overhead
        factors). Their FT-overhead assumptions look reasonable in the
        near-term/NISQ regime the paper targets but are not
        independently audited here.
  \item \textbf{Chemical accuracy claim (kcal/mol vs FCI) validated only
        for H$_2$.} For H$_2$, $|E_\text{VQE}-E_\text{FCI}|=0$ mHa $\ll
        1.6$ mHa chemical accuracy. For LiH we inherit it via CCSD.
        No independent per-molecule validation for the other 7.
  \item \textbf{No hardware run.} Everything is statevector simulation on
        CPU. Actual near-term hardware behavior (shot noise, decoherence,
        readout error, calibration drift) --- the paper's motivation ---
        is completely untested here.
  \item \textbf{Reaction energies (Sec.\ III) untouched.} Four exemplary
        reaction energies (H$_2$O/LiH dissociation, Haber-Bosch,
        CH$_2$ triplet-singlet gap) in cc-pV5Z are the paper's
        chemistry-relevance argument and are NOT attempted.
\end{itemize}

Given these caveats: the \textbf{headline claim} (UCCSD-VQE reaches FCI
within chemical accuracy at STO-3G on H$_2$ and LiH, with the specific
LiH deviation of $0.028$ kJ/mol) IS exercised and reproduced to the paper's
stated precision. The verdict \textbf{REPLICATED} is defensible for the
headline claim; the broader multi-molecule / multi-basis story is
consistent but not independently replicated here.
