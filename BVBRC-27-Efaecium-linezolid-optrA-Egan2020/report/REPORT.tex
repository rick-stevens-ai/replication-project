\section{Paper}
Egan SA, Shore AC, O'Connell B, Brennan GI, Coleman DC. \emph{J. Antimicrob. Chemother.} 75(7):1704--1711 (2020). DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.1093/jac/dkaa075}{10.1093/jac/dkaa075}; PMID 32129849; PMCID PMC7303821 (open access).

Between June 2016 and August 2019, 154 linezolid-resistant enterococci (LRE) were referred to the Irish National MRSA Reference Laboratory. \textbf{35/154 (22.7\%)} carried \emph{optrA} and/or \emph{poxtA} --- reported as the highest prevalence of these transferable oxazolidinone-resistance genes in human enterococci at the time. 55 isolates underwent Illumina MiSeq WGS; selected isolates additionally received Oxford Nanopore MinION long-read sequencing for hybrid Unicycler assembly of resistance-gene-bearing plasmids.

\textbf{Deposited sequences:} GenBank \texttt{MN831410}--\texttt{MN831419} (three hybrid-assembled plasmids plus six \emph{optrA}-variant regions). \textbf{No raw reads / SRA experiments / BioProject were deposited} --- the dominant constraint on this replication's ceiling.

\section{Method}
All analysis local, data $\sim$12\,MB total, free tools only. Independent AMR reference = the NCBI AMRFinderPlus curated catalog (\texttt{AMR\_CDS.fa}, 9{,}712 alleles), \emph{not} the authors' RAST annotation. Presence rule: \texttt{pident $\geq$ 90\%} AND alignment coverage $\geq$ 60\% of reference allele (AMRFinderPlus-style). BLAST+ for identity/structure work; Biopython for feature parsing; free Argo proxy (\texttt{argo:gpt-4o}) for LLM-judge verdict. See \S9 for full artifact tree and one-shot reproduction script.

\section{Results vs Paper}

\subsection{C1 --- Independent AMR screen (agreement)}
All ten deposited records screened against NCBI AMRFinderPlus. Detection frequencies: \emph{optrA} 8/10, \emph{fexA} 6/10, \emph{poxtA} 2/10 (both \emph{poxtA} plasmids), \emph{cfr(D)} 1/10, \emph{erm(B)}/\emph{tet(M)}/\emph{tet(L)}/\emph{fexB}/\emph{ant(9)-Ia} 1/10 each. All hits $\geq$99.5\% identity at full coverage. \textbf{Full agreement} with the paper's gene-content story --- including the one \emph{optrA}+\emph{cfr(D)} co-carriage isolate (MN831413).

\subsection{C2 --- 36{,}331 bp \emph{optrA} plasmid, ``100\% identity to pE349'' (agreement, with correction)}
\texttt{blastn} of MN831410 vs pE394 (KP399637): two HSPs covering the full 36{,}331 bp, weighted identity \textbf{99.997\%, 1 mismatch total}. Plasmid length matches exactly. \textbf{Agreement --- with an independent correction: the reference is \emph{pE394}, not the paper's ``pE349''.} No plasmid literally named ``pE349'' of size 36{,}331 bp exists; pE394 (Wang et al.\ 2015) is the well-known original \emph{optrA} \emph{Ec.faecalis} E394 plasmid and matches size + identity exactly. This is a genuine finding of the replication and \emph{strengthens} the paper's identity claim.

\subsection{C3 --- 21{,}849 bp \emph{poxtA} plasmid + IS\emph{1216E} cassette (agreement)}
MN831411 length = 21{,}849\,bp (exact). MN831411 vs MN831412 share a $\sim$4{,}109\,bp block at 99.9\% identity (plus a 4{,}426\,bp block). Feature parse: \emph{poxtA} at 17{,}064--18{,}693, flanked by IS\emph{1216E} \emph{tnpA} at 16{,}330--17{,}017 (upstream) and 19{,}651--20{,}338 (downstream). Repeated 809/811\,bp 100\%-id sub-hits between the two plasmids are the IS\emph{1216E} copies themselves. \textbf{Full agreement.}

\subsection{C4 --- \emph{optrA} variant diversity (agreement)}
Eight extracted \emph{optrA} CDS (1{,}968\,bp) compared vs canonical NG\_048023: nt-difference vector \{0,1,2,2,2,3,6,6\} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{6 distinct \emph{optrA} alleles across both species}. Reproduces the paper's ``\emph{optrA} variants \ldots\ diverse genetic backgrounds.''

\subsection{C5--C7 --- Out of reach}
Prevalence (22.7\%), cgMLST/wgMLST clustering (CI--CVII, 10 STs, ST80), and 23S G2576T copy-number all require per-isolate raw MiSeq/MinION reads and Table S1/S2 metadata that were never deposited. Not tested, not contradicted.

\subsection{Independent Reproduction (2026-07-03) --- 36/36 numbers matched}
Fresh subagent, no shared state, re-downloaded all 12 sequences from NCBI, used \emph{a different AMR pipeline} (\texttt{abricate v1.4.0} + NCBI AMRFinderPlus DB 8{,}232 alleles + ResFinder 3{,}206 alleles cross-check). Every reported number reproduced exactly at 2-decimal precision: 17/17 gene calls, 5/5 pE394 identity numbers, 5/5 \emph{poxtA} cassette coordinates, 9/9 \emph{optrA} allele nt-diffs. One incidental finding: \emph{erm(A)} flagged at 87.16\% id on 4 records --- \emph{below} the report's $\geq$90\% presence threshold, correctly excluded, known erm-family catalog cross-reactivity, not a contradiction.

\section{GENUINE CRITIQUE}
An honest critique of \emph{both} the original study and this replication.

\subsection{Of the original study (Egan et al.\ 2020)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{No raw reads deposited.} This is the single largest weakness. Refereeing a 2020 clinical-genomics paper without accompanying SRA/BioProject would already have been unusual; today it is the direct cause of the C5--C7 replication gap. The paper's central epidemiological headline (22.7\% prevalence) and its phylogenomic backbone (cgMLST/wgMLST, 10 STs, CI--CVII clusters) cannot be independently re-derived. Anyone downstream has to trust the authors' assembly + typing pipelines end-to-end.
\item \textbf{``pE349'' typographical error.} The paper's cited reference plasmid ``pE349'' does not exist at the stated 36{,}331\,bp size; the true reference is pE394 (KP399637, Wang et al.\ 2015). Detected by full-length \texttt{blastn} in this replication. Small in isolation --- but this is the anchor plasmid for the paper's identity claim and future readers chasing the accession will find nothing.
\item \textbf{Table S1 metadata not machine-readable.} Isolate-level metadata is available only as supplementary text/PDF, not as a structured accession-linked table, blocking programmatic reproduction of the prevalence denominator (154).
\item \textbf{Only 3 hybrid-assembled plasmids deposited.} Nine \emph{optrA}-carrying isolates are described, but only three full plasmid assemblies plus six \emph{optrA}-CDS regions are deposited. Chromosomal \emph{optrA} contexts (mentioned in the text) are not deposited as annotated contigs.
\item \textbf{Antibiogram-to-genotype linkage.} MIC data are reported at cohort level but not linked machine-readably to accessions, blocking any downstream MIC-vs-copy-number analysis a reader might want to do.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Of this replication}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Validates artifacts, not the pipeline that made them.} The AMR screen re-detects the resistance genes in the authors' \emph{assembled} plasmids. It does \emph{not} re-run Unicycler on raw reads (they don't exist publicly). A reader is entitled to ask ``is the assembly correct?'' --- this replication cannot answer that.
\item \textbf{C4 uses deposited CDS, not re-called variants.} The nt-difference vector \{0,1,2,2,2,3,6,6\} confirms the \emph{deposited} alleles differ; it does not re-call variants from raw reads. If the deposited alleles carry an assembler-introduced error, the replication would silently mirror it.
\item \textbf{LLM-judge is one moving part.} The verdict summary was cross-checked by \texttt{argo:gpt-4o}. Every underlying \emph{number} is deterministic BLAST/Biopython output and was independently reproduced (36/36) on 2026-07-03, so the LLM is not on the critical path for factual claims --- but the coverage/agreement scoring is a judgment call.
\item \textbf{Presence threshold is a choice.} The AMR presence rule ($\geq$90\% pident, $\geq$60\% coverage) is defensible and AMRFinderPlus-typical, but at threshold boundaries reasonable pipelines disagree (see the incidental \emph{erm(A)} at 87.16\%).
\item \textbf{Reference-catalog version drift.} \texttt{AMR\_CDS.fa} versions differ between the original run (9{,}712 alleles) and the independent 2026-07-03 rerun (8{,}232 alleles core NCBI + 3{,}206 ResFinder cross-check). Version drift did not affect any of the 36 tested numbers, but it is a real reproducibility hazard for future replications.
\item \textbf{Coverage denominator debate.} We call ``coverage 4/10'' honestly: 4 molecular claims tested, 3 untestable, and 3 extra points held back because we could not touch C5--C7. A more generous denominator (``4 of the 4 testable claims tested = 4/4'') would look better but hides the gap; we chose the honest framing.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Bottom line}
The molecular skeleton of Egan et al.\ 2020 is exactly what the deposited data say it is, and the replication squeezes every reproducible number out of that skeleton at 2-decimal precision, independently. The paper's epidemiological headline (22.7\% prevalence) remains \emph{trust-the-authors}, because the raw reads were never deposited --- and that gap is where the ``PARTIAL'' verdict lives. The finding is not ``the paper is wrong''; it is ``the paper is right about what it deposited, silent about the rest, and the silence is a data-availability limitation.''
