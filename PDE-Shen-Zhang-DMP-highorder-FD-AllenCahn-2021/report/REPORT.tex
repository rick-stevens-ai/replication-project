\begin{abstract}
We independently replicate, from equations only, two of the four core claims
of Shen \& Zhang (Comm.\ Math.\ Sci.\ 20(5) 1447--1474, 2022;
arXiv:2104.11813). Our replication implements a stabilized IMEX
backward-Euler solver for the Allen--Cahn PDE $u_t = \mu \Delta u -
\varepsilon^{-1} f(u)$ with $f(u) = u^3 - u$, using a from-scratch scipy
sparse-Laplacian discretization (2nd-order 3-point and 4th-order compact
5-point stencils), and drives it in real time-stepping in 1D and 2D at
$\varepsilon\in\{0.01, 0.1\}$. We (a) reproduce the paper's $O(h^2)$ rate
for the companion 2nd-order scheme with observed rates $1.82 \to 1.96 \to
2.00$ (1D) and (b) empirically verify the discrete maximum principle
(DMP): $\max_i |u_i^n| \le 1$ in all six dynamics runs, with peak
$0.9970$ at $\varepsilon=0.01$. The paper's exact Q2 spectral-element
4th-order alternating stencils and the operator-monotonicity theorem
(Thm.\ 3.9) are \emph{not} implemented in this angle -- they are handled by
the sibling replication directory
\texttt{PDE-allen-cahn-maxprinciple-shen-zhang-2021}, which achieves
paper-matching $O(h^4)$ rates. Overall LLM-judge verdict (argo:gpt-5.4
via LiteLLM aggregator localhost:4000): \textbf{PARTIAL}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper claims (numbered)}

\begin{description}
  \item[C1] The paper's Q2-based 4th-order FD scheme is $O(h^4)$ accurate
    on smooth Allen--Cahn problems (Tables~6.1, 6.2).
  \item[C2] The companion 2nd-order FD scheme is $O(h^2)$.
  \item[C3] Discrete Maximum Principle: $|\phi^n_i| \le 1$ for all $i,n$
    under mesh + time-step constraints (Thms~3.9, 4.1).
  \item[C4] The 4th-order operator is inverse-positive (monotone) under
    $\Delta t\,\mu/h^2 \ge 3$ (Thm~3.9; the paper's key novelty --- a
    \emph{lower} bound on $\Delta t$).
\end{description}

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Discretization.}
We build sparse periodic 1D Laplacians of two orders on a uniform mesh of
$n$ cells covering $\Omega = (-1,1)$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Order 2: standard $[1,-2,1]/h^2$.
  \item Order 4: classical compact 5-point $[-1,16,-30,16,-1]/(12 h^2)$.
\end{itemize}
The 2D Laplacian is $A_{2D} = I \otimes A_{1D} + A_{1D} \otimes I$
(\texttt{scipy.sparse.kron}). This is \emph{not} the paper's Q2
alternating-stencil scheme (which uses different center-vs-endpoint
formulas) -- our order-4 variant is a complementary check.

\paragraph{Time stepping.}
Stabilized IMEX backward-Euler with linear stabilizer $s = 2/\varepsilon$:
\begin{equation*}
  (I - \Delta t\, \mu A + \Delta t\, s I)\, u^{n+1}
   = (1 + \Delta t\, s)\, u^n - (\Delta t / \varepsilon)\,(u^n)^{\circ 3} + (\Delta t /\varepsilon)\, u^n,
\end{equation*}
solved with a single \texttt{splu} factorization reused across steps.

\paragraph{Accuracy test (manufactured solution).}
$u_{\mathrm{exact}}(x,t) = 0.9 \cos(\pi x) e^{-t}$ (1D) or the tensor
product (2D). Add forcing $g = u_t^{\mathrm{ex}} - \mu \Delta u^{\mathrm{ex}}
+ \varepsilon^{-1}(u_{\mathrm{ex}}^3 - u_{\mathrm{ex}})$ to the RHS so
$u_{\mathrm{exact}}$ is a solution of the modified PDE; measure the error
at $T$ over a sequence $n \in \{32,64,128,256\}$ (1D) or
$\{16,32,64,96\}$ (2D).

\subsection{Convergence, order 4 compact stencil -- honest observation}

The compact 4th-order variant saturates at $\sim 6.78\times 10^{-6}$
(1D) and $\sim 1.4\times 10^{-5}$ (2D) already at $n=32$. This is the
temporal-error floor of the 1st-order backward-Euler time stepping: at
$h \approx 6 \times 10^{-2}$ the space error $\sim h^4 \sim 10^{-5}$ is
already comparable to $\tau_{\rm time} \sim \Delta t = 10^{-4}$. To
recover the pure spatial rate one would need $\Delta t \sim 10^{-6}$,
which would cost $100\times$ more time-steps. This is an honest
observation --- not a claim of success on C1 (which the sibling
replication handles with the paper's exact stencil).

\section{What worked, what didn't (per claim)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{C1 (O($h^4$) space accuracy of paper's Q2 scheme):}
    not attempted in this angle; handled by sibling replication which
    achieves rates $\approx 4.0$. Here, the compact 4th-order Laplacian
    is used and shows the expected time-error floor.
  \item \textbf{C2 (O($h^2$) companion scheme):} \emph{worked cleanly}.
    Rates $\to 2.00$ in 1D and consistent-with-2 in 2D. Reproduced.
  \item \textbf{C3 (DMP during dynamics):} \emph{worked} in all six runs.
    The most-stressed case ($\varepsilon = 0.01$, 1D) peaks at 0.9970,
    well below 1. Empirical support, not a re-proof of the theorem.
  \item \textbf{C4 (Thm 3.9 inverse-positivity under $\Delta t\mu/h^2\ge 3$):}
    not tested for the paper's stencil here; sibling replication
    confirmed it (and confirmed the lower bound is tight -- 16\% negative
    inverse entries when violated).
\end{itemize}
