\begin{abstract}
We reproduce the reproducible core of Christensen \emph{et al.}: the coupled
imaginary-CDW (iCDW, loop currents) and real-CDW (rCDW, bond distortions)
Landau free energy on the kagome metal AV$_3$Sb$_5$. We construct the
symmetry-allowed free energy [their Eqs.~(10)--(13)] from scratch, minimize it
numerically over the six-dimensional order-parameter space
$(N_1,N_2,N_3;\Phi_1,\Phi_2,\Phi_3)$, and map the phase diagram in the bare
quadratic coefficients $(a_r,a_i)\propto(T-T_r,\,T-T_i)$. We confirm the paper's
headline: the coupled theory yields exactly \textbf{two generic mixed
charge-ordered states}, $\mathbf{3Q\text{-}3Q}$ and $\mathbf{2Q\text{-}1Q}$,
selected by the higher-order coefficients; pure iCDW is not a stand-alone
minimum; and the $2Q\text{-}1Q$ state breaks $C_3$ (orthorhombic). We skip the
DFT/group-theory VHS symmetry-content derivation. \textbf{Verdict: REPLICATED}
(Landau core).
\end{abstract}

\section{Model}
The order parameters live on the three symmetry-related $M$ points
$\mathbf{Q}_1,\mathbf{Q}_2,\mathbf{Q}_3$ (the $2\times2$ order): a real
(TRS-even) rCDW $N_i$ transforming as $M_1^+$, and an imaginary (TRS-odd) iCDW
$\Phi_i$ (loop current). The free energy is $F=F_r+F_i+F_{i\text{-}r}$:
\begin{align}
F_r &= \tfrac{a_r}{2}N^2 + \tfrac{\gamma_r}{3}N_1N_2N_3
       + \tfrac{u_r}{4}N^4 + \tfrac{\lambda_r}{4}\!\!\sum_{i<j}\!N_i^2N_j^2,\\
F_i &= \tfrac{a_i}{2}\Phi^2 + \tfrac{u_i}{4}\Phi^4
       + \tfrac{\lambda_i}{4}\!\!\sum_{i<j}\!\Phi_i^2\Phi_j^2,\\
F_{i\text{-}r} &= \tfrac{\gamma_{ir}}{3}(N_1\Phi_2\Phi_3+\Phi_1N_2\Phi_3+\Phi_1\Phi_2N_3)
   + \tfrac{\kappa_{ir}}{4}\!\!\sum_{i<j}\!N_iN_j\Phi_i\Phi_j \nonumber\\
&\quad + \tfrac{\lambda_{ir}^{(1)}}{4}\sum_i N_i^2\Phi_i^2
       + \tfrac{\lambda_{ir}^{(2)}}{4}N^2\Phi^2 .
\end{align}
The decisive symmetry input: TRS forbids a cubic $\Phi_1\Phi_2\Phi_3$ term
(odd power of the imaginary order parameter), so the \emph{only} cubic invariant
that can lower the iCDW energy is the mixed trilinear $\gamma_{ir}$. It is
minimized by exactly two configurations, $3Q\text{-}3Q$ (all $N_i,\Phi_i\neq0$)
and $2Q\text{-}1Q$ ($\Phi_i,\Phi_j,N_l\neq0$).

\section{Method}
Multistart Nelder--Mead minimization (33 structured + random seeds) of $F$ on a
$6\times6$ grid in $(a_r,a_i)\in[-2,0.5]^2$, for two coefficient sets. Global
minima are classified by counting nonzero $N_i$ and $\Phi_i$. Loop-current
interpretation follows the shared \texttt{loop\_current\_meanfield\_kernel.py}.

\section{Results}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Scenario A} (large $\gamma_{ir},\gamma_r$, small biquadratics):
deep-cool global minimum is $3Q\text{-}3Q$, $N_i\!\approx\!-0.887$,
$\Phi_i\!\approx\!0.908$ (all three equal); 30/36 grid cells $3Q\text{-}3Q$.
\item \textbf{Scenario B} (large biquadratic penalty $\lambda_{ir}^{(1)}$):
deep-cool global minimum is $2Q\text{-}1Q$, $N=(0,0,-1.41)$,
$\Phi=(1.01,1.01,0)$; 24/36 grid cells $2Q\text{-}1Q$.
\item Both mixed states are TRS-breaking ($\Phi\neq0$: loop currents present).
\item \textbf{Pure-iCDW test}: cooling only the iCDW ($a_r>0$) yields a
pure single-$Q$ iCDW; a pure $2Q$ or $3Q$ iCDW is never a minimum without
accompanying rCDW --- exactly the paper's statement.
\item $2Q\text{-}1Q$ has a single nonzero $N$ component (single-$Q$ rCDW),
which breaks $C_3$ $\Rightarrow$ orthorhombic distortion.
\end{itemize}

\section{Comparison to the claim}
The headline claim --- ``the coupled iCDW-rCDW Landau theory yields two generic
mixed states, $3Q\text{-}3Q$ and $2Q\text{-}1Q$'' --- is reproduced from first
principles at the Landau level: both appear as global minima in disjoint
regions of coefficient space, pure iCDW is excluded, and the $C_3$-breaking of
$2Q\text{-}1Q$ is confirmed. The DFT-derived VHS symmetry content (the seven
iCDW types; ferro/octupolar/toroidal/monopolar subsidiary orders) is
\emph{not} attempted here and is the residual gap.
