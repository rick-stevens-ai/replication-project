# Brief

Independent replication of the *classically-tractable subset* of Leimkuhler & Whaley, "A quantum eigenvalue solver based on tensor networks" (*npj Quantum Information* 11:184, 2025; OSTI 3365708). The full TNQE algorithm requires a QPU simulator to evaluate off-diagonal matrix elements between MPSs in different orbital bases; that is out of scope for a lightweight CPU-only replication. Instead we independently implement, from scratch in pure NumPy/SciPy, (i) matrix-product-state / MPO representations, (ii) two-site DMRG on canonical 1D spin Hamiltonians (transverse-field Ising and Heisenberg antiferromagnet), and (iii) the paper's **LC-MPS** variant — a linear combination of *M* matrix product states in the same basis solved via a generalized eigenvalue problem *H*c = *E S*c. We reproduce the two key methodological claims that underpin TNQE: DMRG systematically converges to the exact ground state with bond dimension χ, and pooling several undertrained MPSs into a subspace and solving the generalized eigenvalue problem materially lowers the energy relative to any single MPS of the same bond dimension. Full molecular chemistry benchmarks (H2O, H6 in STO-3G) reported in the paper are not attempted here because they require PySCF-generated electronic-structure MPOs and MPS-in-rotated-orbital-basis machinery outside the scope of a hand-rolled reproduction.
