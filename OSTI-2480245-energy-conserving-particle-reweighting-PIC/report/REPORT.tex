\section{Paper summary}
Kinetic PIC/DSMC plasma simulations represent many real particles by weighted \emph{macroparticles}. When plasma density varies by orders of magnitude, the macroparticle count per cell must be adjusted (``reweighting''). The paper introduces a particle-based reweighting scheme --- implemented in Sandia's Aleph PIC-DSMC code --- with two novel operators:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Split} (Sec.~3.1, Eqs.~4--5): one parent macroparticle becomes parent~$+$~two children, displaced symmetrically about the parent (preserving center of mass/charge) with a field-parallel velocity correction so that total energy (KE~$+$~$q\Phi$) is conserved. Splits requiring imaginary child velocities are rejected --- the paper's stated key accuracy improvement.
  \item \textbf{Merge} (Sec.~3.2, Eqs.~6--12): two macroparticles collapse to one at their center of mass with summed weight; a modified COM velocity (Eq.~7) accounts for field displacement; a mass-averaged COM velocity (Eq.~10) conserves momentum but intentionally loses a small amount of thermal KE (Eq.~11). Merges are gated by speed/angle cutoffs (Eq.~9: $\Delta v < v_{\mathrm{thermal}}$, $\alpha < 30^\circ$).
\end{itemize}
Two demonstrations: Test~4.1 (exponential sheath potential with bump-on-tail beam --- reweighted VDFs indistinguishable from fixed-weight); Test~4.2 (0D H$_2$ ionization swarm --- real-electron growth rate independent of target computational count $N_c$).

\section{Method}
Python 3.14, numpy 2.5.0, scipy 1.18.0 in \texttt{work/venv} (CherryRd). Operators reimplemented in \texttt{work/reweight.py} from Eqs.~2--12 of the paper. Aleph is Sandia-proprietary and has no public code/data package; strategy was operator-level reimplementation plus abstract 0D scaling test. Conservation errors measured across 20{,}000 random splits and $\sim$18{,}600 random merges of electron macroparticles in the Test-4.1 exponential-sheath field. Growth-independence test: 0D ionization at $\nu=3.06$~s$^{-1}$ with a reweighting control loop keeping the computational count within $\pm10\%$ of $N_c\in\{10,100,1000,10000\}$; growth rate $\beta$ fit via scipy \texttt{curve\_fit}. An LLM-judge verdict was obtained from free Argo \texttt{gpt-5.2}.

Every mathematically-specified invariant reproduces to floating-point roundoff. The energy-conserving split with imaginary-velocity rejection --- the paper's headline accuracy claim --- behaves exactly as described.

\subsection*{Growth-rate independence (Test 4.2, abstracted)}
Fitted $\beta$ was $3.0507$~s$^{-1}$ for all $N_c\in\{10,100,1000,10000\}$; relative spread $\sim10^{-15}$. Computational count stayed within $[N_c, 1.11 N_c]$. This matches the paper's central Test-4.2 structural claim (rate independent of $N_c$; count bounded $N_c$--$1.1 N_c$) and its absolute value ($\sim 3.06$~s$^{-1}$).

\section{Honest critique --- what was exercised vs.\ what was not}

\textbf{Exercised (independent, quantitative):}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Both operators (split, merge) reimplemented from published equations, not from Aleph source.
  \item All seven declared mathematical invariants measured to machine precision on tens of thousands of random events.
  \item Imaginary-velocity-split rejection logic (the paper's headline correctness feature) exercised: 0 leaks across 20000 attempts.
  \item Cutoff gating (Eq.~9, $\Delta v < v_{\mathrm{th}}$, $\alpha < 30^\circ$) exercised: 1388/18612 pairs rejected.
  \item Test-4.2 structural scaling: $\beta(N_c)$ constant to $\sim10^{-15}$ across four decades of $N_c$.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{NOT exercised (out of independent reach or not isolable):}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Full coupled PIC/DSMC re-run of Test 4.1 (requires Aleph). VDF-indistinguishability plots, sheath dynamics, and wall-time-vs-density plots are not independently reproduced --- only asserted-preserved by verified operator invariants.
  \item Full DSMC-with-cross-sections physics of Test 4.2: absolute EEDF shape, Bolsig+ comparison, and absolute reaction-rate values were not rerun. Our 0D reproduces only the \emph{structural} scaling claim, not the physics-faithful swarm outcome.
  \item C7 (precision improves with $N_c$): our abstracted 0D bookkeeping model recovers $\beta$ to machine precision at every $N_c$ because it lacks Aleph's stochastic DSMC event noise. Reported as not-isolable rather than over-claimed.
  \item Headline figures of the paper (Figs.~4--10, VDFs, wall-time curves) were \textbf{not} reproduced pixel-for-pixel or trend-for-trend.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Justification of PARTIAL:} The mathematical core of the method (novel split/merge operators with all stated invariants including the energy-conserving imaginary-velocity rejection) is fully vindicated independently. The end-to-end coupled-code simulation demonstrations are strictly out of reach without access to Aleph. This is neither a REPLICATED (which would require reproducing the coupled-sim figures) nor a SPOT-CHECK (which would imply only cursory checks); it is a genuine PARTIAL where the algorithm is confirmed but the full deployed system is not.
