2. **COVERAGE:** 4/10  
3. **AGREEMENT:** 4/4  

**Justification:** The molecular and plasmid-level claims in the paper were rigorously replicated using real deposited GenBank data and free tools, with strong agreement across all tested claims (C1-C4). The independent AMR screen confirmed the presence of optrA, poxtA, and other MDR genes, matching the reported phenotypes. Plasmid sizes, alignments, and annotations corroborated the paper's descriptions, though the reference plasmid was misidentified as "pE349" instead of the correct "pE394." However, epidemiological claims (prevalence, cgMLST/wgMLST clustering, and 23S mutations) could not be tested due to the lack of raw sequencing reads and isolate metadata. This limits the scope of replication to molecular findings only, resulting in a "PARTIAL" verdict.
