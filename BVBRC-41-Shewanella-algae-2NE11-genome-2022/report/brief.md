# Brief — BVBRC-41

**Paper:** Lizárraga et al. (2022), "Complete genome sequence of *Shewanella algae* strain 2NE11, a decolorizing bacterium isolated from industrial effluent in Peru," *Biotechnology Reports* 33:e00704. DOI 10.1016/j.btre.2022.e00704. PMC8816663 / PMID 35145887. CC BY (open access).

**What/why:** The paper is a genome-announcement + functional-characterization of a dye-decolorizing marine bacterium (GenBank CP055159, assembly GCF_014263185.1). We independently re-download the actual public genome from NCBI, recompute all assembly statistics, re-annotate it with an independent Prokka pipeline (paper used PGAP/RAST), verify the specific decolorization/metal-resistance/carbohydrate/CRISPR gene-content claims in the deposited annotation, and run a from-scratch DIMOB-style genomic-island predictor to test the two-island claim. Free endpoints only (NCBI Datasets, Europe PMC, local BLAST-free feature parsing, Argo LLM judge).
