## 2. What we implemented

1. **Full-grid baseline** (`code/vp_full.py`): Fourier semi-Lagrangian solver with Strang splitting,
   * x-advection: shift `f(x,v) → f(x − v Δt/2, v)` exactly via FFT.
   * v-advection with field: shift `f(x,v) → f(x, v + E(x) Δt)` exactly via FFT.
   * Poisson: `∂_x E = 1 − ρ` solved spectrally.
   This is the classical Cheng–Knorr (1976) scheme, which we use as the reference high-resolution truth.

2. **Dynamical low-rank (DLR) projector-splitting integrator** (`code/vp_lowrank.py`): the KSL ("K-step / S-step / L-step") algorithm of Lubich–Oseledets (BIT 2014) specialized to Vlasov–Poisson as in Einkemmer–Lubich §3:
   * Ansatz `f(x,v,t) ≈ Σ_{kl} X_k(x,t) S_kl(t) V_l(v,t)`, with X, V orthonormal (in discrete L²) and S an r×r coefficient matrix.
   * **K-step**: evolve `K = X·S` under
     ∂_t K = −∂_x K · (V^T diag(v) V)^T + diag(E(x)) · K · (V^T ∂_v V)^T  
     for time Δt, then re-orthogonalize via QR to get new X and S.
   * **S-step** (reverse sign): integrate the small r×r ODE
     ∂_t S = +(X^T ∂_x X) S (V^T diag(v) V)^T − (X^T diag(E) X) S (V^T ∂_v V)^T
     by RK4 with adaptive sub-cycling.
   * **L-step**: evolve `L = V·S^T` under
     ∂_t L = −diag(v) L · (X^T ∂_x X)^T + ∂_v L · (X^T diag(E) X)^T,
     re-orthogonalize via QR to update V and S.
   The composition K→S→L is the first-order Lie variant; we kept it for clarity (a Strang composition `step_KSL_strang` is also provided).

3. **Diagnostics**: total mass, kinetic energy, ½∫E²dx (electric energy), L² norm of f, and final-time L² error vs the full-grid solution.

All advection sub-steps use exact spectral shifts; reaction sub-steps use matrix exponentials via eigendecomposition with arg-clipping for numerical safety.

## 3. Numerical experiments

### 3.1 Linear Landau damping (paper §4.1)

* IC: f₀(x,v) = (1 + 0.01 cos(0.5 x)) · e^{−v²/2} / √(2π)
* Domain: x ∈ [0, 4π] (2 wavelengths), v ∈ [−6, 6], grid 64 × 128.
* T = 40, Δt = 0.05.
* Theoretical damping rate γ = 0.1533 (Bohm–Gross root of dispersion relation for k=0.5).

**Result — figure `figures/landau_E_envelope.png`:**

* Full grid: clean exponential decay of `√(2 E_e(t))` with envelope slope ≈ 0.154 (fit over t∈[2,25] peaks), reaching ~1e-4 at t=40.
* DLR r=4, r=8, r=16: track full-grid envelope within plot-line width down to the numerical floor.
* DLR r=2: decays at the right rate for a few cycles but plateaus near t ≈ 15 — **insufficient rank** for linear Landau (which requires at least the perturbation mode plus the Maxwellian plus their advected derivative ≈ 3 effective modes).

**Quantitative fit:**

| Quantity | Full grid | Analytic theory | Agreement |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Damping rate γ | 0.1540 (envelope fit) | 0.1533 | 0.5% |
| Initial E-energy (½ ∫E² dx) | 1.257 × 10⁻³ | (α/k)² · L/4 = 1.257 × 10⁻³ | exact |
| Mass drift (full grid) | 5.3 × 10⁻¹⁵ | 0 | round-off |
| Mass drift (DLR r=4) | 1.3 × 10⁻⁵ | 0 | KSL does not formally conserve mass; small drift |

### 3.2 Two-stream instability (paper §4.2 analogue)

* IC: f₀(x,v) = (1 + 0.05 cos(0.5 x)) · ½ [G(v−2.4) + G(v+2.4)], G = e^{−v²/2}/√(2π).
* Domain: x ∈ [0, 4π], v ∈ [−8, 8], grid 128 × 256.
* T = 40, Δt = 0.05 (full + DLR r=4), Δt = 0.01 for r=8, 0.005 for r=16, 0.0025 for r=32 (see §4).

**Result — figures `figures/twostream_E.png`, `figures/twostream_phase_space.png`:**

* All DLR runs capture the linear growth phase and the saturation peak E_e ≈ 3.14 × 10⁻² correctly.
* Final-time phase-space snapshots: r=4 produces a smooth two-blob structure that misses fine filaments; r=16 resolves the characteristic "eye / vortex" structure and matches the full grid visually.

### 3.3 Rank vs. error vs. cost

`figures/rank_error.png`:

| Test | r | wall (s) | final ‖Δf‖_{L²} |
|------|---|----------|------------------|
| Landau | full grid | 0.78 | — (reference) |
| Landau | 2 | 1.17 | 2.52 × 10⁻² |
| Landau | 4 | 1.36 | 1.21 × 10⁻³ |
| Landau | 8 | 1.69 | 1.17 × 10⁻³ |
| Landau | 16 | 2.89 | 1.13 × 10⁻³ |
| Two-stream | full grid | 2.34 | — (reference) |
| Two-stream | 4 | 1.97 | 3.74 × 10⁻² |
| Two-stream | 8 | 11.14 | 2.54 × 10⁻³ |
| Two-stream | 16 | 35.24 | 5.09 × 10⁻⁴ |
| Two-stream | 32 | 140.1 | 2.17 × 10⁻⁴ |

* For **Landau**, DLR with **r = 4** already saturates the time-discretization-limited error floor (~1.2 × 10⁻³, set by Δt = 0.05 first-order Lie splitting).
* For **two-stream**, error decreases roughly as r⁻² up to r = 32, consistent with the spectral decay of singular values of f for a regular phase-space distribution before filamentation dominates.
* **Cost crossover**: at this small grid (128×256) the DLR is not faster than the full grid in wall time, since the full Fourier solver is already O(N_x N_v log) with tiny constants. The paper's payoff appears at 4D/6D phase space where the full-grid scaling is `N^4`–`N^6` while DLR scales as `r · N^{1 or 2}`. In our 2D test the **memory** savings are real (r × (N_x + N_v) vs N_x × N_v) but wall time does not yet favor DLR.

## 5. Friction tags

* `over-rank-instability`: When r exceeds the effective rank of f(t) (e.g. r=8 during the strongly perturbed two-stream linear growth before structure has developed), the S matrix becomes near-singular. The projector-splitting then amplifies any noise in (X^T ∂_x X) or (V^T ∂_v V) projections, the Poisson-solved E spikes, and the K-step reaction `expm(E·Δt·D2^T)` overflows. **Mitigation in this work:** rank-adaptive Δt (smaller dt for larger r). **Proper fix:** BUG integrator (Ceruti–Lubich 2022), unconventional rank-augmented schemes, or conservative Einkemmer–Joseph (2021).
* `sign-convention-bug` (caught early): First implementation used ρ−1 instead of 1−ρ in the Poisson solve, giving the wrong sign of E and turning Landau damping into Landau **growth**. Caught after the first smoke test by checking that `√(2 E_e(t))` decayed; fixed by inspection. Sanity-check tip for future PDE replications: **always plot the linear damping/growth rate and compare to the dispersion relation before trusting results.**
* `unit-rescaling`: discrete L² inner products are not the same as Euclidean — required keeping track of √dx, √dv factors absorbed into X, V. Documented carefully in `vp_lowrank.py`.

## 7. Limitations

1. **1D1V only.** The paper's main cost-saving result is in 4D/6D phase space; we did not implement hierarchical DLR for 2D2V/3D3V, where the advantage would be quantitative.
2. **First-order Lie KSL.** Strang variant implemented but not benchmarked. Higher-order compositions (Yoshida, BUG) not attempted.
3. **Periodic in v** (we use FFT-based spectral derivatives in v, treating the v-domain as periodic with period 2 v_max). This works because f decays sufficiently at ±v_max, but introduces aliasing in marginal cases.
4. **No conservative variant.** Mass conservation is enforced only approximately (drift ~10⁻⁵ at r=4). The Einkemmer–Joseph (2021) conservative DLR would close this gap.
5. **No 4D plasma-echo test** (Einkemmer–Lubich §4.3) — skipped due to 1D1V scope.
6. **KSL robustness mitigation is dt-based, not algorithmic.** A proper replication of the BUG integrator would remove the need for rank-adaptive Δt; future work.
