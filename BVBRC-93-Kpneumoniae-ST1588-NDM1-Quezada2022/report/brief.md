# Brief — BVBRC-93

**Paper:** Quezada-Aguiluz M et al. (2022). "Novel Megaplasmid Driving NDM-1-Mediated Carbapenem Resistance in *Klebsiella pneumoniae* ST1588 in South America." *Antibiotics* 11:1207. PMID:36139987. DOI:10.3390/antibiotics11091207.

**What:** Genomic characterisation (hybrid Illumina + Nanopore) of *K. pneumoniae* UCO-361, a clinical isolate from Santiago, Chile — reportedly the first NDM-1 K. pneumoniae detected in Chile. Paper describes a novel 314,976 bp megaplasmid (pNDM-1_UCO361) carrying blaNDM-1 in a Tn3000 transposon, a co-resident 197 kb IncFIB(K) helper plasmid with a full tra locus, a low-temperature (27°C) conjugation phenotype, and an extensively-drug-resistant (XDR) phenotype with 13+ resistance genes.

**Why we're testing it:** All sequence data are deposited (JAMJQY010000000 / NZ_JAMJQY01000000X.1) and the paper's molecular claims are directly checkable with standard AMR/plasmid-typing tools. The conjugation-phenotype claim is wet-lab and out of scope, but its mechanistic prerequisites are testable in silico.
