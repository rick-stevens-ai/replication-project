## Paper
**Direct ab initio calculation of magnons in altermagnets: method, spin-space symmetry aspects, and application to MnTe**
L. M. Sandratskii, K. Carva, V. M. Silkin — arXiv:2501.11327v3 [cond-mat.mtrl-sci] (22 Sep 2025)

## Method
First method for direct ab initio (DFT/LDA) calculation of adiabatic magnons in complex
collinear magnets. Two constraints: (1) magnetization-change constraint (deviation of
moments from the ground state); (2) symmetry constraint (generalized translations) fixing
the magnon wave vector q. Avoids mapping to a Heisenberg Hamiltonian. Symmetry analysis
uses spin-space groups (SSG).

## Key results
- alpha-MnTe (NiAs-type hexagonal, a=4.15 A, c=6.71 A) is an altermagnet.
- Altermagnetic spin splitting of electron states in k-space with zero net moment.
- Magnon chirality splitting in q-space shares the SAME reciprocal-space pattern as the
  electronic spin splitting, despite different physical origin.
- Splitting is nodal on high-symmetry line (0,0,kz); nonzero on general line (0.1,0.2,kz).
- Star of a general k has 24 points; Te-induced moments analyzed in magnon states.
- Each magnon carries its own electron band structure.
