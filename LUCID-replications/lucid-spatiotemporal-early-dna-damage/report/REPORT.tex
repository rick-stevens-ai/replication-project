Tobias F, L\"ob D, Lengert N, Durante M, Drossel B, Taucher-Scholz G, Jakob B (2013).
\emph{Spatiotemporal Dynamics of Early DNA Damage Response Proteins on Complex DNA Lesions.}
PLOS ONE 8(2): e57953.
DOI: \url{https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0057953}.
Open access (CC-BY); all supplements downloadable from PLOS.

\section{Paper Summary}

Tobias et al.\ address the earliest ($<$10\,min) spatial and temporal dynamics of the mammalian DNA
double-strand-break (DSB) response, using heavy-ion tracks at the GSI Darmstadt beamline to
produce controlled columns of clustered DNA lesions in live U2OS cells expressing NBS1-GFP,
MDC1-GFP, or 53BP1-GFP. Five interlocking threads make up the paper:
(1) live-cell beamline confocal imaging of protein accumulation kinetics as a function of ion
species and linear energy transfer (LET, 170--14350\,keV/$\mu$m);
(2) FRAP on the resulting foci to extract effective diffusion coefficients
(Soumpasis fits) and effective on/off rate constants (Sprague reaction-diffusion fits via
Stehfest-inverted Laplace solutions);
(3) empirical mono-exponential fits ($\tau_{63}$) to the recruitment curves;
(4) CK2 inhibition (TBB) to isolate ``inner-focus'' NBS1 that binds directly to DSB ends
independent of the H2AX/MDC1 outer scaffold; and
(5) a nine-reaction ODE kinetic model of the MRN--ATM--$\gamma$H2AX--MDC1 loop, fit globally to
all recruitment data with a single parameter set given in File S1.

The central conceptual claim is that early ($<$5\,min) NBS1 recruitment to complex lesions is
dominated by an ``inner-focus'' population binding directly to DSB ends, while MDC1 and later NBS1
outer-focus recruitment is a slower, diffusion-limited, $\gamma$H2AX-scaffold-mediated process
whose rate rises with LET simply because more DSBs per track means more scaffold nucleation sites.
The ODE model quantitatively reproduces the LET dependence of $\tau_{63}$ and the inner-focus
fraction across all 12 experimental panels using a single global parameter set.

\section{Method}

\subsection{Provenance}
Paper PDF fetched from PLOS ONE; parser pipeline documented in \texttt{PARSER\_PROVENANCE.md}.
Body text extracted via \verb|pdftotext -layout|; the two supplement DOCs (Table~S1, File~S1)
were converted with macOS \texttt{textutil}; figure TIFFs were rendered to PNG for digitization.
Sub-minute-to-minute dynamics claims trace to Figures~2, 5, 8, 11, and 12 plus Table~S1.

\subsection{ODE Model Re-implementation}
The 9-reaction / 13-species kinetic model of the MRN--ATM--$\gamma$H2AX--MDC1 loop
(see File~S1) was re-implemented in Python from scratch in
\texttt{code/lucid\_model.py}, using \texttt{scipy.integrate.solve\_ivp} with LSODA
(\texttt{rtol=1e-8}, \texttt{atol=1e-3}, \texttt{max\_step=1.0}).
\emph{All} 10 rate constants, 4 initial protein concentrations, and 12 per-panel scaling
factors are taken \emph{as published}; no re-fitting was performed. DSBs per track are
computed as $28 \times (\mathrm{LET}/170)$ following the paper's linear scaling law
(L\"obrich 1994).

\subsection{Arithmetic and Tabular Claims}
Cheap arithmetic claims (A3--A6) and Table~S1 monotonicity claims (C1--C3) are checked in
dedicated scripts \texttt{c3\_dsb\_fluence\_arithmetic.py},
\texttt{c4\_diffusion\_arithmetic.py}, and \texttt{c5\_tableS1\_trends.py}. Extended ODE queries
(B5--B8) and the diffusive-influx variant (B9) live in
\texttt{c6\_model\_extended\_claims.py} and \texttt{c7\_mdc1\_diffusive\_influx.py}. Each script
writes a JSON result under \texttt{results/} so partial progress survives interruption.

\subsection{Figure Reproduction}
\texttt{code/figure11\_replication.py} reproduces all four panels of Figure~11.
\texttt{code/quantitative\_check.py} compares model output to points digitized from Figure~S1
panels A and L (Panel F's LET label was not confidently readable and is dropped).
\texttt{code/figure\_overlay.py} produces the visual overlay.

\subsection{What we did NOT do}
No global Nelder-Mead re-fit of the 7 free rate constants (raw data not published). No
digitization of Figure~12B for a quantitative low-LET MDC1 comparison of B9 (qualitative check
only). No re-analysis of the raw beamline TIFF stacks (D1), raw FRAP time-series (D2, A7, A8),
or raw confocal stacks (D3) --- the artifacts are not published.

\section{Critique --- Where This Replication Has Real Limits}

This section is deliberately un-whitewashed. The replication succeeds on the mathematical
model layer but leaves genuinely important pieces of the paper untouched or partly checked,
and the paper itself has structural assumptions that neither the original authors nor this
replication fully test.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The ODE parameters were not re-fit from raw data.} We use the published rate
constants as-is. The 7 optimized rate constants were fit globally by the authors against 16
recruitment curves via Nelder-Mead. Because the raw recruitment CSVs are not published, we
cannot test whether the fit is \emph{uniquely determined} or whether the published parameter
set sits in a wide plateau of near-equivalent local minima. A proper replication would
re-derive the parameters from a bootstrap over the raw curves and quote confidence intervals;
we cannot do that here. This is the largest single weakness of the replication.

\item \textbf{One parameter-mapping ambiguity in File S1 is resolved by us, not by the paper.}
The supplement text ``and''-joins two optimized rate values that could plausibly map to either
of two reactions. We document our choice in \texttt{code/lucid\_model.py}, but the alternative
mapping is not ruled out by anything in the paper. All qualitative claims survive both readings;
individual quantitative rate values might not.

\item \textbf{Quantitative agreement is limited by digitization, not by the model.}
Our $\sim$9\% signal RMS and $\sim$20\% $\tau_{1/2}$ RMS on Fig.~S1 panels A and L are dominated
by the noise of manually digitizing plots. Panel F was dropped entirely because its LET label
was unreadable. So the ``7/10 quantitative agreement'' score is a floor set by digitization,
not a genuine model-vs-data comparison. Without raw CSVs this is unfixable.

\item \textbf{B9 (diffusive-influx variant) is verified qualitatively only.} We show the
mod/orig ratio is monotone in time and converges, but we did not digitize Fig.~12B for an
absolute-fit comparison. The ``qualitative REPRODUCED'' verdict on B9 is weaker than the
verdicts on A3--C3 and should be read that way.

\item \textbf{The mono-exponential $\tau_{63}$ metric is model-choice, not physics.}
The paper's own recruitment curves are visibly non-mono-exponential (early lag, later
plateau structure). Fitting a single exponential to the background-subtracted curve is a
convention. Our replicated $\tau_{63}$ values track the paper's monotonicity but should not be
compared 1:1 with printed numbers because we do not per-data-set background-subtract.

\item \textbf{Sub-minute spatial resolution is asserted, not proven.} The paper treats the ion
track as a well-defined cylinder of DSBs and the nucleus as a homogeneous cylinder of ATM,
MRN, and $\gamma$H2AX substrate. Both assumptions are conservative simplifications: real
tracks have $\delta$-electron spurs (radial dose profile falls as $\sim 1/r^2$ out to
$\mu$m radii), and chromatin is not spatially uniform. The paper's $L=6.3\,\mu$m nuclear
radius is a single-cell-line, single-cell-cycle-phase choice that we inherit but do not test.

\item \textbf{Ion-species coverage is uneven.} Argon, uranium, xenon, and carbon are well
sampled; iron, nickel, and titanium contribute one panel each and dominate the outlier
behaviour (see the Ni-ions outlier flagged in C1). We do not exclude these panels, so trend
tests get the paper's own noise floor.

\item \textbf{CK2 inhibition specificity is not tested here.} TBB inhibits CK2 but is not
absolutely specific; the paper's inferred ``inner-focus'' population depends on the TBB
effect being CK2-mediated. We take that at face value.

\item \textbf{Wet-lab layer entirely absent.} D1--D4 blocked claims cover the raw imaging,
raw FRAP, raw confocal, and 53BP1 lag phase --- none of which are in the public record.
Any statement about the paper as an experimental object (rather than as a mathematical model)
that requires these artifacts is out of scope.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Reproducibility of This Replication}

All code is deterministic, depends only on \texttt{scipy}/\texttt{numpy}/\texttt{matplotlib}.
Each claim writes its result JSON under \texttt{results/}. Rerunning
\verb|python code/figure11_replication.py| plus each \verb|cN_*.py| script regenerates every
number in this report. Total wall-clock on a modern laptop: $\sim$3\,min. No GPU, no network,
no seeded RNG dependence in the ODE integrator.
