# Brief — Brandt (1977) Multi-Level Adaptive Solutions to Boundary-Value Problems

Independent replication of the foundational multigrid paper (Brandt, *Math. Comp.* 31 (138), pp. 333–390). We implemented a from-scratch geometric multigrid V(2,1) cycle in Python/NumPy on the exact model problem used in Brandt's Appendix B (Poisson `Δu = sin(3(x+y))` on `(0,1)²` with Dirichlet BC `g = cos(2(x+y))`, 5-point Laplacian, Gauss–Seidel smoothing, full-weighting restriction, bilinear prolongation), then measured (C1) per-cycle residual reduction across grids `N ∈ {33, 65, 129, 257, 513}`, (C2) work-to-tolerance vs `N`, and (C3) discretization order of accuracy on a manufactured solution. All three core claims — grid-independent convergence factor, `O(N)` total work, and 2nd-order accuracy — are reproduced quantitatively. Overall verdict: **REPLICATED**.
