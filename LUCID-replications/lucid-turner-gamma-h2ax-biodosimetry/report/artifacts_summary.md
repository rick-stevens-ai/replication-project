## Friction tags

- **friction:no-individual-data** — paper only publishes group means ±SEM;
  cannot bound biological CV per mouse; caps our ROC AUC at 0.84--0.85
  vs paper's 0.93 (paper's 0.93 likely uses n≈160 individual mice).
- **friction:model-degeneracy** — (r,p) in Q₂ = 1 − (1+rt)^p degenerate in
  the r·t≫1 regime; individual r,p values slide but Q₂ is unchanged.
  Not a replication failure, a real identifiability issue in the paper.
- **friction:no-blind-challenge** — the paper's "validation" MC inversion
  operates on the same data used for fitting; there is no held-out
  activity or held-out mouse. This replication inherits that limitation.
- **friction:biokinetics-spot-check-only** — Eq.3 whole-body retention
  needs raw retention curves that are not published; we use Table 1
  committed doses directly. Physics pipeline is not end-to-end reproduced.
- **friction:LoD-not-measured** — lowest activity 5.74 MBq gives ~3.4 Gy;
  no data at operationally-relevant <0.5 Gy triage cut-off.
- **no-friction:public-data** — 100% public, no author contact, no paywall.
- **no-friction:runtime** — full pipeline <30 s on laptop CPU.
