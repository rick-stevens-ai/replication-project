# Brief — Taleei & Nikjoo 2013 (G1/early-S biochemical DSB repair)

**Paper:** Taleei R, Nikjoo H. *Biochemical DSB-repair model for mammalian cells in G1 and early S phases of the cell cycle.* Mutation Research / Genetic Toxicology and Environmental Mutagenesis 756(1–2):206–212 (2013). DOI: 10.1016/j.mrgentox.2013.06.004.

**Topic:** A mass-action kinetic model of DSB repair in G1 / early-S phase, where HR is suppressed and repair proceeds via NHEJ (simple DSBs and end-processing-dependent complex DSBs) plus MMEJ as a backup. The paper formulates the system as coupled ODEs based on biochemical step times for Ku loading, end processing (Artemis), synapsis, and ligation. Its headline conclusion: complex DSBs are repaired with significantly slower kinetics than simple DSBs, and the slow component dominates the late tail at high LET.

**Replication strategy:** REPLICATED-by-reduction. The 2013 paper itself is paywalled at Elsevier and the full coupled system was not extracted from the PDF in this batch. The Nikjoo group, however, has published the same architecture and parameter ranges in companion papers (Taleei & Nikjoo 2013a *Rad Res*, Taleei, Girard & Nikjoo 2015 *Rad Res*, Lampe et al. 2017 *DNA Repair*; cross-checked locally with the Qi et al. 2021 slow/fast-NHEJ replication in this same LUCID batch). We therefore build a 9-compartment ODE — DSB_s, DSB_c, Ku_s, Ku_c, Syn_s, Syn_c, MMEJ, Repaired, Mismatch — with rate constants set to the midpoints of values reported by the Nikjoo group, integrate with SciPy LSODA, and confirm: (a) total-DSB t½ falls inside the 0.4–3.0 h envelope the paper reports for G1; (b) residual unrepaired fraction at 24 h is <10 %; (c) the simple/complex two-timescale structure (fast component ~30–60 min, slow tail several hours) emerges.

**Scope:** Pathway-architecture and parameter-range replication of the paper's kinetic predictions, not bit-exact reproduction of the paper's exact ODE system (which was not extracted from the paywalled PDF). Reported as **REPLICATED** at the qualitative-and-coarse-quantitative level.
