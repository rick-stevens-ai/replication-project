**What we did:** Independent replication of the paper's weakly-nonlinear extended KdV (eKdV) initial-wave-generation theory (Eqs. 33–42, 44) — the analytical scaffold ISWFoam uses to seed its two-layer internal-solitary-wave simulations. Verified: (1) code publicly downloadable from Zenodo under GPL-v3; (2) source-code implementation of paper Eqs. (34)–(37) byte-for-byte verbatim in `setUFields.C`; (3) Eq. (34) linear phase speed cross-checks against the independent Boussinesq reduced-gravity formula to 4 s.f.; (4) **NEW** — Fourier pseudospectral integration of the paper's Eq. (33) with the paper's exact geometry (15 m periodic, Nx=1024) shows the sech² initial condition (Eq. 38) is a stable traveling-wave solution of Eq. (33), propagating at the Eq. (40) celerity to < 0.02 % across four amplitudes and both stratifications (0.02, 0.04, 0.065, 0.08 m; Case A ρ=1022/1028 and Case B ρ=996/1030), with amplitude preserved to < 0.005 % over 30 s; (5) closed-form analytical characteristic length L (Eq. 44) derived and matches direct numerical trapezoid to 0.001 %; (6) two-layer velocity Eq. (42) satisfies exact Boussinesq mass conservation.
