# BVBRC-97 Brief

**Paper**: Pell et al. 2021, "Antimicrobial susceptibilities and comparative whole genome analysis of two isolates of the probiotic bacterium Lactiplantibacillus plantarum, strain ATCC 202195", Scientific Reports, PMID 34354117.

**Why**: Independent-replication attempt for X-100 BVBRC set (rank 49 of BVBRC_TOPUP85). BVBRC workflow class = Specialty Genes (Virulence Factors — VFDB/Victors).

**What**: Pull the two publicly-deposited L. plantarum ATCC 202195 genomes referenced in the paper, run comparative WGS and specialty-genes / virulence-factor scans (VFDB via BLAST or BV-BRC-equivalent local pipeline), and check whether the paper's core claims (AMR profile, comparative genome features, VF content) are consistent with re-analysis of the real deposited data.
