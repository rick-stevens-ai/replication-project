# Brief — BVBRC-26

Independent replication of **Vassallo et al. 2022, "A functional selection reveals previously undetected anti-phage defence systems in the *E. coli* pangenome"** (*Nat. Microbiol.* 7:1568–1579; PMID 36123438; PMC9519451). The paper used a genomic-context-agnostic functional selection (fosmid libraries from 71 diverse *E. coli* strains challenged with phages T4, λvir, T7 via the "tab" method) to discover **21 novel anti-phage defence systems** (32 protein components), most previously undetected by defence-island computational screens, and showed they are carried on **prophages/mobile genetic elements in defence hotspots**. Using the **BV-BRC** data API (Genome Group mapping + Proteome/Comparative-Systems logic) plus NCBI Datasets, we independently (i) mapped **all 71 source strains** to BV-BRC genomes, (ii) recovered **all 21 systems in exactly their declared source strain** by BLASTP against the 71-strain proteome group, (iii) reproduced the **MGE/prophage + defence-hotspot** genomic context for 16/21 (hotspot 14/21), and (iv) confirmed the **novelty** claim (18/32 components absent from Gao et al. 2020 predictions). The wet-lab functional-defence phenotype (no deposited raw reads) is out of computational reach. LLM-judge verdict: **PARTIAL** (Coverage 8/10, Agreement 9/10).
