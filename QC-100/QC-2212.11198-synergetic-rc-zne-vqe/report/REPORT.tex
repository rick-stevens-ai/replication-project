\section{Paper and headline claim}
Kurita et al.\ (\emph{Quantum} 7, 1184; arXiv:2212.11198) argue that
\textbf{stacking} Randomized Compiling (RC, per-shot Pauli twirl of every
2-qubit gate; Wallman--Emerson 2016) with Zero-Noise Extrapolation (ZNE, Mitiq-style
gate-folding + linear extrapolation) yields a \emph{super-additive} reduction in
VQE energy error under coherent 2-qubit-gate noise --- roughly $1$--$2$ orders of
magnitude better than either RC-only or ZNE-only, on small molecules
(H$_2$, LiH, STO-3G).

\section{Verdict: \textbf{REPLICATED}}
The headline claim (C1) is quantitatively reproduced in an independent Python
+ Qiskit-Aer + Mitiq implementation on H$_2$/STO-3G at $R=0.735$\,\AA{}
(parity-tapered to 2 qubits, O'Malley coefficients). Two independent Argo LLM
judges (\texttt{gpt-4.1}, \texttt{gemini-2.5-pro}) score REPLICATED / high
confidence.

\section{Method (independent reimplementation)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Hamiltonian.} 5-term parity-tapered H$_2$ from O'Malley 2016;
        exact diagonalization $E_{\text{elec,FCI}} = -1.857275$\,Ha.
  \item \textbf{Ansatz.} Deep hardware-efficient (13 params, 6 CX); multistart
        Nelder--Mead converges to FCI to $<10^{-8}$\,Ha noiselessly.
  \item \textbf{Noise model.} Per-CX coherent over-rotation
        $RX(\varepsilon)\otimes RX(\varepsilon)$ + $RZZ(\varepsilon/2)$ +
        small $p_{\text{dep}}=0.002$ depolarizing residual (matching paper's
        Sec.~2.2 regime: dominant coherent 2q noise + tiny stochastic residual).
  \item \textbf{RC.} Standard CX Pauli twirl, $N_{\text{rand}}=30$
        randomizations per evaluation (paper uses 20).
  \item \textbf{ZNE.} \texttt{mitiq.zne.execute\_with\_zne} with
        \texttt{LinearFactory([1,2,3])} + global folding of the \emph{clean}
        ansatz (so folded copies pick up independent noise, mirroring hardware).
  \item \textbf{Four executors} evaluated at the noiseless optimum
        $\boldsymbol{\theta}^\star$: raw / RC-only / ZNE-only / RC+ZNE, across
        $\varepsilon\in\{0.02,0.05,0.08,0.10\}$\,rad.
\end{itemize}

\section{Critique: what was and was NOT independently verified}
\subsection{What is genuinely reproduced}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Headline C1 (RC+ZNE synergy)} on H$_2$/STO-3G:
        9$\times$--35$\times$ reduction across coherent noise strength.
        This is a true independent reimplementation --- own Hamiltonian
        construction, own twirl code, own Mitiq wiring.
  \item \textbf{C2 (generic across noise strength)}: 4 values of $\varepsilon$
        spanning 5$\times$ range, RC+ZNE stays flat.
  \item \textbf{C3 (ZNE-alone is unreliable on coherent noise)}: reproduced
        exactly; ZNE-alone actively \emph{hurts} at $\varepsilon\geq 0.05$.
  \item \textbf{C4 (RC-alone is limited on coherent residual)}: reproduced;
        RC-alone gives only $\sim 15$--$25$\% improvement.
  \item \textbf{Baseline comparison against RC-only, ZNE-only, and no
        mitigation} was made in a single sweep (all four executors on the same
        $\boldsymbol\theta^\star$ and noise realizations, same $\varepsilon$).
  \item \textbf{Super-additive verification (quantitative)}: at
        $\varepsilon=0.10$, raw error 42.7\,mHa, best individual mitigation
        (RC-only) 32.4\,mHa (24\% reduction); combined RC+ZNE 1.23\,mHa
        (97\% reduction). $\text{Additive expectation} = 42.7 - (10.3 +
        \text{ZNE-only gain, which is negative here})$ is meaningless because
        ZNE-alone worsens the estimate --- but the combined result being
        \emph{orders of magnitude better than either individually} is
        precisely the super-additive claim, and it holds.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Caveats and what was NOT tested}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Molecule scope.} Only H$_2$/STO-3G was reproduced. LiH was
        \textbf{not run} (C5 partial). This is a real limitation --- LiH has
        different coherent-error accumulation pattern (more CXs per UCC-SD
        block).
  \item \textbf{Ansatz family.} We use a deep hardware-efficient ansatz, not
        the paper's UCC-SD. This is a substantive substitution; agreement to
        within a factor of $\sim 2$ on the reduction ratio is expected but not
        guaranteed to transfer to more chemically-realistic ans\"atze.
  \item \textbf{Optimizer robustness (C6).} Only Nelder--Mead tested. The
        paper's Fig.~4 uses Powell, and RC+ZNE benefit could interact with the
        optimizer's noise-sensitivity.
  \item \textbf{Finite-shot regime (C7).} We use exact density-matrix
        expectation, which the paper explicitly permits (paragraph above
        Fig.~4) but which sidesteps the shot-noise interaction that would
        matter on real hardware. RC+ZNE on 8k or 100k shots is untested.
  \item \textbf{Non-Pauli noise.} The paper's argument for RC hinges on
        twirling turning arbitrary coherent noise into stochastic \emph{Pauli}
        noise. Non-Pauli stochastic channels (amplitude damping $T_1$-like,
        leakage) would twirl into non-Pauli stochastic form and ZNE's linearity
        assumption could break again. \textbf{Not tested.}
  \item \textbf{Judge coverage.} Third planned judge (\texttt{claude-opus-4.7})
        returned upstream 502; not retried. Two-judge consensus stands.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Honest bottom line}
The \emph{core mechanism} the paper claims --- RC converts coherent noise into a
form ZNE can linearly extrapolate, and the combination is super-additive --- is
independently reproduced on H$_2$/STO-3G with a substituted ansatz. The
\emph{scope} of the paper (multiple molecules, UCC-SD, optimizer variety,
shot-based sampling) was \emph{not} fully covered; the headline is exercised,
the breadth is not.
