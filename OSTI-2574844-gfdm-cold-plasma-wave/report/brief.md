# Brief

Independent replication of Spencer et al. (2025), *"An implementation of a high-order generalized finite difference method for solving the time-harmonic cold plasma wave equation in toroidal geometry"* (Phys. Plasmas 32, 063902; OSTI 2574844). The paper's central verifiable claim is that the Liszka–Orkisz generalized finite-difference (GFD) scheme — Taylor-order-*m* star matrices, column scaling, and a TSVD-regularized pseudoinverse — discretizes the cold-plasma wave equation with error converging at order O(h^{m−1}), verified against an analytic plane wave (Sec. V.A, Fig. 2). We reimplemented the GFD weight machinery from scratch (Eqs. 3–8, Table III) and independently confirmed the convergence order on (C1) the raw derivative operators and (C2) a full plane-wave boundary-value solve. Measured orders match the paper's O(h^{m−1}) prediction (and modestly exceed it) with the same coarse-grid noise the paper reports. Verdict: **REPLICATED**.
