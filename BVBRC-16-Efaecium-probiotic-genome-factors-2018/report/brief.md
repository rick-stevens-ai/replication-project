# BVBRC-16 — Brief

**Paper:** Ghattargi VC, Gaikwad MA, Meti BS, Nimonkar YS, Dixit K, Prakash O, Shouche YS, Pawar SP, Dhotre DP.
*Comparative genome analysis reveals key genetic factors associated with probiotic property in Enterococcus faecium strains.*
BMC Genomics (2018). **DOI:** [10.1186/s12864-018-5043-9](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-018-5043-9) — PMID 30180794 — PMCID PMC6122445.

## Core claims
1. *E. faecium* **17OM39** clusters closely with marketed probiotic strain **T110** on core-genome phylogeny.
2. 17OM39 lacks known vancomycin and tetracycline resistance genes and lacks functional virulence genes.
3. 17OM39 has higher genomic stability than pathogenic strains, attributed to fewer mobile genetic elements / transposons.
4. Probiotic-associated genes (GI-tract colonization, bile/acid stress survival) are shared between T110 and 17OM39 but reduced or absent in pathogenic strains.

## Replication scope (short pass)
- Verify both the candidate strain (**17OM39**) and the marketed probiotic comparator (**T110**) are in BV-BRC with usable genome metadata.
- Confirm ballpark genome size matches the paper.
- Note the limitations: a full AMR + virulence rescreen would require running CARD/ResFinder/VFDB pipelines, which is out of scope.
