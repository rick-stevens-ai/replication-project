# Brief — BVBRC-45

Independent replication of Tetz, Tetz & Vecherkovskaya (2016, *Gut Pathogens* 8:6), which characterized *Paenibacillus* sp. strain VT-400 — an oral-cavity isolate from pediatric leukemia patients — and claimed it was a **novel species** carrying abundant virulence and antibiotic-resistance genes. We pulled the deposited genome (GCF_001029205.1, from WGS LELF01000000), recomputed assembly statistics, tested the novel-species claim with whole-genome ANI (fastANI) against close relatives, and re-called acquired AMR genes with curated AMRFinderPlus 3.12.8. Genome stats reproduce almost exactly and two curated AMR genes (catA, msr) corroborate the paper's chloramphenicol/macrolide resistance phenotype — but the **central novel-species claim is contradicted**: VT-400 shows 97.1% ANI to *P. amylolyticus* and 96.2% to *P. xylanexedens*, both above the ~95% species boundary. Several paper-cited "hemolysin"/"superantigen"/"vancomycin-resistance" genes are now annotated by NCBI as generic proteins, revealing over-interpretation of 2016 RAST annotations. All data and tools free/public.
