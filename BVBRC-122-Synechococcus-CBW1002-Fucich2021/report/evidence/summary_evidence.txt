=== OVERALL VERDICT ===
This is fundamentally a genome announcement paper (Genome Reports section of GBE). The core claims are:
(a) two complete circular chromosomes of stated sizes,
(b) high GC content,
(c) large genome for picocyanobacterium,
(d) no cold shock proteins,
(e) placement in Bornholm Sea / Cyanobium-like clade,
(f) homolog sharing patterns.

Every quantitative claim we tested replicates within pipeline-annotation tolerance,
and one very specific biological finding (no cold-shock proteins) matches EXACTLY.
The RBH count for CBW1002<->CBW1006 (paper 3,023 vs ours 2,949) is within 2.5%.
Rank order of homolog sharing across the panel EXACTLY matches Fig 2.
Phylogeny places CBW1002/1006 closest to Cyanobium_gracile — consistent with paper's
own suggestion that these strains might be better classified as Cyanobium.
