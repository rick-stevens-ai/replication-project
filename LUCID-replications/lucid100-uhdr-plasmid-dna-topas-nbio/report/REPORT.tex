\section{What was actually done}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Re-derived every quantitative headline in the paper (SSB/DSB reductions
        at four DMSO scavenging capacities; UHDR/CONV ratios; Model~2
        oxygen+WR-1065 reductions; bp-threshold sensitivity monotonicity;
        intertrack-regime crossover $\sigma\!\approx\!10^{8}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$)
        \emph{analytically} from Eq.~(4) $k_\mathrm{obs}=1.32\!\times\!10^{7}\sigma^{0.29}$
        plus branching efficiencies $\eta_\mathrm{OH}=0.24$, $\eta_\mathrm{H}=0.008$.
        Compute cost: \textless30~s of single-core Python.
  \item Extracted all 43 chemistry reactions (R1--R43*) verbatim from Table~1 to
        \texttt{scripts/chemistry\_table1.csv}.
  \item Ran a standalone Poisson-position DSB pair-acceptance MC
        (200\,000 iters, fixed seed 1234) to confirm the sign and monotonicity
        of the 5/10/15\,bp threshold sweep. \emph{Not} calibrated to absolute
        G-values.
  \item Confirmed 17 of 18 tested quantitative claims to \textless1\% agreement;
        flagged 2 apparent paper-side text errata (a probable missing exponent
        on one DSB(UHDR) value; an internally inconsistent oxygen-Henry's-law
        algebra in Eq.~(3) --- the \emph{value used} matches Milligan~1995 /
        Sander~2023, but the printed derivation is off by $\approx\!10^{5}$).
\end{itemize}

\section{What was NOT done (critical honesty)}
This is a Monte-Carlo track-structure paper. The full independent reproduction
requires all of the following, none of which occurred:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{The TOPAS-nBio Monte Carlo was never re-run.} No condensed-history
        225\,kVp beam, no volumetric $e^{-}$ track-structure stage, no IRT
        chemistry propagation, no per-strand DSB scoring. Every ``verified''
        absolute G-value in the report is a \emph{read-through} of the paper's
        Table~2 --- the tabulated numbers were used as inputs to the analytic
        checks, not independently computed.
  \item \textbf{FLASH-vs-conventional not independently reproduced at the MC
        level.} The UHDR/CONV \emph{ratio} was re-derived from the analytical
        scavenging model (Eq.~4); the underlying absolute G-values on both sides
        of that ratio come from the paper.
  \item \textbf{Plasmid-to-chromatin extrapolation not addressed.} The paper is
        purely about pUC19 (\textasciitilde 2.7\,kbp naked supercoiled plasmid at
        50\,\textmu g/mL); the replication does not attempt to test whether the
        FLASH sparing signature seen in plasmids translates to eukaryotic
        chromatin structure with histones and higher DNA densities. This is
        left as an open question (\S\ref{sec:openq}).
  \item \textbf{Cross-code corroboration absent.} No cross-check against
        Geant4-DNA standalone or PARTRAC; TOPAS-nBio's ELSEPA-elastic + Meesungnoen
        thermalization patches are unreleased, so no alternative implementation
        could have been driven from the same source.
  \item \textbf{Author code not obtained.} GitHub profiles \texttt{masilela} and
        \texttt{d-kondo} were probed --- both empty. The chemistry decks and
        Python DSB post-processor promised in the paper (``will be released as
        an example in a future version of TOPAS-nBio'') have not landed. No
        author contact was attempted from within this replication effort.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scope audit}
Numerically-reproducible fraction of the paper's headline set: 18/26 tested,
17/18 within tolerance (94\%). Non-tested items are uniformly absolute G-values
(6), a downstream SD calculation (1), or an experimental-comparator
re-derivation from 11 primary studies (1). Coverage of the full scientific
scope (2 damage models $\times$ 4 scavengers $\times$ 2 dose-rate regimes
$\times$ 2 endpoints $\times$ 3 sensitivity sweeps $\times$ 6 figures $\times$
11 comparator studies) is realistically \textbf{$\sim$40\%} at the number
level and \textbf{0\%} at the independent-MC level.

\section{Verdict rationale}
SPOT-CHECK is the correct label. REPLICATED would require an independent MC.
PARTIAL would understate what was done (every reproducible number checks out
to \textless1\%). SPOT-CHECK (analytic) captures: paper-side arithmetic and
mechanism are self-consistent (modulo two flagged typos); the simulator was
not exercised.
