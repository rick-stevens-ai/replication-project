                      Model equations

  Below we present the model equations distinguishing between the new ones and
adopted from our previous model described in [1]. For the stochastic variables we
present reaction propensities, while for deterministic proper ordinary differential
equations (ODEs). In general, for stochastic reaction the first propensity stays for
the increase of the value of proper variable, while the second for the decrease of
that value. For details of the numerical implementation of the hybrid stochastic-
deterministic algorithm please see the supporting information: Numerical imple-
mentation.
