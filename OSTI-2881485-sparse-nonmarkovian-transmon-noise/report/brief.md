# Brief

We independently replicated the headline Fig 9 VQE-H₂ claim of Oda et al., "Sparse non-Markovian Noise Modeling of Transmon-Based Multi-Qubit Operations" (PRX Quantum 7, 020327, 2026; OSTI 2881485; DOI 10.1103/lx8x-z29x) by (a) downloading the authors' Zenodo code + data release (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19612185), (b) recomputing per-bond-length relative errors from the released ibm_algiers experimental energies vs the released IBM-noise-model and custom non-Markovian LME+SchWARMA simulator energies, and (c) rerunning the IBM baseline simulation from scratch with our own Qiskit 2.5.0 + FakeHanoiV2 + Aer noise model (fixed seed, 100k shots/basis). The paper's headline non-Markovian accuracy claim — 0.5 % relative error at R_opt = 0.75 Å — reproduces essentially exactly (we get 0.507 %). The IBM baseline is systematically lower than the paper's stated ~3.6 % in both the released artifact (2.89 %) and our fresh rerun (2.66 %), driven by expected drift of the FakeHanoi backend properties since paper submission, so the resulting "sevenfold" improvement figure comes out at 5.3–5.7× rather than 7×. Overall verdict: **PARTIAL / REPLICATED-core** — the science (NM ≫ Markovian for VQE on IBMQ transmons) fully reproduces; a specific numerical baseline drifts.
