\section{Paper}
Bosma EF, Koehorst JJ, van Hijum SAFT, Renckens B, Vriesendorp B, van de Weijer AHP, Schaap PJ, de Vos WM, van der Oost J, van Kranenburg R. \textit{Standards in Genomic Sciences} 11:52 (2016). DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.1186/s40793-016-0172-8}{10.1186/s40793-016-0172-8}, PMC \texttt{PMC4995803}, CC BY 4.0.

Genome-announcement paper for finished 2-replicon genome of \textit{B.\ smithii} DSM 4216\textsuperscript{T} --- facultatively anaerobic, facultatively thermophilic (optimum 55\,\textdegree{}C, range 25--65\,\textdegree{}C), Gram-positive, spore-forming, biotech-relevant L-lactate producer from lignocellulosic sugars. Chromosome \texttt{CP012024.1} (3{,}368{,}778 bp) + plasmid \texttt{CP012025.1} (12{,}514 bp); assembly \texttt{GCA/GCF\_001050115.1}. Sequenced Illumina + PacBio; assembled CLCbio/SSPACE/GapFiller; annotated RAST + manual curation, Aragorn, RNAmmer, CRISPR-finder, InterPro. Headline biological finding: \textit{B.\ smithii} lacks the canonical bacterial acetate-production pathway (phosphotransacetylase \textit{pta} + acetate kinase \textit{ackA}) and several pyruvate-dissimilation enzymes; the pyruvate-dehydrogenase (PDH) complex is the sole pyruvate$\to$acetyl-CoA route.

\section{Method}
All data public and free; no paid endpoints.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Paper full text} --- Europe PMC REST \texttt{GET /PMC4995803/fullTextXML}.
  \item \textbf{Genome} --- NCBI Datasets v2 CLI: \texttt{datasets download genome accession GCA\_001050115.1 --include genome,protein,gff3,rna,cds} (also GCF).
  \item \textbf{Genome statistics} --- pure-Python \texttt{work/genome\_stats.py} over FASTA + GFF.
  \item \textbf{Name-scan} --- \texttt{work/func\_scan.py} across GFF product/gene/note fields.
  \item \textbf{tblastn present/absent} --- 8 curated UniProt references; \texttt{makeblastdb -dbtype nucl}; \texttt{tblastn -evalue 10 -outfmt 6}; presence rule pident$\geq$40 $\wedge$ qcov$\geq$70 $\wedge$ e$\leq$1e-20.
  \item \textbf{COG} --- \texttt{COGclassifier} v2 (NCBI COG/CDD DB, rpsblast) on the GCA proteome; Pearson/Spearman vs Table 5.
  \item \textbf{LLM-judge} --- Argo proxy \texttt{argo:gpt-5.2}, temperature 0, structured JSON.
\end{enumerate}

\section{GENUINE CRITIQUE}
\textbf{This section honestly reports what did not replicate, what was gated, and where the strong verdict is bounded.}

\subsection{What was NOT reproduced}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{RAST manual-curation pipeline (C11)}: The paper's manual EC-number / functional-role curation on top of the RAST auto-annotation was not re-run. Our present/absent calls use tblastn + name-scan, which is stringent but does not replicate the paper's per-gene expert-curated calls.
  \item \textbf{antiSMASH secondary-metabolite analysis}: Out of scope; the paper's biosynthetic-cluster catalogue is not independently checked here.
  \item \textbf{CRISPR-finder analysis}: Not re-run; the paper's CRISPR array counts and \texttt{cas}-gene inventory are taken at face value.
  \item \textbf{InterPro domainome / EC-rescue}: The paper uses domain-level InterPro hits to rescue borderline enzymes (e.g.\ the methylglyoxal$\to$L-lactate side route). We did not re-run InterProScan, so we cannot audit those rescues.
  \item \textbf{Table 6 comparative-genomics}: The paper's comparison to 14 other Bacillus/Geobacillus genomes was not re-computed; the metrics are public and could be added.
  \item \textbf{Fig.\ 4 metabolism map}: The paper's manually drawn central-metabolism figure was not redrawn.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Honest bounds on the ``strong'' verdict}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{COG comparison is cross-DB-version}, not apples-to-apples. The 2026 NCBI COG DB has drifted substantially from the 2015 IMG/RAST COG set; we report agreement on the stable specific-function categories (r$\approx$0.91) but omit the R/S/D bins from that calculation. This is an honest workaround, not a full replication of Table 5.
  \item \textbf{tblastn e-value threshold is a decision}. We used pident$\geq$40, qcov$\geq$70, e$\leq$1e-20 as the presence rule. Pdc's best hit is 40.0\% pident but only 34\% qcov (partial-domain hit only), so we call ABSENT --- the paper agrees, but a laxer coverage cutoff could have called it PRESENT. Judgement call, reported transparently.
  \item \textbf{CDS count 3{,}619 vs paper 3{,}627} is within 0.2\%, but not exact. Explanation: the GCA (2015) proteome we count from is the RAST-era submission and includes a slightly different pseudogene handling than the paper's internal count. Not a mismatch we can fully close without the paper's exact gene-call list.
  \item \textbf{DNA coding fraction 81.4\% vs paper 82.8\%} differs by 1.4 percentage points. Same annotation-pipeline explanation; independent reproduction attributes it to inclusion of pseudogene CDS in the coding-bp numerator.
  \item \textbf{Single-genome scope}. All calls are on the type strain; strain-to-strain heterogeneity within \textit{B.\ smithii} (e.g.\ 7\_3\_47FAA) is not explored.
  \item \textbf{No wet-lab validation}. Everything here is in-silico; the paper's phenotypic claims (55\,\textdegree{}C growth, L-lactate as major product, spore formation) are not exercised.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What this replication is NOT}
It is \textbf{not} a full replication. The verdict is \textbf{PARTIAL}, and it earns the qualifier ``strong'' only because (a) every quantitative genome-wide claim reproduced, (b) the single headline biological claim reproduced with orthogonal methods (tblastn + name-scan) and on two independent annotation eras (2015 GCA + 2026 GCF), and (c) a fresh-subagent independent audit produced 15/15 matches with own code. It does not certify the RAST/antiSMASH/CRISPR/InterPro workflow layer.
