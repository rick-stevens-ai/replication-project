# Brief — Park et al. 2024 (Predictive DNA damage signalling for low-dose IR)

**Paper:** Park JI, Jung SY, Song KH, Lee DH, Ahn J, Hwang SG, Jung IS, Lim DS, Song JY. *Predictive DNA damage signaling for low-dose ionizing radiation.* International Journal of Molecular Medicine, 2024. DOI: 10.3892/ijmm.2024.5380.

**Topic:** Wet-lab biomarker screen for low-dose IR (γ-rays) response in human lymphoid cell lines (HuT 78, IM-9) and human PBMCs, plus an in vivo mouse radioprotection screen (cinobufagin, KU60019, BML-277, pifithrin-α, nutlin-3a). Readouts are **western blots** of ATM, CHK2, p53, γH2AX activation; mouse bone-marrow cell counts; mouse survival; FACS apoptosis.

**LUCID relevance — what would be replicable:** Quantitative biomarker dose-response if the paper deposited band-intensity tables. The paper does **not** appear to release a model, parameter set, or machine-readable dose-response dataset. There is no microdosimetric / track-structure / DSB-kinetics model. The only "computational" outputs are descriptive (selection of candidate proteins via a public database — not described as a re-runnable pipeline) plus statistical bar charts on western signal.

**Decision:** **NO-GO for computational model replication.** The paper is a wet-lab biomarker discovery + in vivo drug screen; there is no released model, no rate constants, no published parameter table, and no public code repository. A digitisation-of-figures spot-check is theoretically possible but produces no genuine validation — it would just re-eyeball the authors' own bar charts.
