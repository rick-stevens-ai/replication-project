\begin{abstract}
We independently re-implement the closed-form MULTISIG1 kinetic model of Scott
(2011, \emph{Dose-Response} 9:579--601, PMC3315173) for $\gamma$-H2AX foci
dissolution after low-LET photon exposure. All model equations (Eqs.~3, 5, 6, 8,
10--14) are coded in pure Python from the paper's text; all five model figures
(Figs.~1--5) reproduce; six of seven numerical spot-checks match the paper to
$\leq 0.1\%$. The seventh mismatch uncovers a body-text \emph{labeling typo}
(the paper writes ``Att$_2(D) = 46.7\%$'' but the value is actually Att$_1$;
Att$_3$ and Att$_4$ in the same sentence agree with our re-derivation).
Verdict: \textbf{REPLICATED (model / equations)}. Wet-lab agreement (to
Rothkamm \& L\"obrich 2003) is \emph{not} assessed in this pass; that is
listed as an open question.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper and Scope Clarification}
The LUCID-100 master TSV originally tagged Scott 2011 as \emph{omics /
signature replication}. That tag is wrong: the paper contains no gene
expression, RNA-seq, methylation, biomarker panel, or -omics dataset. The word
``epicellcom'' is the author's coinage for ``epiregulated
cell-community-wide'' --- an intercellular-signaling framing of the biology;
the mathematics is deterministic pharmacokinetics of DSB repair, not omics.
The correct QA retag is \emph{model / equations replication} (or
\emph{kinetic model replication}), themes DNA repair / DDR + computational
model / simulation + radiation biology.

The model itself is:
\begin{itemize}
  \item gamma-distributed per-DSB repair times $\phi_n(t)$, formed by
        $n$-fold convolution of an exponential $\phi_1(t)$;
  \item Poisson distribution of breaks over DNA molecules with mean BPM$(D)$;
  \item Poisson-weighted cumulative Cum$(t,D)$ and residual RB$(t,D)$;
  \item parameterisation for confluent MRC-5 lung fibroblasts under 90\,kV
        x-rays, calibrated (per Scott 2010) against Rothkamm \& L\"obrich 2003.
\end{itemize}

\section{Critique --- Beyond Self-Consistency}

\subsection{Cell--cell communication effects: framing vs test}
The paper's title advertises an \emph{Epicellcom} (cell-community-wide,
epiregulated) response to radiation stress. However, the actual mathematics
(Eqs.~3--14) contains \textbf{no explicit cell--cell communication term},
\textbf{no gap-junction coupling}, and \textbf{no bystander-signal
propagation}. The intercellular framing enters only as a narrative motivation
and via the calibration choice of \emph{confluent} MRC-5 monolayers. Any test
of whether bystander/juxtacrine signaling actually modifies the fitted
$\beta$, $B_T$, or $\alpha$ is \emph{absent} from the paper --- it is
\emph{assumed} by choice of confluent culture. This is our single most
important critique: the model title over-promises relative to what the
equations do.

\subsection{Repair kinetics: epithelial-specific or borrowed?}
The parameter set ($\beta = 2.5$\,h, $B_T = 0.10$ foci/cell, $\alpha =
0.035$\,mGy$^{-1}$, $T = 1.4$\,mGy, $m = 46$ molecules/cell) is fit against
Rothkamm \& L\"obrich 2003 MRC-5 lung fibroblasts, \emph{not} an epithelial
line. MRC-5 is fibroblast; the ``Epi-'' in EpiCellCom is a mislead --- it
refers to \emph{epigenetic / epi-regulated}, not \emph{epithelial}. Any
translation to a real epithelial system (BEAS-2B, HBEC-3KT, HMLE, MCF-10A,
or a lung / airway organoid) would need re-fit and is not attempted.

\subsection{Monolayer vs 3D geometry}
Confluent 2D monolayer $\gamma$-H2AX kinetics are well known to
\emph{under-estimate} DSB persistence relative to 3D coculture and organoid
systems, primarily because chromatin state, cell-cycle distribution, and
ROS scavenging differ. The Scott 2011 model has no geometric or 3D-diffusion
term. Fitting the same equations to 3D data would collapse those effects into
$\beta$ and $\mu$ (pathway rates) and thus \emph{mask} the underlying biology.

\subsection{Low-dose bystander threshold}
The parameter $T = 1.4$\,mGy is a hard threshold below which BPM($D$) is
undefined. This is convenient but unphysical: real low-dose bystander data
show a graded (often supralinear) response below 10\,mGy, not a bright-line
cutoff. The model cannot reproduce Rothkamm \& L\"obrich's own reported
5\,mGy hypersensitivity without $T$ effectively becoming a free knob.

\subsection{Absent wet-lab overlay}
Neither the paper nor this replication overlays the model on Rothkamm \&
L\"obrich Fig.~1/Fig.~2 raw foci data. The paper says the 5 and 20\,mGy
curves ``compare favorably''; that claim is not verified here.

\section{Verdict}
\textbf{REPLICATED (model / equations, closed-form).} All equations and
parameters implemented; five figures reproduced; four in-text numerical
claims reproduce to $\leq 0.1\%$; one discrepancy is a paper labeling typo
(not a model error). Wet-lab agreement is deferred; cell-communication
claims in the title are unsupported by the mathematics.

\section{Reproducibility}
Deterministic, pure Python + NumPy + Matplotlib, $<1$\,s runtime, no HPC,
no external data. Full source in \texttt{code/multisig1.py} and
\texttt{code/replicate\_figures.py}; outputs in \texttt{figures/} and
\texttt{results/summary.json}.
