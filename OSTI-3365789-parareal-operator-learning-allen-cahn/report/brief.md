# Brief

Independent replication of **OSTI 3365789** — Geng, Yin, Cyr, Zhang & Ju, *"Parallel-in-Time Solution of Allen-Cahn Equations by Integrating Operator Learning into the Parareal Method"* (J. Sci. Comput. 2026, DOI 10.1007/s10915-026-03337-1). We reimplemented from the paper's equations the fully-discrete **Crank-Nicolson + finite-difference fine solver** for the classical Allen-Cahn equation and the **Parareal parallel-in-time algorithm** (Eqs. 6–14), then independently verified the paper's core numerical/algorithmic claims: 2nd-order accuracy (manufactured solution), the Maximum Bound Principle, energy dissipation, the Parareal "first-k-intervals-exact" invariant and convergence to the fine solution, and the paper's Sec. 4.2.3 claim that a *numerical* coarse propagator makes Parareal for AC ill-behaved. Compute: numpy on uicgpu; LLM-judge = free Argo gpt-5.2. **Verdict: PARTIAL** — the numerical fundamentals (MBP, energy dissipation, 2nd-order space accuracy, Parareal invariant + convergence) reproduce cleanly, but the paper's motivating negative claim about numerical coarse propagators (C5) was **contradicted** by our robust CN-coarse implementation.
