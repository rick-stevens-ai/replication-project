\section{Paper and scope}
Target: H.~Isobe, N.~F.~Q.~Yuan, L.~Fu, \emph{Unconventional Superconductivity
and Density Waves in Twisted Bilayer Graphene}, Phys.\ Rev.\ X \textbf{8}, 041041
(2018); arXiv:1805.06449v2. The paper introduces a \textbf{hot-spot patch
renormalization-group (RG)} model for twisted bilayer graphene near the $n=2$
Van Hove filling: nine momentum-conserving four-fermion couplings $g_{ij}$ flow
under one-loop RG (Eqs.~9--15), and RPA-resummed susceptibilities (Eqs.~16, 24)
diagnose ordering instabilities with channel interaction strengths $V_\eta$
(Eqs.~17--23).

\paragraph{Shared-kernel provenance / scope decision.} The assigned TEXTURES-100
loop-current class ships a kagome tight-binding + Peierls-flux kernel
(\texttt{loop\_current\_kagome\_kernel.py}, built for Fernandes \emph{et al.}
arXiv:2502.16657). It was read first, as required. \textbf{It is out of scope for
this paper's core}: Isobe \emph{et al.} has no tight-binding lattice, no Peierls
flux, no Berry curvature/Chern number. The kernel's \texttt{KagomeModel},
\texttt{chern\_number}, \texttt{bond\_current\_and\_charge}, and
\texttt{triangle\_flux\_from\_config} were therefore \emph{not} imported. Only the
conceptual ``couplings $\rightarrow$ ordering-channel selection'' idea (the
kernel's Box-2 \texttt{patch\_leading\_channel}) is thematically shared; the
actual in-scope core (9-coupling RG) was reimplemented from the paper's equations
in \texttt{isobe2018\_rg.py}. This is an honest class-mismatch flag, not a defect.

\section{Machine-checkable claims and results}
All numbers below are computed at runtime by \texttt{code/run\_checks.py} and
stored in \texttt{work/results.json}; none are transcribed from the paper's
figures.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Eq.~(9): frozen intravalley couplings.} $g_{14},g_{24},g_{44}$ have
identically zero RG beta-function. Verified $\max|\dot g_{i4}|=0$ across 500 random
coupling/nesting states. \textcolor{blue}{PASS}.

\item \textbf{Mean-field vs.\ RG SC (Sec.~III\,C).} In the symmetric
density-density case ($g_{14}{=}g_{24}{=}g_{44}{=}g_{22}{=}g_{32}{=}g_{42}$, no
exchange), mean-field pairing strengths vanish: $V_{d\text{-SC}}(0)=V_{p\text{-SC}}(0)=0$,
while $V_{s\text{-SC}}(0)=+2$ (repulsive). Under RG with nesting $d_{1-}=0.4$ a
negative $g_{42}-g_{32}$ is generated (min $=-68.3$), producing attractive
$V_{d\text{-SC}}$ (min $=-136.7$). RG generates SC absent in mean-field.
\textcolor{blue}{PASS}.

\item \textbf{Coupling flow under nesting (Sec.~III\,C).} With $d_{1-}\!\to\!0$ the
forward-scattering $g_{22}$ grows unchecked ($0.5\!\to\!6.56$) while the
BCS-coupled $g_{42}$ decreases and changes sign ($0.5\!\to\!-43.5$), exactly the
mechanism the paper invokes for $Q_-$ density wave + $d/p$-SC.
\textcolor{blue}{PASS}.

\item \textbf{Nesting selects the order (Fig.~4).} Weak nesting $d_{1-}=0.05$
$\Rightarrow$ leading $d$-SC ($y_c\approx7.6$); strong nesting $d_{1-}=0.45$
$\Rightarrow$ leading CDW$_-$ ($y_c\approx1.02$). Even with much stronger $Q_0$
nesting ($d_{1-}=0.15,d_{2-}=0.45$), the leading order is still the $Q_-$ density
wave (CDW$_-$), reproducing ``$Q_-$ dominates $Q_0$''. \textcolor{blue}{PASS}.

\item \textbf{Degeneracy lifting (Sec.~III\,B).} With no exchange the interaction
strengths are degenerate: $|V_{d\text{-SC}}-V_{p\text{-SC}}|=|V_{s\text{-SC}}-V_{f\text{-SC}}|
=|V_{\text{CDW}_-}-V_{\text{SDW}_-}|=0$. Finite exchange ($g_{31}\neq g_{41}$,
$g_{11}\neq0$) lifts them ($d/p$ split $0.8$, CDW/SDW split $1.2$), per
Eqs.~(17)--(19). \textcolor{blue}{PASS}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Verdict}
The paper's computational core --- the one-loop patch RG flow, the mean-field vs.\
RG contrast for superconductivity, the nesting-driven coupling growth, the
nesting-controlled selection between $d/p$-SC and $Q_-$ density wave, and the
exchange-driven degeneracy lifting --- is \textbf{reproduced 5/5} by independent
numerical integration of Eqs.~(9)--(24). The kagome loop-current shared kernel is
out of scope and was honestly flagged, not forced.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Coverage: 8/10.} Core RG dynamics, susceptibility
selection rules, and phase-diagram trend fully reproduced. Not covered: exact 2D
Fig.~4 phase-boundary digitization, $y$-dependent $d_{a+}(y)$ / Appendix-F
generalized RG, and the Sec.~V CDW$_-$ mean-field $n=2$ gap ($\Delta_c$).

\noindent\textbf{Agreement: 9/10.} Every reproduced claim matches the paper's
qualitative and structural predictions (signs, growth/decay, ordering, degeneracy
lifting) with real computed metrics; only the exact numerical phase-boundary
curves remain unverified.
