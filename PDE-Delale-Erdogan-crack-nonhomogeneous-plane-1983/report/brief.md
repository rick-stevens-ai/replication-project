# Brief

**Paper:** F. Delale & F. Erdogan, *The Crack Problem for a Nonhomogeneous Plane*, J. Appl. Mech. **50**(3):609–614 (1983); OA as NASA CR-166001 (NGR 39-007-011).

**What/why:** A Griffith (center) crack |x|<a lies on y=0 in an infinite elastic plane whose Young's modulus grades exponentially *parallel* to the crack, E(x)=E₀e^{βx}, with constant Poisson ratio ν. The paper reduces this graded-elasticity boundary-value problem to a Cauchy-type singular integral equation (SIE) for the crack-opening-slope density and computes Mode-I stress intensity factors (SIF) k₁(±a) versus the nonhomogeneity parameter βa. It is a foundational (791-cite) result in functionally-graded-material (FGM) fracture mechanics. We independently re-derive the SIE kernel from first principles, solve it from scratch with Gauss–Chebyshev quadrature, validate against exact homogeneous-crack SIFs at β=0, and reproduce the headline SIF tables.
