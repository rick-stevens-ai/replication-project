# Brief — Grover (2005) π/3 fixed-point search

Independent replication of Lov Grover, *"A different kind of quantum search"* (arXiv:quant-ph/0503205). Implemented the paper's π/3-phase-shift transformation `U R_s U† R_t U` and its recursion `U_{m+1} = U_m R_s U_m† R_t U_m` in pure numpy statevector, for N=16 (4 qubits) with one marked element, and compared against standard Grover amplitude amplification. Reproduced (i) the exact closed-form identity `P(target) = 1 − ε^(3^m)` to numerical precision (~1e-14 error), (ii) monotone convergence of the π/3 recursion (0.0625 → 0.176 → 0.441 → 0.825 → 0.995 across m=0..4), and (iii) the characteristic oscillation of standard Grover (0.06 → 0.96 → 0.02 → 0.99 → 0.001 across k=0..12) — exactly the qualitative contrast shown in the paper's Figure 1. Verdict: REPLICATED.
