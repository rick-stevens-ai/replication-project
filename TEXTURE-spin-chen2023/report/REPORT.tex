\section{Paper summary}
Using linear spin-wave theory on a Kitaev-$\Gamma$ honeycomb magnet hosting a triple-meron crystal
(TmX, 18 spins/cell), the paper shows that the topological magnon bands carry nonzero Chern numbers,
that tuning an external field drives \emph{successive topological phase transitions} at which the
Chern numbers change and the magnon thermal Hall conductivity $\kappa_{xy}$ \emph{changes sign}, and
that chiral magnon edge modes appear in a nanoribbon.

\section{Scope statement}
The full 18-band TmX LSWT is out of scope. We replicate the \emph{universal mechanism} the results
embody, on a minimal Haldane/DMI-class 2-band topological-magnon model: nonzero Chern number, a
field-driven Chern transition, and the accompanying $\kappa_{xy}$ reversal.

\section{Method}
Honeycomb magnon Bloch Hamiltonian $H(\mathbf k)=E_0 I + \mathbf d\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$: NN exchange
$\to d_x,d_y$ (Dirac cones); a Haldane NNN DMI term $\to$ valley-odd mass (Chern-inducing); a uniform
Semenoff-like control mass $m$ (field proxy). Chern numbers via the Fukui-Hatsugai-Suzuki plaquette
method; magnon thermal Hall $\kappa_{xy}=-\sum_{n,\mathbf k}c_2(E_n/T)\,\Omega_n$ with the standard
Bose $c_2(\rho)$ weight (dilogarithm via \texttt{scipy.special.spence}). Sweep $m\in[-4,4]$ across the
DMI gap $\sim 3\sqrt3\,D\approx2.08$. Tools: Python 3.13, NumPy, SciPy.

\section{Per-claim what worked / what didn't}
\textbf{C1 (worked).} The gapped magnon band carries $C=-1$. \textbf{C2 (worked).} Sweeping the field
proxy $m$ through the DMI gap flips $C:-1\to0$ --- a topological phase transition. \textbf{C3 (worked).}
$\kappa_{xy}$ is large ($+2.35$) in the $C=-1$ phase and reverses to small negative ($-0.13$) in the
trivial phase --- the Berry-curvature-driven thermal-Hall sign change tied to the Chern transition.
\textbf{C4 (not done).} Nanoribbon edge modes not computed. \textbf{C5 (reduced).} The 2-band model
shows a single $C:-1\leftrightarrow0$ transition rather than the TmX's successive multi-Chern sequence.

\section{Critique of the replication}
This reproduces the paper's \emph{physical mechanism} (topological magnon $\to$ Chern transition $\to$
$\kappa_{xy}$ sign change) with a proper Berry-curvature + thermal-Hall calculation, but on a drastically
reduced 2-band model. It does NOT reproduce the paper's specific 18-band spectrum, the number/sequence
of successive transitions, the TmX transition fields, nonreciprocal magnons, or the edge spectra.
Two bugs were fixed en route (Haldane mass sign structure so a topological gap actually opens; a
divergent hand-rolled dilogarithm replaced by \texttt{scipy.special.spence}). LLM-judge: PARTIAL
(coverage 6, agreement 5), adopted. Energies/units are model-normalized.

\section{Verdict}
\textbf{PARTIAL} --- nonzero magnon Chern number, a field-driven topological transition (Chern change),
and the accompanying $\kappa_{xy}$ sign reversal reproduced on a minimal model; the full 18-band TmX
multi-transition sequence and edge modes are reduced/out of scope.
