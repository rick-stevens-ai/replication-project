# Brief — BVBRC-55

**Paper:** Hinc K, Kabała M, Iwanicki A, Martirosian G, Negri A, Obuchowski M. (2021) "Complete genome sequence of the newly discovered temperate *Clostridioides difficile* bacteriophage phiCDKH01 of the family Siphoviridae." *Archives of Virology* 166:2305–2310. DOI [10.1007/s00705-021-05092-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00705-021-05092-0). PMID 34014385, PMC8270841. CC BY 4.0.

**What/why:** A genome-announcement paper describing a novel temperate siphovirus, phiCDKH01, induced from a clinical *C. difficile* isolate (CD34-Sr). It reports the phage's genome statistics (45,089 bp, 28.7% GC, 66 ORFs), a CRISPR array, its closest relative (phiCD24-1, ~89% identity, same ICTV genus), its phylogenetic novelty among C. difficile siphoviruses, and the prophage's integration locus in the host genome. We independently replicated the descriptive and comparative-genomics claims by pulling the deposited phage genome (MN718463), the relative phiCD24-1 (LN681534), a panel of 11 other C. difficile phages, and the host assembly contig (JACSDL010000003.1) from NCBI, then recomputing genome stats, the CRISPR array (minced), whole-genome intergenomic identity (VIRIDIC-style BLASTn), and prophage localization (BLASTn phage-vs-host). This maps cleanly onto BV-BRC's Codon Tree / Phylogenetic Tree + comparative-genomics workflow. **Verdict: REPLICATED** (LLM-judge agreement 93/100).
