# Brief

Independent replication of Brassard, Høyer, and Tapp's 1998 paper *Quantum Counting* (arXiv:quant-ph/9805082): built an analytic-QPE simulator of the paper's `Count(F,P)` algorithm and cross-validated it against a gate-level Qiskit statevector circuit to L∞ ≤ 3×10⁻¹⁵ across 7 cases, then swept 90 configurations (N ∈ {16,32,64}; varying t; counting qubits p ∈ {3..8}) and verified in every case that (i) the argmax estimate lies within Theorem 5's error bound |t−t̃| < (2π/P)√(tN) + (π²/P²)N and (ii) the exact success probability meets or exceeds 8/π² ≈ 0.811. Because C1 (general amplitude-amplification speedup) and C4 (heuristic-search speedup) were not numerically exercised, the LLM-judge (free Argo endpoint) verdict is **PARTIAL** with agreement 1.00 and coverage 0.67 on the tested claims.
