# Brief — BVBRC-110

Independent replication spot-check of Al-Trad et al. 2023 ("The Plasmidomic Landscape of Clinical MRSA Isolates from Malaysia", *Antibiotics* 12:733, PMID 37107095). Verified BioProject **PRJNA722830** is live (88 SRA runs, 92 assemblies), pulled the specific deposited plasmid contigs cited in the paper (pSauR23-1, pSauR165-1, pSauR3-3, plus reference plasmids pSK41, pC194, pT181, SAP078A, pSAZ10A), and re-ran the paper's key comparative-genomics BLASTs with `ncbi-blast+` on the real deposited nucleotide sequences. Independent BLAST reproduced the paper's numbers essentially exactly: pSAZ10A vs pSK41 = 99.3% ident / 87.6% query cov (paper: 99.9% / ~88%); pSauR165-1 subregion vs pC194 = **99.78% ident over 2753 bp, matching subject nts 162-2910 exactly** (paper: 99% / 2751 bp / 162-2910); pSauR165-1 subregion vs pT181 = 99.6% / 3725 bp (paper: 99% / 3829 bp). Independently downloaded a RepL plasmid (pSauR3-3, CP098730.1, 2473 bp) and confirmed via the NCBI-PGAP annotation that it carries the claimed RepL replicase + ermCL leader + erm(C) methyltransferase — proving the paper's core "63 small RepL plasmids each encoding ermC" claim on a real deposited example. Assembly method (Unicycler v0.4.8) also matches. Full re-assembly of all 79 raw read sets and full PlasmidFinder/MOB/ARG scans across all 189 plasmids were out of scope for this replication window and are documented as untested.
