\section{TL;DR}

The paper claims that the number of DNA damage sites per particle track, in a
1\,$\mu$m spherical scoring volume, follows a Gamma distribution whose shape
$\alpha(y_F)$ and rate $\beta(y_F)$ parameters vary quadratically with the
frequency-mean lineal energy $y_F$. The claim is supported by TOPAS-nBio
Monte-Carlo simulations of six monoenergetic protons, nine monoenergetic alpha
particles, and a 250\,keV X-ray reference, producing per-track SDD-format
damage tables that are then fit with a Gamma.

We reproduced the entire \emph{downstream analytical layer} (MGM parametric forms,
quadratic $\alpha(y_F)/\beta(y_F)$ fits, closed-form Gamma PDF, end-to-end
pipeline against the author-bundled X-ray microdosimetric spectrum) using the
authors' public code (\texttt{github.com/MGHPhysicsResearch/MGM}). All Figure 2 and
Figure 3 curves were regenerated (\texttt{figures/fig2\_damage\_vs\_yF.png},
\texttt{figures/fig3\_complexity\_and\_gamma\_params.png}).

We did \textbf{not} re-run the upstream TOPAS-nBio MC (the raw SDD files that the
Gammas were fit to are not deposited; a full re-run is GBs and outside slot
budget), and we did \textbf{not} re-fit any of the $(\alpha, \beta)$ pairs from raw
damage histograms. We evaluated the authors' published parameter constants
verbatim.

\section{Genuine critique --- what the paper glosses over}
\label{sec:critique}

\subsection{Issue \#1: author-code Gamma call does not match paper text formula}

The paper's Methods states the Gamma PDF as
$f(C; y_F) = \beta^\alpha / \Gamma(\alpha) \cdot C^{\alpha-1} \cdot e^{-\beta C}$
(standard rate-parameterization). In SciPy this is
\texttt{scipy.stats.gamma(a, scale=1/b).pdf(C)}.

However, \texttt{src/mgm.py} in the official repo calls
\texttt{scipy.stats.gamma(a, b).pdf(C)}, which by positional signature passes
$b$ as the \emph{location} (shift) parameter rather than the rate. This produces
a location-shifted Gamma, not the paper's rate-parameterized Gamma.

Empirically the shapes are qualitatively similar (both unimodal, low-complexity
peaked, monotone-decaying tails), and the fits are internally self-consistent
(whichever convention was actually used at fit time is the one the published
$\alpha, \beta$ pairs live in), so the published figures are correct
\emph{internally}. But any downstream user of the public code will silently
compute a different PDF than the paper's stated formula. Two-character fix:
\texttt{scipy.stats.gamma(a, scale=1/b).pdf(C)}. See
\texttt{evidence/gamma\_form\_audit.csv}.

\subsection{Issue \#2: $\beta(y_F)$ goes negative inside the claimed validity window}

The paper claims MGM is applicable for $y_F \in [\sim 2, \sim 200]$ keV/$\mu$m
(100-MeV protons $\to$ 2-MeV alphas). But the fitted quadratic
$\beta(y_F) = -6.62 \times 10^{-5} y_F^2 + 1.48 \times 10^{-3} y_F + 1.494$
has roots near $y_F \approx 175$ keV/$\mu$m; at $y_F = 200$ we measure
$\beta = -0.859$, which makes the paper's stated rate-Gamma
$\beta^\alpha/\Gamma(\alpha) \cdot C^{\alpha-1} e^{-\beta C}$ an improper
(divergent) distribution. The author-code loc-shifted variant does not blow up
here, but that is coincidence, not physics. The paper does not warn about this
upper-$y_F$ limit.

\subsection{Issue \#3 (distribution family): Gamma is asserted, alternatives not tabulated}

The paper reports $R^2 > 0.999$ for the Gamma fit across all beams, but does
\emph{not} tabulate residuals or compare against alternative candidate
distributions: log-normal (physically motivated by multiplicative processes),
negative-binomial (natural for count-per-track data), truncated exponential
(minimal-parameter null hypothesis), or Gamma-mixture models (which would be
natural if high-LET tracks produce a bimodal population of ``core'' vs
``penumbra'' damage clusters). The MC-histogram raw data would let this
comparison be done at essentially zero extra cost, but is not deposited.

Also unreported: whether the same Gamma shape describes the complexity
distribution equally well at very low LET (X-rays, secondary electrons from
protons at $y_F \sim 0.7$ keV/$\mu$m) as at very high LET
($y_F \gtrsim 100$ keV/$\mu$m). Physical intuition suggests the underlying
generative process differs qualitatively (Poisson-thin sparse damage vs
dense track-core clustering), so a single unimodal Gamma fitting both
regimes deserves more skepticism than the paper offers.

\subsection{Issue \#4: LET dependence of $(\alpha, \beta)$ is a fitted phenomenology, not a mechanism}

The quadratic $\alpha(y_F)$ and $\beta(y_F)$ are pure phenomenology --- no
mechanistic reason is given why the complexity Gamma parameters should be
quadratic in $y_F$ specifically (rather than, say, linear-plus-saturating like
their own SBI and BDI functions). The paper does not discuss goodness-of-fit
alternatives for the meta-fit (i.e., how well would $\alpha, \beta$ be
described by linear or saturating functions of $y_F$?).

\subsection{Issue \#5: MC re-run was skipped; the claim ``R$^2 > 0.999$'' is unfalsifiable from the deposit}

The core reproducibility problem: with no raw SDD files deposited, the
central quantitative claim of the paper (Gamma fits to per-track damage
complexity, $R^2 > 0.999$) can be neither verified nor refuted independently.
An independent replicator can only confirm that the \emph{analytical downstream}
--- once you accept the published $(\alpha_j, \beta_j)$ pairs and their quadratic
summary --- is internally consistent. That is a real result, but it is much
weaker than ``the paper is reproduced.''

\section{Verdict cross-check with queue label}
\label{sec:verdict-mismatch}

Queue label: \textbf{REPLICATED}.

Honest assessment: the replication is \textbf{split-verdict} --- REPRODUCED
(analytical / model-parameter layer) / NOT-REPRODUCED (upstream MC data-generation
layer). Under the LUCID four-tier rubric (REPLICATED / PARTIAL / SPOT-CHECK /
NO-GO), the case most naturally maps to \textbf{PARTIAL} or \textbf{SPOT-CHECK},
not full \textbf{REPLICATED} --- because the upstream Monte-Carlo, which is
where the central paper claim actually lives, was never independently
re-executed.

Session-level context: 12/31 verdicts in the current backfill session were
mismatches ($\sim$39\%); this is the classic MC-code pattern flagged in the
brief (``if the actual MC was never re-run, only the analytical fit, that's
likely a REPLICATED $\to$ PARTIAL/SPOT-CHECK mismatch''). Preserved as
REPLICATED per instruction, mismatch flagged here for downstream re-adjudication.

\section{Reproducibility blockers}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Raw SDD damage files not deposited} (hard blocker). Per-track
  damage tables for 6 protons + 9 alphas + 250\,keV X-ray reference + 2 validation
  runs --- not in Frontiers SM, not in MGM repo, not in any TOPAS-nBio public
  dataset located.
\item \textbf{Microdosimetric spectra not deposited} (medium). Only one bundled
  spectrum (250\,keV X-ray).
\item \textbf{Per-beam $(\alpha_j, \beta_j)$ pairs not tabulated} (soft). Only
  the summary quadratics are given; the eight raw pairs (that the quadratics
  fit) are not in main text or repo.
\item \textbf{Author-code Gamma call parameterization} (minor code bug). See
  Issue \#1.
\end{enumerate}
