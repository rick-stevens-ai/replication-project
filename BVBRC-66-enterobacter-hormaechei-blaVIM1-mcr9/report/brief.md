# Brief: BVBRC-66 · Sadek et al. (2020) *Pathogens* 9:687

**One paragraph.** Independent replication of *"First Genomic Characterization of blaVIM-1 and mcr-9-Coharbouring Enterobacter hormaechei Isolated from Food of Animal Origin"* (Sadek et al., 2020, *Pathogens* 9(9):687, doi:10.3390/pathogens9090687, PMID 32842587). The paper reports one *E. hormaechei* isolate (strain MS37 / BioSample SAMN14534668) from an Egyptian beef patty coharbouring blaVIM-1 and mcr-9 on a single 270.9 kb IncHI2 plasmid pMS-37a, with mcr-9 flanked by IS903 upstream / IS1 downstream and lacking its downstream qseC/qseB regulatory pair. All five circular replicons of the complete hybrid Illumina+Nanopore Unicycler assembly are public (CP053190–CP053194, RefSeq assembly GCF_013265685.1). We independently pulled the assembly from NCBI, ran (a) an in-silico 7-gene PubMLST *E. cloacae* complex typing → **called ST279 with 7/7 alleles at 100% id/100% cov (exact match to paper)**, (b) a ResFinder-DB tblastn/blastn AMR scan → **confirmed blaVIM-1 + mcr-9 both at 100% id/100% cov on CP053191.1 (pMS-37a) plus every other AMR gene the paper lists**, (c) PlasmidFinder Inc typing on all four plasmids → **pMS-37a is IncHI2+IncHI2A at 100% id (matches paper)**, and (d) a manual IS903/IS1/qseB/qseC blast on the mcr-9 flanking region → **IS903 87.6% id immediately 5' of mcr-9, IS1 99.87% id immediately 3', and qseB/qseC absent from the plasmid (only weak paralog for qseC at 27%, no qseB hit at any threshold; chromosomal true copies exist)**. LLM-judge scored the reproduction **REPLICATED** (Claude Sonnet 4.6 via Argo, free endpoint). The only ceiling on the verdict is that the SRA run SRR11478637 is essentially empty (5 dummy spots, no usable raw reads), so a *de novo* re-assembly from reads is impossible; we verified the assembled molecules directly.
