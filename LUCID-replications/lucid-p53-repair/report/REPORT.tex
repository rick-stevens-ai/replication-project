\section*{Verdict}
\textbf{PARTIAL} --- 6/8 qualitative claims reproduced (3 fully, 3 partially,
0 contradicted), but the paper's headline stochastic apoptosis percentages
and sustained p53 limit-cycle oscillations are \emph{not} exercised
quantitatively. The deterministic ODE core (Hat 2016 substrate + LUCID TGF$\beta$
extension) is independently re-implemented and cross-checked against the
recovered MDPI supplement. Legitimate PARTIAL, not upgradeable to REPLICATED.

\section{Implementation}
Independent Python re-implementation using SciPy \texttt{solve\_ivp} with
LSODA. Two-stage integration: 24-h warmup at zero dose to homeostasis, then a
600-s square IR pulse followed by 72 h of observation. 27 explicit species
(DSB, ATM/ATMp, SIAH1u/p, HIPK2, p53 in three phospho-forms, Wip1 mRNA/protein,
Mdm2 in four compartments/phospho-states, PTEN mRNA/protein, PIP3, AKTp,
Bax/mRNA, Casp, p21/mRNA, TGF$\beta$); PIP2 and AKTu enforced as algebraic
conservations of PIPtot and Akttot. Parameters taken verbatim from Hat 2016
S1 Text (Tables B, C); three protein-degradation constants ($g_6$ for PTEN,
$g_9$ for Bax, $g_{19}$ for p21) were raised from Hat's gene-state values
($10^{-13}$/s giving $>10^5$-year half-lives) to effective $\sim$1-h rates
because the buffering binding-network proteins are omitted from the reduced
system (Hat's deterministic limit blows up without them). This substitution is
explicitly tagged \texttt{model-substitution}. TGF$\beta$ collapses LUCID's
p21$\to$GADD45$\to$p38$\to$TGF$\beta$ chain into a first-order chain with
$k_T = 10^{-4}$/s and $g_T = 10^{-5}$/s driven by p21.

\section{What the paper claims vs.\ what we exercised}

\textbf{Fully exercised.}
Deterministic mean-field ODE trajectories for ATMp, p53 phospho-forms,
Mdm2 compartments, Wip1, Bax, p21, TGF$\beta$ at 2/4/8 Gy across two ATM
Hill thresholds ($M \in \{0.14, 0.5\}$ Gy). Dose-response monotonicity of
apoptotic propensity (Bax/AKTp) and TGF$\beta$ accumulation. Cross-check
that the recovered LUCID supplement Tables S1--S3 use the same reactions,
rate laws, and rate constants as Hat 2016.

\textbf{Partially exercised.}
The p53/Mdm2/Wip1 oscillation \emph{period} ($\approx 8$ h) is recovered
as a \emph{single damped cycle}, not the sustained limit-cycle the LUCID
stochastic simulation produces. Cycle-arrest dwell-time increases with dose
in LUCID's stochastic version but is compressed to a fast p21 plateau in the
deterministic limit.

\textbf{Not exercised (headline gaps).}
(1) The 72-h \emph{apoptosis percentages} in LUCID Fig.\ 6 come from a
Gillespie ensemble of $\geq 100$ cells with the Bogda\l{} 2013 apoptotic
gate; we report a deterministic \emph{propensity ratio} (Bax/AKTp), not a
population-fraction estimate. (2) NASIC track-structure per-particle DSB
generation is replaced by an analytical \texttt{DSBGy $\cdot$ dose} pulse ---
faithful to Hat 2016 and to LUCID Eq.\ 4 but silent on radiation-quality (LET)
physics. (3) No parameter re-fitting was performed; we adopt Hat 2016's
values verbatim (LUCID inherits them). (4) No single-cell timelapse data
were quantitatively matched --- the LUCID paper itself only compares to
literature qualitative behavior, so this gap is inherited from the paper.

\section{Genuine critique}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The paper's headline apoptosis quantities are a \emph{stochastic}
        ensemble output; the deterministic ODE we run cannot in principle
        produce that number. Reporting Bax/AKTp as a surrogate is honest but
        not the same experiment. A REPLICATED verdict would require
        implementing the Bogda\l{} 2013 gate and running $\geq 100$-cell
        Gillespie ensembles.
  \item The three raised degradation constants ($g_6, g_9, g_{19}$) are a
        genuine model change, not a numerical tweak. They keep the reduced
        system bounded but change the effective network topology relative to
        Hat 2016. A stronger replication would re-introduce the buffering
        binding chain (Bax/BclxL/Badu/14-3-3) and revert to Hat's gene-state
        values.
  \item The TGF$\beta$ chain collapse to a first-order p21-driven relaxation is
        a modelling choice, not a LUCID equation. The weak dose separation we
        report in Fig.\ 5 is a consequence of that collapse plus p21
        saturation; the LUCID stochastic figure shows stronger separation.
  \item No LUCID original code was inspected (none exists publicly) so we
        cannot rule out that LUCID's Gillespie implementation uses slightly
        different reaction propensities from the deterministic ODE.
  \item Verdict: \textbf{PARTIAL}. Deterministic backbone independently
        reproduced; stochastic apoptosis \emph{quantities} and NASIC track
        physics not exercised.
\end{itemize}
