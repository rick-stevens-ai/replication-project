# Brief — BVBRC-87 — Gancz et al. 2021 (Kpnu95 ST1412)

**Paper:** Gancz A, Kondratyeva K, Cohen-Eli D, Navon-Venezia S. "Genomics and Virulence of *Klebsiella pneumoniae* Kpnu95 ST1412 Harboring a Novel IncF Plasmid Encoding *bla*<sub>CTX-M-15</sub> and *qnrS1* Causing Community Urinary Tract Infection." *Microorganisms* 9(5):1022 (2021). DOI 10.3390/microorganisms9051022; PMID 34068663; PMC8151138; OA CC BY.

**What/Why:** Independent replication of a single-isolate WGS + plasmidology paper on a community-UTI ESBL-producing *K. pneumoniae*. The paper's testable computational claims — sequence type ST1412, capsule locus, complete 180 kb IncFIB(K) plasmid pKpnU95 (GenBank MK552109) size/GC/CDS count, replicon identity to *K. oxytoca* pCAV1099-114, and the 10-gene plasmid resistome including *bla*<sub>CTX-M-15</sub> and *qnrS1* — were all reproducible from public data (BioProject PRJNA494961, assembly GCA_015714665.1, plasmid MK552109). Independent re-analysis with **mlst 2.35.0** (klebsiella scheme), **Kleborate 3.2.4** (kpsc preset), **PlasmidFinder** BLAST DB, and direct GenBank feature audit reproduces all these claims exactly. Wet-lab claims (*C. elegans* killing, plasmid curing/reconstitution, artificial-urine growth, copper tolerance) require the physical isolate and are not re-testable from public data. **Verdict: PARTIAL (strong)** — every testable computational claim replicates on real public data.
