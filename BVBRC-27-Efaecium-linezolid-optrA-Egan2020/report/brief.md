# Brief — BVBRC-27

**What:** Independent replication of Egan et al. 2020 (*J Antimicrob Chemother*, PMID:32129849, DOI:10.1093/jac/dkaa075), which reports a high prevalence of the transferable oxazolidinone/phenicol resistance genes **optrA** and **poxtA** in linezolid-resistant *Enterococcus faecium* and *E. faecalis* from hospitalized Irish patients, carried on diverse plasmids and genetic backgrounds.

**Why / result:** The paper's raw Illumina/MinION reads were never deposited (no SRA/BioProject), but the authors deposited 10 assembled plasmid/region sequences in GenBank (MN831410–MN831419). I pulled all 10 real sequences plus the full NCBI AMRFinderPlus curated AMR catalog (9,712 alleles) and the canonical optrA (NG_048023) and pE394 (KP399637) references, then independently re-detected the resistance genes and re-verified the plasmid-structure claims. **optrA/poxtA/cfr(D)/fexA detection, the 36,331 bp optrA plasmid = pE394 at 99.997% identity, the 21,849 bp poxtA plasmid with its identical ~4 kb IS1216E-flanked poxtA cassette, and the 6 distinct optrA variants were all reproduced on real data.** The epidemiological prevalence (22.7%) and cgMLST/wgMLST clustering could not be tested (raw reads not public). **Verdict: PARTIAL** (molecular claims strongly replicated; epidemiology out of reach). Also independently caught that the paper's cited reference plasmid "pE349" is really **pE394**.
