In this paper, we establish a large deviation principle for the nonlinear one-dimensional stochastic heat
equation driven by a Gaussian noise which is white in time and which has the covariance of a fractional
Brownian motion with Hurst parameter H ∈ ( 14 , 12 ) in the space variable.

Keywords: fractional Brownian motion; large deviations; stochastic heat equation
