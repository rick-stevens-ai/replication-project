\begin{abstract}
We replicate the \textbf{model half} of Oh \emph{et al.} (arXiv:2605.21124), which
argues that the well-isolated $s$-orbital bands of chiral tellurium carry orbital
angular momentum (OAM) of \emph{purely interatomic} origin --- ``spin-free
interatomic OAM.'' Because $s$ orbitals carry zero intra-atomic OAM
($\vec{L}_{\mathrm{Atom}}=0$), \emph{all} OAM in these bands is itinerant
($\vec{L}_{\mathrm{Itin}}$), generated by the geometric phase acquired through
interatomic hopping along a chiral helical chain. We reproduce the paper's minimal
theoretical model --- a 3-band tight-binding (TB) Hamiltonian of a single
right-handed $3_1$ helical chain (G\"obel/Schimpf/Mertig, Ref.~[27]) --- and its
central prediction, the chirality-induced orbital sign texture (CIOS): the axial
itinerant OAM $L_{\mathrm{Itin},x}$ \emph{locks} to $\mathrm{sign}(\partial E/\partial k)$
and \emph{flips sign} when the chain handedness is reversed. Both effects reproduce
to machine precision (sign-match $=1.0$, $|\mathrm{corr}(L,\partial E/\partial k)|=0.999$,
chirality flip $L_{\mathrm{L}}=-L_{\mathrm{R}}$ at $100\%$ of $k$-points).
\textbf{Verdict: REPLICATED (model headline).} The material-specific
DFT+Wannier bulk-Te OAM magnitudes and the CD-ARPES/SARPES observations are out of
scope (they require Supplemental-Material DFT parameters plus VASP/Wannier90, and a
synchrotron beamline, respectively).
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper summary}
The paper addresses a central obstacle in orbitronics: atomic spin--orbit coupling
$H_{\mathrm{SOC}}\sim -\vec{L}\cdot\vec{S}$ inevitably entangles OAM with spin. The
authors propose a route to genuinely \emph{spin-free} OAM by exploiting $s$-orbital
bands, which have $\vec{L}_{\mathrm{Atom}}=0$ and therefore no atomic-SOC channel to
spin. In chiral Te they resolve (by ARPES) a set of $s$-orbital bands
($E-E_F \approx -15$ to $-8$~eV) cleanly separated from the $p$ manifold. These bands
nonetheless carry finite OAM --- necessarily itinerant $\vec{L}_{\mathrm{Itin}}$,
arising from the Bloch-wavefunction geometric phase of interatomic hopping on the
chiral lattice. Circular-dichroism ARPES (CD-ARPES) shows two crossing branches of
opposite CD sign (the chirality-induced orbital sign texture, CIOS), and
spin-resolved ARPES (SARPES) confirms the absence of any spin-angular-momentum
polarization --- decisive evidence of spin-free OAM. Theory support comes from
(i) a minimal single-helical-chain TB model, and (ii) first-principles VASP+Wannier90
for bulk Te, where the modern theory of orbital magnetization yields finite global
$\vec{L}$ while the atomic-centered approximation (ACA) vanishes.

\section{Method}
The full reproduction is a self-contained pure-NumPy script,
\texttt{work/reproduce.py} ($\sim$1\,s on one CPU core).

\subsection{3-band $3_1$ helical-chain TB model}
The unit cell is 3 sites (one per screw step) of a single right-handed helix. Site
$j\in\{0,1,2\}$ sits at azimuth $\phi_j = 2\pi j/3$, radius $r$, axial rise $c/3$.
One $s$-orbital per site, nearest-neighbour hopping $-t$ along the helix, and the
inter-cell hop ($2\to0'$) carries the axial Bloch/screw phase $e^{ikc}$:
\begin{equation}
H(k) = -t
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 1 & e^{-ikc}\\
1 & 0 & 1\\
e^{ikc} & 1 & 0
\end{pmatrix},
\end{equation}
a flux-threaded 3-site triangle --- the minimal analytic realization of the
G\"obel/Schimpf/Mertig chiral-chain model. Diagonalized over
$k\in(-\pi/c,\pi/c]$ (401 points) it gives 3 screw-labelled, generically
nondegenerate bands.

\subsection{Itinerant OAM operator}
We adapt the shared itinerant-OAM kernel
$L=\tfrac12(\vec{R}\times\vec{v})$, $v_a=i[H,R_a]$, to the axial component,
\begin{equation}
L_{\mathrm{Itin}} \;=\; \tfrac12\big(Y\,v_Z - Z\,v_Y\big),
\qquad v_a=i[H(k),A],
\end{equation}
with diagonal position operators $Y=\mathrm{diag}(Y_j)$, $Z=\mathrm{diag}(Z_j)$,
$X_j=r\cos\phi_j$, $Y_j=r\sin\phi_j$, $Z_j=(c/3)j$. Because every site carries an
$s$-orbital, the atomic OAM vanishes and the entire $L_{\mathrm{Itin}}$ is the
interatomic circulation in the helix cross-section --- exactly the paper's C2
distinction. We evaluate $L_{\mathrm{Itin}}$ per band per $k$, together with
$\partial E/\partial k$ by finite difference, and measure both the sign-match and
Pearson correlation between them.

\subsection{Chirality flip --- a methodological subtlety}
A left-handed helix is the \emph{mirror image} of a right-handed one. The correct
mirror reverses only the geometric azimuth ($\phi_j\to-\phi_j$) while leaving the
axial screw-advance direction of the Bloch phase intact --- hence $H(k)$ and $E(k)$
are unchanged, and only the sign of the circulating current flips
($L_{\mathrm{L}}=-L_{\mathrm{R}}$). \textbf{An initial version reversed both the
azimuth \emph{and} the screw phase; that is just the trivial $k\to-k$ symmetry and
produced identical $L$ (no flip)}. Once the transform was corrected to mirror only
the geometry, the two chiralities came out energy-degenerate with exactly opposite
OAM, matching the paper. This is worth flagging: getting the CIOS flip right hinges
on applying a \emph{mirror}, not a $k$-inversion.

\section{Results}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{C1 --- 3-band structure.} Three nondegenerate dispersive bands,
      bandwidth $4.0\,t$, split over $>99\%$ of the BZ
      (frac.\ split $01=0.995$, $12=0.9975$), touching only at isolated
      screw-protected crossings. See Fig.~\ref{fig:bands}.
\item \textbf{C2a --- OAM locks to group velocity.} Per-band
      $\mathrm{sign}(L_{\mathrm{Itin},x})=\mathrm{sign}(\partial E/\partial k)$
      at $100\%$ of $k$-points on all three bands; correlations
      $0.9983/0.9994/1.0000$ (mean $|\mathrm{corr}|=0.999$). See
      Fig.~\ref{fig:oam}.
\item \textbf{C2b --- chirality flip.} RH and LH chains share $E(k)$ but
      $L_{\mathrm{L}}=-L_{\mathrm{R}}$ exactly (flip corr $=1.00$; $100\%$ of
      $k$-points change sign). See Fig.~\ref{fig:flip}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Honest critique}
The reproducible headline reproduces \emph{perfectly}: the chirality-locked,
spin-free interatomic OAM texture --- the paper's central conceptual claim --- comes
out to machine precision from a minimal, transparent, pure-NumPy model. The physics
is clean and robust: because $s$ orbitals carry $L_{\mathrm{Atom}}=0$, the model is a
genuine test that OAM can be purely itinerant, and the CIOS flip is exact by mirror
symmetry.

What we did \emph{not} reproduce is the \emph{material-specific} half:
\begin{itemize}
\item The quantitative DFT+Wannier OAM magnitudes for bulk Te (the ACA-vanishes /
      modern-theory-finite contrast, and $L_{\mathrm{Glob},y/z}$ from interchain
      hopping) require the Supplemental-Material DFT parameters (plane-wave cutoff,
      $k$-mesh, SOC settings), a VASP (or QE) + Wannier90 + \texttt{post\_wan}
      toolchain, and the 3D Te cell. The single-chain model captures only the axial
      $L_{\mathrm{Itin},x}$ texture, not the absolute magnitudes or the $y/z$
      components.
\item The CD-ARPES ($\mp17\%$ at $-9.2$~eV) and SARPES (no SAM) observations are
      synchrotron-beamline experiments and cannot be computed.
\end{itemize}
These are scope limits, not contradictions: nothing in our results conflicts with
the paper. The model successfully explains \emph{why} the effect exists; a full
material replication would quantify \emph{how large} it is in real Te.

\section*{Open Questions}
\begin{description}
\item[Q1.] Does the modern theory of orbital magnetization ($L_{\mathrm{Glob}}$) for
bulk Te give the same $\mathrm{sign}(\partial E/\partial k)$ locking as the itinerant
chain $L$, or does interchain coupling reorganize the sign texture?
\item[Q2.] How does finite inter-chain hopping in bulk Te modify the single-chain
OAM texture --- does it merely renormalize magnitudes or introduce new
$L_{\mathrm{Glob},y/z}$ structure absent from a single chain?
\item[Q3.] What is the quantitative OAM magnitude on the $E-E_F=-9.5$~eV iso-energy
surface, and how does it compare (in absolute units) to the dimensionless
single-chain model?
\item[Q4.] How does the ACA (atomic-centered) versus modern-theory OAM differ for
$s$ electrons, where $L_{\mathrm{Atom}}=0$ by construction --- is the ACA/modern-theory
gap exactly the full itinerant part here?
\item[Q5.] Does the screw-protected band crossing carry a quantized OAM winding
(a topological orbital charge), and is that winding chirality-dependent?
\end{description}
