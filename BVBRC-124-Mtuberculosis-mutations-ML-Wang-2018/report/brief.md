# Brief — BVBRC-124

**Paper:** Kavvas et al. 2018, Nat Commun 9:4306 (PMID 30333483) — "Machine learning and structural analysis of Mycobacterium tuberculosis pan-genome identifies genetic signatures of antibiotic resistance."

**Why replicate (3rd pass, distinct angle):** Two sibling replications already exist for this paper: BVBRC-25 rebuilt the MI + ensemble-SVM feature-selection code, and BVBRC-90 verified supplementary XLSX consistency. BVBRC-90 explicitly flagged one paper claim as untestable — "AUC > 0.80 for 8 of 13 antibiotics: cannot refit without raw data." The raw per-strain × per-allele matrix IS distributed on the authors' GitHub, so this pass (BVBRC-124) does the actual refit: real 5-fold stratified CV on the 1595×15,367 allele matrix using both L1-SVM (paper's family) and L2-logistic (independent baseline). Result: **9/15 drugs with mean AUC > 0.80** (both classifiers agree exactly), consistent with the paper's 8/13 claim. As a bonus, a programmatic RCSB probe of 20 canonical AMR genes across 8 drug classes finds structural data for 19/20 — supporting the availability half of the paper's 3-D structural-mapping claim (Claim 4). Verdict: **PARTIAL** — the previously-untested ML-AUC claim is now supported on real data, while the exact structural-mapping counts (254/20/50) remain a spot-check.
