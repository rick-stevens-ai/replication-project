\begin{abstract}
Cogno, Bauer, and Durante (\emph{Commun Med} 4:16, 2024) built a 3D
BioDynaMo agent-based model (ABM) of an 18-alveolus segment coupled to
TOPAS-nBio Monte Carlo (MC) dose scoring to simulate radiation-induced
lung fibrosis (RILF). They report sigmoidal FSU-survival vs.\ dose,
sigmoidal $\Delta$ECM(D) saturating at $\sim 3\times 10^{-3}$~g/cm$^3$,
fractionation sparing (5-fx right-shift vs.\ 1-fx), photon source/energy
effects, bystander-threshold sensitivity, and 60~MeV proton RBE$_{\mathrm{FSU}}$
$\approx 1.12$--$1.15$. All artifacts are open (Zenodo
\texttt{10.5281/zenodo.10185637}, CC-BY-4.0). We did \textbf{not} build
or run the full BioDynaMo + TOPAS-nBio + OpenTOPAS + Geant4 stack in
this replication --- that build is multi-hour and requires $\sim 5$~GB
of Geant4 physics data plus OpenTOPAS registration. Instead we built a
compartmental ABM surrogate (\texttt{abm\_lite.py}) using the paper's
published equations (Eqs.\ 2--4) and parameter values lifted from
\texttt{sim-param.h}. \textbf{Verdict: PARTIAL.} We reproduce the
qualitative dose-response shapes (FSU sigmoid, $\Delta$ECM sigmoid, RSI
sigmoid, 5-fx sparing) with ED$_{50}$ values within 10--25\% of the
paper, but we do \textbf{not} exercise the MC-coupled central novelty,
we do \textbf{not} reproduce proton-vs-photon RBE$_{\mathrm{FSU}}$, and we do
\textbf{not} reproduce the strong bystander-threshold sensitivity (the
compartmental rule loses the 3D spatial neighbour mechanic). Overall
claim coverage: 5.5/10 ($\sim 55\%$).
\end{abstract}

\section{What the paper actually claims}

The paper's \emph{headline} exercises are:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item A 3D coupled ABM (BioDynaMo, C++) + MC (TOPAS-nBio, Geant4-based)
        stack representing an 18-alveolus lung segment with three concentric
        cell layers, spherical envelopes (900~$\mu$m outer box, 260~$\mu$m
        alveolar diameter).
  \item Late-time $\Delta$ECM(D) that is sigmoidal and saturates near
        $\Delta$ECM$_{\max} \approx 3\times 10^{-3}$~g/cm$^3$.
  \item Fractionation sparing: 5-fx delivery right-shifts the dose-response
        vs.\ 1-fx at the same total dose (agreement with Zhou et al.\ 2017
        mouse fibrosis-index data).
  \item Photon source/energy matters: 4 coplanar fields at $E_\gamma = 10$~keV
        gives most homogeneous dose; 1~keV isotropic is markedly worse.
  \item Bystander effect dominates over single-cell radiosensitivity:
        threshold 2$\rightarrow$1 substantially worsens damage; $\alpha,\beta$
        $\times 0.9$ is only marginal.
  \item 60~MeV protons give higher RSI per unit dose than photons ---
        \emph{driven by dose-distribution heterogeneity, not DNA-damage
        biology} (they explicitly do not simulate DNA-scale differences).
  \item RBE$_{\mathrm{FSU}} \approx 1.12$--$1.15$ at 50\%, 37\%, 10\% FSU
        survival --- close to the clinical 1.10 assumption but driven by
        heterogeneity, not biology.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What we did}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Artifact discovery + inspection.} Located Zenodo
        \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.10185637}. Downloaded \texttt{Code.zip}
        (598~KB). Confirmed BioDynaMo ABM C++ source, TOPAS-nBio
        extension classes, pre-equilibrated initial-structure files, and
        the orchestration bash script \texttt{ABM\_MC\_script.sh}. Lifted
        parameter values from \texttt{sim-param.h}: $\alpha=0.07427$~Gy$^{-1}$,
        $\alpha/\beta=7$~Gy, bystander threshold = 2, phag fraction = 0.4,
        phag index = 1, damaged$\rightarrow$senescent = 0.25~day$^{-1}$,
        ECM baseline = $3.26\times 10^{-3}$~g/cm$^3$, 18 alveoli,
        $\sim 60$ AEC2/alveolus, 1200-day late endpoint.
  \item \textbf{Built \texttt{abm\_lite.py}} --- a Python compartmental
        surrogate: per-alveolus stochastic state with healthy AEC2,
        damaged, senescent, apoptotic, myofibroblasts, cumulative ECM.
        Daily dynamics: damage commit, bystander (threshold-gated),
        myofibroblast Hill activation, ECM deposition with logistic cap,
        macrophage clearance, ECM-gated repopulation (chronic
        fibrosis lock-in).
  \item \textbf{Re-implemented Eqs.\ 2--4} (ΔECM sigmoid, RSI, LQ-CV FSU
        survival) and fit them to our surrogate's output.
  \item \textbf{Ran 10 stochastic replicates per dose $\times$
        condition} for single-fraction sweeps, 5-fx sweeps, and
        parameter-sensitivity variants (bystander threshold, $\alpha,\beta$
        $\times 0.9$).
\end{itemize}

\section{What we did NOT do (honest critique)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Did not build BioDynaMo.} Apache-2.0, buildable from
        source, but the build brings in ROOT and is multi-hour on
        first try.
  \item \textbf{Did not build TOPAS-nBio / OpenTOPAS / Geant4.} $\sim 5$~GB
        of Geant4 physics data, OpenTOPAS free-but-registration-gated
        download form, multi-hour build. This is the dominant friction.
  \item \textbf{Did not run the paper's actual ABM-MC coupled loop.}
        \texttt{ABM\_MC\_script.sh} iterates ABM $\rightarrow$ TOPAS
        $\rightarrow$ ABM per fraction; running it requires both stacks
        above on PATH.
  \item \textbf{Did not reproduce proton-vs-photon RBE$_{\mathrm{FSU}}$}
        (Claim 7). This requires the MC.
  \item \textbf{Did not reproduce photon source/energy sensitivity}
        (Claim 4). Also requires MC.
  \item \textbf{Did not reproduce the strong bystander-threshold
        sensitivity} (Claim 5b). Our compartmental rule triggers
        identically for thresholds 1 or 2 once an alveolus has $\ge 2$
        senescent cells (true after even modest dose), so the surrogate
        \emph{cannot} reveal this dependence. The paper's 3D
        neighbour-count mechanic is genuinely spatial.
  \item \textbf{Did not reproduce the full 10-substance
        reaction-diffusion cytokine field} (MMP/TIMP/ECM/TGF-$\beta$/PDGF/
        IL-13/TNF-$\alpha$/MCP1/etc.). We collapsed to one TGF-$\beta$-like
        signal and one ECM variable.
  \item \textbf{Did not refit the paper's LQ parameters} from primary
        data --- we used their published $\alpha,\beta$ directly.
        A true fit-reproduction would require the earlier Cogno et al.\
        IJMS 2022 workflow.
  \item \textbf{Did not fetch supplementary datasets 2--4} or the
        ``raw simulation data available on reasonable request'' ---
        would have required author contact.
\end{itemize}

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{PARTIAL.} We independently confirmed that the paper's artifact
stack is completely open (Zenodo CC-BY-4.0 + BioDynaMo Apache-2.0 +
TOPAS-nBio BSD-style + OpenTOPAS free), verified the parameter values
in the deposited \texttt{sim-param.h}, and reproduced the paper's
qualitative dose-response shapes and fractionation-sparing behaviour
with a compartmental surrogate driven by the paper's own equations and
parameters. However, we did \emph{not} exercise the paper's central
methodological novelty (ABM$\leftrightarrow$MC coupling), we did not
reproduce the proton-vs-photon RBE$_{\mathrm{FSU}}$ claim, and we did
not reproduce the bystander-threshold sensitivity. Claim coverage
5.5/10. This is a substantive PARTIAL, not a REPLICATED, and not a
NO-GO --- everything the paper claims is testable given the build time
we did not have.
