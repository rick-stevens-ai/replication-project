## Cross-reference to paper
- Paper's Sec. V.A "true ground state energy of hydrogen is -1.1373 hartree" -> our FCI/exact = -1.137306 Ha. Match to 4 decimal places.
- Paper's shot-noise claim (std ~ 1/sqrt(N)) -> our fit exponent -0.524 vs theory -0.5. Match within 0.024.
- Paper's depolarizing claim (monotonic degradation, linear-in-p at small p) -> our slope ~36 Ha/p, saturation at large p. Order-of-magnitude match with the naive n_gates * ||H|| prediction.
- Paper's C5 (12-ansatz ranking under noise) and C6 (expressibility correlation) NOT tested here.
