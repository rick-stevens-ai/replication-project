# Method Extract — Şaşıoğlu et al. 2026 (arXiv:2606.08757)
**Title:** Chiral-Angle-Controlled Altermagnetic Spin Splitting in Nanotubes
**Texture class:** spin (altermagnetic momentum-space spin texture, d-wave)

- **Core physics:** Rolling a 2D d-wave **altermagnet** into a nanotube projects its momentum-dependent (d-wave) spin splitting onto quantized 1D subbands ("dimensional projection"), converting k-space spin texture into a chiral-angle-controlled 1D spin splitting.
- **Headline claim (replication target):** The nanotube spin splitting follows a **cos(2θ)** dependence on chiral angle θ — **vanishing at nodal orientations, maximal at antinodal**, robust across a broad class of tubes.
- **Key equations:** Minimal **tight-binding model** of a square-lattice d-wave altermagnet (spin-dependent hopping producing dx²−y² spin structure); zone-folding / dimensional-projection of k onto nanotube subbands; spin-splitting ∝ cos(2θ). This TB model is the tractable core.
- **Methods used:** (1) minimal tight-binding model (analytic/small numeric), (2) first-principles **DFT** confirmation.
