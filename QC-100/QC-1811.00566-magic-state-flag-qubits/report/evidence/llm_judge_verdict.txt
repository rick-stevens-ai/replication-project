JUSTIFICATION: The replication successfully reproduces the core fault-tolerance scaling claim: the log-log fit yields a slope of 2.415, consistent with the predicted p² (slope = 2) behavior, confirming that post-selected logical errors scale quadratically with physical error rate as the paper claims. The acceptance probability formula (1-p)^75 is matched to within 15% across three orders of magnitude in p, strongly validating the 75-fault-location count. However, the fitted prefactor of 3.79 falls below the paper's claimed range of [4.41, 9.95], and the ratio column shows systematic underestimation (0.08–0.54) across all measured points, indicating the prefactor is not fully reproduced. This discrepancy is physically explained by the circuit variant used: the replication employs 6 syndrome ancillas without flag gadgets plus a perfect final round, whereas the paper's circuit uses the Chao-Reichardt flag-gadget architecture with 3 ancillas total (Fig. 3), which has a different set of fault paths contributing to the leading-order coefficient. The slope agreement and acceptance formula match constitute meaningful partial replication of the headline claims, but the prefactor mismatch—outside the paper's stated range—and the use of a structurally different circuit prevent a full REPLICATED verdict.
