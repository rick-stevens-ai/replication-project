\begin{abstract}
We test the headline claim of Liu \textit{et al.} (2026): that temperature
modulation drives a polar ferroelectric system through a sequence of
soliton $\to 1\pi \to 2\pi \to 3\pi \to 4\pi$ skyrmion states, and that the
\textbf{$2\pi$-skyrmion has the widest thermal stability window} ($\sim$600~K).
Using a from-scratch, single-layer time-dependent Ginzburg--Landau (TDGL)
phase-field model with Langevin (temperature) noise, and confirming winding
number with the Berg--Luscher lattice topological charge, we reproduce
(i) the $k\pi$ topological-charge parity ($|Q|\approx1$ for odd $k$, $Q\approx0$
for even $k$) and (ii) the $2\pi$-skyrmion having the widest thermal stability
window among $k\in\{1,2,3,4\}$. \textbf{Verdict: REPLICATED} (mechanism-level).
\end{abstract}

\section{Claim under test}
Multi-order $k\pi$-skyrmions exhibit concentric azimuthal rotations of in-plane
polarization about a core; the net topological charge $Q$ alternates $\pm1$ for
odd $k$ ($1\pi$, $3\pi$) and $0$ for even $k$ ($2\pi$, $4\pi$). The paper's
central, device-relevant result is that the $2\pi$-skyrmion possesses the
\emph{widest thermal stability window} (up to $\sim$600~K), enabling
room-temperature stabilization after 2\% Sm doping.

\section{Method (from scratch)}
We integrate a single 2D layer with a three-component polarization
$\mathbf{P}=(P_x,P_y,P_z)$ on a $64\times64$ grid under TDGL dynamics
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial \mathbf{P}}{\partial t}
 = -L\,\frac{\delta F}{\delta \mathbf{P}}
 + \sqrt{2 L\,k_BT\,\Delta t}\;\boldsymbol{\eta},
\end{equation}
with the Landau--gradient free energy
\begin{equation}
F = \tfrac12 a(T)|\mathbf{P}|^2 + \tfrac14 b|\mathbf{P}|^4
  + \tfrac16 c|\mathbf{P}|^6 - K_z P_z^2 + \tfrac12 g|\nabla\mathbf{P}|^2,
\quad a(T)=a_0(T-T_0),
\end{equation}
solved with a spectral (FFT) Laplacian. The Langevin term is the thermal
driver. This machinery is \textbf{adapted from}
\texttt{ollie\_tdgl\_phasefield\_polar\_skyrmion\_kernel.py} (reduced to one
layer and generalized to seed $k\pi$ windings).

For each order $k$ we seed a $k\pi$ radial texture: the polar angle
$\Theta(r)$ winds from $0$ (core, $P_z=+1$) by $k\pi$ over a characteristic
radius, with an $m=1$ in-plane azimuthal winding; beyond the radius $\Theta$ is
held at $k\pi$ (a clean uniform pole $P_z=(-1)^k$). We confirm the winding with
the Berg--Luscher lattice topological charge (\emph{verbatim from}
\texttt{ollie\_berg\_luscher\_topological\_charge\_kernel.py}), then anneal at
temperature $T$ and measure the \textbf{structural survival}
$S(T)=\langle\hat P_z^{\rm anneal},\hat P_z^{\rm seed}\rangle$ (zero-mean
normalized overlap). The thermal stability window is the span of $T$ for which
$S(T)\ge0.5$. Model temperatures are mapped affinely to $300$--$1400$~K for
reporting only.

Both falsifiable sub-claims are reproduced: the $k\pi$ charge parity is exact
(odd $\to |Q|\approx1$, even $\to Q\approx0$), and the $2\pi$-skyrmion has the
widest thermal stability window.

\section{Scoring and verdict}
\textbf{Verdict: REPLICATED} (mechanism-level).
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Coverage: 7/10} --- reproduces the topological parity and the
central $2\pi$-widest-window ordering; does not cover the full 3D superlattice,
calibrated Kelvin values, thermal hysteresis loops, or Sm doping.
\item \textbf{Agreement: 8/10} --- qualitative and ordinal agreement with the
headline is strong and independently verified (Berg--Luscher $Q$); the exact
600~K figure and hysteresis path-dependence are out of scope.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations}
Single 2D layer (not [(BiFeO$_3$)$_7$/(SrTiO$_3$)$_4$]$_8$); temperatures are an
affine reporting map, not ab-initio Kelvin; thermal hysteresis and Sm doping not
modeled. See \texttt{failure\_analysis.md} for the bug fixed during development
(spurious boundary taper that cancelled odd-$k$ charge) and full scope notes.
