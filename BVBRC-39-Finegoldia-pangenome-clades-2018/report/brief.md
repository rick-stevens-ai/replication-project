# Brief — BVBRC-39

**What:** Independent replication of Brüggemann et al. 2018 (*Sci Rep* 8:266), "Pan-genome analysis of the genus *Finegoldia* identifies two distinct clades, strain-specific heterogeneity, and putative virulence factors."

**Why/how:** All 17 genomes analyzed in the paper (10 newly sequenced Swedish isolates + 7 previously published *F. magna*) were mapped 1:1 from their paper GenBank WGS accessions to current NCBI GCA assemblies and re-downloaded via NCBI Datasets. The paper's three central analyses were then independently re-run with *different* tools than the authors used: (1) two-clade structure + inter-clade ANI via **fastANI** (paper used Parsnp core-SNP tree + JSpeciesWS ANI); (2) core/pan-genome via **CD-HIT** protein clustering (paper used ProteinOrtho bidirectional BLAST); (3) virulence-factor presence/heterogeneity via **blastp** of curated UniProt *F. magna* reference proteins against all 17 proteomes. The central quantitative claims reproduced tightly on the same real data: two clades at inter-clade ANI 90.67% (paper 90.7%), a 4-magna/8-*nericia* split among the 12-genome subset (exact), core proteome of 1209 orthologs (paper 1202), mean 1759 CDS/genome (paper 1760), and protein L in 11% of strains (paper ~10%). **Verdict: REPLICATED.**
