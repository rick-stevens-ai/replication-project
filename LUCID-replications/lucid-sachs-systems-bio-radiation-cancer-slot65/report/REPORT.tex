\begin{abstract}
This slot audits Little, Heidenreich, Moolgavkar, Sch\"ollnberger \& Thomas (2008),
``Systems biological and mechanistic modelling of radiation-induced cancer,''
\textit{Radiation and Environmental Biophysics} \textbf{47}:39--47
(DOI 10.1007/s00411-007-0150-z). The paper is a 9-page workshop summary of
five separate talks at the 1st International Workshop on Systems Radiation
Biology (GSF, 14--16 Feb 2007). It is structurally a review/overview: it
publishes no novel equations of its own (other than one Thomas/WECARE logit
skeleton), no novel datasets, and no novel fitted parameter sets. Each of
its five subsections defers to a primary paper for the actual machinery.
Verdict: \textbf{PARTIAL} --- 4/8 testable quantitative claims verified at
equation level (MVK plateau at $1.4\times 10^{-9}$ relative error, MVK
monotonicity, SVM U-shape and $k_{\mathrm{ap}}$ ordering, WECARE
Newton--Raphson logit MLE recovery), 3/8 data-blocked by named inaccessible
primary datasets, 1/8 documented as spot-check. Note: slot name
\texttt{sachs-systems-bio-radiation-cancer} is a queue-labelling artifact;
the actual paper is by Little et al., not Sachs. Rainer K. Sachs's
two-track / dual-radiation-action models are not the subject of this paper.
\end{abstract}

\section{Methods}

We re-implemented the two model families the paper exposes explicitly
(two-stage MVK / TSCE closed-form hazard used by talks 1--3, and
Sch\"ollnberger's State-Vector-Model bystander U-shape used by talk 4)
plus the WECARE conditional-logit skeleton (talk 5) on synthetic data.
All code is pure Python 3 with numpy and matplotlib (Agg backend); total
wall time under 3 seconds on a laptop. Scripts:

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{code/smoke\_replication.py}: three-parameter MVK hazard sweep
      over ages 0--90 and two-panel SVM bystander figure over
      $D=0$--$1$~Gy (immediate + delayed plating).
\item \texttt{code/claim\_audit.py}: enumerated ledger over eight testable
      claims (C1--C8), writes JSON + run-log.
\end{itemize}

\section{Critique --- Honest Assessment of What Was Done vs.\ Paper's Headline}

\subsection{What was exercised}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Equation-level headline: two-stage MVK/TSCE closed form.}
      Verified analytically vs.\ numerically to machine precision
      ($1.4\times 10^{-9}$). This is a genuine equation-level replication
      of the plateau identity $h(\infty)=(\mu_0 N/\alpha)\,b$ that is
      implicit throughout the paper's Talks 1--3.
\item \textbf{Equation-level headline: SVM bystander $k_{\mathrm{ap}}$ ordering.}
      The paper's two \emph{quoted} parameter values (0.054/d delayed,
      0.022/d immediate) were plugged into an analytic SVM skeleton;
      the U-shape and the ordering ``delayed protection $>$ immediate
      protection'' were both reproduced qualitatively.
\item \textbf{Equation-level headline: WECARE first-level logit.}
      The only explicit equation typeset in the paper (Thomas §5) was
      recovered by Newton--Raphson MLE on synthetic case-control data at
      $n=8000$ within $\leq 4\%$ relative error on all three coefficients.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What was NOT exercised (the paper's actual scientific weight)}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The ODE / integrated dose-response models were not
      independently integrated from raw microdata.} The SVM's
      $k_{\mathrm{ap}}$ values were \emph{used as given}, not re-fit from
      the Redpath 2001 CGL1 transformation-per-dose per-replicate data
      (which is not publicly archived). Our SVM run demonstrates that
      the paper's quoted numbers give the paper's claimed U-shape, not
      that the numbers themselves are correct.
\item \textbf{Epidemiological cohort fits were not reproduced.} None of
      the paper's four data figures (Heidenreich rat-radon ERR;
      Heidenreich JANUS mouse; Little \& Li 2007 SEER colon
      model-comparison; Sch\"ollnberger CGL1 dose-response) were re-fit
      from the underlying microdata. The paper's central
      \emph{comparative} claim --- that 2-stage MVK and 2-stage
      Little-Wright co-tie for best SEER-colon fit, with 5-stage models
      significantly worse ($P<0.01$) --- was not tested at all (C6 data-
      blocked). This is the single largest gap.
\item \textbf{ERR / EAR predictions vs.\ epidemiological data were not
      benchmarked.} No BEIR-VII / UNSCEAR / A-bomb LSS comparison was
      run. The paper's implicit claim that MVK/TSCE-family models
      qualitatively track observed excess relative risk vs.\ age-at-exposure
      and time-since-exposure patterns is neither confirmed nor
      contradicted by this audit.
\item \textbf{The WECARE result itself is untested.} The C5 logit
      recovery uses synthetic data seeded by us. It proves the
      \emph{equation is well-posed} and Newton--Raphson converges,
      not that the WECARE ATM/BRCA $\times$ radiation dose coefficients
      are what Bernstein et al.\ reported. The WECARE microdata is
      permanent NO-GO under LUCID's protocol.
\item \textbf{Legitimate PARTIAL$\to$REPLICATED upgrade was considered
      and rejected.} The queue brief noted that a purely
      analytical/review paper with no MC + no wet-lab could be
      upgraded if the report substantiates full headline recovery.
      \emph{This paper does not meet that bar}: its headlines
      (best-fit model comparison; ERR predictions; parameter values
      with 95\% CIs) live in the primary papers it summarizes, not
      in its own body. The equation-only surface was fully recovered
      (3/3), but the paper's actual scientific claims (4 data-blocked
      rows above) were not. PARTIAL is the substantively correct
      verdict.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Sensitivity of the verdict}

If ``headline'' is defined as ``the paper's own equations,'' this is a
clean REPLICATED (3/3 equation-level, machine-precision agreement). If
``headline'' is defined as ``the numerical claims the paper makes about
the world'' (best-fit stage counts; $k_{\mathrm{ap}}$ CIs re-derivable;
Thorotrast risk distribution; ERR curves), this is closer to
SPOT-CHECK. PARTIAL is the honest midpoint and matches the on-disk
REPORT.md.

\section{Conclusion}

\textbf{Verdict: PARTIAL.} Coverage 5/10, Agreement 9/10. The PARTIAL
verdict is driven by \emph{scope} (four data-blocked dataset-level
claims naming specific missing artifacts), not by \emph{disagreement}
(no claim contradicted at any tolerance we could test). Every equation
we could reproduce from the paper alone agrees at machine precision or
within $\leq 4\%$ MLE tolerance.
