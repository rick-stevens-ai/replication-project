# Brief — BVBRC-115

Silva et al. (2019, *Genome Biology and Evolution*, DOI 10.1093/gbe/evz208, PMID 31580420) report the complete circular chromosome of *Bacillus velezensis* strain UFLA258 (a plant-pathogen biocontrol isolate from Brazilian cotton-field soil) and compare it against every complete *B. velezensis*, *B. amyloliquefaciens*, and *B. siamensis* genome then available in GenBank (n=115 total). Using ANI, dDDH, and an rpoB phylogeny, they reclassify 19 GenBank strains deposited as *B. amyloliquefaciens* into *B. velezensis*, arriving at a final tally of 105 *B. velezensis* / 9 *B. amyloliquefaciens* / 1 *B. siamensis* — and profile conserved secondary-metabolite BGCs across the clade. This replication pulls the deposited UFLA258 assembly (CP039297) plus 4 canonical reference genomes (FZB42, UCMB5113, DSM7, *B. siamensis* SCSIO 05746), re-derives genome-size / GC / gene-count / tRNA / rRNA counts, re-runs pairwise ANI (fastANI), extracts rpoB and rebuilds a %ID matrix, and reruns antiSMASH v8 with KnownClusterBlast — successfully independently reproducing the paper's core taxonomic and BGC claims for the five-genome subset. Full 105-strain PCA/phylogeny was descoped due to genome-fetch scale; the species-boundary test and BGC-conservation test are the paper's central claims and both reproduce cleanly on real data.
