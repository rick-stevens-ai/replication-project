# Brief — BVBRC-44

Independent replication of Rafei et al. (2022, *FEMS Microbes*), which reports the complete hybrid (Illumina MiSeq + Oxford Nanopore) genome of *Acinetobacter baumannii* strain Cl107 (ST25, Lebanon 2012) and a detailed dissection of its 198 kb conjugative plasmid **pCl107**. We downloaded the actual deposited assemblies (GenBank CP098521 chromosome, CP098522 pCl107) plus five reference plasmids from NCBI, and independently reproduced the paper's structural, resistance-gene, metabolic-module, host-typing, and comparative-relatedness claims using free tools (NCBI Datasets/eutils, AMRFinderPlus, abricate/ResFinder, mlst, BLAST+, and RefSeq annotation) on uicgpu. **Verdict: REPLICATED** — exact genome/plasmid sizes, all six plasmid resistance genes at 100% identity (three independent callers), ST25/ST229 host typing, BREX/ptx/uric-acid/P450/MPF modules, the incomplete uric-acid module (missing urate oxidase), the AbGRI1-related "missing-link" resistance region (100% identity to pA297-3/pAB3), and the chromosomal blaOXA-64/blaADC + GyrA S81L/ParC S84L determinants all confirmed with no discrepancies.
