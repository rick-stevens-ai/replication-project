# Brief — OSTI-3365432 (Bian & Shi, Applied Energy 2025)

Independent replication of the paper's headline learning-accuracy claim (Table 3) and inference-speedup claim (Table 5) for the "operator learning for energy-efficient building ventilation control with CFD" study. The paper trains an ensemble of 5 GNOT-family neural-operator transformers (MIOEGPT_meanvariance, ~570k params each) on 300 ANSYS-Fluent transient simulations of a UCSD classroom and reports 10.90% test-set rel-L2 error for the ensemble (vs 11.82-13.01% per individual model) with ~250,000× wall-clock speedup vs a CFD forward call. Using the authors' own released trained checkpoints and processed test pickle from `alwaysbyx/Bear-CFD-dataset` on Hugging Face, and the released training code from `alwaysbyx/BuildingControlCFD`, we re-ran all 5 models over the full 1,126-sample test set on 1×A100 80GB and reproduced the ensemble L2 error to **11.03% (paper 10.90%, Δ+0.13pp)** with per-model errors matching within 0.32pp. Forward-pass latency = 4.5 ms/call, matching the paper's ~5 ms and consistent with the ~250,000× CFD speedup claim. **Verdict: REPLICATED.**
