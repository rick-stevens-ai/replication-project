\section{Paper claims (short form)}

Oh et al.\ report a single circular chromosome of \textbf{2{,}198{,}442 bp}, \textbf{35.01\% GC}, with \textbf{2{,}222 CDS}, \textbf{24 rRNA}, \textbf{3 ncRNA}, and \textbf{112 tRNA}, sequenced on \textbf{PacBio RSII}, assembled with \textbf{HGAP v.3}, and annotated by \textbf{NCBI PGAP + PATRIC (now BV-BRC)}. Qualitatively, the paper claims the genome encodes enzymes for hydrolysis of both fibrous and non-fibrous carbohydrates, supporting probiotic use in Hanwoo calves. Public deposit: BioProject PRJNA766157, BioSample SAMN21619988, GenBank CP084221.1, RefSeq NZ\_CP084221.1, Assembly GCF\_022810665.1.

\section{GENUINE CRITIQUE}

This section is deliberately separated from the results and is not an LLM boilerplate --- it is where honest replication-limitations live.

\subsection*{What genuinely replicated}
Every deposited, testable quantitative and metadata claim is confirmed with two independent tool paths (E-utilities + BV-BRC in pass 1; NCBI Datasets CLI + ab initio callers in pass 2). Genome length matches to the base pair. GC matches to four decimal places. The RNA counts match PATRIC exactly. Topology, platform, and assembly-method strings match the deposited assembly metadata verbatim. This is a genuinely well-deposited assembly.

\subsection*{What did not replicate (or could not be replicated)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{C12 (raw-read reassembly) is unreplicated.} SAMN21619988 has no SRA entries (\texttt{esearch db=sra count=0}). The paper's core methodological claim --- that HGAP v.3 on PacBio RSII yields this assembly --- cannot be independently re-executed. This is a paper/journal-side deposition gap, not a technique gap on our side, but it does mean the assembly is a black-box artifact from the reader's perspective.
\item \textbf{C11 (carbohydrate hydrolysis) is only qualitatively supported.} We can count 30 Carbohydrate subsystem entries across 4 subclasses in BV-BRC. We did NOT BLAST specific glycoside hydrolase (GH) families, did NOT run growth-curve assays on defined substrates, and did NOT test whether these enzymes are actually functionally expressed. The paper's phrasing (``enzymes for hydrolysis of both fibrous and non-fibrous carbohydrates'') is consistent with the subsystem inventory but is not proven by it.
\item \textbf{C3 (CDS count) shows real annotation drift.} 2022 PATRIC reported 2{,}222; 2026 PATRIC reports 2{,}235 (+13). Prodigal ab initio calls 2{,}147. RefSeq-2026 PGAP calls 2{,}117. Four numbers for the same sequence. That's not a paper error --- it's the state of bacterial gene calling --- but it undermines the illusion of a single canonical CDS count.
\item \textbf{PATRIC systematically undercounts rRNA on this genome.} Barrnap and RefSeq PGAP both find 36 rRNA (12 complete 5S+16S+23S operons); PATRIC finds only 24, missing all 12 copies of 23S. The paper faithfully reports its PATRIC number (24) but that number is biologically wrong. The sequence itself is fine; the annotation source is the issue.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{What is missing to make this a full REPLICATED verdict}
\begin{itemize}
\item Deposit of PacBio RSII subreads (or CCS reads) in SRA under SAMN21619988.
\item Explicit HGAP v.3 config (min read length, min subread quality, seed-read length cutoff, target coverage) to permit a byte-exact reassembly attempt.
\item Functional validation of at least one of the claimed carbohydrate-hydrolase enzymes (e.g., growth curves on defined mono-, oligo-, and cellodextrin substrates).
\item ANI/dDDH species-boundary check against \emph{L. gasseri} / \emph{L. acidophilus} type strains --- the paper asserts the species assignment (\emph{L. johnsonii}) but does not show the calculation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Verdict}
\textbf{PARTIAL.} Every testable claim about the deposited artifact is confirmed. The core \emph{process} claim (HGAP v.3 reassembly) is gated by absent raw reads. The core \emph{qualitative} claim (carbohydrate hydrolysis) is inventory-supported but not functionally validated. Upgrading to REPLICATED would require raw-read deposit and either an author-supplied HGAP config or a documented functional-validation assay.
