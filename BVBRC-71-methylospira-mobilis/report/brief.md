# Brief — BVBRC-71

**Paper:** Oshkin, Miroshnikov, Danilova, Hakobyan, Liesack, Dedysh (2019). *Thriving in Wetlands: Ecophysiology of the Spiral-Shaped Methanotroph Methylospira mobilis as Revealed by the Complete Genome Sequence.* Microorganisms 7(12):683. PMID 31835835.

**What we did:** Downloaded the two public GenBank complete-genome records at the heart of the paper — the announced *Ca.* Methylospira mobilis Shm1 chromosome (CP044205.1, 4.70 Mbp) and its reference comparator *Methylococcus capsulatus* Bath (AE017282.2, 3.30 Mbp) — parsed both with Biopython, and independently checked 21 concrete claims from the abstract and Results: genome length, GC content, rRNA-operon copies, tRNA count, CDS count, 16S rRNA identity between the two organisms, and presence/absence of pMMO/sMMO clusters, methanol dehydrogenases, Mo-Fe *and* V-Fe nitrogenases, high- and low-affinity terminal oxidases, chemotaxis / flagellar machinery, CRISPR-Cas, IS-element load, and PEP carboxylase asymmetry.

**Why:** This is a complete-genome announcement paper — the BV-BRC Comprehensive Genome Analysis workflow (RASTtk annotation + comparative gene-cluster mining) is exactly the kind of analysis whose central deliverables (genome stats + gene-presence tables) are directly verifiable against the deposited GenBank record, so real independent replication is achievable end-to-end from public data without needing to re-culture the bug.

**Result:** LLM-judged **PARTIAL** (coverage 100 %, agreement 86 %) — all 15 quantitative genome-level claims match within tool tolerance (16S identity 93.89 % vs paper 94.06 %, GC 54.05 % vs 54, 3 rRNA operons, 194 IS-related CDS vs paper's ">200"); pathway-gene presence claims (pMMO, sMMO, nif+vnf, CRISPR, PEP carboxylase asymmetry, chemotaxis expansion) are all confirmed from CDS product qualifiers. Only genuine gap: paper's RAST-predicted CDS count (4858) is ~13 % higher than the deposited PGAP annotation (4214), a well-known annotation-pipeline effect, not a data problem.
