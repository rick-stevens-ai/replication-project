\section{Open questions and next probes}

The following five questions were not resolved by this replication and represent natural follow-on
probes. Each is stated with its evidentiary basis and a concrete experimental next step.

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{Time-dependent Hamiltonians.}
Does the static-MPF quadratic error-order improvement ($p \to p+2$) hold for $H(t)$, where the
base Trotter $S_2$ itself acquires additional error from time ordering (Dyson / Magnus corrections)?
\emph{Basis.} Paper's Section~V numerics and Theorem~1 are stated for time-independent $H$. Dynamic
MPF (Section~VI) allows time-dependent coefficients but the commutator-scaling proof for
time-dependent $H$ is not spelled out. Our replication tested only static $H$.
\emph{Next steps.} Driven transverse-field Ising $h_x(t) = h_0 + A\sin(\omega t)$ at $n=4\text{--}6$;
exact reference via fine RK / Magnus-4; apply MPF with $k = \lambda\cdot(4,13,17)$,
$c = (0.016088, -1.794934, 2.778846)$ using a Magnus-based $S_2$; fit slope vs $\lambda$ and
sweep $\omega$ across adiabatic/non-adiabatic regimes.

\item \textbf{Qubitization / LCU comparison.}
How does the static MPF compare to (and possibly compose with) qubitization / LCU on the same
Hamiltonian in a matched-query-complexity comparison?
\emph{Basis.} Qubitization achieves optimal query complexity $O(t + \log 1/\varepsilon)$ but with
larger per-query overhead and ancilla count; MPF has far lower ancilla. A head-to-head Pareto
frontier (queries vs error vs ancilla) on Childs--Maslov was not in the paper.
\emph{Next steps.} Implement qubitization via Qiskit block-encoding utilities at $n=4\text{--}8$;
report (T-count, ancilla, error) at several precision targets; overlay MPF curves; identify
crossovers. Also test MPF as an LCU building block (each $\rho_k$ as an LCU term).

\item \textbf{Chemistry and lattice-gauge benchmarks.}
Does the fixed-coefficient MPF advantage persist for realistic chemistry (electronic-structure) and
lattice-gauge Hamiltonians whose commutator structure differs sharply from disordered Heisenberg?
\emph{Basis.} Paper's numerics only cover Childs--Maslov Heisenberg. Chemistry has all-to-all
fermionic 2-body terms with non-uniform commutator norms; lattice gauge theories have
gauge-constraint structure. The coefficient triple may be non-optimal there.
\emph{Next steps.} Chemistry: JW-mapped H$_2$/LiH (4, 12 qubits) from PySCF; run the same MPF, fit
$\lambda$-slope. Lattice: 1+1D Schwinger at 4--6 sites, repeat. Report whether the $-4$ slope holds
and whether re-optimizing $(k_i, c_i)$ per Hamiltonian gives further improvement.

\item \textbf{Noise robustness under realistic gate noise.}
How robust is the static MPF (paper's fixed $c$) to realistic 2-qubit gate noise (coherent +
incoherent), and where is the crossover error rate at which noise dominates the Trotter error the
MPF is designed to cancel?
\emph{Basis.} Minimax MPF (Section~VII) targets noise but was not exercised in our replication.
Under gate noise the $c_i$ act on noisy density matrices; the cancellation giving $O(1/\lambda^4)$
may fail.
\emph{Next steps.} Qiskit \texttt{AerSimulator} with depolarizing 2-qubit rates
$\in \{10^{-4}, 10^{-3}, 10^{-2}\}$; same $k = \lambda\cdot(4,13,17)$ MPF at $n=4$, $t=1$,
$\lambda \in \{1..4\}$; identify the $\lambda$ at which noise depth dominates. Compare against a
re-optimized Minimax MPF.

\item \textbf{Sample-cost Pareto vs standard Trotter.}
On a total-shot-count Pareto frontier, does the static MPF still beat standard Trotter after
amortizing the constant-factor circuit-repetition overhead and shot-noise cost of estimating each
$\rho_{k_i}$?
\emph{Basis.} Paper's cost accounting is in circuit depth / gate count at fixed error. Real
experiments pay in shots: large $|c_2| = 1.79$ and $|c_3| = 2.78$ amplify shot noise relative to a
single-circuit Trotter estimate.
\emph{Next steps.} Compute observable estimation error $\langle O \rangle$ (e.g., $O = Z_0 Z_1$)
under both approaches at matched total shot budget $N \in \{10^4, 10^5, 10^6\}$; derive analytic
variance $\propto \sum c_i^2$; plot shot budget vs observable error for MPF vs Trotter at matched
$k_{\max}$; identify the shot-budget regime where MPF wins; cross-check against Minimax coefficients
designed to minimize the sample-cost objective.

\end{enumerate}
