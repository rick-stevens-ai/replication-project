# Brief — BVBRC-83

Gómez-Martínez et al. (2022, *Microorganisms*) characterized a 27,635 bp plasmid **pPE52IMP** (GenBank **CP102481.1**) from *P. aeruginosa* strain PE52 (Mexican clinical isolate), carrying **blaIMP-56** in a class-1 integron. They claim the plasmid defines a **novel resistance plasmid family** in *P. aeruginosa*: MOBP11 subfamily, non-typeable by PBRT, with a shared backbone shared with pMATVIM-7 (AM778842.1), pD5170990 (KX169264.1), pMRVIM0713 (KP975076.1), unnamed FDAARGOS_570 (CP033834.1), and p4130-KPC (MN336501.1). This replication independently pulls **the deposited plasmid sequence** and re-tests the structural claims: size, %GC, presence of blaIMP-56/blaOXA-2/aadA1 class-1 integron cassette, mer operon, MOBP11 relaxase, and RepA phylogenetic clustering with the five sibling plasmids.
