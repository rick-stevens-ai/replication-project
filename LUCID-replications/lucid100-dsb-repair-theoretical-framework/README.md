## What the paper does

Builds a minimal stochastic chemistry model of γH2AX-pATM dynamics at a single DSB site, derives mean-field ODEs by moment closure, fits 6 rate constants per cell line (MCF7 and MDA-MB-468) to time-resolved γH2AX foci + DSB counts after 4 Gy γ-irradiation, then extends the model to study two perturbations:

1. **Case study 1:** anti-γH2AX-TAT antibody binding to γH2AX. Prediction (validated experimentally via neutral comet): DSB kinetics are *not* perturbed by the antibody — the image tracer principle holds.
2. **Case study 2:** ¹¹¹In-labelled anti-γH2AX-TAT, with Auger electrons inducing additional DSBs. Prediction: DSB persistence (AUC) rises monotonically with specific activity R, qualitatively matching the inverse correlation between R and clonogenic survival.

## What this folder is

A first-pass artifact harvest plus a minimal local reimplementation of all three model variants:

- **Mean-field ODE (eq 2.5)** for the base model.
- **Antibody extension (eq 4.1)** for case study 1.
- **Auger extension (eqs 4.3–4.4)** for case study 2.
- **Tau-leap stochastic master-equation simulator** for the proportionality claim in §3.3 / Fig 5.

Uses Table 1 fitted parameters and Table 2 scaling constants verbatim. No new fitting (no public experimental time series).
