\begin{abstract}
We independently reproduce as much of Janicki et al.\ (2025) as the public artifacts support. The two SPPARKS branches referenced by the paper (\texttt{tdjanic-snl/spparks}, branches \texttt{nonorth} and \texttt{resveratrol}) are live on GitHub. The \texttt{resveratrol} branch compiles cleanly on a Linux workstation with system mpicxx and \texttt{-std=c++17}. The infrastructure claims (HCP lattice, hex region, 3D random deposition mode) build and run at paper-scale ($48{\times}16{\times}24$ HCP unit cells, $\approx$36{,}864 sites) with the paper's parameters ($T=0.0270$ eV/$k_\text{B}$, $\nu=0.1$ s$^{-1}$, capture 5.0~\AA, coord [1,9], tree solver). However, the paper's headline software addition --- the \texttt{diffusion/disphere} app style and the \texttt{resv} lattice style described in Section~2.2 --- \emph{are not actually present} in the public \texttt{resveratrol} branch as of commit \texttt{f6bcc3b} (2025-03-31). The 74-point DFT binding-energy library and the input scripts promised in ``Supplemental Materials'' are behind a captcha-blocked IOP link. A 10-seed control sweep with an isotropic monotonic Arrhenius ladder produces distinctly more isotropic aspect ratios ($W$:$L=0.53\pm0.03$, $H$:$L=0.87\pm0.04$) than the paper's experimental peaks ($W$:$L\!\approx\!0.35$, $H$:$L\!\approx\!0.55$) --- consistent with the paper's central claim that anisotropy requires the bound-sphere logic, but not itself a positive test of Figure~7. Verdict: \textbf{PARTIAL}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper summary}

\subsection{Method as described}

The paper introduces two enhancements to the Sandia SPPARKS on-lattice KMC package:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Non-orthogonal (HCP / hex) simulation boxes.} A new \texttt{lattice HCP a} style, a new \texttt{region hex \dots} region style, and a new 3D-random deposition mode of \texttt{deposition event \dots} in which passing incident vector $(0,0,0)$ replaces the default top-face restriction with random sampling of candidate deposition points anywhere in the box.
  \item \textbf{Bound-sphere disphere app.} A new \texttt{diffusion/disphere} app style and a new \texttt{lattice resv} lattice style in which each resveratrol molecule is represented by two hard spheres bound to a nearest-neighbor pair. Coordination-indexed binding energies (\texttt{ecoord}) become 2D-indexed to account for the two halves of the monomer with different OH substituents.
\end{enumerate}

The rate expression is the standard Arrhenius form,
\begin{equation}
\Gamma(i) = \nu \exp\!\left[\frac{E_\text{bind}(i)}{k_\text{B} T}\right],
\end{equation}
with $E_\text{bind}(i)$ tabulated by coordination and $\nu$ chosen from experimental precursor flow/concentration.

The enhancements are applied to $trans$-resveratrol (P2$_1$/c) with a 74-entry DFT binding-energy library (FHI-aims, PBE + Tkatchenko-Scheffler vdW, tier-1 basis, ``light'' settings, $3\!\times\!3\!\times\!1$ k-mesh, $>$20~\AA{} vacuum, bottom layer(s) fixed). Production simulations use a $48\!\times\!16\!\times\!24$-unit-cell HCP box ($\approx 200\!\times\!250\!\times\!210$~nm) at $T=20\,^\circ$C ($k_\text{B} T=0.0270$~eV), $\nu=0.1$~s$^{-1}$, capture 5.0~\AA, coord $[1,9]$, \texttt{tree} solver, $5\!\times\!10^6$ trial steps. 5 initial-nucleus geometries $\times$ 112 seeds = 560 replicates.

\subsection{Experimental validation as described}

2\,g resveratrol dissolved in 20\,g 2:1 ethanol/water at 70\,$^\circ$C for 24\,h, cooled to 20\,$^\circ$C for 5 days. Micro-CT (Bruker SKYSCAN 1272, 0.45\,$\mu$m) with Dragonfly~2024.1 watershed segmentation into 1207 crystals. Aspect-ratio comparison of $H\!:\!L$ and $W\!:\!L$ histograms (Figure~7). Paper reports ``peaks overlap well''.

\section{Method used in this replication}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Fetch.} \verb|curl https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/2583708 -o paper.pdf| on uicgpu (1{,}304{,}946 B). scp back.
  \item \textbf{Extract.} \verb|pdftotext -layout| (poppler 26.06.0) $\to$ \verb|work/paper_layout.txt|; publisher HTML scrape for table structure. Marker/Nougat not available on uicgpu; produced Marker-flavored (\verb|extraction/marker.md|) and Nougat-flavored (\verb|extraction/nougat.mmd|) extractions from the pdftotext+HTML output, with a stated fidelity caveat.
  \item \textbf{Discover public code.} GitHub API $\to$ branches \texttt{master}, \texttt{nonorth}, \texttt{resveratrol}. Cloned all three.
  \item \textbf{Diff.} \verb|git diff master resveratrol -- src/| shows 31 files changed, +577/$-$133 lines. Additions: \texttt{lattice.cpp} (HCP style only), \texttt{region\_hex.\{cpp,h\}} (new), \texttt{create\_sites.cpp} (HCP + 3D deposition), \texttt{app\_diffusion.cpp} (3D random deposition mode). \emph{No} disphere app. \emph{No} resv lattice.
  \item \textbf{Build.} Copied \texttt{spparks-resv/} to uicgpu, wrote \texttt{src/MAKE/Makefile.uic} (mpicxx wrapper, \texttt{-std=c++17 -O2}, no libjpeg). \verb|make uic -j 8|: clean; produced \verb|spk_uic| (895\,KB). SPPARKS reports version banner ``27 Nov 2024''.
  \item \textbf{Smoke.} \texttt{app\_style diffusion nonlinear hop} + \texttt{lattice hcp 1.0} + \texttt{region mybox hex 0 8 0 8 0 4} + 3D-random deposition. Passes.
  \item \textbf{Paper-scale sweep.} 10 seeds, box \texttt{hex 0 48 0 16 0 24} (36{,}864 sites, box $56 \times 13.86 \times 39.19$ with xy-tilt 8, matching primitive HCP for a $48\!\times\!16\!\times\!24$ cell replication). Nucleus \texttt{block 20 28 6 10 10 14}. $T=0.0270$, $\nu=0.1$, capture 5.0, coord [1,9], \texttt{tree} solver, 2000 KMC time-units. Isotropic monotonic ecoord ladder $n \to -0.2n$ eV for $n\in[1,12]$ (surrogate, because the paper's 74-entry DFT library is not accessible).
  \item \textbf{Analyze.} Python parses the final-frame dump, extracts positions of \texttt{OCCUPIED} (\texttt{i1==2}) sites, computes axis spans, sorts to give $L$ (largest), $M$ (middle), $S$ (smallest), then $W\!:\!L = S/L$, $H\!:\!L = M/L$.
\end{enumerate}

Paper Figure~7 (visual read of PDF): experimental peaks near $W\!:\!L \approx 0.30$--$0.40$ and $H\!:\!L \approx 0.50$--$0.60$. Our isotropic-surrogate is at $W\!:\!L=0.53$, $H\!:\!L=0.87$ --- both distinctly more isotropic (higher $W\!:\!L$, higher $H\!:\!L$) than the experimental peaks. This is exactly the behaviour the paper predicts should happen without the bound-sphere anisotropy logic. So the result is \emph{consistent with} the paper's central claim, but is not a positive test of Figure~7.

\subsection{Contradiction on C3}

The paper's Section~2.2 explicitly claims a \texttt{diffusion/disphere} app style and a \texttt{resv} lattice style are present in the \texttt{resveratrol} branch. Neither string appears in the source tree of the public \texttt{resveratrol} branch:

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{PARTIAL.}

\begin{itemize}
  \item C1, C2 (infrastructure): \textbf{REPLICATED}.
  \item C3 (disphere/resv software claim): \textbf{CONTRADICTED} on the current public artifact state.
  \item C4--C6 (DFT ecoord, aspect-ratio validation, nucleus-shape sensitivity): \textbf{BLOCKED} by missing supplementary and missing disphere code. Only a consistency spot-check is possible with the isotropic surrogate, and that spot-check is consistent with (but not a positive test of) the paper.
\end{itemize}

We replicate half the paper's method claim and none of the specific numerical Figure~7 comparison.

\section{Open Questions}

\begin{description}
  \item[Q1.] Ablation: how much of the reported anisotropy in Figure~7 is the bound-sphere geometric constraint per se, and how much is the 2D-indexed DFT ecoord table? Section~2.2 conflates two independent mechanisms and no ablation is reported.

  \item[Q2.] Sensitivity: does the ``arbitrarily large barrier'' imputation for un-tabulated coordinations bias the reported facet ordering toward the specific 74-point sampling geometry of the DFT library? No sensitivity study is reported.

  \item[Q3.] Unit-cell $\leftrightarrow$ nm mapping: from P2$_1$/c resveratrol cell parameters (a$\approx$7.6\,\AA, b$\approx$24.5\,\AA, c$\approx$15.9\,\AA), a $48\!\times\!16\!\times\!24$ replication is $\approx 365 \times 392 \times 382$~nm, 1.5--2$\times$ the stated $200\!\times\!250\!\times\!210$~nm. Which is correct?

  \item[Q4.] Convergence: is $5\!\times\!10^6$ trial steps per run $\times$ 560 replicates enough to converge the aspect-ratio histogram, or does the reported ``much narrower simulated distribution than experiment'' reflect under-sampling?

  \item[Q5.] Inverse problem: can an isotropic 1D ecoord ladder inverse-fit to Micro-CT aspect ratios approximately recover the DFT 2D rate table, and if so how much of the parametric machinery is compressible?
\end{description}

Each question with concrete next-steps is in \texttt{report/open\_questions.json}.
