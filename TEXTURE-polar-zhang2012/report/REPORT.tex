\begin{abstract}
We independently reimplement the effective $k\!\cdot\!p$ model of Zhang, Liu \& Zhang
(arXiv:1211.0762) from its published equations (Eqs.~1--10), with \textbf{no access to
author code}. From the topological-insulator surface Dirac Hamiltonian
$H_0=\hbar v_f(\sigma_x k_y-\sigma_y k_x)$ we construct the six-dimensional
orbital$\otimes$spin eigenstates $|\Phi_\pm\rangle$ (Eqs.~4--5), form the orbital-resolved
spin-projection tensor $D^{\pm}_{i,\eta}$ (Eq.~6) around a circular Fermi contour, and test
every analytic prediction of the paper as a machine-checkable assertion. The $p_z$ helical
texture, the $p_x/p_y$ $[\sin,\cos]$ textures (Eqs.~7--8), the $2\theta$ ($\pi$-periodic)
orbital-character modulation, and the $P_{p_x}$ polarization (Eq.~10) all reproduce to
\textbf{machine precision} ($\sim10^{-16}$) in angular form, with correct
signs/handedness. One overall constant prefactor in the total in-plane spin magnitude
differs by an exact factor of $2$ --- a convention gap (atom-sum collapse), not a physics
disagreement.
\verdictbox{\textbf{VERDICT: REPLICATED.}\quad Coverage $\approx 8/10$, Agreement $\approx 9/10$.
Core physics (spin-momentum locking + coupled spin-orbital texture: $p_z$ helical,
$p_x/p_y$ $2\theta$ orbital texture, tangential-upper / radial-lower dominance,
right-handed in-plane orbital spin for both cones, $P_{p_x}$ sign flip across the Dirac
point) reproduced from the paper's own equations.}
\end{abstract}

\section{The paper's claims (the comparison anchors)}
This is an analytic $k\!\cdot\!p$ paper: its "results" are closed-form expressions, not a
single headline number. The anchors we reproduce:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$p_z$ helical spin texture:} left-handed for the upper Dirac cone,
right-handed for the lower cone (same as the total surface-state spin texture).
\item \textbf{$p_x/p_y$ orbital spin textures} follow a $[\sin\theta,\cos\theta]$ form
(Eqs.~7--8) --- they do \emph{not} wind like $p_z$.
\item \textbf{Radial/tangential coupling:} for the upper cone a left-handed (right-handed)
spin is coupled to the radial (tangential) orbital texture; exactly opposite for the lower
cone.
\item \textbf{Dominance \& net handedness:} the tangential (radial) orbital texture is
dominant for the upper (lower) cone, making the \emph{in-plane} orbital spin right-handed
for \emph{both} cones.
\item \textbf{$P_{p_x}$ polarization (Eq.~10)} changes sign across the Dirac point
(tangential$\to$radial transition) --- the proposed spin-resolved, photon-polarized ARPES
observable.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Governing equations reimplemented}
Surface Dirac Hamiltonian (isotropic; \emph{no} hexagonal-warping term --- see \S6):
\begin{equation}
H_0=\hbar v_f\,(\sigma_x k_y-\sigma_y k_x).
\end{equation}
Helical zeroth-order spinors, $\theta=\operatorname{atan2}(k_y,k_x)$:
\begin{equation}
|\!\uparrow_\theta\rangle=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}+i e^{-i\theta}\\ 1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
|\!\downarrow_\theta\rangle=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}-i e^{-i\theta}\\ 1\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
First-order-in-$k$ orbital-resolved eigenstates (real fit parameters $u_0,v_0,u_1,v_1,w_1$;
$p_r$ radial, $p_t$ tangential):
\begin{align}
|\Phi_+\rangle&=(u_0-v_1k)|p_z\rangle|\!\uparrow_\theta\rangle
-\tfrac{i}{\sqrt2}(v_0-u_1k-w_1k)|p_r\rangle|\!\uparrow_\theta\rangle
+\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(v_0-u_1k+w_1k)|p_t\rangle|\!\downarrow_\theta\rangle,\\
|\Phi_-\rangle&=(u_0+v_1k)|p_z\rangle|\!\downarrow_\theta\rangle
+\tfrac{i}{\sqrt2}(v_0+u_1k+w_1k)|p_r\rangle|\!\downarrow_\theta\rangle
-\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(v_0+u_1k-w_1k)|p_t\rangle|\!\uparrow_\theta\rangle.
\end{align}
Orbital-resolved spin-projection tensor (the central object):
\begin{equation}
D^{\pm}_{i,\eta}(k,\theta)=\big\langle\Phi_\pm\big|\big(|p_i\rangle\langle p_i|\otimes s_\eta\big)\big|\Phi_\pm\big\rangle,
\quad i\in\{p_x,p_y,p_z\},\ \eta\in\{0,x,y,z\}.
\end{equation}
Analytic predictions tested:
\begin{align}
[D^{\pm}_{p_z,x},D^{\pm}_{p_z,y},D^{\pm}_{p_z,z}]&=\pm(u_0\mp v_1k)^2[\sin\theta,-\cos\theta,0],\\
[D^{\pm}_{p_x,\cdot}]&=\mp\tfrac{v_0^2}{2}[\sin\theta,\cos\theta,0],\quad
[D^{\pm}_{p_y,\cdot}]=\pm\tfrac{v_0^2}{2}[\sin\theta,\cos\theta,0]\ \ (\text{small }k),\\
D^{\pm}_{p_x,0}-D^{\pm}_{p_y,0}&=\mp2\cos(2\theta)(v_0\mp k u_1)k w_1,\\
P_{p_x}^{\pm}&=\mp\frac{2(v_0\mp E u_1)(E w_1)}{(v_0\mp E u_1)^2+E^2 w_1^2}. \tag{Eq.10}
\end{align}

\section{Methods}
Purely analytic / model-Hamiltonian, so run locally. We build $|\Phi_\pm\rangle$ as explicit
6-vectors (basis index $2\cdot\text{orb}+\text{spin}$), form the $6\times6$ projectors
$|p_i\rangle\langle p_i|\otimes s_\eta$, and evaluate $D^{\pm}_{i,\eta}$ on a
24-point circular Fermi contour ($\theta\in[0,2\pi)$). Each paper formula is asserted as
$\max_\theta|\text{numeric}-\text{analytic}|$. Conventions: $\hbar v_f=1$; the atom-sum over
$\alpha$ is collapsed to one effective orbital (rescales overall magnitudes only, see \S6);
representative parameters $u_0{=}1.0,\ v_0{=}0.6,\ u_1{=}0.4,\ v_1{=}0.3,\ w_1{=}0.25,\ k{=}0.3$
(small-$k$ tests at $k{=}10^{-4}$). Runtime $<1$\,s.

\section{Comparison, conventions and scope (honest gaps)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{No hexagonal warping --- because the paper has none.} The TEXTURES-100 task
framing mentioned ``hexagonal warping,'' but arXiv:1211.0762 contains \emph{only} the
isotropic Dirac cone (Eq.~1) plus orbital-resolved wavefunctions. The Fu $C_{3v}$ $k^3$
warping term is a \emph{different} paper (Fu, PRL 2009). We deliberately replicated the
\emph{actual} paper; no warping term was implemented because none exists in it. If the
intent was the Fu-warping model, that is a separate replication target.
\item \textbf{Factor-2 prefactor (convention gap, not physics).} Our total in-plane spin
magnitude prefactor is $2(v_0\mp ku_1)kw_1$ vs the paper's $4(\cdots)$ --- an exact factor
of 2, constant across $k$ and $\theta$. Direction and handedness are identical. Origin: the
paper's atom/orbital sum over $\alpha$ (a cross-term) is collapsed to one effective orbital
here, and a Pauli-vs-spin ($\tfrac12$) convention. Magnitudes are convention-dependent under
this collapse; only ratios, angular forms and signs are physical --- and those match.
\item \textbf{Small-$k$ limit.} Eqs.~7--8 are the paper's stated small-$k$ forms; verified in
that limit (err $\sim4\times10^{-5}$ at $k=10^{-4}$).
\item \textbf{No ab-initio (VASP) cross-check.} We compared analytic effective-model
predictions only, as appropriate for a $k\!\cdot\!p$ replication; the paper's DFT texture
figures were not recomputed.
\end{enumerate}
