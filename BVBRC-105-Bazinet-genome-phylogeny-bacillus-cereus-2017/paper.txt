KEY CLAIMS (verifiable)
-----------------------
C1. Species delineation via a rapid k-mer approach: Mash (MinHash sketches) +
    FastME distance tree was used to decide which species belong to
    B. cereus sensu lato (s.l.).
C2. Pan-genome (built with Prokka annotation + Roary clustering) of B. cereus
    s.l. consists of ~60,000 genes.
C3. Core genome is ~600 genes (present in >=99% of taxa sampled).
C4. The pan-genome is OPEN (grows as genomes are added); accessory (pan) genes
    improve phylogenetic support (bootstrap) over core-only analyses.
C5. Phylogenies recapitulate two prior classification systems; taxa consistently
    assigned to the same major clade/group; B. anthracis nests within the group
    (very closely related to certain B. cereus / B. thuringiensis).
C6. Bayesian population genetics (hierBAPS) recommends 9 clusters within the
    three major clades.

Tools used by the paper: Mash, FastME, Prokka, Roary, Scoary, HaMStR, RAxML,
DendroPy, ClonalFrameML, PAUP*, SplitsTree, hierBAPS, GSI.

WHAT WE REPLICATE (method level, small public surrogate, free tools)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Full 498-genome analysis is out of scope on a laptop, but the CORE METHODS are
reproducible on a small surrogate set of complete B. cereus s.l. genomes
downloaded from NCBI RefSeq (+ outgroups):
 - C1: a from-scratch Mash MinHash (k=21, sketch=1000) genome-distance matrix
   + NJ tree (surrogate for Mash+FastME) to test species clustering & that
   B. anthracis/B. thuringiensis are nearest neighbours and outgroups separate.
 - C2/C3/C4: a Prokka(=PGAP proteins)+Roary(=CD-HIT 95% identity) pan/core
   genome estimate on the B. cereus s.l. genomes: pan size, core size,
   core fraction, and an accumulation curve testing the OPEN pan-genome claim.
