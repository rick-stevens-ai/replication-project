# Method Extract — Fukami/Sato et al. 2026 (arXiv:2605.18009)
**Title:** Multi-rotational switching in a noncollinear antiferromagnet by spin-orbit torque
**Texture class:** spin (noncollinear AFM order — Mn3Sn-type triangular spin texture)

- **Core physics:** Spin-orbit-torque (SOT) driven switching of a *noncollinear antiferromagnet* nanodot. The 120°-ordered AFM order parameter can undergo **multiple full rotations** before reversal completes ("multi-rotational switching"), rather than a single 180° flip.
- **Headline claim (replication target):** A **threshold current density that is nearly independent of pulse duration** in "switch-back" events — an unconventional insensitivity that is the experimental fingerprint of the multi-rotational process.
- **Mechanism (3 ingredients):** (1) current-driven coherent rotation of the noncollinear AFM order, (2) field-induced reorientation of the small uncompensated net magnetization, (3) thermal fluctuations selecting the final state.
- **Key equations:** Macrospin / generalized **Landau-Lifshitz-Gilbert (LLG) equation for the AFM order parameter** with an SOT (spin-Hall/damping-like) term + Zeeman coupling of the uncompensated moment + a Langevin thermal field. Rotation angle vs pulse-duration/amplitude phase diagram is the reproducible object.
- **Reproducible figure:** threshold-current-vs-pulse-duration curve showing plateau; count of rotations vs current amplitude.
- **DFT-heavy or theory?** **THEORY / MODEL — tractable in-process.** The theoretical analysis is a low-dimensional macrospin/LLG ODE (a few coupled angles) with stochastic noise. No DFT needed to reproduce the *theory* claim; the experiment itself is not replicable but the dynamical model is. Good candidate for a numerical LLG replication now.
- **Caveat:** paper is experiment-forward; replication should target the *theory section's* dynamical model, not the fabrication/measurement.
