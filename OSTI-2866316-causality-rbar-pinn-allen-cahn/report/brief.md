# Brief

**Paper:** Wang, Wong, Ruan, Goswami (2026). *Causality-Respecting Adaptive Refinement for PINNs: Enabling Precise Interface Evolution in Phase Field Modeling.* OSTI 2866316 / arXiv:2410.20212 / J. Big-Data Analytics in Transportation-adjacent venue, DOI 10.1007/s44379-026-00058-y.

**What/why:** The paper's core algorithmic contribution is a *causality-training* scheme for physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) — Eqs. (9)–(11), with causal weights ω_k = exp(−ε Σ_{m<k} L_r(t_m)) and ε=10 — combined with residual-based adaptive refinement (RBAR), applied to Allen-Cahn phase-field dynamics. We independently reimplement the causality-training mechanism in PyTorch and test it on the canonical 1-D Allen-Cahn PINN benchmark (u_t = 1e-4 u_xx + 5u − 5u³, u(0,x)=x²cos(πx), periodic BC) — the exact testbed introduced by the causality paper this work builds on (its ref [18], Wang–Sankaran–Perdikaris 2022) and for which a spectral reference solution is computable without proprietary COMSOL data. We verify the paper's central claim that causality training corrects the erroneous convergence of vanilla PINNs on Allen-Cahn, yielding a large relative-L2 error reduction against a ground-truth spectral solution.
