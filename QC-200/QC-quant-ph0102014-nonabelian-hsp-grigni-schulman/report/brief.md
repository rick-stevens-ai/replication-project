# Brief

Independent replication of **Ivanyos, Magniez, Santha (2001)**, *Efficient quantum algorithms for some instances of the non-Abelian hidden subgroup problem*, arXiv:quant-ph/0102014. (The task ticket originally attributed this arXiv id to Grigni/Schulman/Vazirani/Vazirani, but the actual paper at that identifier is Ivanyos–Magniez–Santha; the GSV V paper appears as its reference [12].) The paper is pure math with theorem-proof-only content and no numerical experiments to check. We independently implemented two of its core constructive claims — **Lemma 9** (Abelian HSP under a genuine quantum-state oracle) and **Theorem 13** (non-Abelian HSP in groups with an elementary Abelian normal 2-subgroup and small quotient, via reduction to Abelian HSP on Z₂ × N) — in Qiskit statevector on the wreath product G = Z₂ᵏ ⋊ Z₂ for k = 1..4 (|G| up to 512). All 24 planted-subgroup instances of Theorem 13 recovered the hidden subgroup correctly; Lemma 9's marginal distribution matched uniform-on-H⊥ to machine precision (~10⁻¹⁶) across 20 random instances up to n = 5 bits. LLM-judge (argo:gpt-5) verdict: **PARTIAL** — the paper's other core claims (Thm 8 for solvable/permutation groups, Thm 11 for small-commutator groups, Cor 12 for extra-special p-groups) rely on much heavier classical group-theory machinery and were not independently re-run in this pass.
