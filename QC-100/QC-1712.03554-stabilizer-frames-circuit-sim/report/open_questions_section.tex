\section{Open questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Behavior on magic-state distillation circuits.} Does the simulator
retain exact $\chi = 2^t$ scaling and machine-precision fidelity on structured
distillation protocols (e.g.\ 15-to-1, Bravyi--Haah), where $T$-gates are
correlated on ancilla registers and Clifford postselection may (or may not)
collapse $\chi$?
\emph{Next:} encode a 15-to-1 distillation subroutine ($n=16$, $t=15$),
run the frame simulator, and measure whether syndrome postselection reduces
$\chi$ below $2^{15}$; validate distilled-state fidelity vs
$1 - 35 p^3$. Free-endpoint on m1/uicgpu.

\item \textbf{Hybrid frame + tensor-network encoding.} Can each frame branch
be stored as an MPS rather than a Clifford-prefix, giving cost
$\mathrm{poly}(n)\cdot\mathrm{poly}(\chi_{\text{MPS}})\cdot 2^{t_{\text{local}}}$
and extending the practical $T$-count regime past 20?
\emph{Next:} prototype an MPS-per-branch backend (quimb or Qiskit MPS)
and compare peak memory + wall-clock against pure-frame on QFT$_n$ at
$n=16$, $t=20$; verify MPS truncation error stays $<\epsilon_{\text{machine}}$.

\item \textbf{GPU acceleration of per-branch statevector render.} Does
batched-branch execution on GPU (e.g.\ qiskit-aer + cuQuantum) shift the
observed $\sim 2.35\times$-per-$T$-gate runtime constant closer to the
theoretical $2\times$?
\emph{Next:} port the per-branch render to \texttt{AerSimulator(device='GPU')}
with batching; benchmark on uicgpu A100 (free) at $n=20$, $t\in\{8,10,12\}$;
report whether $t=15\text{--}16$ becomes seconds-scale.

\item \textbf{Integration with noisy magic states.} Under a noise-tracking
extension of the frame (each $T$ carries a small amplitude-scaled error
branch), can amplitude-thresholded pruning yield bounded-error simulation
at reduced $\chi$?
\emph{Next:} add a $T_{\text{noisy}}(p)$ primitive with pruning threshold
$\epsilon$; on $t=20$, $p=10^{-3}$, $\epsilon=10^{-6}$ measure output-error
bound + effective $\chi$; cross-check against Bravyi--Smith--Smolin
stabilizer-rank simulator.

\item \textbf{Application to near-Clifford VQE ansatze.} On a variational
ansatz engineered to be near-Clifford (few non-Clifford single-qubit
rotations), can the frame simulator classically evaluate
$\langle\psi|H|\psi\rangle$ competitively vs Qiskit statevector VQE?
\emph{Next:} encode a 12-qubit H$_2$ or LiH UCCSD ansatz truncated to
$t<12$ non-Clifford rotations; run frame-based expectation-value evaluation
in a classical optimizer loop; compare converged energy vs PySCF FCI
(free) and report wall-clock per gradient step.
\end{enumerate}
