# Brief

Independent replication of Huntul & Hussein (2021), *Simultaneous Identification of Thermal Conductivity and Heat Source in the Heat Equation* (Iraqi Journal of Science 62(6):1968–1978, DOI 10.24996/ijs.2021.62.6.22). The paper's forward Crank–Nicolson FDM solver was reimplemented in Python (numpy/scipy) and its Tikhonov-regularized non-linear least-squares inverse problem was reproduced with `scipy.optimize.least_squares` (the free equivalent of MATLAB's `lsqnonlin`). The forward solver was validated to reproduce the paper's Table 1 direct-problem RMSE values (agreement within ~5%). The inverse solver was then run across 14 test cases (Examples 1 & 2, noise levels p ∈ {0, 0.1%, 1%}, regularization parameters β ∈ {0, 1e-3, 1e-2, 1e-1}); all recovered rmse(a) and rmse(f) values are within a factor of ~2 of the paper's Table 2, with the qualitative story confirmed: noiseless recovery is very accurate, noisy data without regularization blows up, and Tikhonov regularization with the paper's recommended β stabilises the solution. **Verdict: PARTIAL.**
