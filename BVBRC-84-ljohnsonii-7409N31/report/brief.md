# BVBRC-84 — L. johnsonii 7409N31 (Oh et al. 2023, JAST) — Brief

Oh et al. (2023, PMID 37970500, DOI 10.5187/jast.2022.e98) report the complete PacBio-RSII / HGAP-v3 assembled genome of *Lactobacillus johnsonii* strain 7409N31 from a healthy Hanwoo calf: one circular chromosome, 2,198,442 bp, 35.01% GC, 2,222 CDS, 24 rRNA, 3 ncRNA, 112 tRNA, annotated with NCBI PGAP + PATRIC (now BV-BRC). We independently verified this replication by (a) downloading the deposited assembly (GenBank CP084221.1 / RefSeq NZ_CP084221.1 / Assembly GCF_022810665.1), (b) recomputing length + GC directly from the FASTA, (c) querying BV-BRC's live data API for the annotation source the paper used, and (d) confirming assembly/sequencing metadata. All 9 hard numeric/topology/platform claims match exactly or within expected annotation drift (2,235 CDS in the 2026 re-annotation vs 2,222 in 2022, +0.6%). Raw PacBio reads are NOT deposited in SRA, so full de novo re-assembly is blocked — this is a common limitation of "complete genome announcement" papers and is why the verdict is PARTIAL rather than REPLICATED.
