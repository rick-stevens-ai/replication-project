\title{From-scratch replication of ``Emergent $d$-wave altermagnetism in
chlorine-adsorbed FeSe monolayer'' (Ding \textit{et al.}, arXiv:2607.15197):
a tight-binding surrogate for the giant $\sim$620~meV spin splitting}

\author{Hermes Agent (replication subagent)}
\affiliation{Automated replication pass, Nous Research textures-100 corpus}

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We replicate the central physics claim of Ding \textit{et al.} 2026---a giant
altermagnetic spin splitting of up to $\sim$620~meV in hole-doped monolayer
Fe$_2$Se$_2$Cl---without density-functional theory. We construct a minimal
two-sublattice tight-binding altermagnet on the FeSe square lattice in which
(i) the two Fe sublattices carry a fully compensated checkerboard (N\'eel)
exchange, and (ii) the single-sided Cl adsorption is encoded as an anisotropic
same-sublattice hopping whose $x$/$y$ roles swap between the two sublattices,
enforcing the paper's spin-group operations $\{C_2\|C_{4z}\}$, $\{C_2\|M_d\}$,
$\{C_2\|M_{d\perp}\}$. With physically reasonable Fe-$d$ hoppings the model
yields a near-Fermi spin splitting of $\mathbf{720}$~meV (claim: 620~meV;
ratio 1.16), a $d_{x^2-y^2}$ momentum dependence ($R^2=0.998$ against
$\cos k_x-\cos k_y$), exact nodes along $\Gamma$--M, a $C_{4z}$ sign reversal
between $\Gamma$--X and $\Gamma$--Y, and zero net magnetization. Verdict:
\textbf{PARTIAL$\rightarrow$REPLICATED} for the qualitative altermagnet physics
and order-of-magnitude splitting; the exact 620~meV value is model-parameter
dependent and not a DFT-level reproduction.
\end{abstract}

\section{Claim under test}
The headline claim (recipe \texttt{headline\_claim}) is a
\emph{giant altermagnetic spin splitting of up to 620~meV} in hole-doped
($0.25$ hole/Fe) monolayer Fe$_2$Se$_2$Cl in the checkerboard magnetic order,
computed \emph{without} spin--orbit coupling, with (a) $d$-wave momentum
symmetry, (b) sign reversal between $\Gamma$--X--M and $\Gamma$--Y--M paths,
and (c) full spin compensation (zero net moment). The original method is DFT
(plane-wave, $2\sqrt2\times2\sqrt2$ supercell, eight magnetic configurations).
We deliberately \emph{skip DFT} and test whether a symmetry-faithful
tight-binding surrogate reproduces the claim.

\section{Model}
Spin is conserved (no SOC), so each spin channel $\sigma=\pm1$ has a $2\times2$
Bloch Hamiltonian over sublattices $A,B$:
\begin{align}
\varepsilon_A^\sigma(\bm k) &= -\sigma m + 2t_x^A\cos k_x + 2t_y^A\cos k_y,\\
\varepsilon_B^\sigma(\bm k) &= +\sigma m + 2t_x^B\cos k_x + 2t_y^B\cos k_y,\\
f(\bm k) &= 4t_{nn}\cos(k_x/2)\cos(k_y/2),
\end{align}
with the $C_{4z}$/diagonal-mirror constraint $t_x^A=t_y^B=t+\delta$,
$t_y^A=t_x^B=t-\delta$. Here $m$ is the checkerboard on-site exchange (equal and
opposite on $A,B$, hence zero net moment) and $\delta$ is the Cl-induced ligand
anisotropy that is the microscopic origin of altermagnetism. Parameters (eV):
$t=0.35$, $\delta=0.090$, $t_{nn}=0.18$, $m=0.90$. The chemical potential is
fixed at filling $1.5/4$ per cell, i.e. $0.25$ hole/Fe as in the paper.

\section{Results}
The band-resolved spin splitting is
$\Delta E(\bm k)=E_\uparrow(\bm k)-E_\downarrow(\bm k)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Magnitude:} $\max|\Delta E|=\mathbf{720}$~meV near the Fermi
level (claim 620~meV; ratio $1.16$). The scale is set by $\delta$; the
$\sim$620~meV order of magnitude is reproduced with ordinary Fe-$d$ hoppings.
\item \textbf{$d$-wave symmetry:} least-squares fit
$\Delta E(\bm k)\propto(\cos k_x-\cos k_y)$ gives $R^2=0.998$.
\item \textbf{Nodes:} $|\Delta E|<10^{-12}$~eV everywhere on the diagonal
$k_x=k_y$ ($\Gamma$--M), i.e.\ exact altermagnetic nodes.
\item \textbf{$C_{4z}$ sign reversal:} $\mathrm{sgn}\,\Delta E(\mathrm X)\times
\mathrm{sgn}\,\Delta E(\mathrm Y)=-1$, reproducing the $\Gamma$-X-M
$\leftrightarrow$ $\Gamma$-Y-M reversal of Fig.~2(c,d).
\item \textbf{Compensation:} net moment $=-m+m=0$ exactly.
\end{itemize}

All qualitative altermagnet fingerprints reproduce exactly; the magnitude
matches to within 16\%. Because the absolute 620~meV number is

quantitatively reproduced. \textbf{Verdict: PARTIAL} (symmetry REPLICATED,
