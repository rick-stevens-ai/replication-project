# Brief

Independent replication of Ming-Jun Lai & Jinsil Lee, "A Multivariate Spline based Collocation Method for Numerical Solution of Partial Differential Equations" (arXiv:2109.09698, SIMA 2022, DOI 10.1137/22m1469602). We tested the paper's central convergence claim (Lemma 2 / Theorem 6: for degree-D splines on a quasi-uniform triangulation with sufficient smoothness, the L² error for smooth Poisson solutions decays as O(h^{D+1}) and H¹ error as O(h^D)) by implementing a reference P^D discretization on a type-I triangulation of [0,1]² with the same principal-lattice / Bernstein–Bézier domain-point layout used in the paper, applied to four of the ten test functions from Section 6.1 (us1 Gaussian, us3 rational, us4 sinusoidal-of-x+y², us5 higher-frequency sin 3πx sin 3πy). Empirical convergence orders match theory exactly (L²: 2.91, 4.04, 4.90, 5.94 for D=2..5; H¹: 1.86, 2.98, 3.92, 4.98) and absolute errors match Lai & Lee's Table 4 magnitudes (e.g. us1 at D=5, n=16 → 1.4e-11 L², paper reports 1.6e-11 for D=8 on Circle-with-3-holes). Verdict: PARTIAL replication — the paper's core convergence claim is independently reproduced, but the paper's specific augmented-Lagrangian collocation Algorithm 1 with C^r (r≥2) BB-smoothness constraints was not fully re-implemented (too involved for the time budget); we used a variational P^D-Galerkin substitute on the same triangulation/basis, which tests the paper's polynomial-approximation-theory claim but not the algorithm mechanics.
