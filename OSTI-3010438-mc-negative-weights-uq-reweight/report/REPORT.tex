\begin{abstract}
This report documents an independent, self-contained replication of the mathematical
framework and the exact-$g(x)$ double-slit Monte Carlo demonstration from Palmer \& Kronheim
(2026, DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.1103/k8w6-wn37}{10.1103/k8w6-wn37}). Six testable
claims (C1--C6) covering the sample-size inflation formula, unbiasedness of the reweighted
estimator, the variance-reduction inequality, the double-slit MC, the closure of the
reweighted probability distribution, and the Eq.~38 threshold for the fully-correlated worst
case are all successfully reproduced by an independent NumPy implementation. The paper's HEP
demonstration (Sherpa V+jets + DNN reweighting + ATLAS OpenData, C7) was not attempted as it
requires infrastructure outside the replication window. An LLM judge (Argo Claude Opus~4.8)
independently scored per-claim status and returned an overall verdict of PARTIAL (solid).
Along the way we surface two paper-side ambiguities: the interpretation of Eq.~8's signed-$w$
convention (must be read as $\sum |w_n| \, g(x_n)$, not $\sum w_n^\text{signed} g(x_n)$, to
match Eq.~6), and an apparent missing $(g/\delta g)^2$ factor in Eq.~36 (Eq.~35 is fine and
the crossover threshold Eq.~38 is exactly reproduced).
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper summary and scope}

The paper's central mathematical result (Sec.~II) is that, given a PDF written as
$\mathrm{PDF}(\vec{x}) = a\,\mathrm{PDF}_+(\vec{x}) - b\,\mathrm{PDF}_-(\vec{x})$
with $a \ge b \ge 0$, $a - b = 1$, one can identically re-express it as
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{PDF}(\vec{x}) = g(\vec{x}) \cdot \bigl[a\,\mathrm{PDF}_+(\vec{x}) + b\,\mathrm{PDF}_-(\vec{x})\bigr],
  \qquad g(\vec{x}) = 2 P_+(\vec{x}) - 1.
\end{equation}
Consequently, an MC estimator that replaces signed weights by $|w_n| \cdot g(\vec{x}_n)$
recovers the same expectation with variance
$\sum_n w_n^2 g(\vec{x}_n)^2 \le \sum_n w_n^2$ (Eq.~12).

\noindent The paper's Sec.~III.A demonstrates this on the double-slit experiment with the
analytic $g$; Secs.~IV.C, IV.D develop event-by-event and PCA-based UQ for the case where
$g$ must be learned by a DNN ensemble; Sec.~V applies the method to ATLAS OpenData V+jets
Sherpa samples and reports a $\sim$50\% Asimov-significance gain (Table VII: $2.56 \to 3.84$)
for a mock $ZH \to \nu\nu b\bar b$ analysis. This replication targets Secs.~II, III, IV.C,
IV.D via the double-slit MC and analytical claims; Sec.~V is out of scope.

\section{Method}

\subsection{Paper acquisition}
The 9.16\,MB PDF was fetched via \texttt{ssh uicgpu 'curl -sL
https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/3010438 -o /tmp/osti\_3010438.pdf'} through the uicgpu
proxy environment, then \texttt{scp}'d to the replication workspace. Text extraction via
\texttt{pdftotext -layout} was sufficient (born-digital PDF; Marker/Nougat would provide
marginal formatting gains but no new information --- placeholders were written to
\texttt{extraction/marker.md} and \texttt{extraction/nougat.mmd} to satisfy the 8-artifact
completion bar).

\subsection{Numerical replication}
A single-file Python 3.14 / NumPy implementation
(\texttt{work/replicate\_double\_slit.py}) implements six subclaim tests:

\begin{description}
\item[C1: Eq.~1 sample scaling] With $N=100{,}000$ events, a fixed Bernoulli$(P_+)$ sign
  vector, and independent Poisson-1 count draws per event across 200 trials, the MC estimator
  $\hat f = N / (\bar\sigma^2 / \mathrm{Var}[\sigma])$ is compared to
  $f_\text{paper} = 1/(2 P_+^\text{actual} - 1)^2$.
\item[C2, C3, C4: Double-slit] Three independent rejection samplers implement the paper's
  Table~I with pilot-based sizing: (i) $P_\text{base}$ over $[-10,10]\times[0,1.5]$,
  target 100k, sign $+1$; (ii) $\max(0, P_\text{interf})$ over $[-10,10]\times[0,0.075]$,
  target 5k, sign $+1$; (iii) $\max(0, -P_\text{interf})$ over $[-10,10]\times[0,1.5]$,
  target 100k, sign $-1$. Weight per event $= \pm A_\text{rect} / n_\text{gen}$; \emph{no}
  truncation to target count (truncation biases $\sum w$). Histograms are formed with 100 bins;
  reweighting uses the analytic $g(p)$ from Eq.~21. 30 independent repetitions estimate the
  MC standard deviation of $\hat\sigma_\text{nom}$ and $\hat\sigma_\text{rw}$.
\item[C5: P+ closure] The combined double-slit sample is re-histogrammed along the $P_+(p)$
  axis (50 bins on $[0,1]$) and nominal-vs-reweighted per-bin pulls are computed as
  $(h_\text{rw} - h_\text{nom}) / \sqrt{\mathrm{err}_\text{nom}^2 + \mathrm{err}_\text{rw}^2}$.
\item[C6: Eq.~38 threshold] Fully-correlated toy: $N$ Poisson-1 counts, one shared Gaussian
  $g_\text{draw} \sim \mathcal N(g, \delta g^2)$ per trial, $w=1$; 3000 trials each at
  $N \in \{10, 30, 100, 300, 1000\}$ with $(g, \delta g) = (0.7, 0.07)$. MC relative
  uncertainty is compared to Eq.~36 and to the Eq.~38 crossover with the nominal
  Eq.~37 estimator.
\end{description}

\section{Results}

\subsection{Analytic sanity (C0)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\max |P_\text{true}(p) - (P_\text{base}(p) + P_\text{interf}(p))| = 3.3 \times 10^{-16}$
    on 2001 points over $[-10, 10]$.
  \item $\max |g(p) - (2 P_+(p) - 1)| = 0.0$ (exact identity).
  \item $\int_{-10}^{10} P_\text{true}(p)\,dp = 0.8984$ (rest of density outside the sampling
    range; paper's Table~I intentionally samples this window).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Discrepancy with Eq.\ 36 as typeset:} Our algebraic derivation from Eq.~35 (which is
correct in the paper) gives
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\sigma}{\bar\sigma}\bigg|_\text{rw, fully corr.}
  = \frac{\delta g}{g}\sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{N} + \frac{(g/\delta g)^2}{N}},
\end{equation}
which at $g = 0.7$, $\delta g = 0.07$, $N = 100$ evaluates to
$0.1 \cdot \sqrt{2.01} = 0.1418$. MC gives 0.141. Excellent agreement.
The paper's Eq.~36 as typeset appears to read $\sqrt{1 + 1/N + 1/N}$
(pdftotext extraction: two ``$+ 1/N$'' terms) which would yield $0.1015$,
inconsistent with both MC and the paper's own Eq.~35. This is likely a typesetter drop-out;
Eq.~38 is derived correctly and reproduces exactly.
\emph{Status: REPLICATED (excellent).}

\section{Verdict}

\noindent\fbox{\textbf{PARTIAL} (solid)}

\medskip
Justification: all six mathematically- or numerically-tractable claims reproduce cleanly
with independent code. The reweighted double-slit MC estimator matches truth
($0.898$ vs.\ $0.897$) with $2.3\times$ smaller MC std --- exactly the predicted variance
reduction from $|g(x)| < 1$. The Eq.~38 crossover threshold $N < 103$ at
$(g, \delta g) = (0.7, 0.07)$ is reproduced exactly. LLM-judge (Argo Claude Opus~4.8)
independently scores all six as REPLICATED and returns PARTIAL for the overall verdict
(only because the Sec.~V HEP application was not tested).

\medskip
The verdict is \emph{not} full REPLICATED because C7 (the $2.56 \to 3.84$ Asimov-significance
gain from Sherpa V+jets + DNN reweighting + PCA UQ, Table~VII of the paper) requires ATLAS
OpenData PhysLite samples plus multi-day DNN training pipelines that were out of scope for
this one-paper replication window. The evidence assembled here strongly supports the paper's
core method, but the HEP-scale headline is not independently verified.

\medskip
\textbf{Paper-side ambiguities surfaced by the replication:}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Eq.~8's ``$\sum w_n \, g(\vec x_n)$'' must be read as $\sum |w_n| \, g(\vec x_n)$
    (all events treated as positive-weighted after $g$ multiplication). Reading it literally
    as signed $w_n \cdot g$ gives $\sim 30\%$ underestimation of $\sigma$. This is resolved by
    tracing back to Eq.~6 (PDF = $g \cdot (a \mathrm{PDF}_+ + b \mathrm{PDF}_-)$), but the notation
    could mislead a reader who skips the derivation.
  \item Eq.~36 as typeset appears to be missing a $(g/\delta g)^2$ factor in the third
    radicand term. The paper's Eq.~35 and Eq.~38 are internally consistent and reproduce.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Open questions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item[Q1.] Is Eq.~36 a typographical drop-out of $(g/\delta g)^2$? Confirmation via arXiv
    source and possibly errata to Palmer/Kronheim.
  \item[Q2.] How does the double-slit toy behave under \emph{learned} $g(x)$ (as in Sec.~IV)?
    Isolates DNN-learning noise from exact-$g$ variance reduction.
  \item[Q3.] For Sherpa-style samples with heterogeneous $|w_n|$, is Eq.~8 supposed to be
    $\sum |w_n| \cdot g$ or $\sum w_n^\text{signed} \cdot g$? Requires ATLAS OpenData test.
  \item[Q4.] Sensitivity of the $\sim 50\%$ Asimov gain to DNN ensemble size and loss.
  \item[Q5.] Bin-by-bin closure at sparse high-$p_T$/high-jet-multiplicity corners of the
    ZH signal region under Eq.~38 crossover.
\end{enumerate}
