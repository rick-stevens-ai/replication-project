# Brief — BVBRC-86

Albuquerque et al. (Marine Drugs 2021, PMID 34822492) sequenced two deep-sea *Streptomyces* isolates from the Madeira Archipelago — **MA3_2.13** (sediment) and **S07_1.15** (sponge) — assembled complete/near-complete genomes with a hybrid Illumina + PacBio strategy (Unicycler), and mined their biosynthetic potential with antiSMASH 5.0. They report: (i) a 7.65 Mb single-chromosome assembly for MA3_2.13 (72.1% GC) and a 7.09 + 0.16 Mb two-contig assembly for S07_1.15 (73.2 / 69.6% GC); (ii) ANI evidence that MA3_2.13 is a **new species** (top ANI 77.9% vs SCSIO 3032) while S07_1.15 is *S. xinghaiensis* (ANI 95.83% vs S187); and (iii) antiSMASH detects **32 BGCs in MA3_2.13** (23.1% of genome, PKS-rich) and **24 BGCs in S07_1.15** (8.8% of genome, RiPP-rich), with named candidates including atratumycin-like NRPS (MA3 BGC #8), triacsins-like PKS (MA3 BGC #14) and arseno-polyketide (MA3 BGC #24). Our independent replication pulled the deposited GenBank assemblies (GCF_020740535.1 and GCF_020739505.1 under BioProject PRJNA754006), recomputed assembly stats + ANI with skani + fastANI, and re-ran antiSMASH 6.1.1 (docker) on 32-cpu uicgpu.
