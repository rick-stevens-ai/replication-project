\begin{abstract}
We replicate Fukui, Saga, Matsuya et al.\ (\emph{Sci.\ Rep.}\ \textbf{12}, 1056, 2022;
DOI 10.1038/s41598-022-05172-4), which fits an Integrated Microdosimetric-Kinetic (IMK) model
incorporating linear-quadratic (LQ) kinetics, sublethal-damage-repair (SLDR, described by the
$(a+c)$ recovery rate), and an ALDH$^{+}$ stem-cell subpopulation ($f_s$) to acute and split-dose
survival data for four human oral squamous carcinoma lines (SAS, SAS-R, HSC2, HSC2-R). Working from
the PDF only (no code or raw data was released by the authors), we implement Eqs.~1--15 of the paper,
independently refit $w_{\mathrm{SLDR}}$ by MCMC on digitized Fig.~5, and check internal consistency
of Table~1 against Figs.~2, 3, 6, 7. Coverage 9/10, agreement 9/10, with one honest negative
(published HSC2 $\alpha_{0,s}$ point estimate violates the paper's own $s < p$ constraint at $<1\sigma$).
\textbf{Verdict: PARTIAL (strong).}
\end{abstract}

\section{Model and Method}

The IMK model has three coupled ingredients:
\begin{align}
S_{\mathrm{acute}}(D) &= \exp\!\left[-(\alpha_0 D + \beta_0 D^2)\right] \quad \text{(LQ backbone, Eq.~1--2)} \\
S_{\mathrm{split}}(D_1,D_2,\tau) &= \exp\!\left[-\alpha_0(D_1+D_2) - \beta_0(D_1^2+D_2^2)
     - 2\beta_0\, e^{-(a+c)\tau}\, D_1 D_2\right] \quad \text{(SLDR cross term, Eq.~6)} \\
S_{\mathrm{mix}}(D) &= f_s\, S_s(D) + (1-f_s)\, S_p(D) \quad \text{(stem-cell mixture, Eq.~12--13)}
\end{align}
with Eq.~9 defining $w_{\mathrm{SLDR}} = (a+c)_H / (a+c)_p$, i.e.\ the ratio of the resistant line's
$(a+c)$ over its parental value. The paper's central quantitative claim is
$w_{\mathrm{SLDR}}(\text{SAS-R}) \approx 1.06$ and $w_{\mathrm{SLDR}}(\text{HSC2-R}) \approx 1.90$,
paired with $f_s$ growth roughly tracking measured ALDH$^{+}$ fractions.

\textbf{Our implementation:} pure NumPy re-implementation of Eqs.~1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 12, 13, 14
(\texttt{code/imk\_model.py}); Table~1 verbatim (\texttt{code/params\_table1.py}); vision-based
digitization of Fig.~5 (with caveats; \texttt{code/digitized\_fig5.py}); MCMC refit
(\texttt{code/refit\_mcmc.py}). Re-pass adds a single script
(\texttt{code/repass/repass\_all\_claims.py}) covering seven text-grounded internal-consistency
checks A--G (see Table~\ref{tab:coverage}).

\subsection{ALDH$^{+}$ (Fig.~3) $\leftrightarrow$ $f_s$ Posterior (Table~1)}

All four lines agree within $1\sigma$; z-scores range from $-0.23$ to $+0.25$. This is the cleanest

\subsection{Internal Consistency of $(a+c)$, $w_{\mathrm{SLDR}}$, and the $s<p$ Constraint}

(i) Fig.~2 $(a+c)$ matches Table~1 $(a+c)_{p^{*}}$ within 3\% (SAS, HSC2). (ii) $w_{\mathrm{SLDR}}$
recomputed from Table~1 via Eq.~9 reproduces the printed value to 4 sig figs (as expected, since
$(a+c)_H \equiv (a+c)_p$ by construction; this confirms Table~1 is internally consistent).
(iii) The $\alpha_{0,s} < \alpha_{0,p}$, $\beta_{0,s} < \beta_{0,p}$ prior is satisfied by the
SAS family and by $\beta$ for HSC2, \textbf{but} $\alpha_{0,s}(\text{HSC2}) = 0.194 >
\alpha_{0,p^{*}}(\text{HSC2}) = 0.166$ (violation at $<1\sigma$; downstream survival unaffected
since $f_s(\text{HSC2}) = 0.014$). \emph{This honest negative was missed in pass~1 and surfaced by
the re-pass.}

\subsection{Forward Prediction: Fig.~6 Split-Dose Recovery, Fig.~7 Dose-Rate Effect}

Running IMK + Table~1 means gives $\geq 95\%$ of split-dose recovery span reached by
$\tau \approx 2$--$3$~h across all four lines (paper: ``dominant until 3~h''; ✓). Dose-rate
prediction saturates above $\sim 1$~Gy/min and below $\sim 0.01$~Gy/min for all four lines
(paper: same; ✓). Resistant/parent ratio widens at high dose rate, as emphasized in Fig.~7.

\section{Critique: What Was and Was Not Done}

\subsection{Headline exercised}

The paper's central quantitative headline --- $w_{\mathrm{SLDR}}(\text{SAS-R}) \approx 1.06$,
$w_{\mathrm{SLDR}}(\text{HSC2-R}) \approx 1.90$ --- was \textbf{independently exercised} by MCMC
refit against the digitized Fig.~5 alone. Both values recovered within $1\sigma$. The paper's
second headline (ALDH$^{+}$ \% correlates with $f_s$) was verified as a Table-1 vs Fig.~3
consistency check with z-scores $|z| \leq 0.25$ for all four cell lines. In this precise sense the
replication \emph{does} exercise the headline.

\subsection{What was \emph{not} done}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Wet-lab was not reproduced.} The clonogenic-survival colony assays that produced
      Fig.~5 (acute), Fig.~6 (split-dose), and the ALDH$^{+}$ flow-cytometry sort behind Fig.~3
      were \emph{not} re-executed by LUCID. This is a computational replication only.
\item \textbf{No raw survival CSVs, no raw flow-cytometry FCS files.} The authors released neither
      code nor raw data. This categorically forecloses a FULL (Tier-1) verdict; the strongest
      possible verdict for this paper without author-supplied artefacts is PARTIAL.
\item \textbf{Fig.~5 digitization is vision-based, with $\sim$factor 1.3--2 noise in $\log_{10}(S)$}
      (particularly at low-survival tail). This propagates into the refit uncertainties in
      $w_{\mathrm{SLDR}}$ (reported $\pm 0.20$--$0.47$).
\item \textbf{Fig.~6 pass-1 digitization was wrong-signed} and is \emph{not} used in the re-pass
      verdict; Fig.~6 is instead exercised by forward prediction only. A vision re-digitization
      was not run because no vision model was available at re-pass time.
\item \textbf{Fig.~7 dose-rate curves were not digitized}, only forward-predicted. Quantitative
      $S$ vs dose-rate residuals against the paper's plotted curves remain unmeasured.
\item \textbf{The IMK microdosimetric kernel (Matsuya 2018 ref.~23) was not re-derived from
      first-principles track-structure inputs} --- $(a+c)_H$ was taken as fit output, not derived
      from a physical LET/track-length calculation.
\item \textbf{No author contact was attempted}, per LUCID free-endpoint / no-outreach policy.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Honest negative}

The re-pass surfaced one claim that \emph{does not hold as stated}: Table~1's HSC2 point estimate
$\alpha_{0,s} = 0.194$ is \emph{greater} than $\alpha_{0,p^{*}} = 0.166$, violating the paper's
own MCMC prior $\alpha_{0,s} < \alpha_{0,p}$. The reported uncertainties ($\sigma_{\alpha_{0,s}}
= 0.110$ vs $\sigma_{\alpha_{0,p^{*}}} = 0.160$) mean this is not a strong violation
($<1\sigma$), and it does not materially affect downstream survival predictions because
$f_s(\text{HSC2}) = 0.014$. But it is a genuine inconsistency in Table~1 that pass~1 missed.

\subsection{Verdict rationale}

FULL requires source-code + raw-data reproduction to $\leq 5\%$. Impossible here (no author code
or data release). PARTIAL (strong) is warranted because: model implemented from equations,
headline independently refit and recovered within $1\sigma$, all seven text-grounded internal
consistency checks pass or fail-honestly. WEAK is too weak (we have a full quantitative
implementation, not narrative agreement). SPOT-CHECK does not apply. Verdict:
\textbf{PARTIAL} (strong; coverage 9/10, agreement 9/10).
