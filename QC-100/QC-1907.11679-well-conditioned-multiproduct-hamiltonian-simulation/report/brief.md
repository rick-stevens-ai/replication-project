# Brief — Well-conditioned multiproduct Hamiltonian simulation (arXiv:1907.11679)

Low, Kliuchnikov, and Wiebe (2019) construct order-2m multiproduct Hamiltonian-simulation formulas that combine second- or fourth-order Suzuki product formulas as a linear combination. Their key advance is choosing the step exponents k_j via Chebyshev interpolation points (Eqs. 8–10) so the coefficient one-norm ‖a‖₁ grows only as Θ(log m) instead of the exponential Θ(eᵐ) of Chin's arithmetic-progression choice — replacing an exponentially-small quantum success probability with an Ω(1/log²m) one. We independently implemented (i) the second- and fourth-order Suzuki product formulas, (ii) Chin's ill-conditioned coefficients (Eq. 5, kⱼ = j), (iii) the paper's closed-form Chebyshev construction (Eqs. 8–9), (iv) the rounded-integer variant (Eq. 10), and (v) the exact rational coefficients from Appendix A Table I (m=2..6, entered verbatim as `fractions.Fraction`), and benchmarked them on a 4-site periodic Heisenberg chain evolved for t=1. Every construction satisfies the Vandermonde cancellation system to machine precision, the global-error slope in the clean regime matches the theoretical −2m to within ≈5% for every order 2m ∈ {2,4,6,8,10,12}, and ‖a‖₁ for Chin grows as 1.4→3.1→6.2→12.7→26.4 (≈2×/step in m) while both well-conditioned families stay bounded (1.4–2.0 in the same range). **Verdict: REPLICATED.**
