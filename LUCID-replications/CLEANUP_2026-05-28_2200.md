## Operational lessons

1. **MDPI bot-blocks the HTML wrapper, not the CDN.** When `/article/.../s1`
   returns 403, try `mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/<journal>/<journal>-<vol>-<art>/article_deploy/<journal>-<vol>-<art>-s001.zip` first.
2. **"Code not released" needs a second check against TOPAS-nBio.** Several
   Manchester-Christie radiobiology toolkits (DaMaRiS, the wider PRECISE
   chemistry chain) live there even when project pages say "available on
   request".
3. **PIDE is registration-gated, not public.** Don't promise survival-fit
   replications that depend on PIDE without the registration step.
