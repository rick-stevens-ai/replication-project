\section*{Open Questions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Open Questions}
Five open research questions arising from this replication (machine-readable form in
\texttt{report/open\_questions.json}):

\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textbf{Q1}] \textbf{The 20-min Table-S1 anomaly.} Is the 20-minute-gap row
(A=100.9, B=0.69) a real outlier, a typo/unit error, or a breakdown of the
offset-sum model at sub-foci-formation intervals? \emph{Next:} refit the 20-min
condition directly from digitized Fig-5A points independent of Table~S1; if it
lands near $\sim$30 foci, the row is a typo; else test a saturating second-exposure
induction term.

\item[\textbf{Q2}] \textbf{Superposition vs kinetic coupling.} Does Eq.~(4)'s
independent-additive second exposure hold mechanistically, or is there real coupling
(repair-protein depletion, chromatin remodeling)? \emph{Next:} build a coupled
two-compartment model where 2nd-exposure decay depends on residual 1st-exposure load
and use AIC/BIC vs the additive model.

\item[\textbf{Q3}] \textbf{Parameter identifiability.} How unique is the published
5-parameter fit; is Table~S1 a global minimum or one of many near-equivalent fits?
\emph{Next:} profile-likelihood / MCMC uncertainty analysis on all 7 fits, reporting
CIs, parameter correlations, and the sampling needed to identify $B$ and $E$.

\item[\textbf{Q4}] \textbf{Radiation-quality generalization.} Does the
$\sim$25 foci/cell/Gy yield and the split-dose recovery kinetics generalize beyond
225 kVp (to 30 kVp, MeV photons, ions), and what is the LET dependence?
\emph{Next:} digitize Fig-2 30-kVp curves, fit Eq.~(3), compare yields/half-lives,
and extend to sibling ion-beam LUCID datasets.

\item[\textbf{Q5}] \textbf{Foci kinetics $\to$ clinical fractionation.} Can the
recovery timescale ($\ge$1 h for two resolved peaks; baseline by 12 h) predict an
optimal inter-fraction interval that agrees with LQ-derived repair half-times?
\emph{Next:} convert fitted foci decay half-times to an equivalent Lea--Catcheside
repair half-time and compare against clinical tissue values on a shared cell line.
\end{enumerate}
