# Brief — Knopf & Liu (2020) bulk-surface elliptic systems

Independent replication attempt for **P. Knopf & C. Liu, "On second-order and fourth-order elliptic systems consisting of bulk and surface PDEs: Well-posedness, regularity theory and eigenvalue problems"** (Interfaces Free Bound. 23:4, 507–533, 2021; doi:10.4171/IFB/463; arXiv:2008.00895). This is a **pure analysis** paper — it proves well-posedness, higher-regularity, and discrete-spectrum theorems for coupled bulk-surface Poisson / biharmonic systems with unified Robin (K > 0) / Dirichlet (K = 0) boundary coupling; it contains **no numerical experiments** and **no code**. We independently discretized the natural 1D analogs of the second-order (P1 FEM) and fourth-order (cubic Hermite FEM) eigenvalue problems and checked every structural claim of Theorems 3.3, 4.4, 5.2, 6.4 and Proposition 4.5 that survives finite-dimensional approximation: real spectrum, discreteness, ordering, strict positivity on the compatibility-quotient subspace, mass-orthonormal eigenbasis, minimax variational principle, unified Robin↔Dirichlet handling by a single code path, and well-posedness of the source problem for compatible RHS. All discrete-testable claims pass, verified independently by two LLM judges (argo:gpt-5.2 and argo:claude-sonnet-4.6) both returning **PARTIAL** with the honest note that the higher-regularity claims (Theorem 3.3(b,c) H^{k+2} / C^∞ bootstraps) are not testable by finite-dimensional FEM.
