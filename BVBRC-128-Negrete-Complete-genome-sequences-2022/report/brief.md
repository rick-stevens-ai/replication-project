# Brief — BVBRC-128 Negrete et al. 2022

**Paper:** Negrete FJ, Ko K, Jang H, Hoffmann M, Lehner A, Stephan R, Fanning S, Tall BD, Gopinath GR.
"Complete genome sequences and genomic characterization of five plasmids harbored by environmentally persistent
Cronobacter sakazakii strains ST83 H322 and ST64 GK1025B obtained from powdered infant formula manufacturing
facilities." Gut Pathogens 14:23 (2022). DOI: 10.1186/s13099-022-00500-5. PMID: 35668537.

**What & why:** The paper reports PacBio-based complete closed genomes of two environmentally persistent
Cronobacter sakazakii strains (H322 = ST83/CC83; GK1025B = ST64/CC64) and their 5 associated plasmids
(pH322_1, pH322_2, pGK1025B_1, pGK1025B_2, pGK1025B_3), with in-silico characterization of MLST, prophages
(PHASTER), plasmid replicon typing (PlasmidFinder), Type IV/VI secretion systems, arsenic resistance, and
phospholipase D. This BVBRC replication independently re-fetches the 7 deposited GenBank sequences
(CP078106–CP078112) from NCBI, and re-runs the paper's key Table-1 measurements and the CGE-tooling
predictions (PubMLST live sequence query, PlasmidFinder BLAST at CGE defaults, T4SS/T6SS annotation span
computations, and SSU5 phage homology) — all from real public data, no fabricated numbers.
