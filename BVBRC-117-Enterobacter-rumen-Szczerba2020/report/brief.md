# Brief — BVBRC-117: Enterobacter aerogenes LU2 rumen isolate (Szczerba et al. 2020)

**What:** Independent replication of Szczerba et al. 2020 (*Sci. Rep.* 10:1986, DOI 10.1038/s41598-020-58929-0) — the first complete genome of *Enterobacter aerogenes* LU2 (NCBI-reclassified as *Klebsiella aerogenes*), a rumen isolate reported as a novel bio-based succinic acid producer.
**Why:** Verify the paper's core genome-assembly statistics, comparative-genomics taxonomy, plasmid/CRISPR absence, AMR profile, and reductive-TCA/glyoxylate-pathway metabolic claim against the deposited genome using free BV-BRC–analogous open tools (Mash Similar-Genome-Finder analog, BLAST comparative genomics, PlasmidFinder, AMRFinderPlus, barrnap rRNA re-annotation) on real NCBI data.
**Result:** PARTIAL — 6/10 claims strongly replicated, 1 moderate, 2 weak (PHASTER/BAGEL4 not re-run), 1 blocked (raw reads never deposited, so SPAdes hybrid assembly cannot be independently re-run).
