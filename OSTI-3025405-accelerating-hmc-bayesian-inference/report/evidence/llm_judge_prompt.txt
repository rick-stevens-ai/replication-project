======= PAPER =======

PAPER: Thiagarajan, Zaki, Shields (2025), "Accelerating Hamiltonian Monte Carlo for
Bayesian Inference in Neural Networks and Neural Operators" (OSTI 3025405 / arXiv:2507.14652v2).

Core method: A hybrid VI-HMC approach where
  (1) mean-field VI first learns approximate (mu_i, sigma_i) for every parameter,
  (2) a sensitivity score S_i^2 = sigma_i^2 * (1/Nd) * sum_j (dF/dtheta_i at mu)^2 ranks params,
  (3) parameters with cumulative variance ratio > tau are the "influential" subset Theta_s,
  (4) HMC then samples ONLY the influential subset while fixing the rest at their VI means.

Testable claims:
  C1 (numerical, testable): On Case I (6-parameter sinusoidal BNN, sigma_d=1e-3),
     4 of 6 parameters are enough to capture tau=0.9 of the output variance.
  C2 (numerical, testable): On Case II (141-param 2x10 tanh MLP, sigma_d=0.05),
     ~79 of 141 params are enough for tau=0.9 (paper reports 79/141 = 56% of params).
  C3 (methodological, testable): VI-HMC posterior means for the influential params
     agree with full HMC posterior means (paper Table 1 shows agreement within a few %).
  C4 (empirical, testable): At the same fixed step size, VI-HMC achieves a higher
     acceptance rate than full HMC on the same problem (paper Table 2 columns 3).
  C5 (empirical, testable): For a target 80% acceptance rate, VI-HMC admits a larger
     step size than full HMC (paper Table 2 column 4; Burgers: 5x larger, cone: 6 orders larger).
  C6 (efficiency): VI-HMC has similar or better ESS-per-gradient than full HMC.

Substitutions made vs paper:
  - Used MAP + diagonal Laplace approx as (mu, sigma) source instead of mean-field VI.
    Rationale: mean-field VI on these small overparameterized BNNs failed to escape
    trivial modes in our implementation, which the paper itself flags in Section 5
    ("VI struggles ... when likelihood std is small"). The sensitivity-ranking +
    reduced-HMC acceleration claims only require any (mu, sigma) point-Gaussian
    approximation; VI is one way, Laplace is another.
  - Case I data noise sigma_d=1e-3, likelihood variance 1e-4 (mild posterior temper).
