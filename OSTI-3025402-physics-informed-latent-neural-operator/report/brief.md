# Brief — OSTI 3025402

**Paper:** Karumuri, Graham-Brady, Goswami (2025), *Physics-informed latent neural operator for real-time predictions of time-dependent parametric PDEs* (arXiv:2501.08428v3). JHU. Preprint submitted to Elsevier, Oct 29 2025. Source: `https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/3025402`.

**What / why:** The paper proposes **PI-Latent-NO**, a physics-informed neural operator with two coupled DeepONets — a *Latent-DeepONet* that maps the input function ξ and time t to a low-dimensional latent state z(t)∈R^{n_z}, and a *Reconstruction-DeepONet* that maps (z(t), x) to u(t,x). The physics-informed loss (PDE residual + BCs + IC) is applied to u_hat via automatic differentiation, so the model can be trained **with no ground-truth solution labels (n_train=0)**. Because time-and-space are separable through the two-network split, the trunk-network evaluation count grows as O(n_t + n_x) rather than O(n_t·n_x), giving near-constant scaling with grid size and enabling real-time inference (~0.01 s per new IC vs ~0.4 s for a conventional FDM solver on 1D diffusion-reaction). This replication independently reimplements PI-Latent-NO in PyTorch and re-tests the 1D Diffusion-Reaction benchmark (Table 2 of the paper) with three seeds, using our own physics-informed loss, our own FDM ground truth, and our own timing measurements on an NVIDIA A100. The goal is to verify that the architectural claims (physics-only training reaches high R²/low relative-L2, and inference dominates the classical solver by ≥1 order of magnitude) reproduce independently.
