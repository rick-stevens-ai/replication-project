# Brief — BVBRC-53

**What:** Independent replication of Nakazono et al. (2022, PLOS ONE), which reported the complete sequences of two bacteriocin-encoding plasmids from oral-derived *Staphylococcus epidermidis* — the epidermin plasmid **pEpi56** (strain KSE56) and the nukacin plasmid **pNuk650** (strain KSE650) — and compared them to prior reference clusters/plasmids.

**Why replicable via BV-BRC:** The plasmids are deposited in NCBI (OK031036, OK031035) with the reference pIVK45 (KP702950) also public. The paper's declared BV-BRC workflow is *PlasmidFinder via Similar Genome Finder*, and its central conclusions are sequence-level (plasmid sizes, ORF counts, gene-cluster content, peptide identities, an ~8 kbp insertion in pNuk650 vs pIVK45) — all directly checkable from the deposited sequences with standard tools (efetch, blastn, abricate/PlasmidFinder, amrfinder). The only unreproducible claims are the wet-lab antibacterial-activity assays.
