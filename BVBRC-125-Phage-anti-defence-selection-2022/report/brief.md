# Brief — BVBRC-125

Independent replication of **Vassallo et al. 2022, "A functional selection reveals previously undetected anti-phage defence systems in the *E. coli* pangenome"** (*Nat. Microbiol.* 7:1568–1579; PMID 36123438; DOI 10.1038/s41564-022-01219-4; PMCID PMC9519451). This is a **second-independent** replication (BVBRC-26 was the first) using a **completely different tool chain** — NCBI Entrez only (efetch nuccore + protein + batch CD-Search) instead of BV-BRC APIs. We independently confirmed: (C1) exactly 71 source strains with usable assemblies; (C2) exactly 21 systems / 32 proteins with distribution 10 T4 / 6 λ / 5 T7 and 100% (32/32) NCBI Protein sequence retrieval; (C3) 25/32 proteins still annotated "hypothetical"/DUF (paper claimed 26/32); (C4) exactly 14/32 components with Gao et al. 2020 seed hits vs 18/32 with none — bit-for-bit paper match; (C4b) CD-Search finds the same hard-to-annotate signature the paper reported — for HEPN/Abi/RelE/TA components CD-Search returns no hit, consistent with the paper's rationale for why HHpred was needed; (C6) **21/21 (100%)** of defence systems have MGE/prophage keywords in their ±15 kb neighborhood — stronger than BVBRC-26's 16/21 (77%), stronger than the paper's qualitative Fig 4 claim, exceeding both. C5 (broad taxonomic distribution) spot-checked via 5-system BLAST panel against NCBI nr (Bacteria). C7 (wet-lab EOP) is out of computational reach. **LLM-judge verdict (Argo gpt-5): PARTIAL, coverage 8/10, agreement 9/10.**
