## VERDICT: **REPLICATED**

## Justification
The core factual claims of this genome announcement—public data availability, exact genome size and strand-resolved gene counts, GC content, rRNA operon structure, and the presence of a syntenic pfa PUFA biosynthetic operon with the paper's named homologs—all reproduce with either exact matches or negligible method-dependent deviations. The only Partial claim (C4) reflects a total BGC count discrepancy (53 vs 47) that is fully attributable to using antiSMASH v8 versus the paper's v5, while the dominant-category ordering and rare-BGC composition are faithfully reproduced. Because every claim was independently tested and all substantive scientific conclusions hold, with the single divergence explained by a documented tool-version difference rather than a genuine contradiction, the paper is judged REPLICATED.
