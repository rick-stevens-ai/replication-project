\section{Paper}
R.\ LaRose, A.\ Mari, S.\ Kaiser, P.\ J.\ Karalekas, et al., \emph{Mitiq: A software package for error mitigation on noisy quantum computers}, Quantum 6, 774 (2022), arXiv:2009.04417. Set QC-100.

\section{Verdict}
\textbf{REPLICATED.} Both headline error-mitigation primitives of the Mitiq package --- PEC and ZNE --- were independently reproduced end-to-end using the released Mitiq software on a Cirq density-matrix simulator. The paper's exact unmitigated Fig.~5 value was matched to four significant figures.

\section{Claims exercised}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{C1 (Fig 5 unmitigated).} $\langle 00 | \rho | 00 \rangle = 0.0622$ for the toy circuit $U = \mathrm{CNOT}_{1,2} \circ X_1 \circ H_2$ under per-gate depolarizing $p=0.1$. \emph{Reproduced}: $0.062222$.
\item \textbf{C2 (Fig 5 PEC-mitigated).} PEC with 1000 samples improves the estimate ``by almost an order of magnitude'' to $\approx 0.0071$. \emph{Reproduced}: mean $|err|=0.0097$ over 10 seeds, mean improvement $6.4\times$, 100\% of seeds better than unmitigated. Paper's point value lies within our seed distribution.
\item \textbf{C3 (ZNE benchmark).} ZNE produces mitigated expectation values closer to the noiseless truth than unmitigated across benchmark circuits. \emph{Reproduced}: on 20 randomized-benchmarking 2-qubit circuits at $p=0.01$, mean $|err|$ improved $0.577 \to 0.326$ ($1.77\times$), 20/20 circuits improved.
\end{itemize}

Untested / out of scope: C4 (H$_2$ VQE energy surface) --- same ZNE code path as C3, mechanistically covered; C5 (real IBM/Rigetti hardware) --- no QPU access. CDR (Clifford data regression) was not separately exercised beyond package presence.

\section{Method (headline-exercised)}
Fresh Python 3.12 venv, \texttt{mitiq 1.0.0} + \texttt{cirq 1.6.1}. Backend: \texttt{cirq.DensityMatrixSimulator} (deterministic --- exactly the Fig.~5 setup, no shot noise). Executors return $\mathrm{Re}(\rho[0,0])$. C1/C2 driver: \texttt{work/rep\_pec\_multiseed.py} (10 seeds, 1000 PEC samples each). C3 driver: \texttt{work/rep\_zne.py} (20 RB circuits, default folding + Richardson extrapolation). All numbers reproducible with the same seeds. Judge: free Argo proxy (\texttt{argo:gpt-5.2}) --- concurred REPLICATED.

\section{Critique (honest)}

\paragraph{What went genuinely well.} This is a rare full-substance software-artifact replication. Mitiq was installed independently from PyPI, a specific Fig.~5 benchmark was reproduced from first principles rather than quoted, and the exact unmitigated number matched to four significant figures --- meaning the noise model, gate ordering, and observable were all correctly re-derived from the paper. Both ZNE and PEC were quantitatively verified against a no-mitigation baseline, with the baseline reported side-by-side and the improvement factor computed explicitly (PEC: $\sim 6.4\times$ mean, ZNE: $1.77\times$). PEC's Monte-Carlo variance was correctly characterized across 10 seeds instead of relying on a single realization; 100\% of seeds beat the unmitigated baseline.

\paragraph{Genuine limitations.} (1) \textbf{CDR was not exercised.} Mitiq's third headline primitive (Clifford data regression) is listed as ``supported'' but no CDR benchmark was reproduced --- only package-presence was checked. A more complete replication would run \texttt{execute\_with\_cdr} on a small benchmark and compare to ZNE/PEC on the same circuit. (2) \textbf{H$_2$ VQE surface (Fig 4) not run.} The claim of ``mechanistically covered by C3'' is defensible (identical ZNE code path) but not equivalent to a positive reproduction --- the H$_2$ energy surface has its own numerical signature (bond-length sweep, chemistry accuracy threshold) that C3 does not verify. (3) \textbf{Hardware runs (Fig 3) untested.} All results are on a noiseless-model-plus-depolarizing simulator; real IBM/Rigetti device drift, crosstalk, and $T_1/T_2$ decay were not encountered. This is the standard hardware-access gap but should be flagged. (4) \textbf{Cirq path only.} Multi-backend support (Qiskit, pyQuil, Braket) is a documented Mitiq feature but only the Cirq interface was exercised. (5) \textbf{PDF vision tooling unavailable} at replication time (Anthropic credits exhausted); paper numbers were extracted from the ar5iv HTML rendering, which is a reliable source but a one-step remove from the PDF of record. (6) \textbf{Judge model fallback.} \texttt{argo:claude-opus-4.8} hit a proxy parse bug and the run fell back to \texttt{gpt-5.2}; the verdict is thus single-model rather than multi-model consensus.

\paragraph{What would strengthen the replication.} Adding a small CDR benchmark on the same 2-qubit circuit; reproducing a $\ge 3$-point slice of the Fig.~4 H$_2$ energy surface (VQE step + ZNE post-processing); running the same executor path through Qiskit's noisy Aer simulator to prove the multi-backend claim; re-doing the LLM judgment with a second free model for consensus.
