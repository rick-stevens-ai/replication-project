# BVBRC-78 · Independent Replication Brief

Pradal et al. 2023 (*Viruses* 15:179, PMID 36680219) isolated *Enterococcus faecium* phage vB_EfaH_163 from human feces and classified it as a new member of *Herelleviridae* (subfamily Brockvirinae, genus Schiekvirus) with a 150,836 bp lytic dsDNA genome carrying no AMR or virulence genes, and showed it reduces mortality in a *Galleria mellonella* model of a vanR *E. faecium* clinical isolate (VR-13). We independently pulled the phage genome from ENA (CAJDKA010000002.1) and 7 comparator genomes from NCBI, re-ran ORF prediction (Prodigal), tRNA scanning (ARAGORN), AMR/virulence screening against 7 databases (Abricate: card, ncbi, resfinder, argannot, megares, vfdb, victors), lysogeny-marker BLASTp, whole-genome BLASTn against Herelleviridae + Siphoviridae comparators, and built an MCP-based UPGMA phylogeny mirroring the paper's Fig 4 method. Every accessible computational claim reproduced: 150,836 bp (exact), 37.04% GC, 21 tRNAs (exact), 0 AMR/virulence hits across 7 dbs, 0 lysogeny-marker hits, tight Schiekvirus clustering with iF6/EFDG1/EfV12-phi1/EFP01, and MDA2 on a separate Kochikohdavirus branch — all matching the paper. Wet-lab claims (host range percentages, Galleria mortality) are out of scope, and the VR-13 host genome and phage raw reads were not deposited so PhageTerm/host-AMR verification is not possible from public artifacts.
