# Brief

Independent replication of Wang et al. 2020 (*Int J Genomics*, doi:10.1155/2020/4109186, PMID:32190639),
"Genomic Analysis of *Bacillus megaterium* NCT-2 Reveals Its Genetic Basis for the Bioremediation of
Secondary Salinization Soil." The paper announces the complete hybrid (Illumina HiSeq + PacBio RSII)
genome of soil-bioremediation strain NCT-2 (now *Priestia megaterium*), reports its overall genome
architecture (1 chromosome + 10 plasmids, ~5.88 Mb), annotation totals, a comparative table against
five reference *Bacillus* genomes, whole-genome phylogeny (closest to DSM 319 then QM B1551), and the
presence of gene inventories for nitrogen metabolism, phosphate solubilization, IAA synthesis, and
osmotic/oxidative stress. We independently pulled the deposited genome (GCA_000334875.3, chromosome
CP032527.2 + plasmids CP032528–CP032537) and the five comparator genomes from NCBI and re-derived the
genome statistics, annotation counts, comparative table, ANI-based phylogenetic ordering, and functional
gene presence on real data. This is the BV-BRC-mappable "Comprehensive Genome Analysis + Similar Genome
Finder / PlasmidFinder + Phylogenetic Tree" workflow.
