\section{Paper summary}
PA34 is a multi-drug-resistant \textit{P.\ aeruginosa} isolated in 1997 from the cornea of a
microbial-keratitis patient at L.V.\ Prasad Eye Institute (Hyderabad, India). Subedi et al.\
closed its genome via Illumina + Oxford Nanopore hybrid assembly (SPAdes) into a
6.8 Mbp chromosome + two plasmids (pMKPA34-1 95.4 kbp, pMKPA34-2 26.8 kbp) and dissected
its \emph{accessory genome} through a four-way pangenome (PA34 vs PAO1, PA14, VRFPA04)
with Prokka-uniform annotation + Roary v3.12 pangenome + ResFinder AMR + MAUVE genomic
islands + wet-lab MIC/cytotoxicity. Headline computational findings: pangenome 7{,}643
orthologs; core 5{,}078; PA34 accessory 1{,}213 ($\sim$20\%); 543 strictly private; MLST
ST1284; exoU$^+$; AAC(3)-IId aminoglycoside gene; six plasmid-borne AMR genes.

\section{Method}
Heavy compute on \textbf{uicgpu} (255 cores; conda envs \texttt{bvbrc28} = Prokka 1.12 +
Roary 3.12.0; \texttt{bvbrc14} = abricate + mlst). NCBI Datasets v2 + efetch for genomes
(PA34 GCF\_003332705.2 + plasmid MH547560.1/MH547561.1; PAO1 GCF\_000006765.1; PA14
GCF\_000014625.1; VRFPA04 GCF\_000473745.2). Uniform Prokka annotation on all four
\texttt{.fna} (Bacteria, genus \textit{Pseudomonas}) to match paper's method. Roary at 95\%
BLASTP identity default. Custom \texttt{analyze\_roary.py} parses
\texttt{gene\_presence\_absence.csv} to reproduce Fig-1 Venn decomposition. abricate against
ResFinder + CARD + VFDB on PA34 chromosome + both plasmids. \texttt{mlst} paeruginosa scheme.
Verdict via LLM-judge (\texttt{argo:gpt-5.2}, free Argo proxy \texttt{localhost:44497}) --- no
regex scoring.

\section{Verdict}
\textbf{REPLICATED.} The paper's principal computational contribution --- a four-genome Roary
pangenome yielding 7{,}643 orthologs, 5{,}078-gene core, 1{,}213-gene PA34 accessory with 543
strictly private, plus ST1284 / exoU / AAC(3)-IId / plasmid AMR cluster --- reproduces on the
actual public assemblies with near-exact numerical agreement (12/13 computational claims;
four EXACT, the rest $\Delta{\leq}1$ gene). Only wet-lab MIC/cytotoxicity (C14) and manual
MAUVE genomic islands (C13) were out of reach. LLM-judge (Argo gpt-5.2) concurs: REPLICATED,
$\sim$92\% coverage, VERY HIGH agreement.

\section{Genuine critique (evidence strength, shortcuts, unverified claims)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{Roary at default 95\% BLASTP identity is a hyperparameter, not a physical
constant.} The paper's exact numbers (7{,}643, 5{,}078, etc.) are contingent on this cutoff +
Prokka version + BLAST DB snapshot; our $\Delta{\leq}1$ agreement is remarkable given that,
but a systematic sweep of identity cutoffs 90--98\% was not run. Some of the
``$\Delta=1$'' agreements could shift under small parameter perturbations.
\item \textbf{AMR gene count (16 vs paper's $\geq$12) is a version-drift artifact}, not a
contradiction. We use a newer ResFinder DB (includes \textit{tmexCD3-toprJ3} efflux operon
recognized post-2019); the extra 4 hits are database, not biology. This is honest but means
strict claim-count matching would need pinning to the 2019 DB snapshot, which was not done.
\item \textbf{C13 (genomic islands $\geq$24) was NOT re-run.} The paper's GIs came from a
manual MAUVE 4-way alignment with a bespoke ``4-contiguous-ORF'' rule --- subjective +
low-throughput. We deferred it as out-of-scope; a proper GI screen with IslandViewer,
Phaster prophage, and merging would give an automated re-derivation but wasn't attempted.
\item \textbf{C14 (wet-lab MIC + cytotoxicity) is fundamentally untestable in silico.}
Marking REPLICATED reflects the computational core only; the phenotypic virulence/MDR
narrative is trusted from the paper without cross-check.
\item \textbf{Assembly-provenance shortcut.} We used the RefSeq GCF\_003332705.2 assembly
(paper's chromosome + plasmids curated post-submission) rather than the paper's own SPAdes
hybrid contigs. Almost certainly identical at the sequence level (RefSeq is derived from
GCA), but we did not diff them.
\item \textbf{LLM-judge is single-model.} Standing policy prefers 3-judge; this run used one
Argo judge. The verdict is defensible because the numbers are almost all exact/near-exact,
but the scoring layer is thinner than ideal.
\end{itemize}

\section{Open questions}
See \texttt{report/open\_questions.json}. Five open problems surfaced: (Q1) parameter
sensitivity of the pangenome partition; (Q2) automated re-derivation of the 24 genomic
islands with modern tooling + concordance; (Q3) plasmid mobility + \textit{in trans} spread
of the AMR cluster; (Q4) the 543 unique genes --- how many are functional vs annotation
noise/paralog inflation; (Q5) the eye-isolate-shared 124 orthologs --- is there an
ocular-niche signature or is this an artifact of small comparison set.
