\section{Critique --- honest reading of what was and was not done}

\subsection*{What the paper's ``headline'' is}
The 2021 McMahon \& Prise paper is a \emph{review/synthesis} of the analytic MEDRAS framework, extended with new dose-rate machinery. The headline figures --- Figs~3D, 4, 5, 7 --- \emph{overlay} MEDRAS predictions onto cell-survival datasets (Paganetti proton compilation, PIDE ion database, Lehmann/Newman dose-rate data) to produce the paper's most publishable outputs: MID-vs-cell-line scatter, RBE-vs-LET fits, dose-rate sparing curves.

\subsection*{What was actually re-run (this replication)}
The \emph{mechanistic} Monte Carlo backbone: \texttt{basicXandIon(runs=20)} $\to$ SDD damage files $\to$ \texttt{repairSimulation('Fidelity')} $\to$ 50 MC repeats/condition. This is what the shipped code is designed to compute and what drives every downstream survival/RBE/dose-rate prediction. \textbf{The MC binary WAS re-run from scratch}; the physics-input steps (damage generation from radial-energy tables + repair kinetics MC) were reproduced end-to-end. Total $\approx 5$ min on 1 CPU thread with 20 exposures $\times$ 50 repeats. This is a genuine fresh MC run, not a log-scrape --- distinguishing this run from the classic LUCID ``MC-never-rerun'' pattern.

\subsection*{What was NOT done}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{No MID-vs-cell-line survival scatter (Fig 4).} Requires the Paganetti compilation of proton cell-survival data --- not shipped with the code and not attempted here.
  \item \textbf{No RBE-vs-LET overlays (Fig 5) with data comparison.} We reproduced the \emph{shape} of misrepair-vs-LET, which is the MC ingredient behind Fig 5, but we did not fit $\alpha$/$\beta$ to a real cell line and overlay the PIDE ion-survival points. So the paper's actual chi-square-able figure was NOT reconstructed.
  \item \textbf{No dose-rate sparing curves (Fig 6) vs Lehmann/Newman data.}
  \item \textbf{No dose-rate MID predictions (Fig 7).}
  \item \textbf{PIDE dataset (Fig 5, Fig 7 overlays):} PIDE 3.4 is not anonymously downloadable; access requires institutional-email registration with GSI. Cached documentation only; no numerical replication of the overlays.
  \item \textbf{Coverage of the full paper scope: $\approx 35\%$.} The 65\% gap is the survival/RBE/dose-rate application layer that requires external datasets.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Why the verdict is PARTIAL, not REPLICATED}
The mechanistic MC backbone is 100\% reproduced (7/7 claims qualitative, 2/2 quantitative within paper-stated uncertainty). But the paper's \emph{stated} contribution is the coupling of that MC backbone to cell-survival data --- and that coupling was not exercised. The LUCID convention of tagging such runs \textbf{PARTIAL} when the paper's headline application layer is not touched is preserved here. (Note: this is a friendlier PARTIAL than the classic LUCID ``MC-never-rerun'' cases --- here the MC \emph{was} rerun; only the downstream survival-fit overlays were skipped.)

\subsection*{Reproducibility friction encountered}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{license-without-LICENSE-file}: BSD grant per-file only.
  \item \texttt{no-pinned-deps}: no requirements.txt / pyproject.toml.
  \item \texttt{deterministic-seed-not-exposed}: $\pm 5$--10\% run-to-run variation at 20$\times$50 repeats.
  \item \texttt{kinetics-column-not-self-described}: inferred 0.1-h step from \texttt{kineticLimit}.
  \item \texttt{non-deterministic-output-order}: \texttt{os.listdir} ordering; sorted downstream.
  \item \texttt{registration-required-dataset}: PIDE 3.4 blocked by GSI email-registration wall.
\end{itemize}
