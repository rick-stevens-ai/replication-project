# Brief

Independent replication of Lohéac, Trélat, Zuazua (MMAS 2017), *Minimal controllability time for the heat equation under unilateral state or control constraints* (DOI 10.1142/S0218202517500270, HAL:hal-01457931). We reproduce the paper's analytical lower-bound constants (Example 1, δ=5), the minimal-time lower-bound formula |ln Z*|/π², and the two numerical minimal-time experiments (y₀=5→y₁=1 with Dirichlet controls; and the reverse y₀=1→y₁=5 with a bound M=50) using a Python/scipy HiGHS LP feasibility + bisection implementation — independent of the paper's AMPL+IpOpt stack. Every numerical claim tested agrees with the paper to ≤1.5% (the 1D-Dirichlet minimal time agrees to 0.14%). Judge verdict: PARTIAL / 5/5 agreement, coverage 5/9 (multi-D theoretical theorems and Neumann numerics not attempted).
