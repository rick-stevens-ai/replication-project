PAPER NOTE — PDE-Cockburn-Kanschat-LDG-NavierStokes-2004
=========================================================

Title:   A locally conservative LDG method for the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations
Authors: Bernardo Cockburn, Guido Kanschat, Dominik Schötzau
Venue:   Mathematics of Computation, Vol. 74, No. 251 (2005), pp. 1067-1095
         (electronically published 2004; the collection labels it "2004")
DOI:     10.1090/S0025-5718-04-01718-1

Closely related companion papers by the same authors:
 - "The local discontinuous Galerkin method for the Oseen equations",
   Math. Comp. 73 (2004) 569-593.  DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-03-01552-7
 - "Local discontinuous Galerkin methods for the Stokes system",
   SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 40 (2002) 319-343.

KEY CLAIMS (the paper's central contributions)
----------------------------------------------
C1. An LDG discretization of the steady incompressible Navier-Stokes (and
    Stokes/Oseen) equations that is LOCALLY CONSERVATIVE (mass and momentum
    conserved element-by-element).
C2. The velocity approximation is EXACTLY (pointwise) DIVERGENCE-FREE in a
    suitable sense / the scheme delivers strongly mass-conservative solutions
    (the discrete velocity is divergence-free element-wise up to the chosen
    polynomial space; companion "divergence-free" DG work shows exactly
    div-free solutions).
C3. OPTIMAL ORDER OF CONVERGENCE: with polynomial degree k on each element,
    the velocity converges at order k+1 in the L2 norm and the velocity
    gradient / stress at order k; pressure at order k+1 (optimal).
C4. The method is ENERGY-STABLE (a discrete energy / inf-sup / coercivity
    estimate is proven), independent of the mesh, for a suitable choice of
    the stabilization / penalty parameters.

STANDARD TEST PROBLEMS used in this line of work
------------------------------------------------
 - Kovasznay flow: an exact analytical steady solution of the 2D incompressible
   Navier-Stokes equations on [-0.5,1.5]x[0,2] (or [-0.5,1]x[-0.5,1.5]) with
     u = 1 - e^{lam x} cos(2 pi y)
     v = (lam/2pi) e^{lam x} sin(2 pi y)
     p = 1/2 (1 - e^{2 lam x})
     lam = Re/2 - sqrt(Re^2/4 + 4 pi^2)
   Used to measure convergence order for the full nonlinear NS.
 - Lid-driven cavity at low Reynolds number (qualitative benchmark).
 - Manufactured Stokes solutions for verifying optimal L2 order (k+1).

WHAT WE REPLICATE (method level, small & local, free tools only)
----------------------------------------------------------------
We implement, in Python/NumPy, a nodal Discontinuous Galerkin (interior-penalty
LDG-type) solver for the 2D incompressible STOKES problem (the linear core of
the method; the NS nonlinearity is a Picard/Oseen add-on on top of the same
bilinear form) on a triangular/quad mesh, with a MANUFACTURED exact solution
chosen to be divergence-free. We then measure the empirical L2 convergence
order of velocity and pressure under mesh refinement for polynomial degrees
k=1,2 and check that it matches the paper's claimed OPTIMAL order (k+1 velocity,
k+1 pressure), and that the discrete divergence is small (mass conservation, C2).
We also run a Kovasznay-flow steady NS convergence check via Oseen/Picard
iteration to touch the nonlinear claim.

This is a faithful method-level check of claims C2 (mass conservation /
small divergence) and C3 (optimal convergence order), which are the
quantitatively verifiable heart of the paper on a laptop.
