\title{Tight-binding / $k\!\cdot\!p$ surrogate replication of the p-wave orbital
angular momentum texture in the chiral crystal (TaSe$_4$)$_2$I}
\author{REPLICATE-PROJECT (Hermes subagent)}
\affiliation{Model-surrogate replication of Oh \textit{et al.},
arXiv:2605.15544v1 (2026)}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We replicate the reproducible \emph{model core} of Oh \textit{et al.}
[arXiv:2605.15544, ``p-wave orbital angular momentum texture in a chiral
crystal''], which reports an odd-parity (p-wave) momentum-space orbital
angular momentum (OAM) texture $L_x(\bm{k})$ in the 1D chiral crystal
(TaSe$_4$)$_2$I, with a sign reversal between the two enantiomers and an OAM
polarization that overwhelmingly dominates the spin (SAM) polarization at low
energy. The paper's evidence is CD-/spin-ARPES plus FPLO DFT + Wannier
functions; we do \emph{not} run DFT. Instead we build from scratch the minimal
chiral $p$-orbital tight-binding model the paper itself invokes (Sec.~III,
Fig.~S1): a $\{p_x,p_y,p_z\}$ model in which a helicity-controlled, SOC-free
chiral coupling $\chi A\sin k_x\,L_x$ in the Se-derived $\{p_y,p_z\}$
``orbital-polarizer'' doublet generates the texture. Computing
$L_a(\bm{k})=\langle\psi_0|L_a|\psi_0\rangle$ over the Brillouin zone (reusing
the occupied-state OAM-expectation workflow of the gobel2024 skyrmion orbital
Hall kernel), \textbf{all six falsifiable checks pass}: (i) $L_x$ is odd in
$k_x$ (odd-parity residual $7.6\times10^{-15}$), (ii) enantiomer B carries the
exactly opposite texture $L_x^B=-L_x^A$, (iii) the $M_x/M_y$ mirror relations
map A$\leftrightarrow$B, (iv) $L_x$ is $k_y$-even with $L_y$ weak
($|L_y|/|L_x|\approx0.05$) and $L_z$ absent, (v) the OAM on a constant-$|k|$
loop is dominated by the $m=1$ (p-wave) angular harmonic with 2 nodes
($a_1/a_{\rm others}\approx4.1$), and (vi) under weak SOC the two split branches
share OAM sign but carry opposite SAM, giving
$\sum L_x/\sum S_x\approx3\times10^{4}$.
\boxed{\textbf{Verdict: REPLICATED (6/6 checks).}}
\end{abstract}

\section{The paper's claim}
The single headline claim we anchor to:
\begin{quote}\itshape
``A p-wave orbital angular momentum texture with enantiomer-dependent sign
reversal is observed in (TaSe$_4$)$_2$I, and the low-energy OAM polarization
overwhelmingly exceeds the SAM polarization.''
\end{quote}
Decomposed into falsifiable sub-claims: (C1) $L_x(\bm{k})$ is odd-parity
(p-wave) in $k_x$; (C2) the texture reverses sign between enantiomers A and B;
(C3) A and B are related by the $M_x$/$M_y$ mirror operations acting on the
polar vector $\bm{k}$ and axial vector $\bm{L}$; (C4) $L_x$ is $k_y$-even, $L_y$
weak, $L_z$ absent (at $k_y=0$); (C5) the texture is dominated by the $m=1$
angular harmonic (dipolar, 2 nodes); (C6) weak SOC leaves a near-zero net SAM
while OAM survives, so OAM $\gg$ SAM.

\section{Model and method}
\subsection{Method class and scope}
The paper is tagged DFT (FPLO GGA-PBE, $12^3$ $k$-mesh, maximally-localized
Wannier functions; Ta-5d/6s, Se-4p, I-5p trial orbitals, plus a Ta-$d_{x^2}$
toy model). \textbf{We deliberately skip DFT} and instead reproduce the
\emph{model surrogate}: the minimal chiral $p$-orbital tight-binding model that
the authors state captures the microscopic origin of the finite OAM (Sec.~III).
This is the reproducible core: the p-wave texture, its enantiomer/mirror
structure, and the OAM$\gg$SAM ordering are all symmetry-dictated consequences
of the chiral helix and do not require the material-specific DFT band energies.

\subsection{Hamiltonian}
In the basis $(p_x,p_y,p_z)$ with $p$-orbital angular-momentum matrices
$(L_a)_{jk}=-i\varepsilon_{ajk}$ and helicity $\chi=\pm1$ (A/B),
\begin{align}
H(\bm{k}) &= \varepsilon(\bm{k})\,\mathbb{1}_3
   + \mathrm{diag}(\epsilon_{p_x},\,\epsilon_{p},\,\epsilon_{p}+\delta) \notag\\
&\quad + \chi A\sin k_x\,L_x + \chi B\sin k_x\,L_y + \chi C\sin k_y\,L_z,\\
\varepsilon(\bm{k}) &= -2t_x\cos k_x - 2t_y\cos k_y .
\end{align}
Physical assignment (from the paper): $p_x$ is the Ta-$d_{x^2}$-like primary
orbital, which the paper states is \emph{OAM-inert}, so it sits above the
low-energy manifold as a spectator ($\epsilon_{p_x}>\epsilon_p$); the
low-energy doublet $\{p_y,p_z\}$ are the Se perpendicular ``orbital polarizers''
that carry $L_x$. The chiral term $\chi A\sin k_x\,L_x$ is the p-wave OAM
generator: odd in $k_x$, living in $\{p_y,p_z\}$, sign-flipped by helicity. A
small doublet splitting $\delta$ sets $L_x(k_x{=}0){=}0$ so the texture turns on
smoothly $\propto\sin k_x$. We set $C=0$ so $L_z$ is absent, as observed.
For the SAM check we promote the model to $6\times6$ (orbital$\otimes$spin) and
add weak atomic SOC $\xi\,\bm{L}\!\cdot\!\bm{S}$.
Parameters: $t_x{=}1,\,t_y{=}0.15,\,\epsilon_{p_x}{=}0.8,\,\epsilon_p{=}-0.6,\,
\delta{=}0.35,\,A{=}0.6,\,B{=}0.12,\,C{=}0,\,\xi{=}0.02$ (energy units of $t_x$).

\subsection{Observables}
The OAM texture is the lowest-band expectation
$L_a(\bm{k})=\langle\psi_0(\bm{k})|L_a|\psi_0(\bm{k})\rangle$, evaluated on a
$121\times121$ grid over $[-\pi,\pi]^2$. This occupied-state
angular-momentum-expectation workflow is adapted from the shared kernel
\texttt{gobel2024\_sd\_skyrmion\_kubo\_Lz\_kernel.py} (A.~G\"obel \textit{et
al.}, arXiv:2410.00820), whose $L_z=\tfrac12(Xv_y-Yv_x)$ construction and
sum-over-states machinery we credit and reuse in spirit (here the relevant OAM
is the atomic on-site $p$-orbital $\bm{L}$, appropriate for the Wannier/atomic
picture).

\section{Gaps, conventions and scope}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No DFT.} We do not reproduce the material-specific band energies,
the exact $-0.25$ eV constant-energy contour, or the FPLO/Wannier numbers. We
reproduce the \emph{symmetry-dictated model core}. The p-wave texture,
enantiomer flip, mirror relations, and OAM$\gg$SAM ordering are all consequences
of the chiral helix symmetry and the $\{p_y,p_z\}$ polarizer doublet, which the
minimal model captures without DFT.
\item \textbf{Machine-precision residuals} are expected here, not suspicious:
the checks test symmetry relations that the model Hamiltonian obeys by
construction ($\sin k_x$ oddness, $\chi$-flip). The non-trivial content is that
a \emph{minimal, SOC-free, physically-motivated} model reproduces \emph{all}
the qualitative features the paper reports simultaneously and consistently.
\item \textbf{Units} are dimensionless ($t_x=1$; $\hbar=1$). OAM values are in
units of $\hbar$; $|L_x|\to1$ is the maximal $p$-orbital OAM.
\item \textbf{CD-ARPES not modeled.} We compute the intrinsic $L_x(\bm{k})$
directly; we do not simulate the photoemission matrix elements that turn $L_x$
into a CD intensity. The paper's Eq.~(3) proportionality
$I_{\rm CD}\propto\bm{L}\!\cdot\!\hat{\bm q}$ is assumed.
\end{itemize}
