# Brief

Independent from-scratch (numpy-only) replication of the **lightning Laplace solver** of Gopal & Trefethen, *"New Laplace and Helmholtz solvers"* (PNAS 116(21):10223–10225, 2019; arXiv:1902.00374). The method represents a harmonic solution as `u(z)=Re r(z)` with `r` a rational + polynomial function whose poles are *fixed* and exponentially clustered outside the domain near each corner, reducing the Laplace–Dirichlet problem to a linear least-squares fit on the boundary. We reproduced (a) the paper's NA-Digest L-shape challenge value `u(0.99,0.99)` (paper: `1.02679192610`) to ~7–8 significant digits, and (b) the claimed root-exponential convergence `error ~ exp(-C√N)`. Solver correctness was independently validated to machine precision (3.6e-14) against an exact harmonic boundary function `Re(z³)`. Verdict: **PARTIAL** (core method + convergence confirmed; full 10-digit precision not reached with the simpler uniform clustering).
