\section{Open Questions}

The following questions are genuinely open after this replication --- each is a concrete
follow-up that could be executed on free endpoints (local numpy/scipy/qiskit-aer/PySCF),
does \emph{not} duplicate work already done in the paper, and would materially strengthen
the evidentiary base for (or against) the paper's headline claims.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Does Lemma~2 LCU compilation actually deliver $L$-independent gate scaling
  in practice, on a mid-sized molecular Hamiltonian?}
  \emph{Basis:} This replication substituted an exact statevector oracle for $U_j$,
  bypassing the paper's central algorithmic novelty. Confirming that the compiled $U_j$
  inside Alg.~1 preserves shot-noise scaling (rather than injecting compilation bias
  that dominates at moderate $\delta$) is unaddressed here.
  \emph{Next steps:} Implement Lemma~2 (random LCU with qDRIFT-style term sampling) in
  Qiskit or Cirq for a 6--8 qubit molecule (H$_2$, LiH in STO-3G) with $L\sim 15$--$30$
  Pauli terms. Sweep $\delta$ (compile precision), measure whether
  $\mathrm{std}[\tilde C(x_0)]$ still follows $N^{-1/2}$ at fixed compile cost, and
  whether effective compile bias tracks the paper's $O(\delta)$ prediction. Validate
  against a Qiskit exact $e^{iHt}$ reference. Free (local statevector).

\item \textbf{Head-to-head shot-budget comparison against Bayesian QPE and classical-shadow
  energy estimation?}
  \emph{Basis:} No comparison against Wiebe--Granade Bayesian QPE, robust phase
  estimation (Kimmel et al.), or shadow-based energy estimation (Huang--Kueng--Preskill)
  was attempted here. The paper's Toffoli-count comparison against qDRIFT is quoted, but
  shot-count comparisons vs.\ these newer estimators are the more relevant NISQ-era
  benchmark.
  \emph{Next steps:} Fix a small problem (H$_2$ or LiH, exact FCI known). Implement all
  three estimators in statevector numpy; sweep total shot budget $10^3$--$10^6$; plot RMS
  energy error vs.\ shots; report break-even points as a function of overlap $\eta$.

\item \textbf{Noise-robust randomization schedules?}
  \emph{Basis:} Every simulation here was noiseless. The paper's
  error-suppression-by-sampling argument implicitly assumes coherent errors in $U_j$
  compilation; incoherent hardware noise on the Hadamard-test ancilla (bias in Re/Im
  estimator) changes the story.
  \emph{Next steps:} Add per-shot depolarizing / amplitude-damping to the Hadamard-test
  simulator (qiskit-aer, $p\in\{10^{-4}, 10^{-3}, 10^{-2}\}$). Measure whether $N^{-1/2}$
  slope survives and at what shot count noise-induced bias dominates variance. Then test
  whether reweighting $F_j$ to prefer smaller $|j|$ (shallower $U_j$) recovers precision
  at fixed physical shots.

\item \textbf{Chemical-accuracy validation on a real chemistry Hamiltonian (H$_2$, LiH
  dissociation curves)?}
  \emph{Basis:} The reproduction here uses a 2-qubit TFIM, chosen for laptop
  tractability. The paper's motivating application is chemistry (FeMoco); no chemistry
  Hamiltonian was tested. Whether the \emph{constants} (not just the scaling) yield
  chemical accuracy at plausible shot counts is unaddressed.
  \emph{Next steps:} Build H$_2$/STO-3G via PySCF; compute FCI ground truth at 10 bond
  lengths; run Alg.~1 with exact-$U_j$ oracle; report shot count needed for
  $1.6~\mathrm{mHa}$ at each bond length; compare against the paper's
  $O(\lambda^2/\Delta^2\eta^2)$ prediction using real Hamiltonian $\lambda,\Delta,\eta$.

\item \textbf{Hybrid randomized $+$ adaptive protocol?}
  \emph{Basis:} Alg.~1 uses a fixed pre-sampled distribution over $j$. Modern Bayesian
  QPE demonstrates adaptive choice of query $t$ (and prior updates) can reduce sample
  counts by $10$--$100\times$ on the same underlying oracle. Whether the paper's
  $L$-independence argument survives an adaptive outer loop is open.
  \emph{Next steps:} Prototype an adaptive wrapper: maintain a Gaussian posterior over
  $E_\text{gs}$; at each step choose $j$ (equivalently $t_j$) that maximizes expected
  Fisher information at the current posterior mean; update posterior on the Hadamard-test
  outcome. Compare on the TFIM used here against Alg.~1 fixed schedule at matched
  shots. Argue analytically whether adaptive $j$-selection breaks the sum-over-$|F_j|$
  structure underlying $L$-independence.
\end{enumerate}
