                                                     Deinococcaceae and Trueperaceae (Ho et al., 2016). The Deinococcaceae family encompasses two
                                                     genera: Deinococcus, which currently has 89 species with validly published names, and
                                                     Deinobacterium, which only comprises one species, Deinobacterium chartae (Parte et al., 2020).
