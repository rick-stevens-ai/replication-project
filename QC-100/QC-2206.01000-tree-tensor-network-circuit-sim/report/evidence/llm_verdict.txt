VERDICT: PARTIAL  
JUSTIFICATION: The tree-clusterable circuit shows the expected signature: fidelity increases with χ and TTN clearly outperforms MPS at the same χ, converging to exact by χ≈16. However, the “hard” circuit does not match the paper’s claim that both are hard—TTN reaches very high fidelity (0.892 at χ=32) while MPS remains poor, suggesting the TTN construction/compression method here is substantially stronger than the circuit-evolution TTN setting being compared in the paper.
