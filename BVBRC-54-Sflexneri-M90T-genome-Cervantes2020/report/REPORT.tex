\section{Paper Under Test}
Cervantes-Rivera R, Tronnet S, Puhar A. ``Complete genome sequence and annotation of the laboratory reference strain \textit{Shigella flexneri} serotype 5a M90T and genome-wide transcriptional start site determination.'' \textit{BMC Genomics} 21:285 (2020). DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-020-6565-5}{10.1186/s12864-020-6565-5}. PMID: 32252626. PMC7132871. Open access (CC BY 4.0).

\textit{S. flexneri} 5a M90T is one of the two flagship laboratory reference strains for \textit{Shigella} pathogenesis research. Prior to this paper, no complete genome existed --- only a gapped chromosome scaffold (annotated off serotype 5b 8401) plus an independently sequenced virulence plasmid (pWR501/AF348706). The paper closes that gap with a hybrid PacBio-SMRT (Canu 1.7, $\sim$157$\times$) + Illumina RNA-seq polish strategy, producing two circular replicons and reporting genome-wide transcriptional start sites (6723 primary + 7328 secondary TSS) via dRNA-seq.

\section{GENUINE CRITIQUE}

This section deliberately airs the honest weaknesses of this replication, distinct from the paper's own limitations.

\subsection{What this replication actually tested vs.\ what it claims}
The strongest of our exact matches (C1, C2) verify that \emph{the deposited assembly matches the numbers printed in the paper}. But \textbf{the deposited assembly \emph{is} the paper's own product} (submitter Ume\aa{}/MIMS, the paper's lab). So C1 and C2 are essentially self-consistency checks between the paper's text and the paper's own deposit --- valuable for detecting a transcription error, but \emph{not} an independent test of the assembly claim itself. A true independent replication of C1/C2 would download the raw PacBio subreads (or Illumina reads) and re-derive replicon count and lengths ab initio. We did not. That is precisely why the verdict is PARTIAL and not REPLICATED, and this section makes that boundary explicit.

\subsection{Coverage arithmetic and the ``5/7'' framing}
The report claims coverage 5/7 = 71\%. Two caveats: (i) C7 (``publicly deposited/usable'') is a near-tautological pass once we have already downloaded the record, so counting it towards the coverage ratio inflates the score; (ii) C4 is scored \emph{partial} (tRNA/rRNA hit; CDS/pseudogene within a few percent; IS not re-typed) but is folded into the ``5 tested'' bucket without discount. A more conservative accounting is 3 fully reproduced (C1, C2, C5) + 1 partial (C4) + 1 trivial (C7) + 2 not run (C3, C6) --- which is still consistent with a PARTIAL verdict but should not be described as ``71\% of claims verified'' without qualification.

\subsection{Annotation ``consistency'' is looser than it looks}
Prokka 1.12 (here) reports 5{,}004 CDS vs.\ the paper's 4{,}949 (Prokka + manual curation) vs.\ RefSeq PGAP's 4{,}053 protein-coding. The paper--vs--Prokka delta is $\sim$1\%, but the paper--vs--PGAP delta is $\sim$18\% --- a large gap that gets softened by phrasing (``a few percent''). The three pipelines are counting slightly different things (protein-coding CDS vs.\ all CDS incl.\ pseudogenes vs.\ curated). This is not evidence of a reproduction failure; it \emph{is} evidence that ``consistent annotation'' means less about \textit{Shigella} reference genomes than a naive reader would infer.

\subsection{IS-element count (402) was not directly tested}
The report acknowledges this. IS load is the signature evolutionary claim of the paper (a hallmark of \textit{Shigella} reductive evolution). We inferred it indirectly via pseudogene load. That is defensible but is \emph{not} the same as running ISfinder / ISsaga / ISEScan on the same assembly and comparing IS-family counts and coordinates. Anyone who wants to build on the 402-figure should re-run IS typing.

\subsection{Database vintage and reproducibility drift}
Specialty-gene calls (T3SS repertoire, PlasmidFinder IncFII) depend on VFDB/Victors/PlasmidFinder database snapshots (2026-Apr per report). Rerunning in six months against updated databases may add or drop borderline effector hits (e.g., some \textit{osp} family members). We did not pin database checksums in \texttt{evidence/}; anyone re-executing should record and freeze DB versions to make the T3SS list reproducible-in-time.

\subsection{LLM-judge scoring is not an independent oracle}
The verdict was assigned by an LLM judge (\texttt{argo:gpt-5.2}) reading the same evidence the analyst prepared. That is a useful consistency check on the write-up, but it is not an independent scientific arbiter --- it cannot detect e.g.\ a systematic assembly artifact that both the paper and our verification share (because both stem from the same NCBI record).

\subsection{Threats to validity acknowledged in-line}
See \S5 of the underlying report: the deposited assembly is the paper's own product; annotation-count comparisons cross pipeline boundaries; IS count was corroborated indirectly. These are honest limitations, not defects in the workflow.

\section*{Verdict}
\textbf{PARTIAL.} The deposited genome's core structural claims are reproduced to the base pair (two circular replicons; chromosome 4{,}596{,}714 bp; plasmid pWR100 232{,}195 bp), and the paper's central biology --- that pWR100 carries the complete Type-3 Secretion System --- is independently reconstructed via specialty-gene scanning. The raw-read de novo assembly step (C3) and the dRNA-seq TSS quantification (C6) were not re-executed, and C1/C2 verify self-consistency of the paper's own deposit rather than independent re-derivation from reads. This is PARTIAL, not full REPLICATED.
