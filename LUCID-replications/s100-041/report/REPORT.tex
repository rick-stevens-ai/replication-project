\section*{Paper under review}
Abolfath R, Grosshans D, Mohan R. \emph{Oxygen depletion in FLASH ultra-high-dose-rate radiotherapy: A molecular dynamics simulation.} Med.\ Phys.\ 47(12), Dec 2020. arXiv:2010.00744v1 [physics.med-ph]. DOI: 10.1002/mp.14548.

\section{Scope of the paper}
The paper proposes a three-stage multiscale pipeline linking (i) Geant4-DNA Monte Carlo attosecond track structure, (ii) Car--Parrinello MD (CPMD) / DFT of ionised H\textsubscript{2}O and O\textsubscript{2} disintegration on sub-fs timescales, and (iii) LAMMPS ReaxFF reactive-force-field simulation of ROS production and coupling into non-reactive oxygen species (NROS) chains out to $\sim$100 ps--1 ns, with GROMACS used for solvation. The simulation domain is a $2.6\times 2.6\times 6$~nm periodic water box containing a solvated ds-DNA fragment and variable O\textsubscript{2} concentration (hypoxic, physoxic $\sim$4--5\%, oxyic, supraoxic).

The MD half of the paper (Figs 1--5) is qualitative snapshot rendering; the \emph{quantitative} reproducible content is the coarse-grained macroscopic rate-equation model in \S II.B:
\begin{align}
\dot N_1 &= G - 2 D_f N_1^2 - D_f N_2 N_1 \label{eq:1}\\
\dot N_2 &= D_f N_1^2 \label{eq:2}
\end{align}
where $N_1$ is the ROS population (light species: $\cdot$OH, H\textsubscript{2}O\textsubscript{2}, O, O\textsubscript{2}\textsuperscript{$-$}, {}\textsuperscript{1}O\textsubscript{2}, $\alpha$-O), $N_2$ is the NROS chain population, $G = \mu_1(N_{O_2},N_{H_2O})\cdot\dot z$ is proportional to dose rate $\dot z$, and $D_f$ is an effective ROS diffusion/recombination constant. Dimensionless rescaling ($\ell_1=(D_f/G)^{1/6}$, $t_1=1/\sqrt{D_f G}$) yields analytical scaling limits $N_1\propto Gt$, $N_2\propto G^2 D_f t^3$ (short time) and $N_2\propto (G^2/D_f)^{1/3} t^{1/3}$ (long time), and the headline claim $N_2/N_1\propto G$ (FLASH favours NROS, CDR favours ROS).

\section{What was independently reproduced}
An independent numerical solution of Eqs.~\ref{eq:1}--\ref{eq:2} was implemented with \texttt{scipy.integrate.solve\_ivp} (Radau, stiff-tolerant), using the paper's exact pulse parameters ($G_1=100$~cm\textsuperscript{$-3$}s\textsuperscript{$-1$} for 0.01~s FLASH; $G_2=0.01$~cm\textsuperscript{$-3$}s\textsuperscript{$-1$} for 100~s CDR; $D_f=1$ in the paper's natural units), from zero initial conditions. Independent slope checks over four decades in $t$ recover the three analytical scaling laws (Eqs.~7--9).

\section{What was NOT independently reproduced}
The three MD stages (Geant4-DNA track structure, CPMD DFT, LAMMPS-ReaxFF chemistry) were \textbf{not} independently rerun. Those pipelines require Geant4-DNA + LAMMPS-ReaxFF + GROMACS on HPC; they were audited for internal logic and citation trail only. This means the entire \emph{mechanistic} narrative of the paper (Figs 1--5, the $\cdot$OH--$\cdot$OH coupling animations, the $\alpha$-oxygen intermediates, the DNA-lesion visualisations) remains unverified in this replication.

The rate-equation panel is a 2-ODE toy model that anyone can integrate from the printed equations. It is not, on its own, evidence for the mechanistic claim; it is a downstream consistency demonstration.

\section{Critique --- honest assessment}
This paper is best classified as a \emph{mechanism-proposal / hypothesis paper}, not a calibrated predictive model. The rate-equation section (\S II.B, Figs 6--7) is faithfully reproduced. The MD section (\S II.A, Figs 1--5), which occupies most of the text and figures, produces \textbf{no numerical output that could in principle be independently checked from the manuscript alone}: no G-values in molecules/100~eV, no $\mu$M/Gy O\textsubscript{2} consumption rate, no LAMMPS ReaxFF parameter files, no CPMD input decks, no Geant4-DNA macro, no code/data release (the only ``data'' link is a Facebook photo album, ref 23).

The paper's central quantitative claim --- \emph{``at longer times $N_2$ at UHDR is approximately twice that at CDR''} --- is architecturally an output of Eq.~\ref{eq:1}--\ref{eq:2} with the chosen pulse ratio. It is not a prediction that can be falsified against experiment because neither $G$ nor $D_f$ has been mapped to physical units. The authors are explicit about this in \S II.C: they treat $G$ and $D_f$ as phenomenological constants and select $G_1=100$, $G_2=0.01$ to \emph{illustrate the method}, not to match a specific Gy/s.

Consequently the connection to Montay-Gruel PNAS 2019 (lower H\textsubscript{2}O\textsubscript{2} under FLASH) and to the carbogen-reversal observation is \textbf{qualitative alignment only}: the model can \emph{accommodate} those observations but does not \emph{predict} them at the level of a $\mu$M concentration.

\subsection*{Reproducibility blockers (missing artifacts)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item No code/data availability statement; ref 23 is a Facebook URL to MD movies.
\item No Geant4-DNA macro / physics list / random seeds.
\item No CPMD input, DFT functional, basis, pseudopotential, or version.
\item No ReaxFF force-field parameter file for the DNA + water + O\textsubscript{2} + radical system (only ref 17, Rahaman 2011 glycine FF, cited).
\item No LAMMPS input deck or \texttt{pair\_style reax/c} settings.
\item No numerical value of $D_f$ (the single free constant of the rate equations).
\item No physical mapping $\mu_1(N_{O_2},N_{H_2O})\to \dot z$ [Gy/s]; the equation is defined but never evaluated.
\item No initial conditions $N_1(0), N_2(0)$; not stated whether the pulse starts on a clean system or on a pre-equilibrated background.
\item No quantitative $\Delta$O\textsubscript{2} vs $[\text{O}_2]_0$ sweep to support the ``physoxic 4--5\% optimum, reversed above 10--15\%'' claim; only MD snapshots.
\item No G-values (molecules/100~eV) for any radical species.
\end{enumerate}

The single most damaging missing artifact is $D_f$ + the $\mu_1\to\dot z$ mapping: those two numbers alone would elevate the paper from a mechanism-illustration to a calibrated, falsifiable prediction.
