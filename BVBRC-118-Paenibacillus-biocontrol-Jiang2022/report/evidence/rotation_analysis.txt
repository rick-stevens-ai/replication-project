Circular chromosome origin offset analysis (this Flye assembly vs paper's HGAP assembly)
========================================================================================
Paper Table 4 positions vs my Flye positions for the 5 named BGCs with explicit coordinates:

Compound      Paper_start   My_start     Offset_mod_6007189
fusaricidin   3,650,067     55,878       2,413,000
tridecaptin   89,772        2,492,487    2,402,715
polymyxin     2,710,256     5,115,859    2,405,603
pelgipeptin   485,090       2,657,996    2,172,906   (outlier — possibly labelled wrong)
paenilan      5,331,079     1,732,435    2,408,545

Four of five compounds yield a nearly identical rotation offset of ~2.405 Mb ±5 kb.
Interpretation: Flye and HGAP placed the chromosomal origin at different positions on
the same circular molecule (a well-known artifact of de novo assembly of circular
bacterial chromosomes with no reference orientation). Cluster spans (e.g. paenilan
27,007 bp mine vs 27,006 bp paper — Δ=1) are essentially identical, confirming the
gene-content correspondence is genuine.
