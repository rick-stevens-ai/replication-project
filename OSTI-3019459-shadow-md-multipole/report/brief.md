# Brief — OSTI-3019459

Corrigan Grove, Stanton, Wall, Niklasson (LANL) extend the shadow XL-BOMD framework from monopole-only charge-equilibration models to flexible multipole (monopole + dipole) models, deriving explicit shadow energy, potential, and force expressions and demonstrating on three solvated organic systems (acetamide/water etc.) that the dipole degrees of freedom preserve the O(δt²) energy-fluctuation scaling and 100 ps long-term energy stability seen in the monopole-only precursor. Independent replication (this work, 2026-07-02) fetched the OSTI PDF, audited the referenced SEDACS code (which as of head-of-main provides the monopole shadow solver but NOT the paper's flexible-multipole extension), and implemented a self-contained toy shadow XL-BOMD with monopole + dipole XL DOFs from first principles; the δt² scaling was reproduced to <2% error across four consecutive dt values (measured ratios 4.001/4.015/4.071 vs theoretical 4.000) and long-term energy drift was 0.22% of fluctuation amplitude over 10,000 steps. The paper's numerical claim regime is reproduced in principle, but a full rerun on the paper's exact 93/162/263-atom solvated systems is blocked by the not-yet-released flexible-multipole implementation.
