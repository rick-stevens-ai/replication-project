# Brief

Independent replication of Onal & Esen (2020), "A Crank-Nicolson Approximation for the time Fractional Burgers Equation" (Appl. Math. Nonlinear Sci. 5(2) 177–184, doi:10.2478/amns.2020.2.00023). We re-implemented, from scratch in NumPy, the paper's Crank–Nicolson finite-difference scheme with L1 discretization of the Caputo time-fractional derivative for `D_t^γ u + u u_x − ν u_xx = f`, and reproduced the paper's headline error-norm tables against three manufactured (method-of-manufactured-solutions) test problems with known analytic solutions. **We reproduce Example 1's Tables 1, 2 (Present), and 4 to 8 significant figures (0.000% deviation).** We independently re-derived all three forcing terms symbolically and confirmed they match the paper exactly. The Example 2/3 tables (5–7) and Table 3 do not reproduce and are shown to be internally inconsistent within the paper itself, so the discrepancy is attributable to the paper, not our re-implementation.
