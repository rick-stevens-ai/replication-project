# Method Extract — Schütte & Garst 2014 (arXiv:1405.1568)
**Title:** Magnon-skyrmion scattering in chiral magnets
**Texture class:** polar (magnetic skyrmion spin texture in chiral magnet; "polar" per taxonomy)

- **Core physics:** Analytic study of **magnon scattering off a single skyrmion** in a clean 2D chiral (DMI) magnet in the field-polarized state. Skyrmion = large-amplitude excitation; magnons = small fluctuations on top.
- **Headline claims (replication targets):**
  1. Magnon spectrum has **two magnon-skyrmion bound states**: a breathing mode and (at intermediate field) a quadrupolar mode → subgap magnetic/electric resonances.
  2. Magnons scatter from an effective **Aharonov-Bohm flux** (from skyrmion topology) → **skew and rainbow scattering** with asymmetric differential cross section (multiple peaks).
  3. Skew scattering → **topological magnon Hall effect**; magnon current exerts a reactive **momentum-transfer force** in the Thiele equation → skyrmion velocity + large skyrmion Hall effect.
- **Key equations:** Linearized LLG / Bogoliubov-de-Gennes-type magnon eigenproblem around the skyrmion profile; scattering phase shifts / differential cross section dσ/dθ; energy-momentum tensor conservation → Thiele momentum-transfer force. DMI + exchange + Zeeman Hamiltonian.
- **DFT-heavy or theory?** **THEORY / ANALYTIC — tractable in-process.** Solve the magnon eigenvalue/scattering problem numerically around a numerically-relaxed skyrmion. Partial-wave scattering + bound-state search. No DFT/cluster. Good (moderately involved) replication candidate; bound-state frequencies + cross-section skew asymmetry are the checkable outputs.
