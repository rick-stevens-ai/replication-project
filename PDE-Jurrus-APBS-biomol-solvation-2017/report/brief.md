# Brief

Jurrus et al. 2017 "Improvements to the APBS biomolecular solvation software suite" (Protein Science 27:112–128, DOI 10.1002/pro.3280) is a software-overview paper cataloguing new capabilities added to the APBS + PDB2PQR Poisson–Boltzmann electrostatics toolkit since its 2001 release: PB-AM/PB-SAM analytical solvers, TABI-PB boundary-element solver, geometric-flow solvation, graph-theory pKa, and 3Dmol.js web visualization. We independently installed APBS 3.4.1 + PDB2PQR 3.6.1 via conda-forge on uicgpu (8×A100 host), downloaded 1FAS (fasciculin-2 — the paper's own Figure 4/5 demo protein) and 1CRN (crambin) from RCSB, and executed the paper's canonical workflow (pdb→pdb2pqr→apbs) under linearized PBE, nonlinear PBE, and 0/0.15M NaCl regimes. All runs converged in 1.5–4 s; total electrostatic energy = 1.096×10⁵ kJ/mol (1FAS) and 3.501×10⁴ kJ/mol (1CRN); potential grids show physically-consistent behavior (positive mean potential for +4e-charged 1FAS, near-zero mean for neutral 1CRN, correct Debye screening at 7.86 Å for 0.15M salt). Independent LLM judge (argo:gpt-5.2) verdict: **PARTIAL** — the mainstream mg-auto multigrid workflow is fully reproduced end-to-end, but the paper's headline new-since-2001 solvers (PB-AM, PB-SAM, TABI-PB, geometric-flow) were not individually exercised.
