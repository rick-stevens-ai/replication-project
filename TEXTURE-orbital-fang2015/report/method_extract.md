**Title:** First-principles studies of multiferroic and magnetoelectric materials
**Authors:** Y.-W. Fang, H.-C. Ding, W.-Y. Tong, ... C.-G. Duan (ECNU / Nanjing). Texture class: **orbital**.

- **Core nature:** This is a **REVIEW ARTICLE**, not a single-result research paper. It surveys the application of first-principles (DFT) methods to multiferroics, magnetoelectric effect, and magnetic tunnel junctions.
- **Content:** Introduces the authors' own methods, notably the **orbital selective external potential (OSEP)** method, and reviews three classes of electric-field control of magnetism. Covers origin of ferroelectricity in transition-metal oxides and coexistence of ferroelectricity + magnetism.
- **Headline "claims" (review):** (1) OSEP is a useful tool to isolate orbital contributions to multiferroic mechanisms; (2) first-principles DFT explains and predicts magnetoelectric/multiferroic behavior; (3) surveys electric control of magnetism.
- **Replication target:** There is **no single crisp experiment/number to replicate** — it is a review. A meaningful "replication" would mean reproducing one representative DFT calculation cited (e.g., an OSEP demonstration on a specific oxide), which is itself a DFT job.
- **Compute profile:** **DFT-heavy** by nature (every concrete example is a plane-wave DFT calculation). As a review it is arguably a **poor replication target** — flag for possible substitution or narrow to one sub-result.
