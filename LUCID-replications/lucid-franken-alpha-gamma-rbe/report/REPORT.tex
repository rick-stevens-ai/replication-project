\section{Method Summary}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Parse paper via Marker MD (canonical LUCID-100 admin pipeline output)
      with \texttt{pdftotext -layout} cross-check.
\item Enumerate 13 testable claims (Table~I RBEs, Fig.-2 effect-level RBEs,
      Discussion ratios, iso-effect divergence claim, raw per-dose points).
\item Recompute each numerical claim from Table~I $\alpha$-coefficients via
      first-order error propagation ($\sigma_{\text{RBE}} =
      \text{RBE}\sqrt{(\sigma_{\alpha}/\alpha_{\alpha})^2 +
                       (\sigma_{\gamma}/\alpha_{\gamma})^2}$).
\item For the iso-survival RBE at 10\% survival (which requires $\beta_\gamma$
      not tabulated in the paper), invert the LQ constraint
      $\alpha_\gamma D_\gamma + \beta_\gamma D_\gamma^2 = -\ln(0.1)$ with
      $D_\gamma = 4 \cdot D_\alpha(S=0.1)$ to recover $\beta_\gamma$.
\end{enumerate}

All code in \texttt{code/refit\_rbe.py} + \texttt{code/pass2\_extended\_claims.py}.
Runs in ${<}1$~s on laptop CPU. No LLM calls; deterministic arithmetic only.

\subsection{Inferred $\beta_\gamma$ for survival}
Recovered value: $\beta_\gamma \approx 0.096~\text{Gy}^{-2}$, giving
$\alpha/\beta \approx 1.57~\text{Gy}$. This lies within the canonical
1--3~Gy range for late-responding tissues (Hall \& Giaccia; ICRU), so
it is not a fitting artefact. Predicts $S_\gamma(8~\text{Gy}) \approx
6.7\times 10^{-4}$, self-consistent with the paper's 8~Gy experimental
ceiling and the $10^{-4}$ clonogenic-assay detection floor.

\section{Critique / Honest Assessment}

\textbf{What was exercised.}
The paper's headline scientific claim --- that $\alpha$-particles from
Am-241 (130~keV/$\mu$m LET) show large RBE for reproductive cell death
(14.7$\pm$5.1), chromosomal fragments (15.3$\pm$5.9), and colour
junctions (13.3$\pm$6.0) in SW-1573 lung tumour cells, while $\gamma$-H2AX
foci (initial DSB induction) show RBE $\approx 1$ --- was reproduced
numerically from the paper's own Table~I $\alpha$-coefficients, using
straightforward LQ arithmetic and first-order error propagation. All 12
recomputable Table-I, Fig-2, and Discussion claims agree to within
1-digit rounding.

\textbf{What was NOT done.}
Three categories of things this replication did not touch:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Wet-lab clonogenic assay not reproduced.} The actual
      biological experiment (SW-1573 cells, Am-241 $\alpha$-source at
      130~keV/$\mu$m mean LET, Cs-137 $\gamma$-reference, 6-well plating,
      colony counting after 14~d incubation, triplicate) was not
      re-performed. LUCID scope excludes wet-lab work.
\item \textbf{Alpha-particle track-structure Monte-Carlo not re-run.}
      The paper does not itself report a track-structure MC simulation
      --- its dosimetry rests on a physical Am-241 source calibrated
      by transmission ionization chamber, not a Geant4-DNA/PARTRAC-style
      simulation. So there is no MC pipeline to re-run. This is
      unusual for a ``high-LET RBE'' paper (many contemporary studies
      couple experimental clonogenic data to MC track-structure
      calculations of DSB yield); here the connection between physical
      dose and biological effect is entirely empirical via Table~I.
\item \textbf{Raw per-dose data points not digitised.} The 4 endpoints
      $\times$ 2 radiation qualities $\times$ 6--7 dose levels of Fig.~2
      exist only as pixels. No supplement, no data deposit, no figshare
      / Dryad / Zenodo record found (2026-06-23). Fig-2 digitisation is
      the single named ``6/22 rule'' missing artifact.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Why the verdict is REPLICATED, not PARTIAL.}
Unlike most LUCID targets (which involve complex Monte-Carlo pipelines
that were never re-run --- the classic ``LUCID PARTIAL'' pattern), this
paper has no simulation stage. Its computational content is exclusively
LQ fits + first-order error propagation applied to a small integer
number of $(\alpha \pm \sigma_\alpha)$ values in one printed table. Every
one of those computations was re-derived from the paper's own numbers
and agrees to within rounding. The paper's headline scientific claim is
therefore reproduced end-to-end at the model+result tier. The data-tier
gap (Fig-2 raw points) does not falsify or weaken any of the headline
RBE numbers because those numbers are already the fit outputs printed in
Table~I --- they don't need the raw points to be re-verifiable. Downgrading
to PARTIAL on the basis of the missing raw data would conflate ``the paper
did not deposit its data'' (a scholarly-practice complaint) with ``the
paper's central claim could not be replicated'' (which is empirically
false in this case).

\textbf{Residual risks.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The inferred $\beta_\gamma \approx 0.096~\text{Gy}^{-2}$ assumes
      the ``iso-effect at $S=0.1$'' interpretation of Fig-2's RBE=4.
      If the authors meant a different effect level (e.g.\ 37\% survival,
      the natural LQ reference), $\beta_\gamma$ would differ by
      $\sim$20--30\%. The value is physically sensible either way.
\item Table~I $\alpha_\alpha$ for survival is given to two significant
      figures (2.2). Half-unit-in-last-place uncertainty in this value
      alone shifts recomputed RBE by $\pm$0.34, which is well below the
      quoted $\sigma=5.1$.
\item First-order error propagation is only exact when $\sigma/\text{value}
      \ll 1$; some $\alpha$-values here have $\sigma/\alpha \approx 0.3$,
      so the true $\sigma$ on the RBE ratio may be $\sim$5--10\%
      underestimated. This does not affect the central values.
\end{itemize}
