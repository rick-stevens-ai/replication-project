\section{Target and headline}
The paper computes the \emph{clamped} Pockels tensor of tetragonal BaTiO$_3$ (BTO)
from DFT (Quantum ESPRESSO), the modern theory of polarization, and finite-difference
electro-optic derivatives (AiiDA--Vibroscopy). The technological headline is the largest
Pockels coefficient $r_{51}$, experimentally $730\pm150$\,pm/V when clamped.

\textbf{Corpus mislabel.} The directory is named \texttt{textures-spin-mestral2025},
but the paper contains \emph{no spin physics}; it is a ferroelectric electro-optics
study. The provided \texttt{gobel2024} skyrmion Kubo kernel and \texttt{spin\_ed\_probes}
were inspected and found irrelevant, so they were not used. We replicated the paper's
actual physics.

\section{Physics replicated}
Per Eq.~(4) of the paper, the ionic Pockels response is a sum over $\Gamma$-point
optical modes,
\begin{equation}
r_{ijk} = -\frac{1}{n_i^2 n_j^2}\chi^{(2)}_{ijk}
          -\frac{1}{n_i^2 n_j^2}\sum_m \frac{\alpha^m_{ij}\,p_{m,k}}{\omega_m^2},
\end{equation}
with $\alpha^m$ the Raman susceptibility, $p_{m,k}$ the mode polarity, and $\omega_m$
the mode frequency. Because the in-plane \emph{soft} optical mode has the smallest
$\omega_m$, the $1/\omega_m^2$ factor makes $r_{51}$ soft-mode dominated: as Ti
off-centering is reduced, the soft mode softens and $r_{51}$ diverges.

\paragraph{Model 1 (mode-sum, $1/\omega^2$).} We calibrate
$K=\alpha p/(n_i n_j)$ once at the P4bm ground state (0.466\%, $r_{51}=391.2$\,pm/V,
$\omega=2.0$\,THz) and \emph{predict} the rest of the Table~IV series from the paper's
$\omega$ values alone.

\paragraph{Model 2 (Landau double-well soft mode).} Free energy
$F(Q)=\tfrac12 aQ^2+\tfrac14 bQ^4$ with $a<0,b>0$ gives
$\omega^2(Q)\propto 3q^2-1$ ($q=Q/Q_0$), reproducing the softening trend as
off-centering $Q$ decreases below the ground state.

At 0.45\% displacement the model gives $r_{51}=695.5$\,pm/V, inside the experimental
band $730\pm150$\,pm/V, reproducing the paper's central claim. The crude Landau
$\omega(\text{disp})$ model is qualitatively correct (softening as off-centering drops)
but quantitatively poor (MARE $\approx33\%$): the true $\omega$ collapse is far steeper
than a simple quartic double-well, reflecting strong anharmonicity near the transition.
