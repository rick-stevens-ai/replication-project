# Brief — Brouste et al. 2014, YUIMA (JSS v57i04)

The YUIMA Project paper (Brouste, Fukasawa, Hino, Iacus, Kamatani, Koike, Masuda, Nomura, Ogihara, Shimuzu, Uchida, Yoshida — J. Statistical Software 57(4), 2014, DOI 10.18637/jss.v057.i04) introduces the open-source R package `yuima` for simulation and inference of stochastic differential equations (Wiener/fractional Brownian/Lévy noise, multidimensional, S4 class system). It walks through model construction (`setModel`, `setSampling`, `setYuima`, `simulate`), asymptotic expansion of functionals (`setFunctional`, `asymptotic_term`) for option pricing on CIR/BS-type SDEs, and inference — quasi-maximum likelihood (`qmle`), adaptive Bayes (`adaBayes`), change-point (`CPoint`, `qmleL`, `qmleR`), and LASSO. This replication independently reruns three of the paper's fully-specified, seed-fixed numerical examples (QMLE at n=750 and n=500, asymptotic expansion for a CIR European call, and 2-D volatility change-point estimation) against modern `yuima 1.15.34` on the same seeds, and compares the outputs to the paper's printed numbers.
