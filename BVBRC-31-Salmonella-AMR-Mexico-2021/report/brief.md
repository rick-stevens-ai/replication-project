# Brief — BVBRC-31

Independent replication of Delgado-Suarez et al. 2021 (PLoS ONE, PMID 33951039), a genomic-surveillance study of antimicrobial resistance in non-typhoidal *Salmonella* (NTS) from Mexican cattle lymph nodes and ground beef. **What/why:** the paper reports the genotypic AMR landscape of 77 NTS isolates (BioProject PRJNA480281) — serovar distribution, per-class resistance prevalence, ~26% multi-drug-resistance (MDR), a source-attribution signal (more MDR in ground beef than lymph nodes), and that ~40% of MDR strains are serovar Typhimurium carrying *Salmonella* genomic island 1 (SGI1) with a class-1 integron penta-resistance cassette (aadA2, blaCARB-2, floR, sul1, tetG). I re-ran the paper's actual genotypic pipeline — SeqSero2 serovar typing, AMRFinderPlus AMR-gene profiling, and MLST — on **68 of the 77 study genomes pulled directly from NCBI**, then tallied class prevalence, MDR, source attribution, and the SGI1 cassette independently. Verdict: **PARTIAL** — serovar calls (67/68), overall MDR prevalence (23.5% vs 26%), the Typhimurium MDR fraction (37.5% vs 40%), and the exact SGI1 penta-resistance gene set (6/7 Typhimurium) reproduce cleanly on real data; the source-attribution significance and the point-mutation (QRDR/ramR) claims were not reproduced under standard tool catalogs.
