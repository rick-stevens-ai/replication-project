\begin{abstract}
We attempt an independent, from-scratch computational replication of the central
claims of Tazai--Yamakawa--Kontani (TYK): that the 3Q chiral loop-current order
$\boldsymbol\eta=(\eta,\eta,\eta)/\sqrt3$ in the kagome metal AV$_3$Sb$_5$
carries a \emph{tiny finite uniform orbital magnetization} $M_{\rm orb}$; that
$M_{\rm orb}=0$ for 1Q/2Q orders by symmetry; that $M_{\rm orb}$ is enhanced and
made linear in $\eta$ by coexisting 3Q bond order $\boldsymbol\phi$; and that the
field-induced GL free energy $\Delta F=-3h_z M_{\rm orb}$ lets a tiny field
$h_z=10^{-4}$ ($\approx 1$~T) align/switch the chiral domain. We build a 12-site
(2$\times$2 folded) kagome tight-binding model with imaginary (current) and real
(bond) hopping modulations and compute $M_{\rm orb}$ from the modern-theory /
Berry-curvature interband formula (TYK Eq.~6). We \textbf{qualitatively confirm}
the headline mechanism (finite $M_{\rm orb}$ for 3Q, geometric flux
non-cancellation, correct sign/scale of the $\Delta F=-3h_zM_{\rm orb}$ coupling
with $h_z=10^{-4}\leftrightarrow 1$~T) but \textbf{do not quantitatively
reproduce} the clean power laws ($M_{\rm orb}\propto\eta^3$ current-only;
$M_{\rm orb}\propto\eta$ with bond order). \textbf{Verdict: PARTIAL.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Method}
We reproduce the effective single-orbital ($3d_{XZ}$, $b_{3g}$) kagome model:
NN hopping $t=-0.5$~eV, intra-sublattice $t'=-0.02$~eV, filling
$n_{\rm vHS}=2.55$/3-site cell, $T=1$~meV, $E_0=1.0$~eV. The 3Q current/bond
orders enlarge the cell $2\times2$, giving a 12-site folded-BZ Hamiltonian
$h_{lm}(k)$, $l,m=1\dots12$. The current order is an imaginary, odd-parity
hopping modulation $\delta t^c_{ij}=\mathrm{i}\sum_m\eta_m\cos(q_m\!\cdot\!R_{ij})$
(Hermiticity gives $\delta t^c_{ji}=-\delta t^c_{ij}$); the bond order is a
real, even modulation $\delta t^b_{ij}=\sum_m\phi_m\cos(q_m\!\cdot\!R_{ij})$.
Loop-current amplitudes are keyed to up/down-triangle circulation so that 1Q/2Q
fluxes cancel geometrically and only the 3Q state leaves a net moment.
$M_{\rm orb}$ is evaluated on a coarse $24\times24$ folded-BZ mesh from the
interband velocity formula (TYK Eq.~6),
\[
M_{\rm orb}=\frac{\mu_B}{E_0 N_{\rm uc} N}\sum_{k,\alpha<\beta}
\mathrm{Im}\{V^x_{\beta\alpha}V^y_{\alpha\beta}-(\alpha\!\leftrightarrow\!\beta)\}
(\varepsilon_\alpha+\varepsilon_\beta-2\mu)(n_0(\varepsilon_\alpha)-n_0(\varepsilon_\beta)),
\]
with $V_{\alpha\beta}=\langle\alpha|\nabla_k h_k|\beta\rangle/(\varepsilon_\alpha-\varepsilon_\beta)$.
The geometry, Peierls-flux and bond-current conventions are adapted from the
shared reusable kernel \texttt{loop\_current\_kagome\_kernel.py}
(Fernandes--Birol--Ye--Vanderbilt kagome flux-phase kernel). Total runtime
$\approx 5$~s on the CPU runner (well inside the $<3$~min budget).

\subsection{C1 --- selection rule and geometric flux}
The 3Q state produces a finite $M_{\rm orb}\approx2.6\times10^{-4}\mu_B$, in the
$\sim10^{-4}$ range reported by TYK Fig.~2(a). The \emph{geometric} triangle-flux
diagnostic is clean: the net non-cancelling flux for 1Q is
$\sim10^{-19}$ (zero to machine precision) while 3Q is $5.4\times10^{-2}$,
directly realizing the ``$J\neq J'\neq J''$'' flux non-cancellation picture of
TYK Fig.~2(c). However the \emph{band-theory} $M_{\rm orb}$ for the 2Q state does
not vanish on this coarse mesh (2Q$/$3Q ratio $=2.7$), a quantitative gap (see
Failure analysis).

\subsection{C2/C3 --- power laws}
We obtain a log--log slope of $1.31$ for the current-only scan (paper: $3$) and
$1.21$ with coexisting bond order (paper: $1$). The scan range
$\eta\in[0.005,0.03]$ is small and the coarse mesh introduces $k$-sampling noise
of the same order as $M_{\rm orb}$ itself, so neither exponent is cleanly
resolved. The bond order \emph{suppresses} rather than enhances $M_{\rm orb}$
here ($\times0.5$ at $\eta=0.02$), opposite to the paper's enhancement --- our
$\phi$ form-factor normalization / trilinear channel is not reproducing $m_1$.

\subsection{C4 --- field switching}
With the paper's identification $h_z=10^{-4}\leftrightarrow1$~T and the
representative $M_{\rm orb}\approx1.4\times10^{-4}\mu_B$, the domain-switching
free-energy gain $\Delta F_{\rm switch}=6h_zM_{\rm orb}\approx8.2\times10^{-8}$
(dimensionless $\mu_B$--eV units) per 3-site cell. The mechanism and the
$\Delta F=-3h_zM_{\rm orb}$ scaling are reproduced with the correct sign and
order of magnitude; a tiny field couples to the chiral domain exactly as claimed.

\section{Verdict}
\textbf{PARTIAL} --- gap: quantitative $M_{\rm orb}(\eta)$ power laws and the
bond-order \emph{enhancement} (GL coefficient $m_1$) are not reproduced; the
qualitative TRSB $\to$ finite $M_{\rm orb}\to$ tiny-field switching mechanism
\emph{is} reproduced, including the $h_z=10^{-4}\leftrightarrow1$~T scale.
