# Artifact Harvest

The paper is a **pure numerical-analysis paper**; there is no companion
dataset or public code release to download. The mathematical objects (the
SSP-RK2 / SSP-RK3 schemes) are the artifact and are fully specified by the
paper's equations (4.1) and (4.2). All numerics in this replication are
generated by our own local NumPy implementation.
