\begin{abstract}
We independently attempt to reproduce the $\alpha$-quartz portion of Koneru et al.\ (2026, \textit{JCTC}, DOI 10.1021/acs.jctc.5c01885), which introduces two Tersoff-form silica potentials (Q-Tersoff, ML-Tersoff) trained by hierarchical reinforcement learning on 21 IZA polymorphs. Using the exact potential files and $\alpha$-quartz seed the authors released on GitHub, and running LAMMPS 29Aug2024 on UICGPU, we find that under every relaxation protocol (verbatim \texttt{in.relax}, 0\,K \texttt{aniso}/\texttt{tri} box relaxation, and 298\,K NPT), the $\alpha$-quartz density comes out $20$--$40\%$ below experiment, versus the paper's headline claim of $\sim 0\%$ density error for Q-Tersoff. Both potentials find a non-$\alpha$-quartz global minimum where the hexagonal cell dilates $\sim 50\%$ along the $c$-axis and Si--O--Si angles drive to $168$--$175^{\circ}$. Two independent LLM judges (argo:gpt-5.1 and argo:gemini-2.5-pro) render \textbf{CONTRADICTED} verdicts on the $\alpha$-quartz subset. The full 21-polymorph benchmark cannot be tested here because the other 20 structure files were not shipped in the public repo.
\end{abstract}

\section{What worked / what didn't per claim}

\subsection{C2 --- $\alpha$-quartz density (\FAIL)}
Paper's Fig.\ 4 heatmap: Q-Tersoff density error $\sim 0\%$; ML-Tersoff $\sim 9.2\%$; experimental $\rho = 2.648$~g/cm$^3$.

Our measurements (5$\times$5$\times$5 replicate = 1125 atoms, LAMMPS 29Aug2024):
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
Protocol & ML-Tersoff $\rho$ & ML err (\%) & Q-Tersoff $\rho$ & Q err (\%) \\
\midrule
Verbatim in.relax (iso + NVE) & 1.591 & 39.9 & 1.735 & 34.5 \\
0\,K aniso box/relax          & 1.949 & 26.4 & 2.100 & 20.7 \\
0\,K tri  box/relax           & 1.949 & 26.4 & 2.100 & 20.7 \\
NPT 298\,K, 20\,ps            & 1.856 & 29.9 & 2.029 & 23.4 \\
\midrule
\textbf{Paper claim}          & \textbf{2.404} & \textbf{9.2} & $\approx$ \textbf{2.648} & $\approx$ \textbf{0} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
No protocol reproduces the paper's density numbers. What worked: LAMMPS runs execute cleanly, thermodynamic output is well-converged, and different protocols agree with each other to $< 5\%$. What didn't: the potential's actual equilibrium density is $\sim 2.0$~g/cm$^3$ for both variants, not the $2.4$--$2.65$~g/cm$^3$ implied by the paper.

\subsection{C2 --- $\alpha$-quartz Si--O--Si angle (\FAIL for ML-Tersoff, \PARTIAL for Q-Tersoff)}
Experimental Si--O--Si $= 143.7^{\circ}$; O--Si--O $= 109.47^{\circ}$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
Model (298\,K NPT) & Si--O--Si mean $\pm\sigma$ & Si--O--Si err & O--Si--O mean $\pm\sigma$ & O--Si--O err \\
\midrule
Q-Tersoff (ours)    & $147.55 \pm 27.5$ & $+3.85^{\circ}$ & $108.22 \pm 26.0$ & $-1.25^{\circ}$ \\
Q-Tersoff (paper)   & 145.4             & $1.7^{\circ}$   & 109.77            & $0.3^{\circ}$   \\
ML-Tersoff (ours)   & $128.68 \pm 28.7$ & $\mathbf{-15.02^{\circ}}$ & $105.95 \pm 35.3$ & $-3.52^{\circ}$ \\
ML-Tersoff (paper)  & 150.7             & $+7.0^{\circ}$  & 109.87            & $0.4^{\circ}$   \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Q-Tersoff: within a factor of $\sim 2$--$4$ of the paper's error, same sign, order of magnitude consistent. \PARTIAL.
ML-Tersoff: wrong sign, doubled magnitude, distribution std $\gg$ mean displacement. \FAIL.

\subsection{C6 --- tetrahedral preservation (\FAIL for ML-Tersoff)}
Coordination-number histograms at 298\,K NPT:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Model & \% 4-fold Si & other Si coord \\
\midrule
Q-Tersoff  & 97.6 & 2.4\% 5-fold \\
ML-Tersoff & \textbf{77.0} & 11\% 3-fold, 9\% 5-fold, $\sim 3\%$ others \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Q-Tersoff preserves the tetrahedral network as the paper claims. ML-Tersoff does not: nearly a quarter of Si atoms are non-tetrahedral, and 8\% of O atoms have only one Si neighbour at 298\,K. This is a clear coordination-number breakdown at room temperature, not disclosed in the paper.

\subsection{C3 --- timing (\PARTIAL)}
Both potentials ran at 213 timesteps/s = 0.24~M~atom-step/s per CPU core on the 5$\times$5$\times$5 cell (1125 atoms). This is consistent with published Tersoff throughput. We did not independently run GAP, so the ``$\ge 100\times$ faster than GAP'' claim is plausible but not measured this run.

\subsection{C1, C4, C5 --- \BLOCKED}
These require additional structure files (C1: 20 IZA polymorph geometries), additional evaluation scripts (C4: finite-strain elastic-constant protocol), or long GPU runs (C5: melt-quench for amorphous silica). None of those are shipped in the released repo.

\item Ship evidence packet to \texttt{argo:gpt-5.1} and \texttt{argo:gemini-2.5-pro} via CherryRd LiteLLM \texttt{:4000} aggregator (\texttt{Bearer stevens}); both return \textbf{CONTRADICTED}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Verdict}
\textbf{CONTRADICTED} (scope: $\alpha$-quartz subset). Justified by:
\begin{itemize}
\item Density error $20$--$40\%$ (paper: $0$--$9\%$).
\item Si--O--Si error 25--31\textdegree{} at 0~K, 4--15\textdegree{} at 298~K (paper: 1.7--7\textdegree{}).
\item Both potentials have a non-$\alpha$-quartz global minimum.
\item ML-Tersoff coordination breakdown: 23\% non-tetrahedral Si at 298~K.
\item Both LLM judges (gpt-5.1, gemini-2.5-pro) independently return CONTRADICTED with matching numerical citations.
\end{itemize}
Full-scope replication (21 polymorphs, elastic constants, amorphous $S(q)$) is BLOCKED pending release of the other 20 IZA structure files and the evaluation scripts used for Fig.\ 4.
