### Claim-by-claim replication scorecard

1) **Table 1 genome stats for 4 strains (size/replicons/GC/genes/CDS/tRNA/rRNA)**
- **Reproduced.** All four strains’ genome sizes, replicon counts (chrom/plasmids), GC% (54.54–55.07), CDS counts, and rRNA operon count (8; inferred from 25 rRNA genes) match the paper essentially exactly; minor tRNA differences (e.g., SDM 79 vs 82) are small and plausibly annotation-version effects.

2) **GC content range 54.5–55.1%**
- **Reproduced.** Recomputed GC spans 54.54–55.07%, within the stated range.

3) **Core genome shared by all 4 strains = 3540 CDS**
- **Partially reproduced.** Core = 3345 under the stated RBH+threshold+single-linkage pipeline, a ~5.5% shortfall; directionally consistent but not numerically matching.

4) **Strain-unique (singleton) CDS fractions (~6%, ~6%, 12%, 20%)**
- **Partially reproduced.** Singleton rates are close in pattern and magnitude (7.8/7.1/13.3/19.8%), but ENHKU01 and SDM are notably higher than “~6%”.

5) **Phylogenomic tree from 1732 core genes across 8 Enterobacter; E. cloacae cluster; Enterobacter groups with Klebsiella; Pantoea outgroup**
- **Partially reproduced.** Using a different phylogenomic method (AAI+NJ) still recovers (i) clean Pantoea outgroup separation and (ii) tight E. cloacae clustering; the specific “1732 core genes / FastTree-style core-gene phylogeny” is not directly reproduced, and “Enterobacter–Klebsiella affinity” is only indirectly supported.

6) **ENHKU01 most closely related to ATCC13047 (housekeeping genes)**
- **Not reproduced.** Whole-proteome AAI shows ENHKU01 is marginally closest to SDM (93.96%) rather than ATCC13047 (93.65%), with differences small but not supporting the paper’s specific nearest-neighbor claim.

7) **Fimbriae: 9–13 loci per strain; only 4 conserved across all 4**
- **Partially reproduced.** Keyword-based detection yields noisy 8–19 loci (same order of magnitude but not matching the reported range), and conservation (“only 4 shared”) was not cleanly re-demonstrated with the described approach.

8) **T6SS clusters: ATCC13047 & ENHKU01 have 2; SDM & EcWSU1 have 1**
- **Partially reproduced.** ATCC13047=2 and SDM=1 match; ENHKU01 is 1 plus a fragmented block (not clearly 2), and EcWSU1 is 0 under this annotation-driven criterion—suggesting sensitivity to annotation/curation rather than a clean reproduction.

9) **>640 carbohydrate-utilization genes (13–15% of genome)**
- **Not reproduced.** Keyword-based counts are ~424–432 (~8–10%), substantially below the paper’s level; the replication’s method is acknowledged to be narrower than subsystem-based annotation, but as tested the claim does not hold.
