# Brief

**Paper:** *Intrepid MCMC: Metropolis-Hastings with Exploration* — Promit Chakroborty & Michael D. Shields, Johns Hopkins Univ. / Idaho National Laboratory (INL/JOU-24-82292-Revision-0, Sept 2025). OSTI 3028840; DOI 10.1016/j.cma.2025.118402.

**What & why:** The paper introduces *Intrepid MCMC*, a random-walk Metropolis-Hastings (MH) variant that adds an "exploration" kernel built from a hyperspherical coordinate transform anchored at a fixed point. With probability β the sampler takes an Intrepid step that jumps *along equal-probability contours of a parent distribution* p(x) (potentially reaching far-away disconnected modes in one move); with probability 1−β it takes an ordinary component-wise MH (CMH) step. The central claim is that a small amount of exploration (β≈0.1) dramatically improves mode-finding and convergence for **multimodal / disconnected-support** targets while barely reducing the acceptance rate. We independently reimplemented the algorithm from the paper's equations and reproduced the Section-4.1 benchmark (nine analytical 2-D targets), measuring Total Variation Distance (TVD) vs 3M-sample IID rejection references, error-in-mean, and acceptance rate, sweeping β∈{0,0.01,0.05,0.1,0.3,0.5,1.0} over 30 independent chains each. All compute on uicgpu (A100 node, CPU numpy, 32-way parallel); free endpoints only for the LLM judge.
