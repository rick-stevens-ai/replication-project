# Brief — BVBRC-69 (Chan et al. 2020, AbGRI4)

Chan et al. sequenced four multidrug-resistant *Acinetobacter baumannii* clinical
isolates (ABUH763, ABUH773, ABUH793, ABUH796) with Illumina + Oxford Nanopore
hybrid assembly, closing complete chromosomes and plasmids, and identified a
novel class 1 integron-carrying resistance island — **AbGRI4** — in three of
them (all Pasteur ST2). We independently pulled the four completed genomes
(CP035043–CP035053) from NCBI, ran MLST (mlst 2.33.1), extracted the paper's
Table-1 AbGRI4 coordinates, and re-annotated the region with abricate
(ResFinder/CARD/NCBI) plus IS/GBK feature analysis. Every core paper claim
reproduced cleanly: all 4 strains type as Pasteur ST2; all 3 AbGRI4-positive
isolates carry a byte-identical 8,840-bp island (0 mismatches across strains);
the island is bounded by IS26 transposases; it contains a class 1 integron
carrying **aadB + aadA2 + qacEΔ1 + sul1**; and it sits between the paper's
named locus tags **EP550_07220 (α/β-hydrolase pseudogene) and EP550_07290
(FMN-dependent NADH-azoreductase pseudogene)**, which are truncated fragments
of a target site that is intact and uninterrupted in the reference *A. baumannii*
AB0057 and ATCC 17978 chromosomes — confirming a novel insertion target.
Verdict: **REPLICATED**.
