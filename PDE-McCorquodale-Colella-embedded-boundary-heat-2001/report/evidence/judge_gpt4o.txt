**REPLICATION STATUS: PARTIAL**

**Justification:** The independent implementation provides strong evidence supporting the paper's claims C1 (Poisson solution accuracy) and C2 (gradient stencil behavior), but the results for C3 (Heat equation accuracy) are inconsistent with the paper's claim of second-order accuracy in both space and time. For C1, the measured L2 convergence rates for the Poisson equation are cleanly second-order for both the high and low stencils, consistent with the paper's assertion of uniformly second-order accuracy despite first-order boundary truncation. The max-norm convergence is noisier, as expected due to sliver cells, but does not contradict the claim. For C2, the high-order gradient stencil achieves intrinsic O(h²) accuracy, while the low-order stencil yields O(h) gradient errors but does not degrade the solution's second-order accuracy, matching the paper's description. However, for C3, the Heat equation results show irregular convergence behavior for the high stencil, with negative max-norm order at the finest resolution, which contradicts the claim of robust second-order accuracy in space and time. The low stencil results for the Heat equation are cleanly second-order in spatial refinement, but this does not fully validate the combined space-time accuracy claim. Therefore, C1 and C2 are **REPLICATED**, while C3 is **CONTRADICTED**.
