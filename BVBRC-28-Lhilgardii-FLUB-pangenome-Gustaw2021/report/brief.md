# Brief — BVBRC-28

**Paper:** Gustaw K. et al. (2021) "Genome and Pangenome Analysis of *Lactobacillus hilgardii* FLUB—A New Strain Isolated from Mead." *Int. J. Mol. Sci.* 22(7):3780. DOI 10.3390/ijms22073780. PMID 33917427 / PMC8038741. Open access (CC BY).

**What/why:** The authors sequenced (Illumina MiSeq + ONT MinION hybrid, Unicycler) a novel mead-spoilage *Lactobacillus hilgardii* strain FLUB, reporting a 3.07 Mbp chromosome + 5 plasmids (3.19 Mbp total, the largest *L. hilgardii* genome reported), ~40.1% GC, ~2997 CDS. They performed a 5-strain *L. hilgardii* pangenome (Roary) yielding core 2059 genes (49.3%), accessory 1210 (28.9%), singletons 912 (21.8%) — an open pangenome — plus PATRIC Codon Tree phylogeny placing FLUB nearest LMG 07934 / MGYG-HGUT-01333, and dDDH/ANI species confirmation (ANI ≥ 97% across the set). This replication independently pulls the FLUB assembly + the 4 comparator *L. hilgardii* genomes from NCBI Datasets, recomputes genome statistics, rebuilds the pangenome with a real orthology pipeline, tests pangenome openness, and re-derives the phylogenetic placement — then LLM-judges agreement against the paper's numbers using a free Argo endpoint.
