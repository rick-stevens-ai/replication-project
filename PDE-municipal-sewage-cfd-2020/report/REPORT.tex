\begin{abstract}
Independent replication of Esemu, Masanja, Nampala, Lwanyaga, Awichi \& Semwogerere
(2020), which combines an analytical Manning-equation table of minimum sewer slopes with
an OpenFOAM \texttt{interFoam} VOF + $k$-$\varepsilon$ simulation of a 2-D $20\,\mathrm{m}
\times 0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ pipe. \textbf{Verdict: PARTIAL.} Table~1 is cleanly quantitatively
replicated (back-solved Manning $n = 0.0129 \pm 0.0009$, standard concrete-sewer
roughness). The CFD figures are qualitatively reproduced via an independently-executed
\texttt{interFoam}~v1906 run on uicgpu; the paper reports no numeric CFD tables, so full
quantitative CFD agreement is not verifiable~\emph{by the paper's own reporting}. The
municipal-connections uplift claim (535 $\to$ 1200 $\Rightarrow$ +80\%) is out of scope
(requires utility records). A wave-brief metadata mismatch (brief said ``Al Manazlah,
Saudi Arabia''; the DOI actually resolves to the Tororo, Uganda paper) is flagged and
we replicated the actual paper.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper summary}

Small (10-page) applied CFD / mathematics paper motivated by Tororo Municipality,
Uganda's under-capacity combined sewer system. Three intertwined claims:
\begin{enumerate}
\item An analytical Table~1 of minimum longitudinal slopes for $D \in \{150, 200, 250, 300, 375, 450, 525, 600\}$~mm at self-cleansing velocity $v_0 = 0.6$~m/s.
\item A CFD demonstration using OpenFOAM \texttt{interFoam} (VOF two-phase + $k$-$\varepsilon$) on a 2-D $20\,\mathrm{m}\times0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ pipe (Figures 2--10). No numeric tables from the CFD are given, only field plots.
\item A municipal recommendation: expand connections from 535 (31.2\%) to $\geq 1200$ (70\%) to raise sewage delivery to treatment by $\sim 80\%$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Method}

\subsection{PDF fetch \& extraction}
Crossref $\to$ publisher $\to$ \texttt{citation\_pdf\_url} at \url{https://rajpub.com/index.php/jam/article/download/8345/7894} $\to$ \texttt{paper.pdf} (10~pp, 406~604~B, PDF~v1.7). Text via \texttt{pdftotext -layout} (581 lines); duplicated as \texttt{extraction/marker.md} and \texttt{extraction/nougat.mmd} (central Marker/Nougat manifests not queried for this new PDE-set paper).

\subsection{C1 --- Manning-equation replication}
Manning's equation for a full-flowing circular pipe:
\[ v = \frac{1}{n} R^{2/3} S^{1/2}, \qquad R = D/4. \]
Solve for $S$ at $v = v_0 = 0.6$~m/s:
\[ S = \left(\frac{v_0 n}{(D/4)^{2/3}}\right)^{\!2} . \]
Back-solve $n$ per Table~1 row: $n = \sqrt{S}\,(D/4)^{2/3}/v_0$. Grid-search a best-fit
single-material $n$ over all 8 rows.

\subsection{C2, C3 --- OpenFOAM interFoam replication}

\textbf{Host / software.} uicgpu (Ubuntu 20.04); OpenFOAM v1906
(\texttt{libopenfoam/focal}), source bashrc at \texttt{/usr/share/openfoam/etc/bashrc};
solvers \texttt{blockMesh} and \texttt{interFoam} on \texttt{/usr/bin}.

\textbf{Geometry.} 2-D rectangular domain $L\times H\times \delta = 20 \times 0.5 \times
0.01$~m, one cell thick in $z$ with \texttt{empty} front/back patches $\Rightarrow$ true
2-D. Mesh $400 \times 40 \times 1 = 16\,000$ hex cells (cell size $0.05 \times 0.0125
\times 0.01$~m).

\textbf{Solver.} \texttt{interFoam} (VOF two-phase), RAS $k$-$\varepsilon$ turbulence with
\texttt{kqRWallFunction}, \texttt{epsilonWallFunction}, \texttt{nutkWallFunction}. Water
$\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}, \nu=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$; air $\rho=1, \nu=1.48\times
10^{-5}$; $\sigma=0.07\,\mathrm{N/m}$; $\mathbf{g}=(0,-9.81,0)$.

\textbf{BCs.} Inlet: $\mathbf{U}=(1.0,0,0)$~m/s, $\alpha_{\text{water}}=1$;
outlet: \texttt{inletOutlet U}, \texttt{totalPressure $p_0=0$};
walls: no-slip; front/back: \texttt{empty}. Turbulence: $I=0.05\Rightarrow
k=0.00375\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, $\varepsilon=C_\mu^{3/4}k^{3/2}/l$ with
$l=0.07D=0.035$~m $\Rightarrow \varepsilon = 0.00097\,\mathrm{m^2/s^3}$.

\textbf{Numerics.} Euler ddt; linearUpwind for U; vanLeer for $\alpha$; upwind for
$k,\varepsilon$; Gauss linear for viscous. PIMPLE: 1 outer, 3 correctors. Adjustive dt,
$\mathrm{Co}_\mathrm{max}=1$, $\mathrm{Co}_{\alpha,\mathrm{max}}=1$. $t_\mathrm{end}=5$~s,
write every 0.5~s, wall-clock $\approx 80$~s.

\textbf{Post-processing.} \texttt{postProcess -func sampleDict -latestTime}: centerline (200 pts) and two cross-sections at $x=5, 15$~m (50 pts each), fields $\mathbf{U}, p, p_\mathrm{rgh}, \alpha_\mathrm{water}$.

\section{Verdict + justification}

\textbf{PARTIAL.} C1 is fully quantitatively replicated with a clean Manning derivation
(back-solved $n=0.013$ = standard concrete sewer). C2/C3 are qualitatively replicated via
an actually-executed \texttt{interFoam} v1906 run; the paper reports no numeric CFD
tables, so quantitative CFD agreement is unreachable by the paper's own reporting.
C4 (municipal claim) not tested.

The replication surfaced two substantive concerns the paper does not address:
material-inconsistency between Table~1 (concrete $n=0.013$) and \S4.1's UPVC/HDPE
recommendation, and the 2-D-vs-3-D hydraulic-radius mismatch that makes the paper's own
CFD unable to validate its own Table~1.

\section{Open Questions}

\paragraph{Q1.} Given Table~1's back-solved $n = 0.013$ (concrete) but \S4.1's UPVC/HDPE recommendation ($n\approx 0.009$), which set of minimum slopes should Tororo actually adopt? At $n=0.009$ the minima are $\sim 2.1\times$ steeper --- a materially different design table.\\
\textit{Next steps:} rebuild Table~1 at $n\in\{0.009, 0.011, 0.013\}$; publish a labeled-by-material design table.

\paragraph{Q2.} Is the $\sim 2\times$ entrance-region velocity overshoot in the reproduced Fig.~9 physical, or a fixedValue-U inlet + dry-domain start-up artefact?\\
\textit{Next steps:} rerun with \texttt{flowRateInletVelocity} BC and a pre-filled water domain; compare developed-regime velocities.

\paragraph{Q3.} How much does the paper's 2-D rectangular representation of a 3-D circular pipe shift the developed velocity? For $D=0.5$~m the 2-D hydraulic radius ($R=h/2=0.25$) is $2\times$ the true circular value ($R=D/4=0.125$), so Manning velocity differs by $2^{2/3}\approx 1.59\times$. The paper's own CFD therefore cannot validate its own Table~1.\\
\textit{Next steps:} build a 3-D axisymmetric \texttt{wedge}-patch case; compare developed velocity at $x=15$~m.

\paragraph{Q4.} No mesh-convergence study is reported. What is the interface-diffusion error on $\alpha_\mathrm{water}$ and the developed-velocity Richardson error for $400\times 40 \to 800\times 80 \to 1600\times 160$?\\
\textit{Next steps:} three-level GCI study; publish converged values.

\paragraph{Q5.} Does a full 1-D network-scale (SWMM/EPANET) solve on the real Tororo topology, driven by Table~1 slopes, actually deliver the paper's asserted 80\% collection uplift under peak-wet-weather flow, or is that a linear extrapolation that breaks?\\
\textit{Next steps:} build SWMM model of Tororo sewer network with 535-baseline and 1200-expansion; force with dry-weather and 1-in-2-year storm; report delivered fraction and CSO overflow count.
