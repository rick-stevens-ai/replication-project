# Brief — BVBRC-34

**Paper:** Hossain et al. (2019), "Comparative genomics of *Aeromonas veronii*: Identification of a pathotype impacting aquaculture globally," *PLoS ONE* 14(9):e0221018 (doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0221018).

**What/why:** The paper compares 41 publicly available *A. veronii* genomes (plus the newly sequenced catfish-outbreak strain ML09-123) to profile virulence-factor distribution, secretion systems, and pan/core genome structure, concluding that ML09-123 is a near-clone of the Chinese aquaculture isolate TH0426 — a "pathotype impacting aquaculture globally." I independently re-downloaded all 41 genomes from NCBI (41/41 accessions resolved), reproduced the genome statistics (41/41 match Table 1), recomputed pairwise ANI (fastANI), a mash-distance phylogeny, the pan/core genome (CD-HIT), and a VFDB virulence-factor screen (abricate) on real data with free tools on uicgpu. The central pathotype claim is reproduced by three independent methods (ANI 99.93%, mash nearest-neighbor, identical 136-gene VFDB profile), and the secretion-system conservation pattern and core-genome size match; the pan-genome count differs by clustering algorithm. **Verdict: PARTIAL (strong).**
