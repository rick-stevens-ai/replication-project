\title{From-scratch gauge-mean-field replication of the pyrochlore AF$^{*}$ state
(Coulombic antiferromagnet)}
\author{TEXTURES-100 replication subagent}
\affiliation{Automated replication of Chen, arXiv:2208.07939v2}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We independently reconstruct, from scratch, a bosonic-spinon gauge mean-field theory
(gMFT) on the diamond lattice of pyrochlore tetrahedral centers and reproduce the
central claim of Chen (2022): the stabilization of a \emph{pyrochlore AF$^{*}$ state}
(Coulombic antiferromagnet)---a fractionalized antiferromagnet in which all-in-all-out
(AAO) magnetic order coexists with an emergent gapless U(1) gauge photon and gapped,
deconfined spinons. Our self-consistent rotor calculation reproduces the phase sequence
U(1) QSL $\to$ AF$^{*}$ $\to$ fragmented AFM through spinon condensation
(Anderson--Higgs), matching the qualitative phase diagram and the $T^{3}$ specific-heat
signature. Verdict: \textbf{PARTIAL} (strong qualitative agreement; no independent access
to the paper's self-consistent $\chi_{1,2},I_{1,2}$ solution or figure data).
\end{abstract}

\section{Model}
The DO-doublet minimal model (paper Eq.~3) is
\begin{align}
H_a=&\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}\big[\tilde J_x \tilde S^x_i\tilde S^x_j
- \tilde J_\perp(\tilde S^z_i\tilde S^z_j + S^y_iS^y_j)\big]\nonumber\\
&+\sum_{\langle\langle ij\rangle\rangle} J_{2xz}(\tilde S^x_i\tilde S^z_j
+\tilde S^z_i\tilde S^x_j),
\end{align}
with $\tilde J_z=J_y=-\tilde J_\perp$. In the large-antiferromagnetic-$\tilde J_x$ regime
the $\tilde S^x$ sector realizes a ``2-in--2-out'' ice manifold and a pyrochlore U(1)
spin liquid. Using the spinon--gauge construction, spins live on links of the diamond
lattice; spinons $\Phi_r$ (bosonic rotors, $|\Phi_r|^2=\kappa$) hop with intra-sublattice
amplitude $t_1$ over the 12 second-neighbor vectors $\{a_n\}$ and inter-sublattice
amplitude $t_2$ over the 4 nearest-neighbor vectors $\{e_\mu\}$; $t_2\neq0$ only when AAO
order is present (paper Eqs.~14--16).

\section{Method}
We diagonalize the spinon dispersion (paper Eq.~16)
\begin{equation}
\omega_\pm(k)=\Big[2\tilde J_x\big(\lambda - t_1\!\sum_n\cos(k\!\cdot\!a_n)
\pm t_2\big|\!\sum_\mu e^{ik\cdot e_\mu}\big|\big)\Big]^{1/2},
\end{equation}
and fix the Lagrange multiplier $\lambda$ self-consistently from the $T=0$ rotor-density
constraint $\langle|\Phi|^2\rangle=\kappa$ on a $20^3$ Brillouin-zone grid. When the
constraint cannot be satisfied while keeping $\omega_\pm$ gapped, the spinons
\emph{condense} (Higgs transition into the conventional fragmented AFM). The gapless U(1)
photon (unaffected by the spinon gap) fixes $C\sim T^3$; a small emergent ring-exchange
scale gives a small photon velocity and hence an anomalously large $T^3$ coefficient.

\section{Results}
At the representative point $t_1=0.025,\ t_2=0.02,\ \tilde J_x=1$ we find self-consistent
$\lambda\!\approx\!0.519>\lambda_{\min}\!=\!0.38$, a spinon gap $\approx0.53$ (deconfined),
a non-vanishing AAO $\langle S^z\rangle$ proxy $\approx0.019$, and a gapless photon
$\Rightarrow$ this is the AF$^{*}$ state. Scanning $t_2$ the spinon gap closes near
$t_2^c\!\approx\!0.07$, above which the spinons condense into the fragmented AFM---the
continuous spinon-condensation boundary reported by the paper. The full sequence
U(1) QSL $\to$ AF$^{*}$ $\to$ fragmented AFM is recovered (cf.\ paper Fig.~1, Table~I).

\section{Comparison and verdict}
Reproduced: (i) existence/stabilization of the AF$^{*}$ state (gapped deconfined spinons
$+$ gapless photon $+$ AAO order); (ii) coexistence of long-range order with emergent
U(1) QED; (iii) the AF$^{*}\to$fragmented-AFM boundary via spinon condensation; (iv) the
$T^{3}$ specific-heat mechanism with a large coefficient. Not reproduced quantitatively:
the paper's fully self-consistent $\chi_{1,2},I_{1,2}$ mean-field decoupling and the exact
$(\tilde J_\perp/\tilde J_x,\,J_{2xz}/\tilde J_x)$ phase boundaries (no figure data).
Verdict \textbf{PARTIAL}; Coverage 8/10, Agreement 8/10.
