## Abstract

A Gram-stain-negative, anaerobic, non-motile, rod-shaped bacterium, designated as BGYT1ᵀ, was
isolated from the feces of a cow in Andong, Republic of Korea. It was studied using a polyphasic
method to determine its taxonomic position. Phylogenetic analysis based on 16S rRNA gene sequences
revealed that strain BGYT1ᵀ formed a lineage within the genus *Olsenella* and was most closely
related to *O. umbonate* KCTC 15140ᵀ (98.2%). The complete genome sequence of strain BGYT1ᵀ was
**2,476,083 bp** long with a G + C content of **66.9 mol%** and contained **1835 genes** and
**8 contigs**. The N50 value was **604,117 bp**. There were 50 tRNAs, 6 rRNAs (5S, 16S, 23S), 1778
CDSs and 2 BGCs and 1 tmRNA. The values for **ANI (76.8%), AAI (67.3%), and dDDH (22.2%)** compared
to the closest related species were all below the threshold for bacterial species delineation. In
addition, genes encoding the cell wall degrading enzymes such as chitinases, β-1,3 glucanases, and
proteases were also detected. The strain was able to grow at pH 6.0–8.0 (optimum, pH 7.0), in the
presence of 0.5–1.5% NaCl (optimum, 0.5%, w/v) and at the temperature range of 35–40 °C (optimum,
35 °C). The predominant fatty acids were C₁₆:₀ DMA (20.2%), C₁₆:₀ (20.2%), C₁₈:₀ (10.5%) and C₁₈:₁
cis 9 (17.0%). The polar lipids consisted of an unidentified phospholipid, four unidentified
glycolipids and three unidentified lipids. Based on its phenotypic analyses, phylogenetic and
physiological characteristics, strain BGYT1ᵀ represented a novel species within the genus
*Olsenella*, for which the name **Olsenella intestinalis sp. nov.** is proposed. The type strain is
**BGYT1ᵀ (= KCTC 25379ᵀ = GDMCC 1.3011ᵀ)**.

**Keywords:** 16S rRNA sequence · Novel species · Polyphasic approach · Taxonomy

## Introduction

The bacterial genus of *Olsenella*, belonging to the family *Atopobiaceae*, was first described by
Dewhirst et al. (2001) with *Olsenella uli* as type species. Kraatz et al. (2011) added some
phenotypic characteristics to the genus description and described a new species *Olsenella
umbonata*. Genome-based phylogenetic classification of the phylum *Actinobacteria* showed the
genus *Olsenella* was paraphyletic, but no major emendation for this genus was proposed (Nouioui
et al. 2018). More recently, Zgheib et al. (2021) noticed the paraphyletic nature of *Olsenella*
and reclassified the *Olsenella* species. Lu et al. (2021) transferred *Olsenella gallinarum* to
genus *Thermophilibacter* as *Thermophilibacter gallinarum*. At the end of 2021, the genus
*Olsenella* included seven species with validly published names: *O. massiliensis*, *O.
phocaeensis*, *O. porci*, *O. profusa*, *O. uli*, *O. umbonate* and *O. urininfantis*
(https://lpsn.dsmz.de/genus/olsenella). Typical characteristics of the genus *Olsenella* include
cells that are anaerobic, Gram stain positive and rod shaped, C₁₈:₁ cis 9 as the major fatty
acid, and the G + C content of the DNA is 62–64 mol%.

## Materials and methods

### Isolation, preservation, cultivation conditions

Cow feces from Andong, Republic of Korea. Sample stored in anaerobic pouch (GasPakᵀᴹ EZ),
transported at 4 °C. Isolation/cultivation in anaerobic chamber (Coy Laboratory Products Inc.)
with 7% CO₂, 86% N₂, 7% H₂. Sample in phosphate-buffered saline pH 6.0–8.0, 10-fold serial
dilutions to 10⁻⁵, 150 μl spread on TSA + 5% sheep blood (TSAB). Incubated 35 °C for 3 days;
colonies picked and subcultured. Single colony designated **BGYT1ᵀ**. Long-term preservation:
10% skim milk, −20 °C then −80 °C. Reference strains: *O. umbonata* KCTC 15140ᵀ and *O. profusa*
KCTC 15029ᵀ from KCTC.

### Phylogenetic analyses

16S rRNA amplified with primers 27F, 518F, 805R, 1492R. Near-full-length 1423-bp 16S rRNA gene
assembled with BioEdit, deposited as **GenBank OM533390**. Compared against EzBioCloud
(www.ezbiocloud.net). MSA in Clustal W. Trees in MEGA v7.0 using NJ, ML, ME algorithms with
1000-replicate bootstrap; Kimura's 2-parameter model.

### Genomic analyses

DNA extracted with PowerSoil® Pro Kit (Qiagen 47014). Sequenced with Illumina Nexa + Oxford
Nanopore MinION (SQK-LSK109 library, FLO-MIN106 R9.4 flow cells, MinKnow v1.15.1). Genomic
correlations: ANI via www.ezbiocloud.net/tools/ani, dDDH via GGDC
(http://ggdc.dsmz.de/distcalc2.php), AAI via http://enve-omics.ce.gatech.edu/. Phylogenomic tree
via TYGS (https://tygs.dsmz.de) using GBDP + FastME 2.1.6.1 + SPR postprocessing + 100
pseudo-bootstrap replicates. Annotation with Prokka; functional annotation with KEGG +
KEGG-Decoder + RAST.

### Morphology, biochemistry, physiology

Cells cultivated 2 days at 35 °C anaerobically. SEM: SPI-Dry critical point dryer; gold coating
via Safematic CCU-010HV. Gram stain (Difco). pH range 3–9 (1-unit intervals) in pH-adjusted TSB
via microplate OD. Temperature 15–45 °C in 5 °C intervals on TSAB. NaCl 0.5–6%. Catalase (Bio
Mérieux), oxidase (Bio Mérieux), API 20 NE (nitrate reduction, indole, urease), API ZYM, Rapid ID
32A. Polar lipids: TLC on silica gel 60 F₂₅₄, stained with 50% H₂SO₄ / 0.1% ninhydrin / molybdenum.
Fatty acids: MIDI/Hewlett Packard MIS + Sherlock™ Anaerobic database v6.1 on GC-210 (Shimadzu).
Type strain deposited as **KCTC 25379ᵀ = GDMCC 1.3011ᵀ**.

## Results and discussion

### Phylogenetic characteristics

16S rRNA (1423 bp) EzBioCloud top hits:
- *O. umbonata* KCTC 15140ᵀ — **98.24%**
- *O. profusa* KCTC 15029ᵀ — 97.46%
- *O. uli* DSM 7084ᵀ — 96.55%
- *Fannyhessea vaginae* DSM 15829ᵀ — 94.86%
- *Lancefieldella parvula* DSM 20469ᵀ — 94.07%
- *Paratractidigestivibacter faecalis* KCTC 15699ᵀ — 93.09%

All below the 98.65% novel-species threshold of Kim et al. (2014). ML/ME/NJ trees (Figs. 1, S1,
S2) show BGYT1ᵀ within *Olsenella*, distinct from other species. Outgroup: *Eggerthella
guodeyinii* HF-1101ᵀ (MN203625).

### Phylogenomics and genomic analyses

TYGS tree (Fig. 2) places BGYT1ᵀ in a distinct species branch. dDDH values < 70% cutoff; ANI
values ≤ **76.8%** (95–96% cutoff for species delineation); AAI to *O. umbonata* = **67.3%**;
dDDH to *O. umbonata* = **22.2%**. Table 1 collects AAI/ANI/dDDH vs. nearest neighbors.

Genome of BGYT1ᵀ (paper body text): 1835 genes; 8 contigs; total length **2,453,694 bp**;
N50 = 604,117 bp; 50 tRNAs; 6 rRNAs (5S, 16S, 23S); 1 tmRNA; 1778 CDSs; 2 BGCs; G+C = 66.9%.

> 2,476,083 bp; the Results text (Phylogenomics and genomic analyses) states 2,453,694 bp.
> RefSeq assembly GCF_023276655.1 and GenBank GCA_023276655.1 both report **2,453,694 bp
> in 2 contigs**, matching the paper's body text — but not the paper's abstract or contig

Functional annotation: COG classes with the strongest signals — cell wall/membrane/envelope
biogenesis, translation and ribosomal biogenesis, carbohydrate transport & metabolism,
transcription, signal transduction, nucleotide transport & metabolism, defense mechanisms,
inorganic ion transport & metabolism, lipid transport & metabolism (Fig. 3). Cell wall degrading
enzyme genes detected: chitinases, β-1,3 glucanases, proteases.

### Morphological, physiological and biochemical features

Colonies on TSAB: circular, convex, smooth, entire (35 °C, 2 days). Anaerobic, non-motile,
oxidase- and catalase-negative. Rod-shaped, no flagella; 1.5–3.1 μm × 0.2–0.3 μm (Fig. S3).
Growth: pH 6.0–8.0; T 35–40 °C; NaCl 0.5–1.5% (w/v; optimum 0.5%). Urease detected (API 20A);
positive for alkaline phosphatase, arginine, β-glucosidase, arginine dihydrolase; all other
enzymatic tests negative (Table S2). Dominant fatty acids (>10%): C₁₆:₀ (20.2%), C₁₆:₀ DMA
(20.2%), C₁₈:₀ (10.5%), C₁₈:₁ cis 9 (17.0%). Distinguishable from *O. umbonata* by higher C₁₆:₀
and presence of C₁₈:₂ cis 9,12 (Table S3). Polar lipids: 1 unidentified phospholipid,
4 unidentified glycolipids, 3 unidentified lipids.

### Taxonomic conclusion

ANI 76.8% / AAI 67.3% / dDDH 22.2% all below species-delineation thresholds → BGYT1ᵀ is a novel
species within *Olsenella*. Proposed: **Olsenella intestinalis sp. nov.**

## Description of *Olsenella intestinalis* sp. nov.

*Olsenella intestinalis* (in.tes.ti.na'lis. L. fem. adj. *intestinalis*, pertaining to the
intestine). Cells anaerobic, non-motile, Gram-stain-negative, rod-shaped, 1.5–3.1 × 0.2–0.3 μm.
Colonies on TSAB (35 °C, 2 days) circular, convex, smooth, entire. Grows at pH 6.0–8.0
(optimum 7.0), 35–40 °C (optimum 35 °C), 0.5–1.5% NaCl (optimum 0.5%). Oxidase- and
catalase-negative. Predominant fatty acids: C₁₆:₀ DMA, C₁₆:₀, C₁₈:₀, C₁₈:₁ cis 9. Type strain
**BGYT1ᵀ (= KCTC 25379ᵀ = GDMCC 1.3011ᵀ)**, isolated from cow feces (Andong, Republic of Korea).
Genome G+C = 66.9%. GenBank: 16S rRNA = OM533390; whole genome = JALGRK000000000.
