# Brief — BVBRC-57

**What:** Independent replication of Kang et al. (2020, *Microorganisms* 8:382), the complete PacBio genome of the plant-growth-promoting, heavy-metal-tolerant *Pseudomonas psychrotolerans* CS51 (GenBank CP021645 / assembly GCF_006384975.1).

**Why:** The paper makes a set of concrete, publicly checkable genome-sequence and gene-content claims (genome size/GC/rRNA/tRNA/CDS counts; presence of copper/cobalt-zinc-cadmium/nickel resistance systems; auxin, nitrate/nitrite, phosphate, sulfate pathways; and a pan-/core-genome analysis). We retrieved the real deposited assembly from NCBI, recomputed the genome statistics, re-detected the functional gene categories with RefSeq PGAP + abricate (CARD/VFDB/bacmet2)/AMRFinderPlus, and rebuilt an independent pan-genome (prokka + roary) over 9 conspecific genomes with fastANI, on uicgpu. Genome stats matched exactly, all functional gene categories were confirmed, and the pan-genome shape + phylogenetic placement near PRS08 were reproduced; only the paper's cross-species core-gene *count* (2122) differed (method/genome-set dependent). LLM-judge (Argo gpt-5.2): coverage 100%, agreement 93%, verdict REPLICATED.
