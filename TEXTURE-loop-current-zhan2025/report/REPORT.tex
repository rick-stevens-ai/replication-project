\section{Summary}
Zhan \emph{et al.} study the spinless kagome lattice at the p-type Van Hove
filling ($\mu=0$) with nonlocal density--density repulsion (nearest-neighbor
$V_1$, second-neighbor $V_2$), Eq.~(1):
\[
H=-t\!\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}\!(c^\dagger_i c_j+\text{h.c.})-\mu\sum_i n_i
+V_1\!\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}\! n_i n_j+V_2\!\!\sum_{\langle\langle ij\rangle\rangle}\!\! n_i n_j .
\]
Using unbiased functional renormalization group (FRG), they find that
sublattice interference suppresses onsite CDW and that a \textbf{$2\times2$ (3Q)
loop-current order (LCO)} emerges as the many-body ground state over a
significant region of strong $V_2$. The resulting electronic model with LCO
is a \textbf{Chern insulator with total $C=1$} (quantum anomalous Hall),
analogous to the Haldane model.

We reuse the shared Textures-100 loop-current kagome kernel
(\texttt{loop\_current\_kagome\_kernel.py}; provenance in
\texttt{PROVENANCE.md}) and add a folded $2\times2$ tight-binding model
carrying an imaginary (loop-current) bond order on 1nn and 2nn bonds. The
FRG phase diagram itself is out of scope (HPC method); we replicate the
paper's downstream, machine-checkable single-particle claims.

\section{Claims tested}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textbf{A}] Bare kagome spectrum: flat band, Dirac cone at $K$, p-type
VHS saddle at $E=0$ ($\mu=0$).
\item[\textbf{B}] Imaginary (loop-current) bond order opens a full gap
(Fig.~3d).
\item[\textbf{C}] The gapped LCO state is a Chern insulator, total $C=1$
(Fig.~3d, $\Delta^{\text{LCO}}_{1nn}=0.1t$, $\Delta^{\text{LCO}}_{2nn}=0.15t$).
\item[\textbf{D}] Landau quartic Eq.~(2), $Z_1-Z_2>0$, selects the
equal-weight 3Q state.
\item[\textbf{E}] TRS is broken by the imaginary channel (LCO current $\neq0$),
and the 3Q state carries a net ferromagnetic orbital moment.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion}
All five in-scope claims reproduce. The headline physics --- a spinless kagome
tight-binding model with a $2\times2$ imaginary-bond (loop-current) order that
gaps the p-type-VHS Fermi surface into a $C=1$ Chern insulator, with the 3Q
state selected by the TRS-broken Landau quartic --- is recovered quantitatively
for the spectrum and qualitatively (with the correct topological invariant) for
the many-body-derived electronic model.

The paper's defining \emph{method} (unbiased FRG phase diagram, Fig.~2) is not
reproduced: it requires a dedicated FRG code and HPC. This is documented in
\texttt{failure\_analysis.md}, not obscured. The folded-model current-sense
assignment is geometric rather than the paper's renormalized-susceptibility
eigenvector, so higher-filling Chern values differ; the VHS-relevant filling
carries $C=1$ as claimed.
