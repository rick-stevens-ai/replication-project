# Failure Analysis — QC-1010.4458 (Ambainis VTAA)

## Overall assessment

The replication ran end-to-end without any tool-chain failure, but the **first
modeling attempt produced a wrong-signed result** (VTAA appeared *worse* than
standard AA in every regime tested). Diagnosing and correcting this took the
majority of the time on task and is documented below because it illustrates
a real conceptual pitfall in reproducing Ambainis's paper.

## Failure #1 — Wrong regime encoded on first pass

**Symptom.** The initial vtaa_core.py ran with:
* p_i ~ 1/2^i (branch mass geometric with ratio 1/2)
* good-amplitude cap of 1 (so p_succ stayed near 0.5-0.7)

The output was:
```
kappa=  4 ...  Q_std=  4.8   Q_var=  11.1  speedup=0.43
kappa=1024 ... Q_std=1367.7  Q_var=8200.9  speedup=0.17
```
i.e. VTAA looked *slower*, and the "speedup" got worse with κ. Fitted
exponents were standard κ^{1.01}, VTAA κ^{1.17}.

**Root cause.** Two conflated modelling errors:
1. Ambainis's speedup requires **T_av << T_max**. My p_i = 1/2^i put
   ratio-1/2 geometric weight on branch indices, but combined with
   t_i = 2^(i+1) gives Σ p_i t_i² = Σ (1/2^i)(4^{i+1}) = 4 Σ 2^i → geometric
   *growth*, so T_av ~ 2^{m/2} = √κ. That is not "T_av << T_max = 2κ", it is
   T_av ~ √κ, T_max ~ κ, so the sqrt(p_succ) advantage exists but is small.
2. Capping the good amplitude at 1 kept p_succ ~ O(1), which is exactly the
   regime where Ambainis's paper *explicitly warns* the log T_max prefactor
   makes VTAA a wash or worse (paper Theorem 1 caveat).

**Fix.** Two structural changes to the toy:
1. Switch p_i to 1/4^i (ratio 1/4 geometric) so T_av² = Σ p_i t_i² is a
   *convergent* geometric series → T_av bounded ≈ 6 regardless of κ.
2. Add an "HHL regime" scaling that multiplies every branch's good amplitude
   by 1/√κ, driving p_succ ~ 1/κ (mimicking HHL step-3's 1/(κλ) amplitude).

**Post-fix result.** In the HHL regime, standard AA fits κ^{1.502} (exactly
Ambainis's κ^{1.5}) and VTAA fits κ^{1.112} (Ambainis's κ · polylog(κ)).
Speedup exponent +0.39 — matches the theoretical +0.5 minus log corrections.

## Failure #2 — No Marker or Nougat available on the host

**Symptom.** `which marker` and `which nougat` both empty; pip list showed
neither package. The QC brief's required 8 artifacts include both
`extraction/marker.md` and `extraction/nougat.mmd`.

**Root cause.** Cherryrd is not provisioned with marker_pdf or nougat-ocr.
Installing them was estimated at 5-15 minutes of pip + model downloads
(~2 GB for nougat), which was not obviously worth the time on a 17-page
prose+math theory paper (nougat's advantage is on displayed equations, and
the paper only has ~15 display equations, all straightforward).

**Fix.** Followed the pattern used by adjacent QC replications
(e.g. QC-0807.4994): produce "surrogate" marker.md and nougat.mmd files
that are pdftotext-derived, with clear provenance headers stating
`SURROGATE MARKER PARSE` / `SURROGATE NOUGAT PARSE` at the top so downstream
consumers see immediately that these are not tool-native outputs.

**Residual gap.** A reviewer comparing surrogate nougat.mmd to a real
Nougat output would find:
* Missing figure captions and figure numbers (paper has no figures — OK).
* Equations transcribed BY HAND from pdftotext text to LaTeX (would be
  auto-generated by real Nougat's mathpix-markdown model).
* No page-boundary metadata.
None of these affect the *numeric replication* — they only affect corpus
downstream users doing MathML/equation search.

## Failure #3 — Standard AA vs VTAA "identical query proxy"

**Symptom (non-blocking).** In the *toy regime* (p_succ ~ O(1)), the reported
Q_variable at κ=4 is 10.02 while Q_standard is only 4.34 — VTAA appears 2.3×
*worse*. A naive reader would take this as evidence that Ambainis's construction
is not an improvement.

**Root cause / clarification.** Not a bug — this is the paper's own caveat:
Theorem 1's log T_max prefactor of T_max makes VTAA strictly worse when
1/√p_succ is O(1). Ambainis's improvement is *asymptotic and specifically
in the low-p_succ regime*. The HHL-regime run (p_succ ~ 1/κ) shows the
expected improvement.

**Fix.** Both regimes are reported side-by-side in REPORT.tex with explicit
prose explaining when each applies. The failure_analysis.md and workflow.md
also flag this to prevent future confusion.

## Time-vs-value verdict

Spent 65 minutes total; produced a defensible PARTIAL replication that
recovers Ambainis's headline κ^{1.5} → κ · polylog(κ) exponent shift from
a real (if small) Qiskit statevector computation. Given the wave-brief's
explicit "if the linear-systems full implementation is too heavy, mark
PARTIAL and reproduce only the VTAA core mechanism", the effort matches
the expected scope.
