# Brief

Independent replication of E & Liu (2003) "Gauge Method for Viscous Incompressible Flows" (Comm. Math. Sci., DOI 10.4310/CMS.2003.V1.N2.A6). The paper introduces a gauge formulation of the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations that replaces the pressure Poisson step of a projection method with an auxiliary vector field `a = u + ∇φ` whose divergence-free constraint is enforced via a Poisson solve for the gauge variable `φ`. Claimed advantages: (1) no pressure boundary condition required, (2) second-order accuracy in space/time on standard benchmarks, (3) decoupled pressure recovery. We reimplement the scheme in 2D on the Taylor–Green vortex (analytic solution known) and measure spatial convergence order to test C1 (formulation validity) and C2 (2nd-order convergence).
