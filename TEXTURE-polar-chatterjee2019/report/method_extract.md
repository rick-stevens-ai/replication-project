# Method Extract — Chatterjee, Bultinck, Zaletel 2019 (arXiv:1908.00986)
**Title:** Symmetry breaking and skyrmionic transport in twisted bilayer graphene
**Texture class:** polar (skyrmion textures of the spin/valley pseudospin order in tBLG; "polar" per taxonomy)

- **Core physics:** Correlated insulating state of **magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene** (aligned with hBN) at filling ν=2 electrons/moiré cell. Because the flat bands carry **non-zero Chern number**, the natural charged excitations are **skyrmion textures** of the order parameter rather than ordinary particle-hole carriers.
- **Headline claim (replication target):** The observed **non-monotonic magnetoresistance** vs out-of-plane B is explained by **skyrmions as the dominant charge carriers**: spin Zeeman splitting acts on skyrmion excitations, producing the non-monotonic R(B). Predicts functional dependence of the **activation gap on magnetic field** as an experimental test; discusses skyrmion-pairing → possible superconductivity on doping.
- **Key equations:** Effective **field theory (nonlinear sigma model / CP¹)** for the spin degrees of freedom of the Chern band; skyrmion carries electric charge tied to Chern number (charge–skyrmion binding); Zeeman term shifts skyrmion energy; activation gap Δ(B) from skyrmion creation energy. Underlying Hartree-Fock of the tBLG continuum flat bands.
- **DFT-heavy or theory?** **THEORY / ANALYTIC + Hartree-Fock — tractable in-process (analytic parts).** No ab-initio DFT. The skyrmion-energetics / activation-gap-vs-B argument is analytic (sigma-model), directly reproducible. Full self-consistent Hartree-Fock of the continuum model is heavier but still workstation-scale. Good candidate for reproducing the Δ(B) / non-monotonic-R argument analytically.
