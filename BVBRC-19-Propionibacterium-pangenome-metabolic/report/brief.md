# BVBRC-19 — Brief

**Paper:** McCubbin T, Gonzalez-Garcia RA, Palfreyman RW, Stowers C, Nielsen LK, Marcellin E.
*A Pan-Genome Guided Metabolic Network Reconstruction of Five Propionibacterium Species Reveals Extensive Metabolic Diversity.*
Genes (2020), 11(10):1115. **DOI:** [10.3390/genes11101115](https://doi.org/10.3390/genes11101115) — PMID 32977700 — PMCID PMC7650540.

## Core claims
1. Pan-genome analysis across five (former) *Propionibacterium* species reveals unexpected diversity in central carbon metabolism.
2. Authors build the first high-quality genome-scale metabolic model (GEM) for *Propionibacterium* and a pan-metabolic model spanning all five species.
3. Reveal a novel **ferredoxin-linked energy conservation strategy**.
4. Metabolic differences reflect environmental adaptation; provide a platform for rational strain design.

## Replication scope (short pass)
- Confirm *Propionibacterium* + Cutibacterium (reclassified members) genomes are abundant in BV-BRC.
- Confirm typical genome sizes match the paper's expectation for high-GC *Propionibacterium*.
- Note that GEM reconstruction and FBA reruns are out of scope; the paper's models are openly available.
