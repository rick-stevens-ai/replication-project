\begin{abstract}
We reproduce, from scratch, the self-consistent mean-field (MFT) description of the
hidden-order (HO) phase of URu$_2$Si$_2$ as a chiral $d_{xy}+i\,d_{x^2-y^2}$ spin
density wave (d-SDW). Solving the paper's free-energy functional (Eq.~C1) on a
Brillouin-zone grid with \emph{only} the paper's material parameters, we recover the
zero-field driving gap $\Delta_2(0)\approx1.87$~meV (paper: $1.55$~meV) and an
ordering temperature $T_{\rm HO}\approx20$~K (paper: $17.5$~K). Using the paper's own
Landau reduction (Appendix~D) we reproduce the two structural headlines: the $d_{xy}$
component is \emph{strictly field-induced} ($\Delta_1(B{=}0)=0$, rising monotonically),
and the ordering temperature is \emph{field-enhanced}, $T_0(B)=T_0+(g^2/\alpha_1)B^2$,
so the chiral HO persists across the whole $B$--$T$ diagram. \textbf{Verdict: PARTIAL}
(Coverage 6/10, Agreement 7/10). Absolute high-field metamagnetic thresholds
($B_{c1}=33.5$~T, $B_{c2}=41$~T) and the Nernst/Kerr transport are out of the
gap-equation scope replicated here.
\end{abstract}

\section{Model and method}
The chiral d-SDW order parameter is
$\Delta^z_Q(k)=\Delta_1\sin k_x\sin k_y - i\,\Delta_2(\cos k_x-\cos k_y)$,
with $\Delta_1$ the chiral $d_{xy}$ component and $\Delta_2$ the driving
$d_{x^2-y^2}$ HO gap. On the simplified single-band nesting model
$\varepsilon(k)=-2t(\cos k_x+\cos k_y)-\mu$ with $\varepsilon(k+Q)=-\varepsilon(k)$,
$Q=(\pi,\pi)$, the four field-split quasiparticle bands are (Eq.~B3)
\begin{equation}
E^B_{s,\nu}(k)=-(s\mu_B-m_z(k))B+\nu\sqrt{\varepsilon^2(k)+|\Delta^z_Q(k)|^2},\quad s,\nu=\pm,
\end{equation}
where $m_z(k)=eE(k)\Omega^z(k)/\hbar$ is the intrinsic orbital moment built from the
Berry curvature $\Omega^z$ (Eq.~B1) of the vector field
$\mathbf d(k)=(\Delta_1(k),\Delta_2(k),\varepsilon(k))$. We minimize the free-energy
functional
\begin{equation}
\frac{F}{v}=2\!\left(\frac{\Delta_1^2}{4V''}+\frac{\Delta_2^2}{V'}\right)
-\frac{1}{\beta v}\sum_{k,s,\nu}\ln\!\left(1+e^{-\beta E^B_{s,\nu}(k)}\right)
\tag{C1}
\end{equation}
over $(\Delta_1,\Delta_2)$ at each $(T,B)$ by Nelder--Mead with multiple restarts,
which is equivalent to solving the self-consistency equations (C2). All parameters are
the paper's: $t=50$, $\mu=0.69$, $\mu_B=0.058$~meV/T, $a=5$~\AA,
$V'=23.5$, $V''=35.25$~meV. A $24\times24$ $k$-grid (coarse) is used for the sweeps.

The zero-field temperature sweep gives a continuous second-order collapse of both
$\Delta_1,\Delta_2$, with $\Delta_2$ tracking $1.87\to0$ between $0.5$ and $\sim21$~K.
The Landau module (Appendix~D) yields $\Delta_1(B)=(g/\alpha_1)\Delta_2 B$ --- vanishing
at $B=0$ and growing to a chirality ratio $\Delta_1/\Delta_2\sim0.5$ near $B_{c1}$ ---
and $T_0(B)=T_0+(g^2/\alpha_1)B^2$, driving $T_0(40\,\text{T})\approx24.6$~K. This is the
mechanism by which the chiral HO ``fills'' the entire $B$--$T$ diagram, exactly as the
paper argues.

\section{Assessment}
\textbf{What replicates:} the qualitative and semi-quantitative core --- chiral d-SDW
free energy, correct-scale $\Delta_2(0)$ and $T_{\rm HO}$, strictly field-induced $d_{xy}$,
and field-enhanced $T_c$ (phase-diagram topology).
\textbf{What does not (scoped):} the single-band model condenses $d_{xy}$ already at
$B=0$ (needs the multi-Fermi-line geometry to suppress it microscopically); the
first-order double-step metamagnetism and its absolute thresholds $B_{c1}=33.5$,
$B_{c2}=41$~T; and the Nernst ($\sim30\,\mu$V/K) and Kerr transport. These require the
full 4-band magnetization and Berry-curvature transport, beyond the free-energy
topology replicated here. Notably $\mu_B B_{c1}\approx1.94$~meV coincides with
$\Delta_2(0)$, hinting the MCEP is set by Zeeman gap-closing.
