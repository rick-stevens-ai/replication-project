Independent replication of Yasuike et al. 2017 (PLoS ONE, PMID 28257489) — the first complete reference genome for the fish pathogen *Nocardia seriolae* UTF1. Pulled the deposited RefSeq assembly (GCF_002356035.1 / AP017900.1, 8,121,733 bp), re-annotated with Prokka 1.12 on uicgpu (8×A100), and ran reciprocal-best-hit BLASTP against the 4 comparator complete *Nocardia* genomes cited in the paper (N. farcinica IFM 10152, N. brasiliensis HUJEG-1, N. cyriacigeorgica GUH-2, N. nova SH22a). All quantitative claims reproduced to within ≤1%: genome length exact, GC 68.14% vs 68.1%, CDS 7,650 vs 7,697, 12 rRNA (4 operons) exact, core orthologs 2,718 vs 2,745 (99.0%), UTF1-unique 1,967 vs 1,982 (99.2%). Paper's central "greater number of mobile elements" claim strongly supported: UTF1 has 3-6× more transposase/integrase/IS-annotated proteins than any comparator (UTF1=127; others 20–43). Mycobacterial-style virulence orthologs (Mce, catalase/SOD, siderophore) all present. Verdict: REPLICATED.
