# Brief

Sato et al. (2021, PRA 104.052409) present a variational quantum algorithm (VQA) for the 1D Poisson equation that reformulates the linear system Au=f as a minimum-potential-energy problem. The cost function is E_h(θ) = -½ ⟨f|ψ(θ)⟩²/⟨ψ(θ)|A|ψ(θ)⟩, requires only O(1) circuit evaluations per cost call (vs O(n) for prior VQA methods), and recovers the solution norm r = 1/√⟨ψ|A²|ψ⟩ a posteriori. I independently reimplemented the method (statevector simulation, hardware-efficient Ry+CNOT-ladder ansatz with 5 layers, L-BFGS-B optimizer, 10 random-init trials each for n∈{2,3,4,5} qubits, Dirichlet BC) and reproduced the paper's headline Fig.-4 claim of mean trace distance ε_tr < 0.01 for every n, plus the Fig.-3(b) n=5 norm figures (quantum 24.66 vs paper 24.6, classical 25.30 vs paper 25.3). Verdict: REPLICATED.
