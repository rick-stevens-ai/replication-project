# Brief — OSTI 3003302 (Mahesh et al. 2025)

**What:** Independent replication of the methodological claims in *"Huge ensembles Part 1: Design of ensemble weather forecasts using spherical Fourier neural operators"* (Mahesh et al., Geosci. Model Dev. 18, 5575–5603, 2025; DOI 10.5194/gmd-18-5575-2025). The paper trains 29 × 1.1 B-parameter SFNOs on ERA5 (1979–2015) and builds an ML-based ensemble weather forecast (SFNO-BVMC) that uses bred-vector initial-condition perturbations plus multiple checkpoints to produce calibrated probabilistic forecasts benchmarked against IFS ENS.

**Why the scoped approach:** A full retrain of 29 checkpoints costs ~119 k A100-hours (paper reports 16 h × 256 A100 per checkpoint) — categorically infeasible for a subagent slot. Instead: (1) confirm every public artifact is live (GitHub HENS branch of `earth2mip-fork`, HuggingFace `maheshankur10/hens` DOI 10.57967/hf/4200 with 29 open-source checkpoints, DataDryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.2rbnzs80n), (2) independently confirm the SFNO model spec from the HuggingFace `config.json` (74 channels, embed_dim 620, 8 layers, sc=2, ERA5 74-var 6-hourly), and (3) independently re-implement the released bred-vector algorithm from `earth2mip-fork/earth2mip/ensemble_utils.py` and verify its four methodological properties (fastest-growing-mode alignment, spread=error calibration, long-lead saturation, spectral fidelity of members vs mean) on a chaotic Lorenz-96 surrogate (the canonical ensemble-DA testbed). Verdict by LLM-judge on collected evidence: **PARTIAL**.
