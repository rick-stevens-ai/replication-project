# Brief

**Paper:** Troy J. Sewell, Ning Bao, Stephen P. Jordan, *"Variational quantum simulation of the critical Ising model with symmetry averaging"*, Phys. Rev. A **107**, 042620 (2023). OSTI 1974586 · DOI 10.1103/physreva.107.042620 · arXiv:2210.15053.

**What/why:** The paper proposes DMERA (deep multi-scale entanglement renormalization) circuits as a variational ansatz for critical-ground-state preparation, benchmarked on the exactly-solvable 1D critical transverse-field Ising model (TFIM), and introduces *symmetry averaging* to suppress systematic error in observables by up to four orders of magnitude. We independently replicated the paper's **exactly-solvable reference physics** (the ground truth all its error curves are measured against) from scratch in numpy/scipy: the infinite-volume energy density `-4/π`, the free-fermion↔spin equivalence, finite-size convergence, the shallow-circuit residual-error motivation, and the symmetry-averaging cancellation mechanism. The full DMERA matchgate circuit optimization to D=6 was out of scope (needs the paper's appendix circuit parameters). Verdict: **PARTIAL** (core reference physics + mechanisms replicated; end-to-end DMERA performance curves not rerun).
