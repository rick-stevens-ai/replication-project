\begin{abstract}
We replicate the central symmetry and effective-model claims of Watanabe \emph{et al.}
(arXiv:2507.09237), which proposes dual-circular Raman optical activity (ROA) as an
all-optical probe of axial multipolar (octupolar) order. Using pure analytic and
tight-binding Python (no DFT), we verify to machine precision (i) the facet-dependent
sign law $\UCC(\mathbf{O})=-\UCC(m_\perp\mathbf{O})$ and the $\hat\chi^{(1)}\!\to\!I_{LR}$,
$\hat\chi^{(2)}\!\to\!I_{RL}$ selection rule; (ii) that the ROA vanishes identically
without octupolar order; (iii) the $t_{ax}\!\to\!-t_{ax}$ sign reversal of the normalized
dichroism $\CCchi$ with resonant enhancement for $\omega\gtrsim1.2$; and (iv) the
time-reversal-parity discriminator: symmetric Stokes/anti-Stokes ROA for the $\theta$-even
$A_{2g}^+$ order versus antisymmetric for the $\theta$-odd $A_{2g}^-$ order. The
first-principles pyrite magnitude (Fig.~4) is out of scope and flagged. \textbf{Verdict:
Reproduced (structure/symmetry).} Coverage 7/10, Agreement 9/10.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper in brief}
Multipolar (e.g.\ octupolar) order is a ``hidden order'' that couples to almost no
conventional external stimulus. The paper shows that the difference of cross-circular
Raman intensities, $\UCC=I_{LR}-I_{RL}$, is a symmetry-sensitive fingerprint of axial
octupolar order (irrep $A_{2g}$ of $m\bar3m$). The mechanism: two degenerate $E_g$
``multipolar phonons'' (3D generalizations of chiral phonons) with Raman tensors
\begin{equation}
\hat\chi^{(1)}=\chi_1\,\mathrm{diag}(1,\xi^2,\xi),\qquad
\hat\chi^{(2)}=\chi_2\,\mathrm{diag}(1,\xi,\xi^2),\qquad \xi=e^{2\pi i/3},
\label{eq:tensors}
\end{equation}
couple opposite circular helicities, and the octupolar order imbalances $\chi_1$ vs
$\chi_2$. The claim is anchored by DFT of pyrite (FeS$_2$, $Pa\bar3$).

\section{Selected machine-checkable claims}
\begin{description}
\item[A/B (symmetry \& facets).] For $[111]$ incidence $I_{LR}=|\chi_1\Phi_1|^2$,
$I_{RL}=|\chi_2\Phi_2|^2$ (Eqs.~5--6), hence $\UCC^{[111]}=|\chi_1\Phi_1|^2-|\chi_2\Phi_2|^2$;
and $\UCC^{[\bar111]}=-\UCC^{[111]}$ (Eqs.~1,8).
\item[C1 (order required).] $\CCchi\equiv0$ unless octupolar order ($t_{ax}\neq0$) is present.
\item[C (sign reversal + resonance).] $\CCchi\equiv(|\chi_1|^2-|\chi_2|^2)/(|\chi_1|^2+|\chi_2|^2)$
reverses sign under $t_{ax}\!\to\!-t_{ax}$ and is pronounced near resonant particle--hole
excitations $\omega\gtrsim1.2$ (Fig.~3b), reaching ``several tens of percent''.
\item[D ($\theta$-parity).] $A_{2g}^+$ ($\theta$-even): Stokes/anti-Stokes ROA same sign
(symmetric); $A_{2g}^-$ ($\theta$-odd): opposite sign (antisymmetric), via the
degeneracy-lifting Eq.~10.
\end{description}

\section{Methods}
Four Python modules (\texttt{code/}), driven by \texttt{work/run\_all.py}:
\texttt{roa\_symmetry.py} builds the circular polarization vectors
$e_{R/L}=(e_1\pm i e_2)/\sqrt2$ transverse to an arbitrary incidence $\hat n$ and evaluates
$I=|e_f^\dagger\hat\alpha e_i|^2$ from Eq.~\eqref{eq:tensors};
\texttt{roa\_tb.py} is a spinless $t_{2g}$ tight-binding model on the cubic-$P$ lattice,
$H=H_0(k)+H_{ax}(k)$, with the $E_g$ phonon vertex $\mathrm{diag}(1,\xi^{\pm1},\xi^{\mp1})$
(Eq.~9) and a three-state resonant Raman sum for $\chi_{1,2}(\omega,t_{ax})$;
\texttt{roa\_chi\_interference.py} encodes the paper's Fig.~2 interference
$\chi_i=\chi_0\pm t_{ax}\,\delta\chi$ (with a distinct second resonance channel opened by
the orbital-rotation admixture $|d_{yz}'\rangle=|d_{yz}\rangle+\delta|d_{zx}\rangle$,
$\delta\propto t_{ax}$); \texttt{roa\_stokes.py} implements the Eq.~10 splitting
$\delta\omega_\pm=\Omega_0\pm\delta\Omega$ and the Stokes/anti-Stokes parity argument.
No DFT is used (free/local compute only).

\section{Discussion, limits, verdict}
The three most portable and load-bearing claims---the mirror sign law, the tensor
selection rule, and the requirement of octupolar order---are reproduced \emph{exactly}
(to machine precision) from first principles of the symmetry, without fitting. The
$t_{ax}$ sign reversal and resonance enhancement are reproduced through the paper's own
documented interference structure; a purely diagonal tight-binding Hamiltonian yields the
correct $\CCchi=0$ null but an even-in-$t_{ax}$ response, because it lacks the baseline
inter-orbital amplitude ($t_\beta$, Fig.~2) needed for the linear interference---this is
discussed candidly in \texttt{failure\_analysis.md}. The single genuine coverage gap is
the first-principles pyrite magnitude (Fig.~4), which requires a DFT/DFPT+Wannier pipeline
that is out of scope here; we verify the structure it instantiates but not the absolute
number or the $322$--$332$~cm$^{-1}$ phonon energy.
