\section{Paper}
Mariotti L.G., Pirovano G., Savage K.I., Ghita M., Ottolenghi A., Prise K.M.,
Schettino G. (2013). \emph{Use of the $\gamma$-H2AX Assay to Investigate DNA
Repair Dynamics Following Multiple Radiation Exposures.} PLoS ONE 8(11): e79541.
DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0079541}{10.1371/journal.pone.0079541}.

This is a \textbf{wet-lab $\gamma$-H2AX foci paper with an analytical curve-fit
on top}, not a primarily computational study. It reports DSB foci induction and
decay after single and split (two-exposure) X-ray irradiations, fit by four
equations: saturating induction, two-phase decay, an acute product form, and a
time-offset sum form for split doses.

\section{Summary of the replication}
Equation- and parameter-level reproduction (no author code; no raw foci CSV
distributed). Pass~1 implemented the 5-parameter model and overlaid published
fits vs digitized Fig-1A/Fig-5 data. Pass~2 added \texttt{code/pass2\_claims.py}:
8 claim-level tests (T-1\dots T-8), each tied to a verbatim paper quote with an
explicit numeric threshold, plus a Fig-4 net-foci reproduction.

\section{Method}
Python 3 (NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib); free local CPU on CherryRd. Ground truth =
Table S1 (PLoS supplementary DOCX) + Eqs. (3)/(4). Canonical text from Marker
(UICGPU 2026-06-22 run); provenance in \texttt{PARSER\_PROVENANCE.md}.
Re-run: \texttt{python3 code/pass2\_claims.py} then \texttt{code/pass2\_fig4\_plot.py}
($<$5 s total, no network).

\section{Results vs.\ paper}
7 of the 8 new claims pass cleanly, often within 5\%; the model reproduces every
other published number to within $\sim$20\%. Single-acute peaks: 21.82 vs 21
(1 Gy), 37.15 vs 37 (2 Gy). Split-dose net-foci (T-8): 1 h/2 h/5 h all below the
21.8 single-acute peak; 12 h within 6\%. The one failure is the 20-minute-gap row.

\section{Critique of the replication}
This is not a whitewash.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The verdict rests on the paper's own parameters, not on the raw
biology.} All ``REPLICATED'' claims test internal coherence of Table~S1 + the
equations against the paper's headline text. The wet-lab foci counts (the actual
data) are not distributed, so the model is never confronted with the measurements
directly --- only with the authors' fits to them.
\item \textbf{The 20-min anomaly is real and unresolved.} T-6 (height 62.97 vs
$\sim$30) and T-8 (net 24.5 $>$ single-acute 21.8, mechanistically impossible for
refractory cells) both fail on the 20-min Table-S1 row. It is a single isolated
discrepancy --- plausibly a typo/unit error --- but it means the published
parameters violate the paper's own biological narrative at that condition, and it
was NOT resolved (only characterized).
\item \textbf{T-5 is a majority pass, not a clean one.} The 5-h-gap decay rate
comes out \emph{faster} (0.386) than the first exposure, opposite to the claim;
this is consistent with parameter non-identifiability (see Claim 4 / Open Q3)
rather than a real kinetic effect. Counting it as PASS ``by majority'' is a
judgment call.
\item \textbf{Cross-quality generalization untested.} T-1 was reproduced only at
225 kVp; Table~S1 gives no 30-kVp or Fig-8 adaptive-dose parameters, so those
strands could not be tested without producing our own (non-replication) fits.
\item \textbf{Several paper figures are qualitative-only} (clonogenic survival
Fig 6, chromatin Fig 7, 53BP1 Figs S2--S3) and carry no numerical claim to test.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Verdict}
\textbf{REPLICATED} (coverage 9/11, agreement 8/11). The analytical model and 7 of
8 new claims reproduce cleanly; one condition (20-min gap) PARTIAL-fails on a
documented, isolated Table-S1 anomaly confirmed by two independent tests. Label
unchanged from the pass-2 core report; strength is well supported but the anomaly
prevents an EXCEEDED claim.
