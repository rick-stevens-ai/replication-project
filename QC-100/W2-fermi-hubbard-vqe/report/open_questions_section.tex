\section{Open questions}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Scaling beyond 12 qubits.} Does the HV depth-vs-accuracy scaling
      hold on genuinely 2D lattices ($3{\times}3$, $4{\times}4$, $2{\times}6$)
      where Fermi--Hubbard physics is interesting? \emph{Next:} replay the HV
      pipeline on an MPS/tensor-network backend (quimb, ITensor) up to 18--32
      qubits; compare against DMRG ground truth; report depth to reach $10^{-3}$
      relative error as a function of $L$ for 1D vs.\ 2D.
\item \textbf{HV vs.\ modern adaptive ans\"atze.} How does HV compare
      quantitatively to ADAPT-VQE / qubit-ADAPT / k-UpCCGSD at matched
      parameter or CNOT count on the same lattices? \emph{Next:} reimplement
      ADAPT-VQE with a fermion-hopping$+$onsite operator pool; publish a
      head-to-head plot of energy error vs.\ two-qubit-gate count.
\item \textbf{Strong-coupling and doped regimes.} This replication only
      exercised $U/t=2$ at half-filling. Is HV still robust at $U/t\in\{8,12,20\}$
      and at fillings $n\in\{0.5,0.75,0.875\}$? \emph{Next:} sweep $U/t$ and
      $n$ on the 2$\times$2 and 1$\times$6 lattices; compare $U=0$ reference
      against Hartree--Fock and Gutzwiller-projected references; report depth
      needed for $10^{-3}$ error, or failure to converge.
\item \textbf{Shot noise and gate error.} At the depths ($\sim8$) needed for
      12-qubit convergence, how far does the reproduced error deteriorate under
      realistic sampling and two-qubit noise? \emph{Next:} rewrap the HV
      circuits in Qiskit Aer with configurable shots ($10^3$--$10^5$) and
      depolarizing channels ($p\in\{10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2}\}$); map the
      $(\text{depth},p)$ frontier beyond which depth stops helping.
\item \textbf{ML-selected ansatz structure.} Can a symmetry-aware or
      GNN-trained ranker propose better HV block orderings/splittings than the
      hand-designed onsite/horizontal-hop/vertical-hop scheme at matched depth?
      \emph{Next:} enumerate HV permutations on 2$\times$2 and 1$\times$4;
      train a small ranker on depth-1 gradient norm and depth-4 optimized
      energy; apply to 2$\times$3 / 1$\times$6 and check whether the top pick
      beats vanilla HV at matched depth.
\end{enumerate}
