# Brief — BVBRC-51

**What:** Independent replication of Pal, Sharma & Subramanian (2021, *BMC Genomics*), the complete-genome announcement of the myxobacterium *Minicystis rosea* DSM 24000T — reported as the **largest bacterial genome sequenced to date** (16.04 Mbp) — and the identification of its polyunsaturated-fatty-acid (PUFA) *pfa* biosynthetic gene cluster.

**Why replicable:** The genome is a single complete circular chromosome deposited publicly (GenBank CP016211.1 / assembly GCA_001931535.1, BioProject PRJNA321464). The paper's headline claims are genome statistics (size, GC, gene counts, coding density), an antiSMASH BGC survey, and BLAST/synteny identification of the *pfa* PUFA cluster — all reproducible from the public assembly + free tools (NCBI Datasets, BLAST+, antiSMASH). BV-BRC-mappable workflow: Comprehensive Genome Analysis (assembly stats + RASTtk-style annotation) + specialty/secondary-metabolite gene detection (antiSMASH).
