# Brief

Independent replication of Jurrus et al. 2017/2018 *"Improvements to the APBS biomolecular solvation software suite"* (DOI 10.1002/pro.3280, Protein Science 27:112–128, cited ~2081 times). APBS is an open-source Poisson–Boltzmann electrostatics solver; the paper documents the v1.5-era package plus the PDB2PQR structure-preparation front-end and bundles regression tests with published expected values across all historic APBS releases. I installed APBS 3.4.1 + PDB2PQR 3.6.1 fresh via conda-forge, ran the paper's own bundled regression tests (Born ion analytical, methanol/methoxide solvation with `srfm mol` and `srfm smol`), and exercised the end-to-end pipeline on hen egg-white lysozyme (PDB 1AKI) with focused multigrid at 150 mM ionic strength. All bundled tests matched documented per-version values to ≥4 decimals (methanol/methoxide to 6+ decimals), the Born-ion analytical benchmark matched to 0.45%, and the lysozyme pipeline produced physically reasonable polar solvation energies (~-4.3 MJ/mol, 2% grid-convergence). LLM judge (Argo `gpt-5`, free endpoint) verdict: **REPLICATED**, coverage 92%, agreement 99%.
