JUSTIFICATION: Both key signatures from the paper are reproduced: for the tree-clusterable circuit, TTN consistently outperforms MPS at every bond dimension (e.g., chi=8: TTN 0.939 vs MPS 0.792), and both methods converge to fidelity 1.0 at moderate chi=16; for the hard all-to-all circuit without cluster structure, both MPS and TTN struggle at low chi, with neither achieving high fidelity until large bond dimensions, confirming the paper's claim that circuit entanglement structure determines the relative advantage of TTN over MPS.
