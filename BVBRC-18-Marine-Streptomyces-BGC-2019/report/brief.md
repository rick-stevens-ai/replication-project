# BVBRC-18 — Brief

**Paper:** Xu L, Ye KX, Dai WH, Sun C, Xu LH, Han BN.
*Comparative Genomic Insights into Secondary Metabolism Biosynthetic Gene Cluster Distributions of Marine Streptomyces.*
Marine Drugs (2019). **DOI:** [10.3390/md17090498](https://doi.org/10.3390/md17090498) — PMID 31454987 — PMCID PMC6780079.

## Core claims
1. 87 marine *Streptomyces* genomes show high genetic diversity (123,302 orthologous clusters in the pan-genome).
2. Phylogenomic analysis sorts the strains into **3 clades** — Clade I (23 strains), Clade II (38), Clade III (22).
3. Secondary-metabolism biosynthetic gene clusters (**SMBGCs**) per genome range from **16 to 84**.
4. SMBGC distribution correlates with phylotype (clade) and ecotype (sediment vs invertebrate source). Clade I + sediment strains carry more *specific* SMBGCs; Clade II + invertebrate-derived strains carry more total SMBGCs.

## Replication scope (short pass)
- Confirm enough marine *Streptomyces* genomes exist in BV-BRC to reconstruct the 87-strain corpus.
- Confirm typical genome sizes match what would produce the observed 16–84 SMBGC range under antiSMASH.
- Note that pan-genome + antiSMASH + phylogenomics rerun is out of scope for a metadata pass.
