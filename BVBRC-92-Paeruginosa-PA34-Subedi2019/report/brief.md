# Brief

**Paper:** Subedi D, Kohli GS, Vijay AK, Willcox M, Rice SA (2019). *Accessory genome of the multi-drug resistant ocular isolate of Pseudomonas aeruginosa PA34.* PLoS ONE 14(4):e0215038. PMID 30986237. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0215038.

**What it claims (in one paragraph):** The authors sequenced (Illumina + ONT MinION hybrid, closed by PCR) the complete genome of *P. aeruginosa* PA34 — a multi-drug-resistant microbial keratitis isolate from an Indian eye — yielding a 6.81 Mbp chromosome (66.1% GC, 6,462 CDS, 6,314 protein-coding) plus two plasmids pMKPA34-1 (95.4 kbp, 57% GC, 98 CDS) and pMKPA34-2 (26.8 kbp, 61% GC, 33 CDS). Pan-genome comparison with PAO1/PA14/VRFPA04 (Roary) gave 7,643 total orthologs of which 5,078 form the core and PA34's accessory portion is 1,213 genes (543 unique). They identified 24 regions of genomic plasticity (RGPs / GIs) — including two novel loci MKPA34-GI1 (chromate + mercury, 68.6 kbp) and MKPA34-GI2 (phage MP38, 35.9 kbp) — three integrative conjugative elements, an exoU island (RGP7, functional cytotoxic effector), AAC(3)-IId in RGP23, two independent mercury resistance operons, and six antibiotic resistance genes on pMKPA34-1 (dfrA15/cmlA1/APH(3")-Ib/APH(6)-Id/blaNPS-1/acrB) with a Tn3-like transposon + class I integron In1427, plus mepA + Tn7 on pMKPA34-2. Phenotypically, PA34 was highly mercury-tolerant (p<0.05 vs PAO1) and cytotoxic to human corneal epithelial cells.

**What we did:** Independent replication using the three deposited GenBank records (CP032552, MH547560, MH547561) plus the three reference genomes (PAO1 AE004091, PA14 CP000438, VRFPA04 CP008739). Computed Table 2 statistics from scratch. Re-ran the pan-genome analysis with DIAMOND + MCL clustering (Roary-style). Verified every specific AMR / virulence / metal-resistance / phage gene claim by parsing the deposited GenBank annotations. Cross-checked against BV-BRC's independent Antibiotic-Resistance annotation of the same isolate (genome_id 287.6355, BioSample SAMN08435059).

**Verdict:** **PARTIAL REPLICATION (strong)** — Table 2 statistics reproduce exactly (6,810,079 bp / 6,544 genes / 6,462 CDS / 6,314 proteins / 65 tRNAs / 12 rRNAs), plasmid sizes / GC / CDS counts reproduce, PA34 accessory-genome size reproduces within 1% (1,206 vs paper's 1,213), every specific AMR gene claim on both plasmids and the two chromosomal AMR/virulence hotspots (RGP7 exoU, RGP23 AAC(3)-IId + tunicamycin + copper + gp37, two independent mer operons in MKPA34-GI1 + RGP5, chromate in MKPA34-GI1) is directly verified from the deposited annotation. Pan-genome core / unique-gene *counts* differ by 8–22% because we used DIAMOND+MCL at 50% identity instead of the paper's Roary defaults — the top-level accessory number matches, but the internal breakdown does not reproduce exactly. Phenotypic assays (MIC to Hg/Cu/Co, cytotoxicity) were not re-attempted (no strain in hand, biosafety).
