# BVBRC-74 Brief

**Paper:** Satti SM, Castro-Aguirre E, Shah AA, Marsh TL, Auras R. *Genome Annotation of Poly(lactic acid) Degrading Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Sphingobacterium sp. and Geobacillus sp.* Int J Mol Sci **22**(14):7385, 2021. DOI 10.3390/ijms22147385. PMID 34299026. PMC8305213.

**Why:** BV-BRC workflow claim (Genome Assembly + Comprehensive Genome Analysis / RASTtk annotation) on Illumina reads from three PLA-degrading compost isolates: *P. aeruginosa* S3, *Sphingobacterium* sp. S2, *Geobacillus* sp. EC-3. Independent SPAdes assembly (SPAdes 4.3.0, `--isolate` mode) of the paper's own raw reads (PRJNA721072/SRP149807) is used to test the paper's quantitative genome-summary claims (Table 1): contigs, GC content, genome length, CDS count, tRNA/rRNA count, plus the paper's ANI-based species assignments to *P. aeruginosa* PSE305, *S. thalpophilum* NCTC11429/DSM11723, and *G. thermoleovorans* CCB_US3_UF5.
