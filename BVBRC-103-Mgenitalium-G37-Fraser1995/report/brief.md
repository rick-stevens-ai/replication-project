# Brief

Fraser et al. (1995) reported the complete 580,070-bp genome of *Mycoplasma genitalium* G37 — at the time the smallest known genome of any self-replicating organism — with ~470 protein-coding genes, ~32% G+C, a single rRNA operon, and 36 tRNAs covering all 20 amino acids. This replication independently redownloaded the same genome via free NCBI E-utilities (RefSeq NC_000908.2, the current curated assembly of the Fraser/TIGR 1995 sequence with minor post-publication corrections) and recomputed every headline quantitative claim directly with Biopython 1.87 on local CPU. Purpose: verify that the paper's abstract-level numbers hold up 30 years later against the current authoritative annotation and that the minimal-genome landmark result is independently checkable from public artifacts alone.
