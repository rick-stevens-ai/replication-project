\section{Open Questions}

The following five questions are genuinely open after the replication:
the paper is a competent single-cell-pair single-source study, and its
generalizability, its independence-from-wet-lab-fit, and its dosimetric
detail all admit further work using \emph{free / open} computational
endpoints.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Source-modality invariance of the HepG2-R phenotype.}
        The near-zero-$\alpha$, larger-$\beta$ LQ shape of HepG2-R was fit
        on a single Co-60 geometry. Does it survive a LINAC 6-MV vs
        orthovoltage 250-kVp swap?
        \emph{Next steps:} meta-analysis of published HepG2/HepG2-R
        clonogenic data across sources; refit LQ per series; test
        source-invariance. PubMed + Google Scholar only.

  \item \textbf{First-principles RBE prediction independent of the wet-lab fit.}
        Can the RBE amplification $\mathrm{RBE}_{\mathrm{HepG2\text{-}R}}
        / \mathrm{RBE}_{\mathrm{HepG2}} \approx 1.62$ be predicted from the
        LET spectrum of the thermal-neutron + $^{10}$B(n,$\alpha$) field
        via MKM/LEM-IV, \emph{without} using the paper's wet-lab
        dose-survival fit?
        \emph{Next steps:} feed a published open THOR-reactor MCNP/Geant4
        deck into MKM with HepG2-family parameters; compute predicted
        $\mathrm{RBE}_{10}$; compare vs 3.68 / 5.97. Single-node CPU.

  \item \textbf{Cross-tumor-type generalization of BNCT's selective
        advantage on radioresistant lines.}
        Does BNCT retain its selective edge on other radioresistant
        tumors (glioblastoma stem cells, PDAC, radioresistant melanoma)
        with different repair backgrounds (BRCA1/2, ATM)?
        \emph{Next steps:} DepMap + Cellosaurus cross-reference of
        published BNCT clonogenic panels; rank resistance archetypes
        by expected BNCT benefit.

  \item \textbf{Sensitivity of RBE to intra-tumoral $^{10}$B uptake
        heterogeneity.}
        Because $^{10}$B(n,$\alpha$) dose scales linearly with local B-10
        concentration and the $\alpha$ range is $\sim 10~\mu$m, uptake
        heterogeneity translates directly to dose heterogeneity. The paper
        reports only a mean 58--59 ppm plateau.
        \emph{Next steps:} lognormal per-cell uptake with CV in
        $\{0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 1.0\}$; compute predicted SF distribution
        vs paper mean. Python notebook, no wet lab.

  \item \textbf{ML-driven boron-carrier / dose-schedule optimization.}
        Can a Bayesian-optimization pipeline over F-18-BPA PET voxel B-10
        maps + thermal-neutron fluence maps produce a per-fraction dose
        that minimizes normal-liver dose subject to a tumor $D_{95}$
        constraint?
        \emph{Next steps:} prototype with QIN/QIBA BPA-PET phantoms.
        Simulation only.
\end{enumerate}
