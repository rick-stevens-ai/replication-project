   A significant motivation for using a tile-based instruction set is
that tiles are a natural basis for the placement3 and scheduling of lat-                              Z
tice surgery operations. While quantum circuits nominally concern
logical qubits only, data movement and/or non-local operations

generalized this protocol to implement long-range CNOT opera-

the targeted qubit tiles. We find that tiles are convenient units for
mapping out patches, identifying paths between them, and tracking
